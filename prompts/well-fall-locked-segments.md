## A — Lip of the Well (0:00–0:10) · clean harmonics + drip

**Start still**
```
An old round well in dry California hills at late afternoon, a waist-high ring of mortared fieldstone and wet red brick, the mouth a perfect black circle with no visible bottom. No bucket, no rope, no people. Bleached golden grass, a single live oak far off, a pale sky with no clouds. The job of the frame: this is the last level ground before the fall. We stand at the lip looking down at a slight angle, the near stones close enough to touch, moss and a dark water stain running down the inner face, one drip catching light as it leaves a brick. Low warm sun raking across the stone, inside the mouth everything drops to crushed black within two courses. Photoreal cinematic still, 35mm anamorphic, 2.39:1, shallow depth on the near lip, gentle halation on the grass, film grain. Continuity lock: circular wet brick and fieldstone well, crushed blacks, no text, no titles, no HUD, no band, no people.
```

**Motion (10s)**
```
Ten-second photoreal POV shot. Subject: an old fieldstone and brick well mouth in dry California hills. The camera holds on the lip for three seconds, a single drip falls from an inner brick into the dark, then the camera tips forward over the edge and commits: the horizon swings up and out of frame, the stone ring passes the lens, and the circular masonry becomes a tunnel rushing upward around us. By second ten we are in full free fall, wet brick courses streaking past in a spiral blur, the mouth above us a shrinking disk of warm sky. No hands, no body, no cut, no slow motion, the fall accelerates and never stops. Light goes from raking golden sun to cold blue spill from the mouth. Photoreal cinematic, 2.39:1 anamorphic, crushed blacks, film grain. Continuity lock: POV fall in a circular wet brick shaft, no text, no titles, no HUD, no band, no people. Audio muted.
```
