STYLE:

Sincere 2000s Dutch and Belgian jumpstyle with a hands-up trance melody, 150 BPM, A minor, about two and a half minutes. Hard distorted jumpstyle kick on every beat, offbeat bass stabs, a bright hands-up supersaw lead, piano breakdown, rolling snare builds, and a crowd shout of "Hey!" on the first beat of every section. Lead one: bright, conspiratorial, a hype MC with sudden energy spikes. Lead two: dry, slightly raspy, flat, counting floor numbers between sections, getting very slightly out of breath on floor five only. The kicks stop for one bar at each landing, then restart. Ends on the sixth-floor landing with silence and a single elevator ding. Pumping, euphoric, sincere, no parody, no comedy sound effects except the ding. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
RUBELLA: Car two is holding a position.
LILLITH: Then we jump.

[Verse 1]
LILLITH: Bags in your hands and your heels in your bag
Trainers on, trainers on, pick up the flag
Step on the kick and the kick is a stair
Everybody jump, we're going up there

[Chorus]
LILLITH: Jump, jump, up the stairs
RUBELLA: One.
LILLITH: Jump, jump, nobody cares
RUBELLA: Two.
LILLITH: Jump, jump, the lift won't come
RUBELLA: Three.
LILLITH: Jump, jump, we're nearly done
RUBELLA: We're not.

[Verse 2]
LILLITH: Kick to the left and a kick to the right
Mind the bicycle, mind the light
Every landing's a festival floor
RUBELLA: Four.

[Breakdown]
RUBELLA: Five.
I need a moment.
That was the moment.

[Final Chorus]
LILLITH: Jump, jump, up the stairs
RUBELLA: Five.
LILLITH: Jump, jump, nobody cares
RUBELLA: Six.
LILLITH: Jump, jump, the lift won't come
RUBELLA: Six.
LILLITH: Jump, jump, and we're home!

[Outro]

NOTES:

**Callbacks:**
- **"Car two is holding a position."** The maintenance-ticket line for the freight elevator
  (a portable form from the bank). Nobody says it's broken; malfunction versus negotiation is
  never ruled on.
- The **ding** at the very end: the elevator arrives at six the moment they get there.
  Nobody comments.

**Do-not:** no thirteenth floor, no elevator singing or speaking (it says floor numbers only,
and here it doesn't even do that). Nobody falls.
