# Le Croissant

**Genre collision:** 1900s café-concert chanson × late-1970s Paris disco (string section, four-on-the-floor, wah guitar, a mirror ball). The verses are in French and the hook is in English.

**Visual concept:** It's the morning after *La Baguette*, in the same small town at the same bakery, now with a café table outside. Rubella sits down with a coffee and one croissant. Lillith sits opposite with a wah-pedal guitar. Rubella takes one bite, and the flakes don't stop: a gold snowfall of pastry flakes falls on the terrace, then the street, then the whole town. Cars are buried to their aerials, children build a snowman with a madeleine nose, a snowplough pushes back a golden wall, and every pigeon in France arrives for lunch. Rubella's black lace stays perfectly clean except for one flake.

**Laugh (kid words):** *She takes one bite of a croissant and the crumbs snow over the whole town.*

**BPM:** 118, four-on-the-floor, A minor, lifting to C major

**Target length:** ~2:20 · 13 segments
