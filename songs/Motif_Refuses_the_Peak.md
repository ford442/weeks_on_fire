STYLE:

Melodic techno, progressive techno, 126 BPM, G minor. Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics, no choir, no crowd, no DJ announcement. Starts already moving: four-on-the-floor kick, short analog bass, dry hat pattern. Adds one layer every thirty-two bars — open hat, second chord voicing, higher sequenced arp, wider bass. Arrangement reaches its fullest point in the second half and then refuses the peak: the high arp pulls back, the kick and the original two-note motif keep running to the end. No festival riser, no white-noise jet, no mute-the-kick drop setup, no vocal chop, no guitar, no orchestral trailer drums. Work-session peak that stays usable. Clean modern club production, wide stereo, analog filters. About six minutes.
