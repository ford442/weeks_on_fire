# Guitar Boat — more ideas (Yacht Guitar Orchestra drawer)

**Kind:** idea seeds, drawer only · `promote?: no`
**Parent:** `ideas/yacht-guitar-orchestra.md` · scenes A–T in `prompts/yacht-guitar-orchestra-segments.md`
**Beds:** Yacht Guitar Orchestra (96 BPM daylight) · Nofo i te Vai (88 BPM dark island) · Seoul at Night (128 BPM Busan)
**Farm:** picture → [#22](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/22) · songs → [#18](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/18)/[#33](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/33) · never #42

These are seeds only. The three-song journey (daylight → dark island → Busan night, scenes A–T)
stays the spine and is not rewritten here. Every idea below is either an insert that drops into
that cut, an alternate corridor for a later re-cut, or a longer visual experiment. Each one keeps the
continuity lock from the segments doc:
- the same white yacht, with Rubella and Lillith on their two white lounges in black lace and sunglasses, not performing, mouths closed;
- the ivory guitar line along the whole rail, facing out, swaying slowly, mouths closed;
- no landing, inert cannons, hard cuts between beds, Imagine audio muted.

Each world keeps its light: daylight is bright, the dark island is orange/red only, Busan is RGB only.

Nobody on the yacht ever reacts. The joke is that the world keeps time with the sway and the boat
doesn't notice.

## 1. The pelican sits in

- **Laugh:** A pelican lands on the rail in the gap between two guitar players and sways along with them.
- **Fits:** any-bar insert, daylight leg (after B or C).
- **Core visual:** Medium telephoto along the rail. A brown pelican glides in and settles on the chrome rail in the one arm's-length gap between two ivory dresses. It folds its wings and starts leaning left and right in perfect unison with the line. Nobody looks at it.
- **Drop / change:** It lands on a downbeat and starts swaying exactly on the next one.
- **Length:** 6 s clip, trims to 4–5 s.
- **Locks:** The pelican is an animal, not cast, and it holds no guitar. Nobody boards, since it's a bird on the rail. Daylight light only, no world props.

## 2. Shadow clock

- **Laugh:** From straight above, the guitar shadows tick across the deck like one big clock.
- **Fits:** daylight leg, after D (chord ripple).
- **Core visual:** Locked overhead on the pale side deck at midday. A row of guitar-and-woman shadows falls across the planks, and all the necks lean left together, then right together. The real players are at the frame edge and only their shadows are fully in frame.
- **Drop / change:** On the bed's swell, the shadows swing one full cycle as a single shape.
- **Length:** 10 s clip, trims to 8 s.
- **Locks:** The sway stays at the locked five-second cycle, so nothing speeds up. No faces, so mouths are not an issue. Same deck, same boat.

## 3. The sea keeps time

- **Laugh:** The whole ocean starts rocking in time with the band.
- **Fits:** daylight leg, between C and D.
- **Core visual:** Low wide shot from the water with the yacht side-on. A long, slow swell rises and falls exactly with the line's lean, so the horizon tips a few degrees with them. The yacht stays level, and the sea is the thing that moves.
- **Drop / change:** The swell locks to the sway on the bar where Harmonic Choir stacks in.
- **Length:** 10 s clip.
- **Locks:** The water stays gentle, with no peril and no spray. The yacht never moves toward land. One effect only: the swell.

## 4. Kite string

- **Laugh:** One guitar string comes loose and floats away in the breeze like a kite string.
- **Fits:** any-bar insert, daylight leg (a sibling to G, the pick over the rail, so don't use both back to back).
- **Core visual:** Close on a headstock at the rail. The top string slips its tuning peg and lifts out into the breeze, a long silver thread curling up into blue sky over turquoise water. The player keeps holding the chord shape, and her face stays out of frame.
- **Drop / change:** The string lifts free on the downbeat and is gone by the next bar.
- **Length:** 6 s clip, trims to 4 s.
- **Locks:** The players keep playing on, with no strumming and no reaction. There's no sound gag; the bed owns the sound.

## 5. Iceberg lane (alternate corridor)

- **Laugh:** The guitar boat sails through icebergs, and the penguins on them sway along too.
- **Fits:** alternate hard-cut destination. Replaces or precedes the daylight island in a later re-cut.
- **Core visual:** The white yacht glides slowly down a channel of tall white-blue icebergs on dark glassy water, so the white boat sits among white ice. On one low berg a row of penguins stands facing outward, leaning in unison with the ivory line on the rail. Rubella and Lillith lie on their lounges in black lace, the only dark shapes in a white world.
- **Drop / change:** A hard cut on the downbeat into the corridor. The penguins pick up the sway one beat late, then lock to it.
- **Length:** 15 s clip.
- **Bed:** Fits Yacht Guitar Orchestra, Low Orbit layer. If it's used as a fourth leg, it needs a **new short bed** (flag it to #18/#33). Don't borrow a fourth existing song.
- **Locks:** There is no landing and nobody steps onto the ice. Cold daylight only. The penguins are props, not cast. No snow or flowers, so it's not the mountain lion's saved snow corridor.

## 6. Sea arch duck

- **Laugh:** The boat goes under a rock arch, and the whole band dips their guitars at once to fit.
- **Fits:** alternate corridor, daylight leg (before the island gets large).
- **Core visual:** Wide from the water. The yacht passes through a natural sea arch of dark volcanic rock, and the roof only just clears the guitar necks. As the bow goes under, every player tips her neck down together, then back up on the far side. The lounges pass beneath in shadow and nobody stirs.
- **Drop / change:** All the necks dip on one beat and rise on the next.
- **Length:** 10 s clip.
- **Locks:** It's the same island rock, but the boat doesn't touch it. The guitar dip is the one subject motion, and the sway resumes after. Daylight only.

## 7. Glowing wake (Busan approach)

- **Laugh:** The boat leaves a glowing green trail behind it like a giant snail.
- **Fits:** night approach, before P or between R and S.
- **Core visual:** High stern view on black open water. The yacht's wake glows with blue-green bioluminescence, a long luminous V running back into the dark. The guitar line on the stern rail sways in silhouette above it, lit from below.
- **Drop / change:** The glow switches on along the wake on the first downbeat of a chorus.
- **Length:** 10 s clip, trims to 8 s.
- **Locks:** Busan leg only, because green and blue are in the RGB palette. No orange, no other boats, and the city is not yet in frame. **Do not put this on the dark island**, which is orange and red only.

## 8. Fog line

- **Laugh:** The fog rolls in so thick that only the guitar necks stick out, like a row of fence posts.
- **Fits:** dark island, between N and O.
- **Core visual:** Night, side-on from the water. A low bank of fog slides over the yacht's deck up to chest height, swallowing the dresses and lounges. Only the guitar headstocks show above it in a neat row, still swaying, lit orange from the beach fires.
- **Drop / change:** The fog covers the line player by player across one bar. Hold, then hard cut to O.
- **Length:** 10 s clip, trims to 8 s.
- **Locks:** Orange and red light only. The lounges are just covered by the fog, not emptied. Nobody leaves.

## 9. Something big underneath

- **Laugh:** A giant whale shadow slides under the boat, and nobody looks down.
- **Fits:** any-bar insert, daylight leg (best near H, but not adjacent to it).
- **Core visual:** High drone over turquoise water. A huge dark whale shape glides slowly beneath the white yacht from stern to bow, and its shadow is longer than the boat. The guitar line keeps swaying and facing outward, and the two lounges stay still.
- **Drop / change:** The shadow's head emerges past the bow exactly on a bar line.
- **Length:** 10 s clip.
- **Locks:** The whale doesn't surface and doesn't touch the boat, so there's no peril. It's one animal for one insert, with no dolphin in the same shot.

## 10. Coffee rings

- **Laugh:** Even the coffee in the mug is dancing.
- **Fits:** dark island (the mug is three-quarters full there). The Busan mug is empty, so this is island only.
- **Core visual:** Macro on the plain white mug on the side table, with its text turned away. The surface of the coffee shows slow concentric rings, one ring per sway cycle, catching the orange firelight. Soft black lace is out of focus behind it.
- **Drop / change:** Each ring starts on the sway's turn.
- **Length:** 6 s clip, trims to 4 s.
- **Locks:** This uses an existing continuity prop, so it doesn't become a subject beyond this one insert. Nobody touches the mug, and the light is orange only.

## 11. The fires lean too

- **Laugh:** The campfires on the beach lean left and right with the band.
- **Fits:** dark island, after K.
- **Core visual:** Long lens on the row of small fires on the black sand. Every flame leans the same few degrees left, then right, in unison with the line, though there is no wind. The rusted cannons sit dark and still at the end of the row.
- **Drop / change:** The flames start leaning on the first beat of the chorus.
- **Length:** 10 s clip, trims to 8 s.
- **Locks:** The fires stay small and controlled and don't spread. The cannons stay inert with no smoke. Orange and red only. This is the beach-fire sibling of F's vine variation, so use one or the other.

## 12. The bridge slows down

- **Laugh:** The fast flashing city bridge slows down to match the sleepy guitar band.
- **Fits:** night approach, between Q and R.
- **Core visual:** A wide shot of the lit suspension bridge across black water, its RGB waves running fast along the cables. The yacht's guitar line is in silhouette along the bottom of frame. The bridge's color waves gradually slow until each wave takes exactly one sway cycle.
- **Drop / change:** The bridge locks to the sway as the bed hits its pre-chorus. The bed stays at 128; only the picture slows.
- **Length:** 15 s clip, trims to 10 s.
- **Locks:** The sway itself never speeds up, which keeps the lock. RGB only, no orange, no other boats, no landing, and the gap stays open.

## 13. One day, one shot (longer experiment, ~30 s)

- **Spectacle:** A whole day passes in one shot from above, and the band never stops swaying.
- **Fits:** daylight leg, as an alternate to B–D, or as a stand-alone experiment before the 1:30 hard cut.
- **Core visual:** Locked overhead on the yacht, which is drifting on open turquoise sea. In time-lapse, the shadows of the guitar line wheel slowly around the deck as the sun crosses, and the water turns from turquoise to gold. The players' sway stays at real-time speed, a calm metronome in a fast world. The lounges never move, and the two black-lace shapes stay exactly in place.
- **Drop / change:** It ends on a bar line at late-afternoon gold, then hard cuts to J's night. It stops before sunset orange so it doesn't steal the dark island's palette.
- **Length:** Two chained 15 s clips (A-style start → mid-afternoon last still → late-gold last still), about 30 s total.
- **Bed:** Yacht Guitar Orchestra, full stack. No new bed is needed.
- **Locks:** The camera is locked. The one effect is the time-lapse, with the sway exempt so it stays readable. There's no land and no other boats.

## 14. From underneath (longer experiment, ~20–25 s)

- **Spectacle:** We go underwater and look up at the guitar boat from below, and the pick from earlier sinks past us.
- **Fits:** daylight leg, right after G → H (it continues the pick's fall).
- **Core visual:** An underwater camera in clear turquoise water looking up at the white hull. Above, the wobbling surface shows the line's ivory dresses and guitar necks as swaying, broken reflections. A single tortoiseshell pick spins slowly down past the lens into the blue. Sunbeams fan through the water in time with the sway.
- **Drop / change:** The pick passes the lens on the downbeat. In the second clip the camera rises to break the surface at the last bar, then hard cuts to I.
- **Length:** 15 s plus 10 s chained (the pick sinks, then the camera rises), trimmed to 20–25 s.
- **Bed:** Yacht Guitar Orchestra, Low Orbit layer. No new bed is needed.
- **Locks:** The camera is underwater, not a person, so there's no swimmer. It reuses G's pick, so there are no new props. No dolphin in frame, which keeps H unique.
