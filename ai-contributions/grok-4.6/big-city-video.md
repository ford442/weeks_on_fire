## How to use the audio prompt

Imagine treats the mp3 as a motion clock and a mouth clock. Do not upload 2:47 as one generate. Slice stems to the clip you want back. 126 BPM → 4 bars ≈ 7.6s.

Each generate: start frame + last frame + that stem (hard-cut) + a mouths on/off line.

- Stage stems: mouths on. Snare = micro-push.
- Hair-city stems: **no lip sync; the hair performs the beat.**
- Kenji tags: mouth barely open, not sold.

Mute baked clip audio in the stack. Ride option_a. Keep baked audio only when a stage take locks lips.
