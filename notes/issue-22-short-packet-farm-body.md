## Comment 1 — the farm sheet

**Short packet farm — 18 cutaways on the joke / strange-3D shelf**

Not an episode, not EP5. Eighteen small packets across four shelves: six seamless **loop
tiles** (2–8s), six **joke interrupts** (3–10s), four **gag sketches** (~30s), plus one
bridge and one musical-optional held back. One room each, one job each, one punch each.

The joke lane is building bureaucracy: forms, inventory, HOA, elevator negotiations, bags
at chest height. The punch is visual and the line — when there is one — is off camera. The
strange-3D lane is one object with a job, doing the job wrong or too well: porcelain,
marble insert, honey-oak, brass, wet enamel on the ground only, geometric white panels,
short honeycomb guards, pale pink lamp-eyes. Locked camera or slow orbit. Nothing mystical.

Siblings to the cowboy chimp, the bird flock, Marble Tick Tack, the Picard lounge, the
night-alley EM walk, Home Sweet Void and Ultra Screech — **not** replacements for any of
them. Worlds still do not mix.

Three marked generate-first, one per shelf:

- **`bag-at-chest-height`** — 5s loop. Three porcelain hands pass a kraft bag around an
  oak island at exactly chest height, forever.
- **`we-will-stand`** — 7s joke. A folding chair is offered; it unfolds into a second
  folding chair.
- **`the-approval-desk`** — 30s gag. A brass lamp with a stamp arm approves the form, the
  desk, the stamp, and finally itself.

Also in this pass: a **loop grammar page** (last frame = first frame, one moving system,
hold a rest pose, silent but for one diegetic click) and a **kill list** of five ideas
retired on purpose — Riley on the fire escape, the elevator singing back, the HOA meeting
finding the marble cabinet, the Pizza Guy in the space lounge, and a second Glam commercial.

## Comment 2 — `bag-at-chest-height` (loop tile, 5s)

**Loop tile — the bag never leaves chest height**

Mail room. An oval honey-oak island with the outer edge rubbed pale at chest height from
decades of the same pass. Three porcelain hands on short brass wrist-posts around it, no
arms and no bodies. One kraft bag with a single crisp fold goes hand to hand to hand,
completes the circuit, and arrives back in the first hand with the fold facing camera
exactly as it started. Five seconds. Forever.

The job is **orbit** and it is the only moving system in the room — the pigeonhole wall,
the face-down date stamp and the honeycomb vent guard are furniture and stay furniture.
Silent picture, one dry paper crease per handover.

Loop discipline: in-point equals out-point on the pose, the fold angle *and* the bulb
intensity. The bag does not soften or gain a crease per lap, no paper dust collects, the
caged bulb does not warm. Rest hold at 4.4–5.0 is the landing pad for the slideshow. If
lap two looks worn against lap one, regenerate — do not stitch, the seam shows on loop.

Reads as an EyeWash wallpaper plate behind a card, and the station never comments on it.

Five stills + motion prompt on the board. No stacker needed.

## Comment 3 — `we-will-stand` (joke interrupt, 7s)

**Joke interrupt — the chair solves it itself**

Clubhouse. A gloved hand sets one beige folding chair down, opens it, withdraws. Two full
seconds of nothing. Off camera: *"We'll stand."* Then the seat hinges once more than it
should and a second identical chair separates out and stands itself beside the first.
Both empty. Cut.

The Two are never on camera and the polyester half of the line is never said. The
building's answer to any objection is more of the thing you objected to, and nobody
reacts. Sibling to the bird flock — same echo-then-punch shelf — but the flock's second
half is the listening twin and this one's is furniture.

Prior-scene echo plus a hinge creak and one chirp. No new Minimax.

Wants a small stacker pass: set-down / dead hold / the split / two chairs. Straight cuts
only — a wipe would sell it harder than the joke, and the joke is that the room did not
notice.

Five stills on the board.

## Comment 4 — `the-approval-desk` (gag sketch, 30s, strange 3D)

**Gag sketch — an object that finishes its job**

Honey-oak office, grey marble desk insert. An articulated brass lamp with two small pale
pink lenses in the shade rim and a second arm ending in a rubber stamp. Two forms arrive
and are approved; the second slightly faster than the first. Then no form arrives.

Wrong turn: it stamps the bare marble. The desk edge. The drawer pull. Its own base. The
camera starts a slow orbit while it works down the inventory. Land: the arm folds all the
way up and over and stamps its **own shade**, the pink lenses go dark for one beat and come
back, the arm returns to rest — and a fresh form slides onto the marble and is not stamped.
It is done.

No face, no mouth, nothing but the two lenses, and it never looks at camera. Pink stays
inside the lenses. Nothing is destroyed, nothing escalates. Not S&P Skeleton's stamp and
not a second PROBABLY FINE.

Eight-slot stacker on the board, and the one real trick in it is reusing the rest take as
the wait — same framing, so the audience reads patience instead of a repeat. The 3–7s
approval beat is liftable later as its own 4s loop tile, cut fresh rather than carved out.

Six stills + motion prompt on the board.
