# La Bicyclette

**Genre collision:** bal-musette java (fast 3/4, accordion, banjo) × village brass fanfare (tuba, cornets, a bass drum on one). A Sunday dance that turns into a race-day parade.

**Visual concept:** It's a Saturday morning in a French market town. Rubella pedals an old black tandem at walking speed to buy six eggs. Lillith sits on the back seat with her feet up, playing guitar, safety glasses pushed up in her hair, not pedalling. A huge professional bike race (a hundred riders in bright jerseys, motorbikes, team cars, crowds with flags) catches up with them on the same road. Nobody overtakes the lady with the eggs, so the whole race slows down and follows her through the market at her pace. The only thing that passes her is the Pizza Guy on a delivery scooter, at his usual speed. She buys her eggs, rides on, and crosses the finish line first. They hand her a winner's bouquet and she puts it in the basket next to the eggs.

**Laugh (kid words):** *She rides her bike really slowly to buy eggs, and she wins the big bike race because nobody wants to pass her.*

**BPM:** 168 in 3/4 (java), G major, up a step to A for the final chorus

**Target length:** ~2:15 · 13 segments
