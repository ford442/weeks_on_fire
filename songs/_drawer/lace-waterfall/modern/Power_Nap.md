STYLE:

Sincere big-room festival EDM anthem, 128 BPM, F minor, about two and a half minutes. Huge four-on-the-floor kick, festival snare roll builds, white-noise risers, a massive detuned saw lead, side-chained pads, claps, and a crowd-chant vocal. Lead one: bright, conspiratorial hype MC, shouting the build-ups into a stadium. Lead two: dry, slightly raspy, sleepy, singing the hook slowly and quietly over the build, half yawning. Every build rises to a huge pause, and the "drop" is not a synth: it's a deep, slow, rhythmic snore that locks to the kick, with the bass following its pitch. The second drop is two snores in harmony. The track ends with three plain knocks and silence. Huge, glossy, sincere, no parody. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
LILLITH: Are you ready? Are you tired?

[Verse 1]
RUBELLA: Twenty minutes, set the alarm
Pillow on the lighting rig, blanket on my arm
Close the curtains on the main stage, dim the sun
LILLITH: When I say lie, you say down!

[Build]
LILLITH: Lie! (Down!) Lie! (Down!)
Everybody hands in the air
Now put them under your head
Five, four, three, two

[Drop]

[Verse 2]
RUBELLA: Hundred thousand blankets on a field of grass
Somebody is sleeping in the VIP pass
Fireworks go quiet, fire cannons on low
LILLITH: When I say nap, you say now!

[Build]
LILLITH: Nap! (Now!) Nap! (Now!)
Everybody jump, jump, jump
Okay, now just lie there
Five, four, three, two

[Drop]

[Breakdown]
RUBELLA: Five more minutes.
LILLITH: Five more minutes!
RUBELLA: Five more minutes.

[Outro]

NOTES:

**The drop is a snore** locked to the kick. It must be a real deep breath-in, breath-out
snore, rhythmic and musical, not a cartoon "zzz" effect. If MiniMax gives a synth drop
instead, regenerate with "the drop is a snore, no synth lead."

**Callback:** the outro's **three plain knocks**. Someone at the door at the worst possible
moment, cut before it opens (a portable form of the Pizza Guy arriving at the peak). Nobody
answers. Nobody wakes up.
