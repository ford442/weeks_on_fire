STYLE:

Lo-fi shoegaze recorded on a cassette four-track in a concrete stairwell, 84 BPM, D major, about two minutes forty. Heavily distorted, washed-out guitars through fuzz and long reverb, a soft drum machine, a warm buzzing bass, and a huge natural concrete-stairwell echo on everything. Two younger female voices in close unison, half-whispered, blurred and dreamy, one brighter, one drier and slightly raspy. Three times in the song the wall of sound is interrupted by a heavy fire door opening with a clunk, footsteps passing, and the door swinging shut; the band keeps playing through it without stopping. Ends with the four-track's stop button clicking. Tape hiss, warmth, sincerity, no parody. Use Lillith and Rubella voices, both younger.

NOTES:

**The [Break] sections** are where the doors open. Three doors, three interruptions,
written into the STYLE. The song never stops for them.

**Why it's early:** this is before the building. The stairwell is somewhere else, unnamed,
and the band has no elevator yet with opinions worth negotiating with.

**Do-not:** this is not the sixth-floor corridor (that's the building, later). No EXIT sign
here. Don't say whose stairwell it is or who lived there.
