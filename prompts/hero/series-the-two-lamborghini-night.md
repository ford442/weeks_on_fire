# H03 · The Two, night drive two-shot (series)

**Shot:** H03
**Episode / beat:** series visual foundation · reference stills
[`characters/image_003.png`](../../characters/image_003.png) (interior two-shot) and
[`characters/image_002.jpg`](../../characters/image_002.jpg) (the car) ·
[`characters/the-two.md`](../../characters/the-two.md) Quick-start seed "Core duo shot" and
"How They Could Appear" (one of them driving at night, the other in the passenger seat, silent)
**Why it matters:** the swap, made visible. Both in black lace, both lit the same red, a dark
road and nobody talking. At a glance you cannot tell who is driving whose life; only the hair
tells you. This is the frame musical cutaways cut back to.
**Locks:** [`the-two-identity`](../locks/the-two-identity.lock.md) ·
[`house-negative`](../locks/house-negative.lock.md)
**Aspect / model:** 16:9 · `grok-imagine-image-quality` (repo default)

**Geography (all three framings):** a winding two-lane mountain road at night, white edge line,
dark hedges and pines, stars. Left-hand drive. **Liliane drives** (frame left from inside),
**Vivienne in the passenger seat** (frame right), turned slightly toward Liliane, champagne flute
in hand, watching her and not the road. Both in black lace: a swap is live. The car is described,
never named: a low gunmetal-silver Italian V12 supercar with scissor doors, black wheels, red
brake calipers, a thin red sill accent, no readable badge or logo anywhere (wheel hub, dash,
grille). Interior: black Alcantara, a thin red ambient strip across the dash, white-on-black
gauges, a center screen glowing dim red with no readable UI.

**Bible exception (recorded, not repeated):** Vivienne does not sit; a scene that seats her is the
scene's emotional event ([`vivienne-vale.md`](../../characters/bible/vivienne-vale.md)
micro-gesture 6). The overview's own car seed puts one of the two in the passenger seat, and the
reference still is seated, so this hero is the **one sanctioned seated Vivienne**. Do not reuse
these framings as a casual "they sit" reference elsewhere. Liliane, the one who remembers the exits,
has the wheel.

**Callbacks spent (as image, not dialog):** the swap (both in lace, driver unreadable at a glance) ·
the flute she never sets down (even here) · silver, never gold · the Lamborghini is theirs, not the
house's (the-two.md: never cut from this car to the sleeping cars on Christina hill).

## Regenerate notes

- **If a logo or badge appears** (wheel hub, dash script, grille), regenerate; the reference still
  has one, and it must not come back. Do not name the make to "steer" the shape; the description
  carries it.
- **If the passenger looks at the road,** the swap reads as a road trip: the passenger watching the
  driver is the shot. Move that clause up next to her block.
- **If the two come back with the same hair,** the red light is flattening the silhouettes; that is
  partly the point, but the hair must still differ. Regenerate rather than brightening.
- **If W loses the cabin glow,** add nothing to the lock; raise the red-cabin clause to directly
  after the car description.
- **Do not reuse** these framings as a seated-Vivienne template (see the bible exception above).
- **Trim order:** micro-detail → road furniture (post, wall, valley lights) → grade adjectives.
  Never the lock blocks, never the lighting sentence.
