STYLE:

Upbeat accordion pop, 116 BPM, C major, about one minute forty. Short lines, specific nouns, deadpan sincerity, a small argument that does not settle. Accordion plays a bouncing two-bar figure, major, no minor turn, no musette parody. A single coin rattle is the only percussion, dry and close. Two female voices, no belting, no vibrato, no wink. Lillith brighter, starts the claim. Rubella drier, answers. Do not sing their names. The quarter leaves a brass return cup and is held at chest height. The song is whose quarter it was: washer against dryer. Structural flaw: the coin rattle lands one beat late on every return, including the last, and the voices stay on the grid and never wait for it. Sing only the lines below. No drum kit, no guitar, no male voice, no third voice, no product name. Mood: both claims are sincere and the coin is late. Runtime about 1:40.

LYRICS:

[Intro]

[Verse]

LILLITH: The cup gave a quarter back.
RUBELLA: It stopped at chest height.
LILLITH: I fed the washer.
RUBELLA: I fed the dryer.

[Chorus]

LILLITH: Whose quarter.
RUBELLA: It was already mine.
LILLITH: The brass cup returned it.
RUBELLA: The hand did not put it back.

[Verse]

LILLITH: The slot is on the left.
RUBELLA: Warm metal. One quarter.
LILLITH: Mine from the wash.
RUBELLA: Mine from the dry.

[Chorus]

LILLITH: Whose quarter.
RUBELLA: Mine from the washer.
LILLITH: It came back to the hand.
RUBELLA: The hand stayed at the chest.

[Outro]

LILLITH: Whose quarter.
RUBELLA: Whose quarter.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: the coin rattle is one beat late on every return. The voices stay on the grid and never wait. The last chorus does not move to meet the coin, and the argument is not awarded.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not the coin-return card.** That card is a silent hold: the quarter comes out and stays. This is the argument about whose quarter it was.
- **Not Pepperoni or Margherita.** Nobody delivers anything. The hand at chest height is holding a quarter.
- **Not the bag.** The bag rule stays on its own card. It is not sung, not carried, and not in the room.
- **Not Receipt Dated Thursday.** No store, no date, no slip.
