STYLE:

Sincere 2010s European song-contest power ballad that turns into a Euro-dance anthem, about three minutes. Opens at 76 BPM in A flat major: a lone grand piano, soft strings, a breathy lead vocal. The pre-chorus builds with taiko drums and a choir. At the first chorus a four-on-the-floor kick and bright supersaws kick in at 128 BPM, with a soaring vocal and a big ethnic-flute hook. The final chorus has the classic key change up a whole step to B flat major, a gong, and a huge wall of sound. Lead one: bright, conspiratorial, powerful belting, total sincerity. Lead two: dry, slightly raspy, singing tight harmony, and reading a short points announcement at the end in a flat voice over silence. Polished, glossy, epic, no parody, no comedy sound effects. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.
