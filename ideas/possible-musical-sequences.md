# Possible Musical Sequences — six generated-video jokes (promote?: no)

*Pages, not picture. 2026-10-11.*

Six short videos that can be generated and then laid under a small original bed.
Each card is one room, one picture language, one committed joke. Text only: no
still, no cutaway id, no Suggestions id, no `content/sequences/` player, no
`songs/*.md`, no bound `songId`. `promote?: no` on every card.

## What it is

A generate-later shelf for comical musical sequences. Picture can be practical,
cartoon, 3D, surreal, flat 2D, or a physical arrangement. The bed is original
and short. The joke is visual and survives a second viewing: somebody, or
something, performs a job extremely well and the job is slightly the wrong job.

Lengths sit at 18–32s so a generator can hold one shot, or two shots with one
cut. The picture may ship silent. The bed drops on afterward. One diegetic
noise is allowed to be both the button and the last musical event.

## What it is not

The joke-10 packets stay in the clubhouse, lobby, corridor, mailroom, boiler
room, and laundry, and they stay unsung. These six leave that building on
purpose. They are not WebGL sequences (`content/sequences/` already plays
Lattice Hymn, the kitchen counter, the rubber-hose errand). They are not
cartoon stills (`content/cartoons/`). They are not Ultra Screech, not Marble
Tick Tack, not Daisy, not Well Fall, not the elevator tango, not Hold Music,
not Fig. 4, not Brad's bins.

## Locks

- One room per card. Worlds do not mix. A callback arrives as a line, a
  number, a tag, or a corner screen.
- Deadpan. Full commitment. Nobody comments on the frame. Nobody is frightened.
  No wink at the lens, no gore, no fight, no explosion.
- No real actor names. If a person is in frame, the face stays off camera.
- The Pizza Guy is never on camera and never named.
- No new lyric. Spoken audio, where it exists, is one already-locked line,
  same take every time.
- Each bed names one structural flaw and leaves it uncorrected.
- `promote?: no` until someone says promote. Then: a `songs/_drawer/` sketch,
  a board, codegen by whoever promotes.

### `domino-inventory` — 24s · physical

**Medium:** real dominoes on a real table. Practical, or generated so it reads
as practical: wood grain, scuffed pips, a shallow drawer. No faces.
**Joke:** The run is a concert and a filing. Every tile falls on a sixteenth
into an open honey-oak drawer and lands already squared. The last domino stays
standing at the near end, tagged **1**, and the drawer slides shut without it.
The performance was one item. The item that did the work is not in the file.
**Picture world:** one dining table, caged bulb, cream wall. A kraft bag rides
a groove along the near edge the whole time, level, chest height, and stops
when the drawer shuts. The bag never opens.
**Start JOB:** first domino tips. **Last JOB:** drawer shut, tag **1** still
standing, bag held.
**Bed:** 120 BPM, no key center. Toy piano, one note per tile, dry woodblock
on the sixteenths, no vocal. Structural flaw: the cadence is the drawer slide.
The piano does not get a last chord.
**Button:** one chair creak, off camera, where applause would go. The chair is
not in frame and does not unfold.
**Do-not:** No marble run, no magnets, no night lot. No second moving system
beyond the bag's single glide. No legible writing except the tag **1**.

### `corral-unison` — 26s · real life

**Medium:** live-action dusk, or photoreal generation that keeps real metal,
real asphalt, real sodium light. One employee, back to camera, plain
windbreaker, clipboard.
**Joke:** Every nested cart has the same opinion. Pull one, the whole corral
squeaks one pitch, in tune, on the beat, with church-hymn commitment. A second
row is the same pitch. The employee conducts. The carts are excellent. They
will not become a chord.
**Picture world:** one grocery corral, one rail. A laminate on the rail reads
DIFFERENT LOCAL. A parked car's dashboard screen shows color bars. Nobody
looks at it. Nobody changes it.
**Start JOB:** first cart pulled. **Last JOB:** carts nested, note stops,
employee walks off at one pace.
**Bed:** 100 BPM, D mixolydian that never leaves the tonic. The cart squeak is
the pitch. A melodica doubles it. A cheap accordion enters at 0:08 convinced a
dominant is coming. Structural flaw: the accordion plays a V; the carts refuse
it and stay on the one.
**Button:** the laminate, still in focus, after the employee is gone.
**Do-not:** No pizza, no uniform rotation, no name tag found by the camera.
No Brad's bins, no shade of blue. The screen is bars, not a scene from the show.

### `same-shift` — 20s · 2D cartoon

**Medium:** limited 2D cartoon, 4:3, flat gouache, thick ink, scissor-cut
edges, two dot eyes each. Not photoreal, not 3D cel, not a shadow-puppet noir.
**Joke:** A paper sun and a paper moon arrive at one time-clock window from
opposite sides, both in tiny paper visors, both on time. The card spits out
**3:44**. They punch again. The second card is the same card, same time, same
take. They trade visors, nod once, and leave the way they came. The paperwork
agrees with itself. The shift already happened.
**Picture world:** a flat paper sky, one window, one clock. No tower, no
control room, no city.
**Start JOB:** both enter. **Last JOB:** both exit, visors swapped, clock
unmoved.
**Bed:** 84 BPM. Music box, an eight-bar phrase played twice, the second pass
identical, including the little gear click. Two-note "ah," no words.
Structural flaw: the phrase does not cadence; it stops because the card has
already been punched.
**Button:** the second card is visibly the first card.
**Do-not:** Do not record a sign-off. The clock is the joke. No Continuity
Voice in this room. No Daisy, no lyric about morning.

### `supply-pit` — 32s · 3D generated

**Medium:** strange 3D. Porcelain, honey-oak, brass, one caged bulb. Camera
locked at the rail, slight enough depth to read eight hands and no bodies.
**Joke:** Eight porcelain hands, cuffs and nothing above them, play a serious
overture on a stapler, a rubber stamp, a hole punch, a letter opener, a
folding ruler, a date stamp, a mug used as a bell, and a teaspoon. The
conductor is a phone on a music stand. The screen reads **23%** and only dims
for the downbeat. The number never changes. They are very good. At the end
every hand sets its tool on the rail in one level row and holds.
**Picture world:** a black-box pit, one rail, no audience seats.
**Start JOB:** phone dims, first note. **Last JOB:** eight tools in a row,
eight-second hold, phone still at 23%.
**Bed:** 120 BPM, G minor, ~32s. Bassoon line, woodblock, muted trumpet,
teaspoon on the mug as the bell. No vocal, no gang chant. Structural flaw:
the rubber stamp lands one beat after the chord has died, and that late stamp
is the only event in the right place.
**Button:** the hold. A chair creaks where the applause would go. They do not
bow.
**Do-not:** No lamp-eyes, no form, no legible stamp text. This is not the
approval desk. No marble cabinet, no night lot. The phone is furniture. The
23% does not change, not even for one frame.

### `the-program-returns` — 30s · surreal

**Medium:** a real small living room, then the room inside the television, then
the room inside that one. Practical lamp, wood-grain CRT, one chair. The
impossibility is the nesting, played straight.
**Joke:** The set clicks on. A warm tire-ad voice says, once, *"We'll return
to your program. We always do."* The picture is this room. Cut into the
screen: same room, the chair one foot closer to the door, and the line plays
again, the same take. Cut in again: the chair is in the doorway, same line,
same take. Third return: the chair is gone, the lamp is on, and the screen
shows the empty room showing the empty room, smaller each time. The program
returned so carefully it left.
**Picture world:** one apartment, no person, no second set.
**Start JOB:** set clicks on. **Last JOB:** empty room, nested screens, lamp
still on. Hold.
**Bed:** 76 BPM. Nylon guitar, one detuned chime, low and short. The voice is
the only vocal, three identical passes, dry. Structural flaw: the guitar
figure is one bar short of a resolution and stays one bar short.
**Button:** the missing chair.
**Do-not:** Do not name the station. Do not shoot a tower. No goldfish, no
orchestra, no telephone on a velvet seat, no person waiting. Do not decide
whether the room is haunted or unscheduled.

#### `the-program-returns` — **PUNCHED** — 30s

Same set, same line, same three identical takes, same nesting. Each return the chair is
further away, and on the third it is gone. Then, on the drop, the chair **comes back**: it
skids into the innermost screen, out through the next screen, into the real room, and
slides into its exact spot. It rocks twice and stops, a little out of breath for a chair.
The lamp is still on.
**Laugh:** *every time the TV comes back the chair has run further away, and then it runs
home.*
**Drop:** reverses (the chair races back through every screen).
**End reaction:** none. The chair settling is the button.
**Bed:** same nylon guitar, still one bar short. The chair's last rock lands where the
missing bar would be.
**Do-not:** Same as the original card. Still no person in the room, and nobody sits in
the chair. It never leaves the frame of a screen on the way back. `promote?: no`

### `item-four-sampler` — 18s · cross-stitch

**Medium:** stop-motion of real thread in a wooden hoop, or a 2D pass that
keeps stitch texture, hoop grain, and a single lamp. One stitch per eighth
note. No face.
**Joke:** The sampler finishes the words ITEM FOUR in a polite serif. It
unpicks them, on the beat, into a small centered purple diamond, which sits
there looking finished. Then the same thread stitches the words back and the
diamond is gone. Both versions are correct. The hoop has adjourned by
renaming.
**Picture world:** a craft-room table, one hoop on a stand, one lamp. A
folding chair at the frame edge.
**Start JOB:** last stitch of the words. **Last JOB:** the words restored,
diamond gone, chair creaks once.
**Bed:** 108 BPM. Harpsichord, prim, close, no vocal. Structural flaw: one
stitch is the wrong color and the harpsichord honks exactly then, then
continues as if the honk were in the score.
**Button:** the creak. The chair does not clap and does not unfold.
**Do-not:** No driveway, no portal, no clipboard, no gavel, no clubhouse. The
purple is thread. Item four stays open.

## Killed here

1. **Dominoes that become a marble ballet.** Roley's night lot owns the
   magnets. A table of dominoes files. It does not travel.
2. **The corral as a delivery.** The Pizza Guy stays off this shelf. Carts are
   the choir.
3. **Sun and moon with a morning lyric.** Same-shift is a time clock. Daisy
   stays in the field.
4. **The pit approves a form.** The approval desk already stamps itself.
   These hands give a concert and set the tools down.
5. **An orchestra inside the returning program.** Hold Music owns the hall
   and the red telephone.
6. **The sampler opens.** A diamond of thread is the whole accent. The light
   stays in the driveway where it already lives.

## Promotion path

A card leaves when someone says promote. Then it gets a drawer bed under
`songs/_drawer/`, a one-page board, and only after that a catalog id. Nothing
in this file is on the hub.
