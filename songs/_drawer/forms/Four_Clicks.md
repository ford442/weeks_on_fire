# Four Clicks — drawer sketch

**Drawer.** `promote?: no`. Motet machine, Marble Tick Tack syllable inventory. Not that cutaway. Farm #18 / #33. Not #42.

| field | value |
|---|---|
| form | motet, a cappella, four-syllable cell |
| text | tih-kah tah-koh |
| who | Lillith, then Rubella. No Nilsson. |
| flaw | Rubella enters one syllable late and a whole step low |

STYLE:

Phonetic motet, a cappella, 72 BPM, about 80 seconds. The only lyric is four syllables, each a quarter note: tih, kah, tah, koh. No English word, no Latin word, no translation, no choir bigger than two. Straight tone, one pitch per syllable, no melisma, no vibrato, no belting. Reverb is a dry hallway, short. Lillith enters first, on D, and keeps the cell where it started. Rubella enters on the second pass. She is one syllable late and a whole step low, on C, and she stays there. Lillith does not follow and does not retune. Three passes after the entry are enough. On the last click they stop together, still offset, still a step apart. No cadence. No names sung. Not a tick-tock instrumental. The clicks are sung.

LYRICS:

[Lillith — on D, one cell alone]
LILLITH: tih kah tah koh

[Rubella enters — one syllable late, on C]
LILLITH: tih kah tah koh
RUBELLA: tih kah tah koh

LILLITH: tih kah tah koh
RUBELLA: tih kah tah koh

LILLITH: tih kah tah koh
RUBELLA: tih kah tah koh

[Stop — still offset, no cadence]
LILLITH: koh
RUBELLA: koh

NOTES:

Phonetic so the form is the whole lyric. Inventory is the Marble click set, sung, not the night-lot bed. Roley's cutaway is untouched.

Not *Same Sentence* (that one is English, and the phase is the only flaw). Here the lateness and the low entry are both refused a correction. Runtime about 80 seconds.
