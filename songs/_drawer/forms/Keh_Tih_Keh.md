STYLE:

Phonetic palindrome, a cappella, 80 BPM, about fifty seconds. One close female voice, dry, no reverb, no instruments, no percussion, no knock, no door. The cell is three syllables, keh tih keh, which read the same forward and backward. Pitches for the forward cell: keh on E, tih on G, keh on B. Section A: the forward cell, four times. Section B: the same syllables with the pitch line in retrograde (B, G, E), four times. Section A again: the forward cell, four times. Straight tone, clipped consonants, no melisma, no slides, no harmony. Structural flaw: the retrograde pass comes in a whole step low (A, F, D instead of B, G, E), and the return to the forward cell stays low (D, F, A). Nothing corrects it. The last forward pass has to be the same performance as the first, just a whole step down. End on the last keh, with no cadence. No words, no English, no Latin, no famous palindrome, no Sator square, no Panama, no Madam I'm Adam, no Tattarrattat, no knock pattern. Mood: a round trip that comes home one floor down.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: the reverse pass is a whole step low, and the next forward pass does not correct it. The syllables are a perfect palindrome. The pitches are not.

Callback: **Sign-off at 3:44, twice, identical.** The two A sections should be the same take, the same phrasing and the same file, only transposed down. Don't re-sing the second one. The repetition is the joke.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not Tattarrattat.** That one owns the palindrome knock: a door, a hallway, Rubella, smoked jazz. This one has no door, no knock and no named singer, and the palindrome is made of three syllables, not a word.
