STYLE:

Sincere hyperpop, 170 BPM, C sharp major, about two minutes. Blown-out distorted 808 bass, crunchy clipped kicks, metallic trap hi-hat rolls, bubbly detuned supersaw chords, chiptune arpeggios, glitch stutter edits, bitcrushed risers, sudden half-second silences. Lead one: bright, conspiratorial, heavily pitched-up and autotuned, chipmunk shine, shouting with joy. Lead two: completely dry and unprocessed, slightly raspy, whispering close to the mic, the quietest element in every section and perfectly clear. Every time lead two says "shh," the whole track drops to near silence for exactly one beat, then slams back in. Final chorus pitches everything up a further whole step. Maximalist, glossy, no parody, no comedy sound effects. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.
