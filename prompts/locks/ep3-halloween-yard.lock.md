One yard, one night. Every Ep3 hero shot pastes `yard` and `light` so the house, the fence, the
shed and the two color temperatures do not drift between stills that will be cut together.
`serpents` and `skeleton` go in only when they are in frame.

## What the blocks lock (and why)

- **The house:** a modest two-story 1960s suburban house, white vinyl siding gone grey at the
  seams, a back porch with a screen door and a single bare tungsten bulb in a cage fixture. A TV
  glows blue through the screen door (EyeWash is always on somewhere; the screen is never readable).
- **The yard:** wet uncut grass with leaves in it, a weathered cedar fence with carved pumpkins on
  the posts, a dead tree with fake cobwebs, a garden shed with overgrown bushes beside it, a
  decorative hay bale (the cat's cover), string lights sagging between the porch posts.
  The address is 117 Elmwood; **no house number is ever legible** (text rule).
- **The serpents:** two, about three feet long, faintly iridescent scales, triangular heads, eyes
  that glow like something powered, not born. One is cyan, one is magenta. Beams are solid light
  that scorches what it touches, not sci-fi lightning.
- **The skeleton:** the cheap, cheerful party-store plastic kind, zip-tied to the fence post until
  it isn't. Visible mould seams, a hollow ribcage. It dances a two-step while it fires. Not a real
  skeleton, not a horror prop.
- **Light physics:** two worlds. Warm 2700K tungsten from the porch (hard, overhead, sharp downward
  shadows) and amber pumpkins; cool 4100K moonlight from a full moon; cyan and magenta laser spill
  that rakes wet grass and gives every wet surface a two-color specular edge. The porch light is
  the brightest source in any frame it is in (the Ep3 "stage set from 1987" click).
- **The finale lawn** (H09) is the same yard after the battle: serpent coils lie defeated on the
  grass, the vintage couch has been dragged out, the hay bale is still smoldering. The battle
  screenplay has the serpents at three feet; the Monster Mash finale has the coils giant. That is
  not adjudicated here: `aftermath` follows the finale file, `serpents` follows the screenplay.
