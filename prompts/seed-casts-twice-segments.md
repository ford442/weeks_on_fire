# Seed Casts Twice — Imagine segments

Silent 30s gag first. Optional 45–70s if Paper Lung exists. Reiniger table. Desmet field flash.
Worlds do not mix. Catalog: [`ideas/seed-casts-twice.md`](../ideas/seed-casts-twice.md) ·
Suggestions id `seed-casts-twice` · Farm [#22](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/22) ·
Song [#18](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/18) / [#33](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/33) · Not [#42](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/42).

**Prompt policy:** S1, S3 and S4 are written long on purpose (~1,000–1,300 characters), one
continuous run, Monster Mash order — **subject → bodies → job → room → light → lens/grade**.
Continuity tokens are repeated *verbatim* between prompts: **black card, tissue-grey street,
amber field, paper-fastener glint, seed-as-disc.** Do not trim them to clean the prompt up. If a
generation comes back mushy, cut **room layers** first, never the job or the light.

**Block labels:** `**Grok Imagine**` is the *start* frame and is what codegen lifts onto the
Suggestions view. `**Last frame**` is the matching *last* frame for the Imagine start/last pair
(markdown only). `**Gemini Omni**` doubles as the Imagine image_to_video motion text.

Visual lock (already folded into every long prompt; paste into any short one):

> Backlit frosted-glass trick table. Solid black cardboard silhouette marionettes, scissors-cut
> edges, wire hinges invisible in the under-light. Backgrounds in stacked tissue paper, near
> darker, far paler. One monochrome film-tint field on the clear gelatin only; figures stay black.
> No eyes drawn on faces. No photoreal skin. No HUD. No readable text. No lace building-cast. No
> wildflower petals. No wet enamel on plants.

Fractal growth = numbered paper generations, swapped like Bartosch waves, never a 3D morph:
**G0** heel-fern → **G1** fork → **G2** canopy → **G3** lace-of-shadow. Close-up of the disc =
**replacement puppet**, not a digital crop. Tint change = splice, not a fade.

## S1 — Split (0:04–0:10)

**Job:** two matched steps, then the third step forks — the lie. Do not show a thief.  
**On screen:** Child enters. Body and shadow agree. On the third step the shadow stays and forks.  
**Start / last:** start = matched; last = child one step on, shadow planted and forked to G1.  
**Motion:** one step. Shadow does not follow. One plate swap G0 → G1.  
**Tint:** amber field. No splice inside the shot.

**Grok Imagine**
```
Lotte Reiniger silhouette animation still, one solid black card puppet of a child about eight years old in strict profile, short coat, small satchel on one strap, flat cap, scissors-cut edges, no eyes drawn, no face detail, no photoreal skin. The body is articulated at shoulder, elbow, hip and knee, with a single paper-fastener glint at the knee where the under-light catches the brass. Mid-stride on the second step, weight on the back foot, front toe just lifting. The child's cast shadow lies on the sidewalk plane directly under the shoes and matches the body exactly, same stride, same cap, one black shape joined at the heels. The job of this frame: body and shadow still agree. Room: a tissue-grey street of stacked tissue tenements, near darker, far paler, an iron stoop and a fire escape in black card, one small stoop lamp, no other figure, no seed-as-disc yet. Light: backlit frosted-glass trick table, one amber field tint on the clear gelatin only, figures stay pure black, no colour inside the puppets. Lens and grade: locked camera, lead-flat plane, no depth of field, 4:3, paper grain, no wet enamel, no HUD, no text, no petals, no field, no tandem, no rivulets, no orb, no 3D mesh. Grok Imagine style
```

**Last frame**
```
Lotte Reiniger silhouette animation still, the same solid black card child about eight in strict profile, short coat, small satchel on one strap, flat cap, scissors-cut edges, no eyes drawn, no face detail, now one full step farther forward on the third step, weight on the front foot, the paper-fastener glint at the knee still catching the under-light. The cast shadow has not followed. It stays planted on the sidewalk plane where the second step was, an empty gap of tissue between the shoes and the shadow's heels, and from the shadow's heel a self-similar black paper fork (G1) has been cut and swapped in: one trunk, two branches each forking twice, flat card, hard scissors edges, no morph. The job of this frame: body and shadow disagree for the first time. Light: backlit frosted-glass trick table, the amber field unchanged on the clear gelatin only, figures stay pure black. ```
```
```
6 seconds, locked camera, silent. A black card silhouette child takes one step forward on a tissue-grey paper street under an amber field. **Hardeners**
```

- Shadow walks with the child → `shadow stays planted on the sidewalk plane, gap of tissue between shoes and shadow heels`
- Fork grows like a mesh → `flat card plate swap, hard scissors edges, no morph, no 3D growth`
- Colour bleeds into the puppet → `figures stay pure black, amber field on the clear gelatin only`
- Face appears → `strict profile, no eyes drawn, no face detail`
- Field or flowers appear → `no field, no tandem, no wildflower petals, no paint rivulets`

**On screen:** Child holds the breath. Branches withdraw a generation. In a gap, the disc.  
**Start / last:** start = G2 mid-withdraw, disc isolated chest-high; last = G1, disc lower, child unmoved.  
**Motion:** child does not move. Two plate swaps back. Disc drifts down one stem.  
**Tint:** amber field. No splice.

The paper-fastener glint at the shoulder is the only bright point. Over and behind the child the black paper canopy is withdrawing: G3 lace swapped out for the G2 canopy plate, branches shorter, flat card, no morph. **Start / last:** start = disc on palm, two stems at frame edge; last = stems gone, shadow locked, frame emptier.  
**Motion:** stems withdraw; nothing else. The frame ends smaller than S2.  
**Tint:** amber field. Cut to black, no fade.
