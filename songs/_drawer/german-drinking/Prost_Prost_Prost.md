STYLE:

German party Schlager call-and-response, 116 BPM, major key. Accordion riff, brass stabs on every crowd answer, simple stomp drums, male lead vocal calling, big crowd shouting each line back, builds in volume each round, festive beer-tent feel, traditional instruments, no electronic production, no rap. Short, about 1:30. Simple German. Sing only the lines below. No character names, no product.

NOTES:

Simple German. Non-speakers catch Prost / Freunde / Heute / Nacht / Glas / Bier / Immer. The build is the joke: each call adds one word until the line is too long to shout, then the crowd refuses to let the lead stop. Catalog only until selected. Picture if any: a beer tent from the back, every glass up. Worlds do not mix.
