# Christina — House Company

*Mara Vell. Who else is in the house, one at a time. 2026-09-28.*

**Kind:** company page for the Sweden heart cutaway — not a cutaway of its own
**House:** [`christina-frozen-house.md`](christina-frozen-house.md) · **Hostess:** [`characters/bible/christina.md`](../characters/bible/christina.md)

## The rule

**One extra body per plate.** Christina plus one. That is a house. Christina plus two is
company. Five faces is a party, and a party is a different show — throw it.

Every other visitor below is **a separate night.** None of them shares a plate with any
other. None of them is on the night-home with Rubella and Lillith.

---

## The night-home (the one with the ladies)

The seats are fixed:

| Seat | Who | How we meet them |
|---|---|---|
| **Gilt hall** | The accompanist, at the harpsichord (piano on the alt) | **Heard first.** The continuo reaches the door before the door opens. We may never see a face — hands, a candle, the lid. |
| **Atrium** | One listener | One person on the white marble, back to the ocean glass, listening. Not introduced. Not named. Leaves when the aria ends or doesn't; either way we cut first. |

Rubella and Lillith pass through for thirty seconds and are gone. They are guests, not seats.

## Other nights — legal, separately

Each is its own night, its own plate, Christina plus one.

### Clerk night — the thin folder

A clerk comes up the hill in the snow with a folder so thin it barely bends. He says the
word *hovsångare*. He hands it over. There is nothing else in it. Christina says it is
enough. He goes. No King, no court, no seal the size of a plate. Paper, and a man who has
to get back down the hill.

### Qing night — sea glass

Qing Rao stands at the ocean-glass wall in the atrium, both hands at her sides for once
because there is nothing to hold. She knows what the glass is. She says so, once. She
knows the gilt in the other room is two hundred years older, and says that too. She
nearly asks a question — the seam where the house changes — and doesn't. The skull stays
home. This is a visit, not a case. *(Sheet: [`bible/qing-rao.md`](../characters/bible/qing-rao.md).)*

### Nova night — the gilt hall, no mic

Nova Chen sits in the gilt hall with printed cue sheets on her knee and no microphone
anywhere in frame. She is listening to the continuo, marking where it dips for the aria
and where it comes back after the last *Ah*. She does not sing. She does not stand. She
turns a page. *(Crew page: [`docs/crew/nova-chen.md`](../docs/crew/nova-chen.md).)*

### Tuner night — the marble line

A piano tuner kneels exactly where the gilt floor meets the white marble. He tunes on one
side of the line, then the other, and finds the house changes century under his knee —
the air is different, so the pitch is different. He writes it down. He does not remark on
it. Christina is out of focus behind him, in the white.

### Wrap-student night

After the last *Ah*, when the continuo comes back up, a conservatory student comes in to
wrap — cover the harpsichord, square the bench, turn off the one candle. She is paid by
the night and it is the end of the night. She says *hej då* to Christina on the way out
and gets the hostess face back. That is the whole scene.

*(Reading of "wrap-student" here is a student at wrap time. If the owner meant something
else, change this paragraph, not the rule.)*
