# One-Panel Gags — Newspaper-Comic Stills for Animation

Single-scene gag concepts in the spirit of a newspaper one-panel or *New Yorker*
cartoon, pushed into the series' glamorous / bored / slightly dangerous / surreal
register. Each is designed as **one striking still** that can later loop or move
for 3–8 seconds in Grok Imagine.

**Tone lock:** dry, elegant, slightly menacing, absurd, or quietly apocalyptic.
No broad slapstick. High-contrast cinematic light. Luxury materials against
industrial or cosmic ones. Strong silhouettes. Negative space.

**Prompt policy:** Grok Imagine stills are written *long on purpose* — subject →
wardrobe → blocking → background layers → light → lens → grade → style tag.
Animation never sells the joke harder than the still. Motion is weather,
machinery, or manners — not a punchline take.

**Do not reuse:** the Ultra-Refreshed plant where the water is on fire; robot
arms assembling something hidden in smoke and deep space; laser snakes;
pizza-porch as the gag; HOA clipboard; hovering melon; technical-difficulties
card.

**How to use:** generate the still first (newspaper-comic test: if it needs a
caption, the still is unfinished). Then animate with the motion prompt only.
Promote winners to `prompts/` as timed segments. Live on the site
**Suggestions** view (`src/data/gagCutaways.ts`).

New short cartoon *seeds* from agents belong in [`content/cartoons/`](../content/cartoons/)
(hub **Cartoons** view). Do not append them here until they are locked like the eight below.

## 1. Closed for Cosmic Reasons

**Filename:** `gag-01-closed-for-cosmic-reasons`  
Still: `closed-for-cosmic-reasons-still.webp` · clip: `closed-for-cosmic-reasons-6s.mp4`

**Register:** grounded surreal with one impossible object.

**One-sentence visual:** Night hotel infinity pool; a laminated sign on a chrome
stanchion reads CLOSED FOR COSMIC REASONS; the entire sheet of water hangs three
feet above the empty basin, still rippling, still reflecting the city; underneath
it a woman in a black maillot and heels sits dry on a deck chair, smoking, reading
a magazine that should be soaked and isn't.

**The gag:** The pool is closed. She is using the shade.

**Connections:** Vivienne — she came to swim and stayed for the shade; Liliane
would have read the notice and left. Black maillot + heels is the swimsuit
version of “if we’re going down, we go in heels.” EyeWash color bars in the
colonnade, never commented on. Sibling to *Rinse Cycle*. Do not put The Two’s
names on the sign.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
```
Photoreal one-panel film still, 2.39:1, night luxury hotel rooftop infinity pool, the entire rectangular sheet of dark chlorinated water hovering three feet above the empty pale-blue tiled basin as one unbroken pane, underside faintly convex, still rippling, still reflecting a distant sodium-and-neon city skyline, a small laminated institutional sign on a chrome stanchion at pool's edge reading CLOSED FOR COSMIC REASONS in polite Helvetica, under the hovering water a bored glamorous woman with long black hair, blood-red lipstick, a black one-piece maillot, and black stiletto heels sitting dry on a white woven deck chair, an open glossy fashion magazine on her lap that is not wet, a thin cigarette between two fingers, smoke rising and flattening against the water's underside like a ceiling, a room-service tumbler of amber liquid on the dry limestone deck, a tiny lobby CRT in the far colonnade playing EyeWash Station color bars, high-contrast silver moonlight from above-right plus one submerged pool lamp shining upward through the levitating water so her face is lit by the moon and by green-cyan caustics, wet chrome, dry stone, lots of negative night sky, 35mm anamorphic, T2.0, fine grain, Grok Imagine style
```

**Motion (6s):**
```
Locked-off 6-second photoreal loop. A slow ripple travels left to right across the hovering water sheet. Cigarette smoke rises, hits the underside, and sheets sideways. The submerged lamp's caustic crawls once across her collarbone. The ember pulses on beat two. The magazine page does not turn. The sign does not flutter. No one enters. Camera: 2 percent push only.
```

**Variations:** Swap the magazine for a room-service tray that is also dry. Or the
sign is handwritten in lipstick.

## 2. The Moon Is Collect

**Filename:** `gag-02-the-moon-is-collect`  
Still: `the-moon-is-collect-still.webp` · clip: `the-moon-is-collect-5s.mp4`

**Register:** impossible.

**One-sentence visual:** Black-lacquer penthouse desk at night; a single red
rotary phone ringing; its coiled cord runs out the open window and continues,
taut and visible, all the way to a huge close moon; empty chair pulled back;
lipstick-stained cigarettes in a crystal ashtray; a pair of black gloves left as
if the owner just stepped away.

**The gag:** Someone is calling collect. The charges are astronomical. She stepped out.

**Connections:** Liliane stepped out — she remembers the exits; the rate card is a
consequence Vivienne would have answered for sport. Gloves, lipstick filters, two
glasses and berries = The Two’s abandoned-table motif, one body already gone.
Ep2 nocturnal / radio-billing cousin. Never show who is calling.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
```
Photoreal one-panel film still, 2.39:1, black-lacquer penthouse interior at 2 a.m., a single cherry-red 1960s rotary telephone ringing on a leather blotter, the coiled red plastic cord running out an open floor-to-ceiling window and stretching taut across high thin cloud all the way to a huge detailed full moon filling the upper right, empty oxblood leather chair pulled back as if someone just stood, crystal ashtray crowded with lipstick-stained filter ends, a pair of black evening gloves abandoned on the blotter with one finger still slightly curled, two wine glasses (one upright, one stem-down) and a few crushed berries on a saucer at the far edge of the desk, city grid far below the window, practical desk lamp off, the only warm light the telephone's tiny indicator, high-contrast red plastic against black lacquer and cold silver moonlight, strong negative space, 35mm, T1.8, quiet menace, Grok Imagine style
```

**Motion (5s):**
```
5-second locked interior. The receiver trembles on three rings. The cord sways in high-altitude wind. The moon brightens a half-stop on each ring and dims between. One ash cylinder lengthens a millimeter. The gloves do not move. No one returns. Photoreal, no whip-pans, no face.
```

**Variations:** The moon has a tiny "NO CHARGES ACCEPTED" sticker. Or the gloves
are still faintly steaming.

## 3. Third Shift Porcelain

**Filename:** `gag-03-third-shift-porcelain`  
Still: `third-shift-porcelain-still.webp` · clip: `third-shift-porcelain-6s.mp4`

**Register:** grounded surreal.

**One-sentence visual:** After-hours luxury department store; a line of couture
mannequins at a factory time-clock; one has already punched out and stands in the
loading-dock doorway smoking a cigarette made of chrome; banks of fluorescent
tubes; a single red security-camera LED.

**The gag:** Beauty is hourly. Overtime is not discussed.

**Connections:** Not The Two as people — they do not clock out. The mannequins are
the labor version of their matching black lace. Contrast cut against any scene
where Vivienne and Liliane are “off.” EyeWash on the stockroom CRT. Pizza Guy may
cross the dock in a different uniform; do not make him the joke.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
```
Photoreal one-panel film still, 3:2, after-hours luxury department store, pale fashion mannequins in black couture, pearls, and identical tired painted smiles lined up at an industrial factory time-clock bolted to a marble column, punch-cards in a steel slot, one mannequin already clocked out standing in a loading-dock doorway with a perfectly chrome cigarette, no lungs, a thin ribbon of metallic smoke, banks of cold fluorescent tubes in receding rows, one bank already dead, marble floor reflecting the lineup, a single red security-camera LED in the upper corner, a tiny wall-mounted CRT behind the clock playing EyeWash Station with the sound off, high contrast, strong silhouettes, no shoppers, no staff, 35mm, T2.8, deadpan and slightly menacing, Grok Imagine style
```

**Motion (6s):**
```
6-second locked wide. Time-clock thunks once. A punch-card drops into the catch. Chrome-cigarette smoke drifts toward the dock air. Fluorescents die one bank at a time, left to right. The camera LED blinks in 4/4. Downbeat of bar 5 is late — the lineup does not move on it. Mannequin faces do not change. Photoreal, no smile animation.
```

**Variations:** Their heels have time-clock stamps on the soles. Or one mannequin's
smile is slightly more tired than the factory paint allows.

## 4. Cities in the Shell

**Filename:** `gag-04-cities-in-the-shell`  
Still: `cities-in-the-shell-still.webp` · clip: `cities-in-the-shell-7s.mp4`

**Register:** impossible.

**One-sentence visual:** Two-a.m. oyster bar, black marble, one Edison bulb; a
woman in a sequined dress shucking; each opened shell holds a tiny, still-lit
city; she squeezes lemon over one and the miniature streetlights flicker; she
does not eat; she sets it aside like a rejected vintage.

**The gag:** Tonight's selection is a little metropolitan. She prefers something quieter.

**Connections:** Vivienne — cruel in small elegant ways; she is tasting cities the
way she tastes other people’s evenings. Miniature burning-town energy from
Episode 1, shrunk until it fits a plate. Liliane would have kept the dark-ocean
shell (she does). Do not put a second sister in the next stool; the empty stool
is enough.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
```
Photoreal one-panel close-to-medium film still, 4:5, 2 a.m. luxury oyster bar, black marble counter beaded with seawater, a single warm Edison bulb on a cloth cord, a glamorous bored woman with long black hair, blood-red lips, and a dark sequined dress shucking with a short silver knife, each opened shell lined in wet nacre holding a tiny still-lit night city — sodium windows, microscopic traffic, a harbor no bigger than a thumbnail — she is squeezing a lemon wedge over one city so a single bead of juice hangs above a boulevard, a small pile of rejected city-oysters to her left, one empty shell containing only dark ocean to her right which she is keeping, no other patrons, high contrast, luxury materials, macro detail in the nacre and the miniature streetlights, 35mm, T1.4, quiet apocalypse, Grok Imagine style
```

**Motion (7s):**
```
7-second macro-leaning lockoff. The lemon bead falls in slow motion and hits the miniature boulevard. One skyscraper window blinks out. Tiny headlights crawl a three-block grid. Her mouth almost smiles and does not. She does not eat. Steam from the lemon oil only. Photoreal, no camera orbit.
```

**Variations:** One oyster contains only a dark ocean. She keeps that one.

## 5. Complaint Window, Deep Field

**Filename:** `gag-05-complaint-window-deep-field`  
Still: `complaint-window-deep-field-still.webp` · clip: `complaint-window-deep-field-8s.mp4`

**Register:** impossible.

**One-sentence visual:** A lone wood-and-brass customer-service window floating in
deep space against a Hubble deep field; a woman in pearls slides a triplicate
form through the slot; only the clerk's hands and a red-ink stamp are visible;
the stamp reads DENIED; the ticket display says NOW SERVING: — and her number is
000000.

**The gag:** The universe has a wait time. It is not taking new tickets.

**Connections:** Liliane files; Vivienne would have leaned on the brass and asked
to speak to a manager who is a nebula. Ticket `000000` and `NOW SERVING: —` are
the 3:44 loop in another font. HOA energy without a clipboard. Sister to *After
Is a Floor*. Do not put the technical-difficulties card in frame.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
```
Photoreal one-panel film still, 2.39:1, a single old-fashioned wood-and-brass customer-service window floating alone in deep space against a Hubble deep-field of distant galaxies, a glamorous woman in a black dress and pearls in microgravity sliding a triplicate carbon form through the slot, only a clerk's pale hands and a red rubber stamp visible inside the booth, the stamp just marking DENIED in wet red ink, a ticket display above the window reading NOW SERVING: — (an em dash, no number), her paper ticket printed 000000, a brass plaque reading PLEASE TAKE A NUMBER, her pearl earring lifting in zero-g, high contrast practical fluorescent window light against cosmic black, strong silhouette, no planet, no ship, 35mm anamorphic, T2.0, dry bureaucratic dread, Grok Imagine style
```

**Motion (8s):**
```
8-second slow drift. The stamp comes down. The form's edges lift as if in a draft vacuum should not have. Stars crawl. The booth's fluorescent tube flickers once. Her earring rotates a quarter turn. The display never advances. Photoreal, camera almost locked, one percent drift to imply no floor.
```

**Variations:** The clerk stamps PLEASE STAND BY. Or her form is a seating chart.

## 6. The Overnight Conservator

**Filename:** `gag-06-the-overnight-conservator`  
Still: `the-overnight-conservator-still.webp` · clip: `the-overnight-conservator-6s.mp4`

**Register:** grounded surreal.

**One-sentence visual:** Museum classical wing, 3 a.m.; a woman in a torn ballgown
dusts a marble kouros; the statue holds her champagne flute; she holds its broken
forearm like a clipboard and is initialing a condition report; hard museum
spotlights; everything else velvet black.

**The gag:** She's restoring it. It's managing her evening.

**Connections:** Either sister after a gala — torn lace, smeared mouth, champagne
flute currently in the kouros’s hand. Condition report is the HOA clipboard’s
cultured cousin; do not use Karen’s board. Sibling hour to *Master Control, 4 AM*.
EyeWash snow on the security monitor. Song: [Condition Report](../songs/Condition_Report.md)
(also on the Songs page) — the clause-numbering skip (3 to 5, clause 4 never
arrives) is this gag's own structural flaw, set to solo cello and una corda piano.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
```
Photoreal one-panel film still, 3:2, classical sculpture gallery at 3 a.m., a woman in a torn black ballgown and smeared red lipstick dusting a white marble kouros with a small conservator's brush, the statue calmly holding her champagne flute at the correct height, she holding the statue's broken marble forearm like a clipboard and initialing a condition-report form in lipstick, hard museum spotlight pools on marble and silk, everything else velvet black, marble dust hanging in the beam, a second statue in the dark holding her black heels by the straps, a tiny security monitor in the far corner playing EyeWash Station snow, high contrast, 35mm, T2.0, elegant and slightly dangerous, Grok Imagine style
```

**Motion (6s):**
```
6-second locked gallery. Dust motes drift through the spotlight. One champagne bubble rises in the statue's glass. She turns a page. Stone eyes catch the light as if they blinked — maybe they did not. The second statue does not move. Photoreal, no dolly, no jump scare.
```

**Variations:** The condition report rates *her* for surface cracks. Or a second
statue in the dark is holding her shoes (already in the hero still).

## 7. Furnace Season

**Filename:** `gag-07-furnace-season`  
Still: `furnace-season-still.webp` · clip: `furnace-season-8s.mp4`

**Register:** grounded surreal.

**One-sentence visual:** A foundry converted for black-tie; a trough of molten
iron pours in a slow orange ribbon behind a string quartet that will not look
back; couture guests stand at a bored, safe distance; one woman in black lace
warms her hands at the pour the way you warm them at a fireplace; sparks die on
mink.

**The gag:** This is just how they do the weather now. Dress warm.

**Connections:** Vivienne — catastrophe as mood lighting; she hired a climate.
Episode 1 burning-town heat brought indoors. Quartet that will not turn is
*Choose Your Basalt* deadpan without the school-film joke. Liliane stands in the
bored middle distance if you need two bodies; she is counting exits, not warming
her hands.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
```
Photoreal one-panel film still, 2.39:1, industrial iron foundry converted into a black-tie gala, a slow ribbon of molten iron pouring in the background from a lip into a trough, a string quartet in evening wear playing without looking back, glamorous guests in couture standing at a bored, safe distance, one woman in a torn black lace dress warming her hands at the pour as if it were a fireplace, orange molten light versus cold chrome and black silk, sparks dying on a mink stole over her shoulders, place cards that look fireproof, heat shimmer between her fingers and the trough, high contrast, strong silhouettes, 35mm anamorphic, T2.0, Grok Imagine style
```

**Motion (8s):**
```
8-second perfect loop. The pour never stops. Sparks drift and extinguish on the mink. One violin bow draws slowly. Heat shimmer. Her fingers flex once toward the iron. The quartet does not look back. Photoreal, locked camera, loop on the pour cycle.
```

**Variations:** Place cards are fireproof (already in the hero still). Or the
quartet's rosin smoke matches the pour.

## 8. The Ice That Exhales

**Filename:** `gag-08-the-ice-that-exhales`  
Still: `the-ice-that-exhales-still.webp` · clip: `the-ice-that-exhales-7s.mp4`

**Register:** grounded surreal with one material swap.

**One-sentence visual:** Banquet table, white linen, silver, one tall ice
sculpture of a woman mid-curtsy; she is melting — but the melt is smoke, not
water; the cloth beneath her is perfectly dry; a waiter in white gloves adjusts
her a quarter-inch, as if this were still a centerpiece problem; lipstick on a
napkin; empty chair.

**The gag:** She's been leaving the party all night. Nobody asked her to stay.

**Connections:** The Two split across states of matter — one already left (empty
chair, lipstick, berries); the other is still being polite as smoke. Waiter is
Pizza Guy professional neutrality without a box. Direct still for *Pearls in the
Ashtray*. After-gala button for Episode 1 energy.

**Grok Imagine prompt:**
```
Photoreal one-panel film still, 3:2, formal banquet table with white linen and polished silver, a tall clear ice sculpture of an elegant woman mid-curtsy that is melting into dense cigarette-colored smoke instead of water, the tablecloth perfectly dry, a waiter in white gloves adjusting the sculpture a quarter inch as if this were still a centerpiece problem, a lipstick-stained napkin, one empty chair, two wine glasses and scattered berries at the far setting, candlelight leaning away from the smoke, high contrast, luxury and unease, 35mm, T2.0, Grok Imagine style
```

**Motion (7s):**
```
7-second lockoff. Smoke thickens from the sculpture's mouth and shoulders. The waiter's gloves make a micro-adjustment. Candle flames lean away. One ice earring drops and becomes a puff. The cloth stays dry. Photoreal, no whip, no face of the waiter.
```

**Variations:** The place card in front of the sculpture has her real name. Or the
smoke spells nothing, which is worse.

## Production notes

- Generate **stills first**. Confirm the joke reads at thumbnail size.
- Animation should never "sell" the joke harder.
- The Two can appear as silhouettes or red-lip close-ups (gags 1, 4, 7) but do
  not need to be named on screen.
- EyeWash Station can live in-frame as a tiny lobby TV (gags 1, 3, 6) without
  comment.
- Copy-ready cards live on the site Suggestions view under the **Gags** filter.
