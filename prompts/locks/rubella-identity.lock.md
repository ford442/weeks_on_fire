## What the blocks lock (and why)

- **Rubella is not one of The Two.** Same surname, no stated relation, never remarked on. Her
  register is *exhausted domestic*: no champagne flute, no paddle, no pendant, no chandelier, no
  corset. If a prompt starts to look like Vivienne at a microphone, the lock is missing.
- **Modern black lace long-sleeve dress** (long sleeves are hers; the Two wear sheer
  three-quarter lace). In a shared frame her lace reads one stop darker than Lillith's.
- **Ep3 only:** the silver vintage microphone. The white spy trench and hat are the Spy vs. Spy
  subplot, a different shot; this lock is the lawn performance.
- **Singing without belting**, eyes half-lidded, standing still. She does not laugh. She never
  flinches above the waist. Heels on the grass.
- **No work coat on the lawn** (the coat is the building register); no mug at the mic either.
