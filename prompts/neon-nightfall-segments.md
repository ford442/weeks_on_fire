# Neon Nightfall — Segment Prompts

Cutaway: [`content/cutaways/neon-nightfall.json`](../content/cutaways/neon-nightfall.json).  
Song: [`songs/Neon_Nightfall.md`](../songs/Neon_Nightfall.md).

Stills-to-video from one locked cyberpunk alley plate. **World lock:** one anonymous runner, one
police-style hover drone, rain-slick brick, magenta-cyan neon, one garish billboard at the far end.
No cast from the series. No readable logos or brand names. No Lace Waterfall, no Glamora, no
Annex, no EyeWash card.

Bed target: Minimax synth-wave at **108 BPM**, **0:30** total. Mute Imagine-baked audio; ride the
generated song stem as master and lay diegetic sounds in the edit (see song NOTES).

**Mouths:** OFF for all 30s. The runner never speaks; no lip-sync.

---

## Continuity lock (paste into every take)

Night alley in a dense Asian megacity, narrow brick canyon, wet asphalt reflecting magenta and cyan
neon from shop signs and window strips. Constant rain, medium-heavy, backlit. One lone runner,
gender-ambiguous, black hooded shell jacket with **white LED trim** strobing on the chest and
forearms in sync with the beat (the trim is the only “character” lighting on them). Dark running
pants, soaked shoes. Face in shadow under the hood; no teeth, no shouting mouth. One matte-grey
quad-rotor **surveillance drone** with a single red status light, no text, no HUD, no laser beam.
At the far end of the alley: one vertical **billboard frame** with abstract color bars and illegible
glyph-like shapes (not English, not a real brand). Photoreal neo-noir, anamorphic 2.39:1, Blade-Runner-
adjacent but not copying a named film still.

**Negatives (every take):** no celebrity face, no police in frame, no car chase, no motorcycle,
no gun, no blood, no wings, no cast from Weeks on Fire, no shampoo bottle, no EXIT sign as hero
prop, no readable words on the billboard, no drone firing anything, no runner looking at camera.
