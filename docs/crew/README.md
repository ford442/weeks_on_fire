## The desk map

Work moves in one direction for picture, and alongside it for music.

```
Mara (page) ──► selected still ──► Soren (directs picture) ──► Elio (prompt + archive)
                     │
                     └──► Julian slates once there is a picture job, or a bed with a door
Nova (beds) ── runs in parallel the whole way; a bed can land before its still
Roley ── owns the Marble Tick Tack lane end to end; the cabinet comes home
```

- **Ideas enter as Mara text.** Not as a Soren shot and not as a Julian slate.
- **Soren only directs selected picture.** No page, no song, no cast.
- **Julian slates after a picture job or a bed-with-a-door.** A page alone is not slateable.
- **Nova is parallel, not downstream.** She is not waiting for the cut.
- **Elio makes the prompt reproducible** and keeps the archive's worlds apart.
- **Roley owns Marble Tick Tack.** Nobody else's room gets the cabinet.

## The group project

The crew **produces** *Lace Waterfall*. It does not **join** it. Nobody on this list is in
the band, sings on the record, or stands on its stage. Julian holds the calendar, Nova holds
the beds, Mara holds the pages. If a draft puts a crew member at the Lace Waterfall mic, the
draft is wrong.
