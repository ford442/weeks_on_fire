## What it is

Local-access. Bad chroma key, one presenter, one visual claim, one spoken claim, a phone
number that is a floor number. The product is a rule the building already has.

## What it is not

**Not Ultra Screech 2**, not Rinse Formula, not a Glam College ad. Not Big City (that is
catalog hair). Not the EyeWash telethon (no phone bank, no tally, no pledge drive). Any
bottle in frame is furniture-scale — a thing on a shelf, never a hero product. Not the #19
season: Decorative Accent Removal is 20s and has no meeting in it.

## Locks

- One presenter per spot. Madelyn is voice and laminate only.
- One visual claim, one spoken claim. The claim is demonstrated, not argued.
- No gang vocal, no scream, no guitar. No Kenji.
- No real actor names. Ladies stay Grok Imagine building-cast.
- Each card says why it is not Ultra Screech, not Big City, not the EyeWash telethon.

### `chest-height-delivery-academy` — 20s

**Product:** Chest-Height Delivery Academy. A certificate course. The certificate is the
bag.
**Presenter:** the Pizza Guy, name tag illegible, phone at 23%. Deadpan, zero vibrato.
**Visual claim:** one continuous shot, bag at exactly chest height, through three doorways
of three different heights. The bag line never moves. The doorways do.
**Spoken claim:** *"Tip is appreciated. Enrolment is not."*
**Start JOB:** first doorway. **Last JOB:** third doorway, bag holds, card: *FLOOR 4.*

Not Ultra Screech: no bottle, no song, no laundry-room shoot, no Kenji. Not Big City: no
hair, no club. Not the telethon: no phone bank, no total, nobody asks for anything.

### `neutral-zone-laundry-minutes` — 18s

**Product:** Neutral-Zone Laundry Minutes. Sold by the minute. The minute is the dryer
timer.
**Presenter:** Madelyn. Intercom voice over a laminate card taped to the dryer. No face.
Crisp, slightly nasal, bureaucratic cabaret.
**Visual claim:** the dryer timer dial, locked shot. It turns from 40 to 39. The laminate
reads *NEUTRAL UNTIL THE MUSIC STARTS.*
**Spoken claim:** *"Neutral until the music starts. Then it is your minute."*
**Start JOB:** intercom click on. **Last JOB:** intercom click off, dial at 39.

Not Ultra Screech: the laundry room is the room, not a set rented by a shampoo company.
Nothing is for sale but time. Not Big City: no hair. Not the telethon: no pledges, no hosts
at desks.

### `elevator-inspection-notice` — 15s

**Product:** Elevator Inspection Notice. The notice is the product.
**Presenter:** the building's announcement voice — flat, institutional, **not** Oz, not
the EyeWash Continuity Voice. It belongs to the building.
**Visual claim:** the inspection certificate behind scratched plexi in the car. The date on
it is next year. The signature is fresh.
**Spoken claim:** *"Inspected. Not approved. Inspected."*
**Start JOB:** doors close on the certificate. **Last JOB:** doors open, same certificate,
one digit different.

Not Ultra Screech: nothing is loud. Not Big City: nothing is tall. Not the telethon: no
studio, no number to call, and EyeWash does not air this one — the building does.

### `decorative-accent-removal` — 20s, not #19

**Product:** Decorative Accent Removal. One pull.
**Presenter:** Lillith. Bright, one sentence, sudden energy, not a tour guide.
**Visual claim:** one purple LED strip around a unit doorframe, peeled off in a single
continuous pull. The strip comes away clean. The hallway is beige again.
**Spoken claim:** *"Accent removed. Ambition retained."*
**Start JOB:** thumb under the corner. **Last JOB:** the strip is a coil in her hand.

Not Ultra Screech: no product hero, no glam, no song. Not Big City: no hair. Not the
telethon. Not the HOA season: no slip, no meeting, no Karen, no gavel, no Madelyn — the
strip comes off because Lillith decided it does. Not the purple-light permit (that song
stays where it is).
