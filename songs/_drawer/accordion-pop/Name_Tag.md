STYLE:

Upbeat toy-piano pop, 126 BPM, C major, about one minute fifty-five. Poemesque, deadpan and sincere, a small argument with itself, no wink at the listener. Lead instrument is a toy piano playing a bouncy two-hand pattern, plinky and a little out of tune, with upright bass walking underneath and a light woodblock and brushed snare. A small brass pair, a muted cornet and a baritone horn, answers the vocal after each tag line with a short three-note figure shaped like a spoken name, close and a little thin, not ska, no offbeat stabs, no upstroke horns, no brass hits on the vocal lines. Vocal: Lillith bright and quick and Rubella dry and flat trading lines, never overlapping, together on the chorus, no belting, no stacked harmonies, no gang vocals. Verses are short lines of four to six words with specific nouns: colours, pens, stickers, hooks, coats. No name is ever sung or spoken anywhere in the song. Room is a small hallway with a coat rail, close mics. No guitar, no accordion, no electric instruments, no laughter, no spoken asides, no mugging. Structural flaw: the brass answers a name that was not sung, a three-syllable figure after every tag line, though the lyric never says a name. It does not stop answering, and it does not get a reply.

NOTES:

The flaw: the brass keeps answering a name, and the lyric never gives one. The figure is three syllables long every time, so a listener hears a name in the shape of the horns and finds none in the words. Do not add a sung name to explain it, and do not let the brass change shape to match a word.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not Recycling Bin Rebellion.** That owns the wrong-shade bin, the gavel snare, the cheap HOA horns and the line about adjusting a name tag. This song has no bin, no gavel, no clubhouse, no Karen, Dale or Brad, and the brass is two soft instruments, not ska.
- **Not the Pizza Guy's tag.** His tag is always illegible and the camera never finds it. Nobody's tag is read aloud here, in this song or by a horn, and he is not in it.
- **Not the near-miss name list.** Nobody is called anything, so no new near-miss is added to Viviane, Vivien, Lilith, Justin, Karyn, Ozzie or Bradley.
- **Not Left the Light / Inventory of the Weather / The Other Button.** Bank-mates with their own beds (nylon, reed organ, accordion). This one is a toy piano and a pair of horns.
- **Not Ground Rent / Article Seven.** No floor line, no fourteen minutes, no clock.
