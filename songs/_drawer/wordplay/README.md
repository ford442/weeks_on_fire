# Wordplay songs — sound tricks as tactics

*Drawer. Text only. 2026-11-02. `promote?: no` on every song. Not codegen'd.*

Five silly songs where a sound trick does the work **inside the lyric**, and the song
itself is about something else entirely: a heat wave, a Ferris wheel, a road trip, bedtime,
a lost car. Nobody sings about words, spelling or diction. If a lyric starts explaining
its own trick, it's wrong.

| Song | Device | Subject | Genre | Voices |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`An_Ice_Man.md`](An_Ice_Man.md) | **oronyms**: one sound split two ways (*a nice man / an ice man*) | heat wave on the block | steel-pan calypso, 112 | Lillith, Rubella, trio |
| [`Top_Spot.md`](Top_Spot.md) | **palindrome structure**: the lyric reads the same by line from either end; the peak line is a letter palindrome | a Ferris wheel ride | fairground waltz-pop, 132 (3/4) | Lillith up, Rubella down |
| [`Sad_Ballad_Bad_Salad.md`](Sad_Ballad_Bad_Salad.md) | **spoonerisms** as call and response, and both versions are true | rainy road trip and a diner | honky-tonk duet, 104 | Lillith calls, Rubella answers |
| [`Dog_Cog_Cot_Cat.md`](Dog_Cog_Cot_Cat.md) | **word ladder**: verse endings change one letter at a time | putting a house to sleep | ukulele lullaby, 66 (6/8) | Rubella, Lillith harmony |
| [`Level_Four_Dub.md`](Level_Four_Dub.md) | **echo answers**: the echo repeats the tail of each question, and the tail is the answer | lost car in a parking garage | roots dub reggae, 72 | Rubella and her echo |

**Production rule for all five:** the trick only works if the ear catches it. Where a
second voice answers (trio, Rubella, echo), it must reuse the first voice's rhythm and
pitch, so the swapped or split words land in the same notes. If a take gives the answer a
new melody, regenerate.

**Neighbours, already owned elsewhere (don't repeat):** *Tattarrattat* owns the knock-word
and "Madam, I'm Adam." *Pad Kid Poured Curd* owns the recall patter. *I Second Peggy
Babcock* owns the name-twister. *Triphthong Table* owns vowel sounds. `../forms/` owns
classical, phonetic and one-word palindrome pieces (*Tenet*). See
[`../../../notes/forms-wordplay.md`](../../../notes/forms-wordplay.md).

**Callbacks spent, as references:**
- World's Okayest Tenant mug (*An Ice Man*).
- The ping-pong table's "Thursday's weather on a Tuesday" (*Top Spot*).
- "Extra napkins are in the bag," said flatly by someone else after a disaster (*Sad Ballad,
  Bad Salad*).
- The 3:44 clock, twice, identical (*Level Four Dub*).

**Cut from this pass:**
- A heteronym song (*wind / wind*, *lead / lead*): the trick only shows on the page, and a
  listener can't hear it.
- An anagram song: also page-only.
- A "cold → warm" word ladder: two temperature songs next to *An Ice Man* was one too many.
