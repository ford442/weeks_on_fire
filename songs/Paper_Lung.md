STYLE:

Instrumental chamber miniature, bellows study, 76 BPM, A minor. Lead is a small accordion played as a paper lung: short bellows strokes, reeds close-miked so the air is audible before the note, never a tango, never a musette waltz, never a chord wall. Under it a muted pizzicato double bass, palm-damped so every note is a click with a pitch, walking only on the exhales and resting on the holds. One dry B-flat clarinet, chalumeau register, single line, no vibrato, no runs, played like someone reading a measurement aloud. Percussion is a paper-shaker only, a folded card rubbed and crimped in time, no kit, no hats, no kick. No 909, no 303, no choir, no soprano, no title chant, no harpsichord, no techno grid, no glam metal, no orchestral trailer, no synth pad, no reverb tail longer than the room. Central production idea: the tune is built from a two-bar cell that gains one extra fold on every exhale, the accordion adding one note each pass like a numbered paper cutout swapped for the next generation, then withholding on the hold, a bar of air only, bellows open and no reed sounding. The peak is four generations deep, dense but never loud, at about two thirds of the runtime. Structural flaw: the clarinet enters one bar late on every phrase and is never corrected, so its line always describes the fold the accordion has already left. Nothing in the arrangement acknowledges this. The bass lands the last note of each cycle a fraction early, a coin set down on wood. Mood: intimate, stopwatch-precise, lamp-warm, a small hard object returned to a small hand; not a fairy-tale orchestra, not whimsical. Dynamic shape: rises by folds, holds, withdraws by folds, and the last 8 to 12 seconds are smaller than the peak, one accordion note, one pizzicato click, one crimp of paper, out. Production: one small dry room, wooden floor, table-top mics, no plate, no hall, no widening, no sidechain, no maximizer, tape hiss, bellows creak and key noise welcome, mono-leaning. Runtime about 45 to 70 seconds, ends on an unresolved A minor with the clarinet still one bar behind.


LYRICS:

[Inst]

[Build Up]
Bellows opens. One more fold per exhale. Bass walks under. Clarinet one bar behind.

[Break]
Hold. Air only. Bellows open, no reed. Clarinet does not resolve.

[Outro]
Smaller than the peak. One note, one click, one crimp of paper. Out.
