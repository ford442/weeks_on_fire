# 1. Year-by-year scene cards

Each card is one room and **one job**. If a card needs two jobs, it is two years, and we do not have a fifth year.

---

## Year One — Live With a Cube

**Carries:** segment C (0:28–0:42)  
**Room:** annex classroom or first-year bay. Pale floor, two painted lines, one white cargo cube. Daylight from high slots. Bunks visible at the edge if the frame is wide.  
**Cast:** Riley only. Slate cadet coat over the sage crop. **No wings** — not even training wings. Year one does not get a rack.  
**One job:** the cube must hover exactly between the two lines, and she must be doing paperwork about it.

**Motion verbs (3):** *hover* (the cube, dead steady, micro-correcting), *write* (she marks a form without looking up), *drift* (dust in the slot-light).

**Sound bed:** room tone. No theme. The theme is not born yet.

**What Imagine will break:**
- Gives her wings because it has decided "space cop" means wings.
- Makes the cube spin, glow, or float dramatically. It is freight. It is boring on purpose.
- Adds a second cadet who is also Riley.
- Prints a hologram readout over the cube.

**Kill criteria — reject the take if:**
- Any wings on Riley.
- Cube is not between the lines, or the lines are not two.
- Text, HUD, or a floating card anywhere in frame.
- The room reads as a cockpit, a bridge, or a hangar with ships.

**Line room:** *"First year you live with a cube. Two lines on the floor. That's the whole degree."*

---

## Year Two — Nights

**Carries:** segment D (0:42–0:58)  
**Room:** night dorm. One desk lamp, one moon in the window, a bunk with another cadet asleep in it. Small training wings on a wall rack — **on the rack, not on her.**  
**Cast:** Riley at the desk. Sleeping bunkmate as a shape, face away.  
**One job:** she is on nights, and the diagram she is studying does not get more interesting.

**Motion verbs (3):** *hover* (practice cube, small, over a berth diagram), *turn* (a page, once), *hold* (the moon, the room, the whole shift).

**Sound bed:** hum. Possibly one distant tick — the tick that later becomes the theme's tick. Do not brass it yet.

**What Imagine will break:**
- Puts the training wings **on** her. They do not lift you. That is the joke.
- Wakes the bunkmate up and stages a conversation.
- Turns the window moon into a planet with rings, cities, or traffic. That is the E and I plates' job, not this one.
- Adds a second light source and makes it a music video.

**Kill criteria:**
- Wings worn.
- Bunkmate's face, or bunkmate awake.
- Two moons in this window (two moons belong to I, and I is a different sky).
- Any scanner lamp. Lamps are hardware. She has not been issued hardware.

**Line room:** *"Second year is nights. One moon. Same diagram."*

---

## Year Three — The Yard, and Further Exercises

**Carries:** segments E (hold) and F (room), 0:58–1:22  
**Room:** open terrace yard cut over a canyon city. Two faded rings on pale concrete. Then the gym/yard class.  
**Cast:** Riley plus a class. Porcelain wedge-head instructor with the long neck. Instructor's neck is longer than the rule and nobody mentions it.  
**One job:** the exercise got harder in the least interesting possible way — hold the cube at chest height until your arms shake.

**Motion verbs (4):** *hold* (cubes at chest, a whole row of them), *shake* (arms, small, honest), *tap* (instructor's pointer, once), *tilt* (one cadet's cube drifts off line).

**Sound bed:** wind on the yard hold. Class tone under F. Still no theme.

**Partner beat (dialogue only, no new room):** her partner filed everything as *floor* and went to **Appendix C**. Appendix C is a round room. Every spine in it is C. That is a sentence, not a set — it lands on the G hold.

**What Imagine will break:**
- Fills the yard with a squad drill, marching, or a fight.
- Gives cadets duck-bill heads. Duck-bills are thrown. Porcelain wedge heads only.
- Grows full hollow-chevron wings on the class. Year three is training wings or nothing.
- Adds yard hologram instruction cards. Those are an **edit layer** (cards 3 / 5 / 9), never a prop in the yard.
- Puts a second Riley in the line.

**Kill criteria:**
- Full wings on anyone but the instructor.
- Duck-bill or grey-alien heads.
- Any floating card, label, or number in the yard.
- Canyon city becomes a foreground set — it stays in the drop.

**Line room:** *"Third year they make it harder. Same cube. Chest height. Until your arms go."*

---

## Year Four — Lamps On

**Carries:** segments G (appendix hold) and H (bestowal), 1:22–1:50  
**Room:** Appendix C first — circular reading room, floor-to-ceiling identical volumes, all marked C. Then the bestowal hall with the painted aisle of two lines.  
**Cast:** Riley, full kit. Instructor. Cadets in a row behind.  
**One job:** she gets issued, and the issue is hardware plus a filing implement shaped like a sword.

**Motion verbs (4):** *turn* (one page, in the round room), *lock* (wings settle open — they arrive **already open**, they do not sprout), *warm* (scanner lamps come up soft pink), *receive* (both hands, sword placed into them).

**Sound bed:** **Annex theme enters here and only here.** Brass hymn plus paperwork tick. She does not sing it.

**What Imagine will break:**
- Animates the wing growth. Never animate the growth. Cut to them locked.
- Turns the lamps into beams. Lamps are hardware, they illuminate a manifest, they never enter a body.
- Makes the sword a katana, a beam sword, or long enough to swing.
- Has her raise it, test it, or point it.
- Puts lace in the Annex. Rubella and Lillith never visit.

**Kill criteria:**
- Feathers, snowflake wings, or hex-chain wings.
- Any beam from any lamp.
- Sword swung, raised overhead, or held one-handed.
- Blood, sparks, applause, or a crowd cheer.
- Rubella or Lillith anywhere in the Annex.

**Line room:** *"Fourth year they turn the lamps on and hand you a key that's been told it's a sword."*

# 3. Bestowal, hardened (`VjpbN`)

This is the beat the preview never reaches. It has to be right the first time.

**The four locks:**

| Element | Locked as | Thrown |
|---|---|---|
| Sword | Short. Too short. Rectangular cutout or a hole through the blade. Honeycomb openwork guard. White manufactured metal, matte, no edge glint. A key that was told it was a sword. | Katana, longsword, beam sword, glow, engraving, gems, a scabbard with a house crest |
| Wings | White hollow-chevron architectural wings, already open, already locked when we cut in | Feathers, snowflake geometry, hex-chain, membrane, growth animation, wings that beat |
| Lamps | Soft pink scanner lamps as **hardware** — mounted, warm, aimed at paperwork | Beams, eye beams, a scan sweeping a body, red, strobe |
| Instructor | Tall porcelain-white swan neck, smooth wedge head, long slate coat. Places the sword with both hands. | Duck-bill, grey alien, helmet, face, a general, a speech |

**Body:** she receives with **two hands**, elbows in, head level. She does not kneel. She does not swing, test, raise, or sight down it. If Imagine gives her a grip, the take is dead.

**The row behind:** cadets standing, still, facing forward. Not saluting. Not cheering. This is a processing queue that happens to have a ceremony in it.

**Theme:** brass hymn plus paperwork tick enters on the placement. It is the only place in the cutaway it plays. It dies under *right away* in J — hard cut, no tail, no reverb ring-out.

**Hardened prompt (drop-in replacement for the H fence if the current one drifts):**
```
Start from the locked bestowal hall: a long painted aisle of exactly two parallel lines on a pale floor, cadets standing still in one row behind, Riley Elaine Smith in a slate coat over a sage backless crop, amethyst pendant, full white hollow-chevron architectural wings already open and locked, soft pink scanner lamps mounted as hardware and aimed low at paperwork, a tall porcelain-white swan-neck instructor with a smooth wedge head in a long slate coat placing a short odd white sword with a honeycomb openwork guard and a rectangular cutout in the blade into her two open hands. Wings hold. Lamps warm. She receives with both hands, elbows in, head level. She does not swing, raise, or grip it. 14 seconds. No wing growth animation, no feathers, no snowflake wings, no hex-chain wings, no katana, no beam sword, no glow on the blade, no eye beams, no beams from the lamps, no kneeling, no saluting, no cheering, no blood, no duck-bill heads, no black lace, no HUD, no text, Grok Imagine style
```

**Kill criteria for the bestowal, in order:** wings wrong → dead. Sword long → dead. Any beam → dead. She swings → dead. Lace in frame → dead.

# 4. Off-ramp

The posting never starts. That is the whole ending.

**I — two moons (1:50–1:56).** Bone moon over copper moon. Aurora in filing lines. Bazaar glow far below. **She is not in this frame.** The plate holds while, off-picture, she inhales to start the story of the first posting — canyon window, a take-a-number that is not a number, a market under two moons. We hear the breath. We never see the market.

**J — right away (1:56–2:04).** Cut back to the same lounge table as A. Sage-crop Riley, blonde, amethyst, **no wings, no lamps, no sword.** Rubella and Lillith in black lace. Jumbo bag. Pale snake still on the marshmallows.

```
RUBELLA
We're expected back down to Earth. Right away.

LILLITH
Before the second verse.
```

Theme dies on Rubella's first word. No fade, no tail.

**Off-ramp kill criteria:**
- Any Annex element in the lounge windows — no canyon, no rings, no two moons.
- Any wing, lamp, sword, or slate coat at the table.
- Riley finishing the market story.
- The snake anywhere near Annex 7. Snake stays on the marshmallows.
- A button line after Lillith. Lillith closes it.

**Return continuity:** the sage crop in J must match A exactly — same hair fall, same pendant position, same seat. If the return reads as a different day, the door didn't work.
