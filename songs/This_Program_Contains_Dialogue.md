STYLE:

Institutional torch / legal-bumper hymn at 84 BPM. Narrow-bandwidth Continuity Voice — warm, sincere, half-step flat on the word “dialogue.” Small chapel organ that sounds like a legal department. Color-bar sine as a pedal tone. Tape hiss. No drums until a single rubber stamp on the last line. No belting. No vibrato. **Structural flaw:** he recites the warning as a song, which is not dialogue; after every chorus he adds one spoken aside to satisfy Standards & Practices; S&P is never satisfied; the card still reads CONTAINS DIALOGUE. The listed contents may grow; the bar count does not. Mood: EyeWash pre-program warning, not a sign-off. Runtime ~1:25–1:45. Use Continuity Voice; the S&P Skeleton may stamp once, dry, off-mic. Do not use Rubella.

NOTES:

## What the song is
The **pre-program warning** for the wordy episode. EyeWash is required to tell the affected that dialogue is coming. Continuity Voice does it as a hymn because that is how the station talks. Standards & Practices (the unionized skeleton, day job) is not satisfied: singing is not dialogue. He waits. Someone off-mic finally says “Is this on.” The stamp comes down. The card never changes.

The joke is the disclosure, not the wordiness. Nobody says “this is the talky one.”

## Craft (keep this off-mic)
- *Rinse Cycle* owns sign-off and “we have been concluding it for years.” This is the **open**, not the close.
- *Haunted or Unscheduled* owns the 4 AM log bed. Do not develop a Rhodes loop here.
- *Pad Kid* owns the late-correction bulletin and clause accretion as a squeeze. If contents grow, they grow as **disclosures**, not recall language.
- Marguerite’s legalese (“aspect ratio,” “zoning matter,” “rinse duration”) is borrowed as station policy, not as her cameo. She does not have to appear.
- *The Long Way Up* owns the eleven-minute climb and the station card **THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM CONTAINS DIALOGUE.** This song is that card with a melody.

## Production notes
- **Episode placement:** EyeWash bumper into [`notes/scenes/the-long-way-up.md`](../notes/scenes/the-long-way-up.md); also usable before any scene that is about to talk more than the show usually allows.
- **Voice:** Continuity Voice. S&P Skeleton stamp only. Off-mic “Is this on” may be Oz or an unnamed booth.
- **Structural flaw:** Sung warning is not dialogue; spoken asides fail S&P until the last two words. Do not “fix” by making the verses spoken-word from the start.
- **Runtime target:** 1:25–1:45.
