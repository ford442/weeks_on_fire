# Le Manège

**Genre collision:** valse-musette (accordion waltz) × a steam fairground band organ (calliope pipes, glockenspiel, a big bass drum) × English music-hall. A carousel's own music, played as a chanson.

**Visual concept:** Dusk in a town square, at an old carousel with painted wooden horses on brass poles. Rubella rides a white horse. Next to her Lillith rides a carousel pig, not holding the pole, playing guitar. They go round a hundred times. The horses are bored, and on the drop they step off the platform and gallop out into the town, riders and all. They keep rising and falling on their brass poles as they go (the poles come with them) along the river, through the market, up the hill steps and over the bridge. On the last chorus they gallop back and click into place on the platform exactly as the music ends.

**Laugh (kid words):** *The merry-go-round horses get bored of going round, so they gallop all over town, and they come back right before the music stops.*

**BPM:** 156 in 3/4, F major, up a step to G for the final chorus

**Target length:** ~2:20 · 13 segments

**English gloss of the French lines (for the room, not for MiniMax):**
- *V1:* A ticket for the carousel, a white horse for me. My friend on a pig, her guitar in her arms. We've been round a hundred times and gone nowhere. The horses have had enough; you can see it in their eyes.

- *Chorus:* The carousel. Tonight, they go everywhere. They get home before the end.
- *V2:* We cross the market, going up and coming down, the brass poles with us and the music following. My friend isn't holding the pole. She's playing on her pig.
- *Outro:* *Encore un tour* = one more go.

## Drop map

**The pole rule:** every wooden animal keeps its brass pole and keeps rising and falling on it in time with the waltz (one rise per bar), whether it is on the platform or galloping down a street. The poles end in the air above the animals, attached to nothing. Nobody ever explains it. The animals are painted wood, with no real horse movement in the faces. **Mouths:** Rubella's mouth is on for the chorus hook and the breakdown. Lillith's mouth stays closed.
