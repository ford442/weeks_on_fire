# Plate credits — Weeks on Fire

Info-page credits for the Imagine cast. **Not real actors.** The series lock holds: building-cast faces are Grok Imagine plates, and no living performer's name goes under a still.

A plate credit is the line under the info still. It is never spoken on screen, never searched as a casting call, and never swapped in for the character name. Crew names (Mara Vell, Julian Rook, and the rest) are already the credit. They do not get a second "played by."

Added 2026-10-05. Pictures for these pages were generated as info stills and are **not** committed (bible rule: no new large binaries; write the prompt). Holding stills live with the production chat, not in `public/cast/`.

## The rule

| Layer | What it is | Spoken? |
|---|---|---|
| Character | Who the show calls them | Yes |
| Plate credit | Invented house name under the info still | Never |
| Crew name | The fictional writer / director / desk | In the pretend room only |

If a plate starts to look like a known person, throw the still. Christina's house already uses a promo-likeness *as location costume*, not as a credited singer. Her plate credit is not that singer's name.

Madelyn still has no face. Her credit is voice only.

## Strand, and the snakes that are not Strand

**The snake's name is Strand.**

Strand is the Home Sweet Void table snake: pale, seated, coffee-house, pleased, industry. Eyes come on. The white building cat starts one sentence and stops. No roar. No beam. Strand does not visit Annex 7, does not ride the lawn, and is not a person who became a snake.

The Episode 3 laser pair stay weather. *Halloween Snake Battle* is their cue, not their name. The union break card may say **7-S North** and **7-S Receipt**. Those are laminate call-signs. Nobody pets them, and nobody introduces them.

Drain snakes in the Ultra Screech shower and the live scales in the Well Fall lining are set dressing. They do not inherit Strand's name.

## Bio adds that were missing under the existing names

**Rubella Vale (Nessa Quill).** Lost the pension the day the company erased HR and the backups of the backups. Keeps contraband detergent that still smells like fabric softener. Trusts no one who smiles before 10 AM. On a cutaway she can sell a love song and a threat assessment in the same breath. She files the elevator ticket and never says the counteroffer into a mic unless the scene is already a song.

**Lillith Vale (Ione Brack).** Former busker, evicted from three sectors for noise and unsanctioned light. The building is her largest installation. Retro tech repaired with parts that do not belong together, glorious for one night. She starts the song. Rubella finishes it on a sigh that still lands on the beat. The one time she is told no, she sits down, and the change is never marked.

**Qing Rao (Sable Keene).** Materials lecturer. The skull is acrylic, injection-moulded, 2019, and it glows anyway. She does not flinch because she has already classified the object. Noodles are the lobby cameo only. Systems knowledge is real and unmystical: she read the manual and kept it. Mailbox says Qing. She uses both syllables.

**Riley Smith (Willa Acre).** The straight woman. Knows things. Family all living. The forest-road "You're walking?" is earlier than the space packet. Wings, lamps, and the honeycomb sword stay in the Annex holds. Earth exit is a Roadster with an empty seat. She is not retrieved.

**Christina (Linnea Holt).** Stays in the house. Ladies may have thirty seconds at the door and leave. Song is already on when we arrive. One extra body per plate. Hovsångare is a thin folder at the threshold, not the King. Swedish words first. The plate credit is Holt so the info page does not borrow the singer's name.

**Kenji Sato (Ren Okada).** Glam-Sham-Poo alumnus, not a resident. Tag line lives on the Big City bed: the hair is in Big City. Bottle is furniture-scale in the city plates, never a hero. No Kenji on the Lace Waterfall sleeve.

**The Pizza Guy (Otto Fenn).** Red jacket, dead eyes, bag at chest height, phone at 23 percent. The universe's only constant. He crosses a paused battlefield like a crossing guard. He and the black cat do not exit in the same shot.

**Madelyn (Harriet Pell, voice).** Laminate and the intercom. Twenty years of middle management. The mug is cracked and she will not say so. Three of the old sample lines on the building page belong to Karen. Do not merge her with Madelin, and do not give Pell a face.
