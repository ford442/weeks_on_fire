STYLE:

Diegetic instrumental loop at 92 BPM, D major, exactly thirty-four seconds, seamless.
The kick is a dryer drum tumbling a single heavy item — a thunk on every beat, slightly uneven.
The drone is a fluorescent ballast hum, tuned to D, never louder than the dryer.
A coin-return rattle plays a two-bar rhythm as the only percussion besides the dryer.
One intercom chime, a detuned major third, sounds once every eight bars and lands a quarter-beat late every time.
No harmony instrument, no bass guitar, no keys, no melody.
Everything is source sound treated as music: found-sound, close-miked, small tiled room.
The loop point is invisible: the last dryer thunk is the first.
No dynamics, no build, no swell.
Mixed low to sit under dialogue; the chime is the only element allowed to poke through.

(instrumental — no vocal)

NOTES:

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not The Laundromat Saints.** That is a 66 BPM song with a congregation. This is a room
  with a dryer. Nobody sings, nobody is saved.
- **Not Rinse Cycle / EyeWash.** No Continuity Voice, no ident, no network. The chime is
  the building's, not the station's.
- **Not Daisy / Hanshaw.** No heart at all; it is a clock.
- **Not Ultra Screech.** The laundry room here is the laundry room, not a set rented by a
  shampoo company. Nothing is loud.
- **Not Big City / Lightning / Well Fall.** No hair, no charge, no drop.

Use: under any laundry card in the house drawer. The lateness of the chime is the joke;
do not quantize it.
