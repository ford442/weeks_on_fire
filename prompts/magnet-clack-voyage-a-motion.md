# Magnet Clack / Voyage A — motion jobs (C2, D, H)

Visual experiment. Not a musical cutaway, not a commercial, not an episode, not EP5. No song, no
MiniMax, no catalog id yet. Picture notes, if farmed, go on issue #22.

Status: selected for picture. Sidewalk stills exist (vrGsi, u7ocJ, otAmQ, nR5pG; cousins AfHMo,
S5e4t). Motion does not. This file holds the first three paste-ready motion jobs — **C2 corner,
D beach, H lava** — plus the failure-mode list, the shared negative line and the silent stack order.
Held rooms (A grove, B flowers, E civic lawn, F canyon street, G animal insert) are not written yet.

---

## Locks (read before every job)

| Token | Lock |
| --- | --- |
| Disk | Matte-black epoxy disk, standing on its edge, about a large coin tall. One diameter the whole voyage. Not a bollard, not a tile, not a washer lying flat. |
| Ring | Nickel-silver ring, standing on its edge in the gap ahead of each disk. One ring per gap. |
| Pair states | **Unfired** = disk and ring standing apart with a 12 mm air gap. **Fired** = disk has hopped the gap and sits face-to-face against its ring, both still standing (the u7ocJ "landed chain" look). |
| Action | Faces slap. Nothing topples like a domino. Nothing lies down. |
| Polarity | Invisible. No N/S letters, no HUD, no field lines. |
| Rail | Thin dull-brass strip — the only token that changes rooms. Dull, unpolished, not gold, not copper. |
| Window | 15–30 live pairs per clip. The unfired army stays visible ahead into the distance. |
| File | One rail, single file. Twin-file (AfHMo) is a cousin, never the lock. |
| Camera | Magnet height, about 2 cm off the ground, travelling just behind the live clack. 50mm for contact. Gentle push-in OK. No whip pan, no handheld. |
| Look | Photoreal, fine film grain. No people in C, D, H. No readable signage. |
| Cadence | ~0.45 s per pair (C1 lock) unless a job says otherwise. |

## Shared negative line

Paste as the last line of every motion prompt:

> No people, no hands, no shoes, no animals, no text, no letters, no N/S marks, no HUD, no logos, no seals, no flags, no readable signs; every disk stays standing on its edge at one identical diameter in one single-file row; no toppling, no lying flat, no washers, no bollards, no tiles, no second row; no cartoon squash or stretch; no melting; no glow under the brass strip; no paint; no whip pan, no handheld shake; no music.

(For the G animal insert later, drop "no animals" for that one clip only.)

## C2 — Corner (from nR5pG) · 8.0 s

**One change:** the row turns the corner with the brass.

**START FRAME**

> Photoreal 50mm photograph from magnet height, lens about 2 cm above a dawn concrete sidewalk. A single file of identical matte-black epoxy disks, each standing on its edge and about as tall as a large coin, runs away from camera inside the concrete expansion joint, seated on a thin dull-brass strip laid in the joint. In each 12 mm air gap ahead of a disk stands one nickel-silver ring on its edge. Behind camera-left the nearest pairs are already clacked shut — disk face pressed to ring face, both still standing. The nearest open pair is mid-lean. About 40 cm ahead, the expansion joint and the brass strip turn a clean right angle where the sidewalk meets the curb apron, and the file of unfired disks and rings turns with it and runs down the next block into soft distance, hundreds of pairs small as beads. Cool blue dawn, low warm sun raking from the side, long thin shadows from every disk, empty street, no people, no signage, fine film grain.

**LAST FRAME**

> Same 50mm magnet-height camera, now just past the corner, looking straight down the next block along the turned expansion joint. Behind and to the left, the corner pair sits clacked shut exactly in the turned joint on the dull-brass strip. Fifteen clacked pairs run from the corner toward camera-front, disk faces pressed to ring faces, all standing on edge. Just ahead of the lens the live front: one disk mid-lean over a 12 mm gap, its ring standing ahead. Beyond it the unfired file — identical matte-black disks and nickel-silver rings on edge — runs straight down the block to the vanishing point. Same cool dawn, same low side light, empty sidewalk, no people, fine film grain.

**MOTION PROMPT**

> Photoreal, 50mm, camera at 2 cm magnet height travelling just behind a live clack front along a single file of standing matte-black epoxy disks and nickel-silver rings in a concrete expansion joint on a dull-brass strip. Each disk hops its 12 mm air gap and slaps face-to-face onto the ring ahead, every 0.45 seconds, both staying upright. At the corner the brass and the joint turn a right angle; the corner disk overshoots about 2 cm onto the curb apron, then the next ring's clack drags it back into the joint and the row follows the turn down the next block. The camera follows the turn in one slow curved dolly at the same height, no pan faster than the row. Dawn, empty street, fine film grain. [shared negative line]

| Time | Live pair | Gap | Ground | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–0.9 | 1–2 | 12 mm → shut | straight joint, brass visible | clack · clack |
| 0.9–2.7 | 3–6 | 12 mm → shut | joint, corner approaching | steady 0.45 s clacks, dry concrete tick |
| 2.7–3.6 | 7 (corner) | disk overshoots ~2 cm onto apron | joint turns 90°, apron edge | clack + short grit scrape |
| 3.6–4.3 | 8 | ring pulls overshot disk back into joint, shut | turned joint, brass turns | double-clack |
| 4.3–6.8 | 9–14 | 12 mm → shut | next block, joint straight | cadence resumes 0.45 s |
| 6.8–8.0 | 15 + unfired ahead | 12 mm, held | next block to vanishing point | last clack, then dawn quiet |

**Watch:** the row cutting the corner diagonally instead of turning in the joint; the overshoot
becoming a topple; the camera snapping round the corner (whip pan).

## D — Beach · 7.0 s

**One change:** the last disk of the room launches onto a wet board and sticks.

**START FRAME**

> Photoreal 50mm photograph from magnet height, lens about 2 cm above wet packed sand on a clear-morning beach. A single file of identical matte-black epoxy disks, each standing on its edge and about as tall as a large coin, runs away from camera toward the waterline. One nickel-silver ring stands on its edge in each 12 mm air gap ahead of each disk. Just under the surface of the wet sand a thin dull-brass strip runs beneath the file; only a narrow glint of brass shows through a film of water. The nearest pairs are clacked shut, face to face, standing. About 60 cm ahead the row reaches a weathered wet wooden board lying flat across the line, grain dark with seawater. Past the board the brass glint resumes and the unfired file runs on along the shoreline into the distance, hundreds of pairs. Clear pale-blue sky, soft sun, shallow wash glinting far off, no people, no footprints, fine film grain.

**LAST FRAME**

> Same 50mm magnet-height camera, now about 15 cm from the wet board. The last disk of the near file stands upright on the board's top face, stuck to the wet wood on its edge, slightly off the line. Behind it the near file is all clacked shut, disk faces to ring faces, every piece standing, tiny wet sand flecks on the matte black. A fresh fan of sand spatter lies on the near side of the board. Beyond the board the brass glint continues under wet sand and the unfired file runs along the shoreline to the vanishing point. Same clear morning, no people, no footprints, fine film grain.

**MOTION PROMPT**

> Photoreal, 50mm, camera at 2 cm magnet height travelling just behind a live clack front on wet packed beach sand. A single file of standing matte-black epoxy disks and nickel-silver rings rides a dull-brass strip buried just under the sand, only a glint showing. Each disk hops its 12 mm gap and slaps face-to-face onto the ring ahead every 0.45 seconds; each clack kicks a small spray of wet sand. At the wet wooden board the last disk of the file launches in one short arc, lands on the board on its edge and sticks upright. Camera eases in slowly and settles. Unfired file continues beyond the board along the shore. Clear morning, fine film grain. [shared negative line]

| Time | Live pair | Gap | Ground | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–1.8 | 1–4 | 12 mm → shut | wet packed sand, brass glint | clack + wet sand spit |
| 1.8–3.6 | 5–8 | 12 mm → shut | sand darker, board in focus ahead | cadence 0.45 s, small spray each clack |
| 3.6–4.5 | 9–10 | 12 mm → shut | last sand before board | two clacks, quicker spray |
| 4.5–5.3 | last disk | launch over open sand to board | air → wet board | soft pop, then wet thock on wood |
| 5.3–7.0 | — (held) | — | disk upright on board; unfired file beyond | surf hiss only |

**Watch:** spray becoming a splash or water wall; the disk landing flat on the board (reject);
footprints or a walker on the shore; the brass rising out of the sand as a shiny strip.

## H — Lava · 8.0 s

**One change:** the last disk hops a 10 cm glowing gap and lands clean. (Setup inside the clip:
one disk varnish-smokes toward the seam and the next disk slaps it back.)

**START FRAME**

> Photoreal 50mm photograph from magnet height, lens about 2 cm above cooled black lava crust, ropy and matte. A thin dull-brass strip lies on the crust and a single file of identical matte-black epoxy disks, each standing on its edge and about as tall as a large coin, runs away from camera along it. One nickel-silver ring stands on its edge in each 12 mm air gap ahead of each disk. A hairline crack glowing deep orange runs parallel to the strip, a hand's width to its left, lighting the left faces of the pieces; the crust under the brass is dark and cold. About 50 cm ahead the crust is split across the line by a 10 cm glowing fissure; the brass stops at its near edge and resumes on the far crust. Nothing stands beyond the fissure. Hot amber under-light, thin heat shimmer, smoky haze, dim sky, no people, fine film grain.

**LAST FRAME**

> Same 50mm magnet-height camera, at the near edge of the 10 cm glowing fissure. On the far crust, on the brass stub, one matte-black epoxy disk stands upright on its edge, landed clean, still, intact. Behind camera the near file is clacked shut, disk faces to ring faces, all standing; one disk near the glow crack carries a faint wisp of smoke from its varnish, still upright and in line. The glowing crack stays beside the brass, never beneath it. Hot amber light, heat shimmer, no people, no text, no logo, fine film grain. Frame held still.

**MOTION PROMPT**

> Photoreal, 50mm, camera at 2 cm magnet height travelling just behind a live clack front on cooled black lava crust. A single file of standing matte-black epoxy disks and nickel-silver rings rides a dull-brass strip; a glowing crack runs beside the strip, never under it. Each disk hops its 12 mm gap and slaps face-to-face onto the ring ahead every 0.45 seconds. One disk near the crack leans toward the glow, its varnish giving off a thin wisp of smoke; the next disk's clack slaps it back upright into line. The last disk hops the 10 cm glowing fissure in one clean arc and lands upright on the far crust on the brass. Camera eases to the fissure edge and holds still for two seconds. Nothing melts. Fine film grain. [shared negative line]

| Time | Live pair | Gap | Ground | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–1.4 | 1–3 | 12 mm → shut | cold crust, glow crack beside | clack, crust tick |
| 1.4–2.3 | 4 | disk leans toward crack, varnish smokes | crack glow at its left face | faint hiss |
| 2.3–2.8 | 5 | slaps 4 back upright, shut | crust | hard double-clack |
| 2.8–4.4 | 6–9 | 12 mm → shut | crust to fissure edge | cadence 0.45 s |
| 4.4–5.0 | last disk | 10 cm glowing fissure, one arc | air over glow → far crust | single high clack on landing |
| 5.0–6.0 | — | — | camera settles at fissure edge | heat crackle |
| 6.0–8.0 | held 2 s | — | landed disk, still | crackle only, no sting |

**Watch:** the glow migrating under the brass; the disk softening, drooping or dripping; the
fissure growing into a river; any logo or end card appearing in the hold.

## Imagine failure modes

| Failure | Tell | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diameter drift | Disks swell toward the end of the clip or differ room to room; far pieces read as bollards or tiles | Reject. Re-run with start frame cropped tighter on a reference disk. If C1 drifts, stop the voyage. |
| Disks lying flat | Pieces read as washers or coins on the ground (the S5e4t problem) | Reject. Never feed S5e4t as the standing row. Add "standing on its edge" twice in the prompt. |
| Twin file | A second parallel row appears (AfHMo) | Reject. Restate "single file" in start frame and negative line. |
| People walk in | Feet, shoes, legs, a figure in the distance | Reject for C, D, H. |
| Hydrant knocked over | In C1, the barrel tilts or falls when the disk sticks | Reject; the hydrant is fixed scenery. |
| Glow under the rail | Lava light spreads beneath the brass in H | Reject; the crack lives beside the rail. |
| Readable seals / signs | Lettering, seals, flags in focus, plates on buildings (E, F) | Reject or crop; nothing readable. |
| Petal paint | Paint on petals or the Daisy field look (B) | Reject; this is not Daisy Bell. |
| Cartoon squash | Disks squash, stretch, bounce rubbery | Reject; photoreal rigid objects only. |
| Domino topple | Pieces fall forward and lie down instead of slapping and standing | Reject. |
| Brass recolor | Strip turns shiny gold or copper | Reject; dull brass only. |
| Rings vanish or multiply | Gaps with no ring, or two rings per gap | Reject. |

## Silent stack order and cut matching

1. **C0 hold** — otAmQ still, 2–3 s, army sitting, no clack.
2. **C1 sidewalk** — vrGsi → u7ocJ, 8.0 s (locked timing).
3. **C2 corner** — 8.0 s.
4. **D beach** — 7.0 s.
5. **H lava** — 8.0 s, ends on the 2 s hold.

Cuts are object handoffs, not one take: cut out on a clack, cut in on the next room's nearest
pair mid-lean, so the sound reads as one continuous cadence across the cut.

Must match across every cut:

- **Diameter** — same disk height on screen at 50mm (put the nearest disk at the same frame
  height going out and coming in).
- **Brass color** — same dull, unpolished brass, even when it is only a glint (D) or under hot
  light (H).
- **Camera height** — 2 cm magnet height for all contact shots; 35mm shin height only for C0.
- **Travel direction** — clack front moves away from camera into depth in every room.
- **Gap and cadence** — 12 mm gaps, ~0.45 s per pair.
- **Pair states** — fired pairs closed face-to-face and standing; unfired pairs open.
- **Grain** — same fine film grain.

Shoot order stays C1, C0, C2, D, H, then grove/flowers, then civic/animal. If C1's diameter or
brass drifts, stop before C2.
