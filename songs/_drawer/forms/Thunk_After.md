STYLE:

Onomatopoeia as a pedal, 72 BPM, about one minute twenty. One dryer in the ladies' building laundry. The only lyric is the word Thunk, once per bar, on beat 1, short and dry. It is the dryer drum, one heavy item turning, not a kick drum, not a sample kit, not a riff. A lied-scale piano, soft pedal, small room, holds one note, G, under the thunks. No other piano notes until the cadence. No chords during the hold. No bass, no kit, no hats, no gang, no second word, no cycle name. Structural flaw: the piano plays a short cadence and stops, and the thunk continues for two full bars after the piano is gone. Nobody cuts the dryer. The piano does not come back. No metal, no distorted guitar, no scream, no marble clicks, no night lot, no intercom, no drip, no landing. Mood: the machine outlasts the cadence.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: the thunk continues two bars after the piano has cadenced. The dryer is not cut. Those two bars have no other sound.

The thunk is the only percussion, and it outlives the music. Nothing is sent in to cover the tail.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not Double Kick the Dryer.** That card is joke metal. The thunks are grouped on beat 2, the gang sings a cycle name, and the tail is after the riff. This is one thunk on beat 1, a piano note, and the tail is after a cadence.
- **Not Marble Tick Tack.** That bed is clicks, magnets, and a night lot, and nobody speaks. This is a laundry room. The word is thunk. There is a piano.
- **Not Neutral Zone (Laundry Bed).** That loop puts a thunk on every beat and calls it the kick, with an intercom chime a quarter-beat late. This thunk is once a bar. The piano cadences. There is no chime.
- **Not Drip on the Landing.** That card is the word plink, a sixteenth late, against a piano note on every downbeat. This word is thunk. It sits on beat 1. The piano holds one note, cadences, and the dryer goes on without it.
- **Not Same Sentence.** No clarinets, no English sentence, no phase. The lyric is the one word.
