## Logline bio

Rubella is a former quality-assurance inspector who lost her pension to a deleted folder and
moved into the building for cheap rent and quiet. She got neither. She is tired, dry and
completely competent: the calmest person in any laundry room that is going wrong. She lives in
Six-C with Lillith, whom she calls "my roommate," and she is not unkind. She is rationed.

### Micro-gestures (use sparingly, never explain)

1. **The two-finger brush.** When something hits her (plaster, glitter, a heckle), she brushes it off once, downward, with two fingers. Never above the waist. Never a flinch.
2. **Mug as evidence.** She sets the mug down like an exhibit, not like a drink.
3. **She does not turn.** A noise behind her gets no head-turn. She already knows which door it was.
4. **Doorframe clock.** At a threshold she stands exactly as long as the door allows (laundry room ten minutes, elevator lobby four, the roof as long as the wind holds, Christina's door thirty seconds) and then goes.
5. **The elbow.** When Lillith is about to go one step too far, Rubella takes her elbow. No line.

## Voice guide

- **Sentence length:** four to nine words. Subject, verb, the damage. She never stacks clauses for effect.
- **Delivery:** dry, slightly raspy, zero urgency. Exhausted cabaret: she can sell a love song and a threat assessment in the same breath. Never shouts. Never speeds up.
- **Vocabulary she overuses:** *file* / *filed* / *ticket* · *rent* ("I already pay rent") · *Item* plus a number · *hazard* · *log* · floor numbers · *Thursday* · *roommate*
- **Vocabulary she refuses:** *sister* (never, about anyone) · *amazing* · *I'm scared* · *we need to talk* · *darling* (that is Vivienne's) · *technically* (that was her old employer's word)
- **Humor type:** deadpan reportage. She describes the impossible thing as a maintenance problem and moves on. No setup, no wink, no punch emphasis. She does not laugh, at her own lines or anyone's.
- **In a Lace Waterfall song:** she comes in second, on the second call, drier than Lillith. She never takes the high part. When the last syllable of "waterfall" spills into the next bar, she lets it.

### Sample monologue: "The Doors" (laundry room, 2 AM, folding)

Rubella, alone, to the laundry, while dryer forty-seven hums the note that attracts things.

> The laundry room is ten minutes. I've timed it.
> The elevator lobby is four. Less if car two is in a mood.
> The roof is as long as the wind holds. The wind always holds longer than you'd think.
> I don't tell Lillith the list. She'd want to fix the doors.
> *(folds a black lace sleeve, exactly in half)*
> Forty-seven is humming again. That's not a malfunction. That's an opinion.

> I'm not going to use it. It knows that.
> *(checks the clock: nine minutes)*
> One more towel. Then I'm a hallway.

## Backstory (committed)

**This is settled.** Alternates are in [Rejected / alternate](#rejected--alternate).

- **Origin.** A low-level quality-assurance inspector for a mega-corp whose products were "technically" safe: a kettle-lamp, a smoke-alarm teddy bear, a fan that was also a toaster. She passed all of them because the form said she had to, and the early Lace Waterfall tape *It Passed* is about it. She has not used the word "technically" since.
- **Formative incident (series-relevant).** The company "accidentally" deleted its entire human-resources department, with the backups and the backups of the backups. Her pension was in a folder called `final_final_2`. She gave the eulogy. Mostly the file name. She moved into the building for cheap rent and quiet; the building found her first.
- **How she met Lillith, when the band started, whether they are related.** **Not stated, on purpose.** They share Six-C. She says "roommate." That is the whole on-screen record. Nobody asks, and nobody answers.
- **Secret she protects.** The door list: every door in the building and how long it is safe to stand at each one. She has never been wrong about a door, and she has never told Lillith the list exists. It is the closest thing she has to a hobby.
- **What would break her on screen.** Someone doing something for her without being asked, and it working. She would not know where to put it. Use it never, or once, very late.

### The ten rules: would never / always

**She would never:**
1. Raise her voice. Volume is a maintenance issue.
2. Flinch above the waist, or turn around for a noise.
3. Use the detergent, or let the extinguisher discharge.
4. Give the telephone-pole lecture again after Riley corrects it (*The Long Way Up*, Beat D). The change is never marked.
5. Call anyone her sister, or remark on anyone's surname.

**She always:**
6. Knows which door she is standing at and how long she has.
7. Files it: ticket, notes app, Item number.
8. Lets Lillith start the song, and finishes it.
9. Takes Lillith's elbow before the one step too far.
10. Leaves before the part where someone explains the building.

## Arc hooks

Seeds, not scenes. Each one keeps the locks above.

1. **"Ticket Number"** *(building, silent)*: The ticket she filed in *Car Twelve* comes back resolved. Nothing in the building has changed. She puts the resolution notice in the basket next to the detergent, standing up. Nobody comments.
2. **"Nine Percent"** *(cousin of `songs/Nine_Percent_Battery.md`)*: Her smoke detector chirps every forty-seven seconds, the only in-tune pitch in the building. Lillith reaches for it. Rubella takes her elbow. They sit through one more chirp and the cut is on the chirp.
3. **"The Door List"** *(late, quiet)*: Lillith finds a door Rubella didn't know about and stands at it for longer than the list allows. Rubella times it on her phone and adds a line. Neither of them mentions it.
4. **"Somebody Else's Lecture"** *(after The Long Way Up)*: A stranger at the laundromat starts a confident wrong lecture in her old register. Rubella says nothing at all, and folds.

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Keep the register domestic: laundry rooms, elevators, corridors, lawns, ridge roads. The lace comes along; the gala does not.
- Let her describe the impossible as a maintenance problem, and file it.
- Keep her the calmest body in frame. Impact gets the two-finger brush, once.
- Let her and Lillith split a duet: Lillith starts, Rubella finishes, on the beat, on a sigh.
- Give her one warm act per scene at most, done physically (the elbow, the clamshell moved out of the rain), never said.

**Don't**

- Don't call her or Lillith "sisters," "the Vale sisters," or "the Vale duo." On screen they are roommates. In notes they are **the building pair**.
- Don't explain the surname, and don't put her in a frame where anyone asks about it.
- Don't merge her with Vivienne or Liliane, and don't import the ballroom (chandelier, paddle, champagne flute, swap, score) into the building.
- Don't make her a spy or a hologram outside the Ep3 Monster Mash finale. Spy vs. Spy and the hologram mic are one cutaway's costume, never a premise, never explained.
- Don't let her laugh, run, scream, or name the horror.
- Don't give her the telephone-pole lecture after *The Long Way Up* Beat D.
- Don't put Kenji in a Lace Waterfall song. The band is the two of them only.
- Don't give her Madelyn's or Karen's lines, or a scene with Madelyn's face (there is none).
