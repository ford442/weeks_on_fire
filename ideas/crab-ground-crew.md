## Tone

**Silly and simple, for kids.** Animals doing a human job very seriously, a rule of three
with a wrong third (gull, gull, *pelican*), and a big wide where everything multiplies. The
leads stay calm: Rubella guards, Lillith watches the operation like she's proud of it.
Nobody shoos, flinches or shouts.

## Beat sheet

1. **The table (0:00–0:06).** Sunset pier. Rubella and Lillith at a small café table, one
   paper cone of fries between them, Rubella's World's Okayest Tenant mug, and a full red
   fire extinguisher standing beside Rubella's chair. A red crab climbs onto the railing
   behind them and raises both claws. The claw tips glow orange like marshalling wands.
2. **Gull one (0:06–0:16).** A gull on approach. The crab marshals it: both claws up
   (straight ahead), claws pointing down (descend), claws crossed (stop). The gull lands on
   the table edge exactly on the cross, takes one fry and lifts off. Rubella moves the cone
   two inches closer to herself.
3. **Gull two (0:16–0:26).** Now three crabs: one waves, one rolls two pebbles under the gull's
   feet as wheel chocks, and one holds a claw up like a thumbs-up. Gull two lands, takes a fry,
   chocks pulled, departs. Lillith watches, delighted and silent, the way she watches anything
   that works.
4. **The wrong third: a pelican (0:26–0:36).** All six crabs on the railing go into full
   heavy-landing mode: claws wide, one crab sweeping the table with its claw. A pelican on long
   final approach, enormous against the sunset. It lands on the table, which dips. It opens its
   beak, scoops the **whole cone** of fries, and takes off heavy. The mug and extinguisher stay
   put. Rubella's hands are folded.
5. **The DROP (0:36–0:42).** Dusk. The crabs spread along the entire railing, glowing claws
   held up in a long double row of runway lights. Two hundred gulls land in perfect formation
   along the rail, every one facing the empty table.
6. **End (0:42–0:48).** Rubella brings one fry out from behind her back. She looks at the
   head crab and eats it. The crab lowers its claws and the glow goes out.

## Music cue

**Original 1960s airport-lounge exotica with a brass section.** Vibraphone, bongos, upright
bass, a bright muted-trumpet theme, 112 BPM, B♭ major. Think of a sincere "the jet age is
here" travel-film score. Every landing gets a **little brass fanfare**, and each fanfare is
bigger than the last.

## Callbacks

- **World's Okayest Tenant mug** on the table. The only thing the pelican leaves alone.
- **Rubella's full extinguisher** stands by her chair, never used and never mentioned. On a
  pier, full of fries, and still sealed.
- **Lillith's energy goes into the thing that works.** She doesn't help the crabs; she admires
  the system.

## Do-not

No crab pinches anyone, and no gull or pelican touches a person. Animals are real-looking
and never get faces, hats or vests. Nobody shoos the birds. No pier signage or brand on the
fry cone. Not the Pizza Guy (he is not on this pier). Not the yacht (`yacht-guitar-orchestra`
is open sea), and not a lecture on a boat.

## Why it's funny

The crabs run a whole airport, with wands, wheel chocks and runway lights, for the sole
purpose of helping seagulls steal fries. And the only person who wins is Rubella, by hiding
one.

**Difficulty: 3 / 5.** Crabs with glowing claws and gulls landing on cue are both doable, and
the leads have no lip-sync. The pelican landing on a small table is the scale risk.

**Riskiest shot: #4, the pelican.** It needs to land on a café table and scoop a cone
without collapsing the table or turning cartoonish. Hardener: *real brown pelican, wings
spread wide, lands with feet on the table edge, table dips 3 cm, beak scoops the paper
cone.* Fallback: split it into the approach (6s, wide) and a 6s insert of the beak closing
on the cone, cut on the tuba.
