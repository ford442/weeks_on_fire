# Christina

**Search alias:** Christine · Christine's house · the Sweden heart cutaway. **Canon name: Christina.**
If a draft, a prompt, or an issue says "Christine," it means this sheet. Fix the spelling on the way past; do not start a second page.

**Status:** recurring
**Primary episodes:** None yet. A musical cutaway lane on **Christina hill** (Sweden, winter, night). No cutaway id and no Suggestions id — this sheet is the source until someone says *promote*.
**Role in one sentence:** A still hostess in an ivory shag gown whose house is a snow mound on the outside and two centuries on the inside, and whose aria is already playing when we arrive.

> **The house is as much the character as she is.** This sheet carries both. Her half is
> short on purpose — she does very little, perfectly. The house half is where the prompts live.

## Logline bio

Christina keeps a house on a hill in Sweden that looks, from outside, like a mound of packed
snow — because the snow *is* the roof. Guests come to the wrong door, carved wood with a gilt
throat, and step through two centuries in one cut. She receives them in the white, with a
hostess face, while the night-home aria plays over a wooden-house continuo. She does not
move much. The gown is the score.

## Appearance (consistent prompt anchors)

### Her

- **Ivory shag gown**, floor length, long pile, heavy — it moves only when the air in the room moves. **The gown is the score:** when the picture needs rhythm, the pile answers, not her hands
- Pale hair up, plain. No tiara, no crown, no jewels that read as royal
- **Hostess face:** a small, closed, pleasant expression that is already welcoming you and already expecting you to go. It does not change for the aria
- **Still.** She stands; she does not pace, wave, gesture, or turn to follow a guest. One hand at the waist is the most she does
- **Stays in the white.** The marble atrium is her ground. She is never framed against the gilt, never at the snow door, never outside
- **Signature props:** none she carries. The house holds the props
- **Never change without a story reason:** the ivory shag · the stillness · the hostess face · the white behind her

### The house (as character)

- **Exterior:** a **packed-snow mound that is the roof**. No visible walls, no windows lit like a cottage, no chimney smoke postcard. From outside it could be a drift. One door
- **The wrong door:** carved dark wood with a **gilt throat** — the carved surround opens into gold, like a mouth that is also a frame. It is "wrong" because nothing about the mound promised it
- **Two centuries in one cut:** gilt foyer (18th-century carved gold, candle warmth, the harpsichord somewhere in it) → **white marble atrium with ocean glass** (modern, cold, a wall of sea-green glass). One cut. **No hallway movie** — we never walk it
- **The tower** exists only as a **reflection in the atrium glass**. We never see it straight on
- **Livestock:** three sleeping hypercars in the snow beside the mound, dusted over, lights off, like animals bedded down for the night. **"The Lambo sleeps."** They are not a showroom and nobody drives them in the cutaway
- **Never change without a story reason:** snow mound = roof · carved wood + gilt throat · gilt → marble in one cut · tower only as reflection · three cars, asleep · no fjord establishing shot

## Voice guide

- **Sentence length:** two to seven words. Formal hospitality: welcome, the offer, the farewell. Nothing in between.
- **Language:** **Swedish when the guests are there.** English only if the cutaway must travel, and then as the English bed, not a translation over the Swedish. **Do not mash the twins.**
- **Vocabulary (overuses / refuses):** overuses *välkommen*, *tack*, *varsågod*, *kom in*, *hej då* · refuses questions about the house, the cars, the tower, the title, and anything that sounds like "how long have you lived here"
- **Humor type:** none on her side. The comedy is the frame — a snow drift that opens into gold, and a hostess who has already said goodbye in her face while saying hello with her mouth.
- **Delivery note:** low, even, pleasant. She speaks before the aria or after the last *Ah*, never across it.

### Sample monologue — "The Threshold" (hostess lines, not a speech)

She says these to whoever is at the door. They are the whole of her script.

> Välkommen. Kom in.
> (beat)
> Ni hittade dörren.
> (beat; the harpsichord is already under this)
> Varsågod. Det är varmt här inne.
> (beat)
> Ni ska inte stanna länge. Det är bra.
> (the aria is about to begin; she stops speaking)
> Tack för att ni kom.
> Hej då.

*(Welcome. Come in. — You found the door. — Please. It is warm in here. — You won't stay long. That's good. — Thank you for coming. — Goodbye.)*

### Sample exchange — with Rubella and Lillith (the 30 seconds)

> **CHRISTINA:** Välkommen.
> **LILLITH:** (looking past her, at the gold) Oh, it's *nice.*
> **RUBELLA:** We're not staying.
> **CHRISTINA:** (pleasant; already agreed) Nej.
> **LILLITH:** Is that a harpsichord?
> **CHRISTINA:** Ja.
> **RUBELLA:** (to Lillith, quietly) Thirty seconds.
> **CHRISTINA:** Tack för att ni kom.
>
> *They leave. She stays in the white. The aria goes on without them.*

### Sample exchange — with the clerk (the thin folder)

> **CLERK:** (holding a very thin folder) Hovsångare.
> **CHRISTINA:** Tack.
> **CLERK:** (nothing else is in the folder) …That's all of it.
> **CHRISTINA:** Ja. Det räcker.

*(That's enough.)* The honor arrives as paperwork, carried by a clerk. It is not a King and it is not a ceremony.

### Sample exchange — with Qing Rao, on the sea-glass night

> **QING RAO:** (at the ocean glass) It's soda-lime. The sea did the rest.
> **CHRISTINA:** Ja.
> **QING RAO:** The gilt in the other room is two hundred years older than this.
> **CHRISTINA:** Ja.
> **QING RAO:** (a beat; she almost asks) …It's a good seam.
> **CHRISTINA:** Tack.

## Music / song affinity (the night-home)

The house has a fixed sound, and it is two files, **never mashed.**

| Layer | What it is | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Night-home aria** | The house song. **Already on when we arrive** — we come in late, never on bar one. | Two twins: **Swedish bed** (when there are guests) and **English bed** (only if the cutaway must travel). Never mashed, never cross-faded into each other. **No third aria. No duet album.** |
| **Wooden House continuo** | Under the night-home. **Harpsichord lead, piano alt, D minor, 68 BPM.** | **Fades 8–12 dB for the aria, comes back up after the last *Ah*.** Separate file from the aria. Never bounced into one stem. |

- The accompanist is in the **gilt hall** and is **heard before she is seen** — the harpsichord reaches the door first.
- **Song files do not exist yet.** There is no `songs/*` entry for the night-home twins or Wooden House in this repo. Do not write one from this sheet without a Nova pass; see [`../../docs/crew/nova-chen.md`](../../docs/crew/nova-chen.md).
- Nothing from Nova's *Night Side* or *Cue Sheets* goes under this house. *Aria Thirty (Night)* is a Night Side techno track and is **not** this aria.

## Backstory (committed)

- **Origin.** She is the hostess of the house on Christina hill. The hill takes its name from her or she from the hill; the series never says which and the house never tells.
- **The house.** Two centuries built into one mound: a carved-and-gilt older house and a white marble newer one, joined by one door frame, roofed by snow. She lives in the newer half and receives in it. The older half is where the music comes from.
- **The title.** She is *hovsångare* on paper. It exists as a **thin folder** a clerk brings once. There is no King, no court, no gala. The title is a document, and the document is thin.
- **Formative incident (series-relevant).** None is shown. Her history is the house. Anything that happened to her happened in one of the two centuries, and she stands in the one where it did not.
- **Secret she protects.** How long guests are allowed to stay. Everyone who comes to the door can feel the number, and it is short. She will not say it aloud.
- **What would break her on screen.** A guest who stays. Not a threat — a guest who takes off their coat. She would stay still, keep the hostess face, and the aria would have to play longer than it was written to. *(Never write this. It is here so nobody writes it by accident.)*

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Arrive late. The aria is already on.
- Let the harpsichord be heard before the accompanist is seen.
- Keep her still, in the ivory shag, in the white, with the hostess face.
- Use the Swedish bed when guests are in frame. English only for a travelling cut.
- Cut gilt foyer → white marble atrium + ocean glass **in one cut**.
- Show the tower **only** as a reflection in the atrium glass.
- Keep three hypercars asleep outside. "The Lambo sleeps."
- Keep **one extra body per plate** on the night-home: the accompanist (gilt hall, heard first) plus one atrium listener. That is the whole house.
- Keep the hovsångare as a thin folder in a clerk's hand.

**Don't**

- Don't let Rubella and Lillith stay the night. **Thirty seconds at the door, then they leave.**
- Don't show the snow mound as a cottage, and don't open on a **fjord establishing shot**.
- Don't walk the hall. **No hallway movie.**
- Don't park the **Forest Roadster** here. Don't wake the cars. Don't turn the cars into a showroom or a chase.
- Don't mash the Swedish and English twins. Don't mash the aria into the continuo. **No third aria. No duet album.**
- Don't make the hovsångare the King, a court, or a **royal gala**.
- Don't put **five faces** in a plate. Five faces is a party — throw it.
- **Throw on sight:** floor directors · Pavarotti (or any real singer's name, face, or sleeve) · Kenji · Riley · a **pizza bag as a second leak** (the tower reflection is the house's one leak; a pizza bag would be a second) · Lace Waterfall opening here ("Lace open") · Ultra Screech or any Glam-Sham-Poo sting · Riley retrieval · HSV worms · Daisy tandem · Night Grove · a royal gala.
- Don't mix worlds. Daisy field / night-lot / HSV table / Annex / **Christina hill** / Night Grove / Glamora / Lightning warehouse stay apart.
- Don't confuse this house with the eight-second fake Sweden return in the Home Sweet Void exit chain. That one belongs to the Glam lane on [#32](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/32) and does not use this door.
