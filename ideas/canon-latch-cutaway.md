Musical cutaway board for the forms-drawer sketch
[`songs/_drawer/forms/Canon_Latch.md`](../songs/_drawer/forms/Canon_Latch.md). Night woods → mountain
lake under a black silk sky. A group of ladies in black lace carry candles. At the lake their
reflections become a second group that runs slightly behind and never catches up.

## The audio is master

The intended form was a Latin canon at the fifth on *Sera manet*: the leader goes first, the
follower comes in one bar later and never catches up. The generated take turned into a quiet,
contemplative chant from about **1:01** ("Krishna Manitta… Sri Ramana…"). We keep the take as it
is. Do not regenerate it to force the Latin back in, and do not subtitle it.

That means **the canon flaw is now visual only.** The picture keeps the form the audio lost: the
leader is on the shore, and the follower is the reflection, about one bar late.

- **Lag length:** about one bar of whatever the chant is doing. At the card's 66 BPM that is about
  3.6s. Measure it against the real take and keep it the same for the whole lake. It is never
  zero, and the reflection never catches up.
- **The chant is the lake hold.** No new picture event lands on the chant's entry except the cut to
  the water.
- **The chant is devotional in sound.** Add no religious imagery to match it: no temples, deities,
  beads, mudras, or on-screen text. The picture stays silk, paper, lace, and candles.

## Visual lock (from the user, unchanged)

- **Sky:** black silk the whole time, with soft folds. Either **one visible seam** or star lines
  stitched in **gold thread**. Pick one per plate and keep it. No moon.
- **Collage media**, so the silk isn't a one-off: **cut-paper / tissue-paper trees**, a
  **stitched-fabric path**, a **painted-paper shore**, and **black-silk or liquid-glass water**.
  The media announce themselves as the camera gets closer. From the hill they read as real woods.
- **The ladies:** a group in **black lace** holding **candles level at chest height**. Mouths
  closed. Faces turned away, in profile, or only seen in the reflection. **No lipsync**, no open
  mouths, no singing faces.
- **Framing:** open over a foggy hill looking down, with distant candle points among the trees.
  Then drop closer as the media declare themselves.
- **Lake arrival:** black roses and floating petals in the foreground (user plate exists). The
  reflections form the second, slightly late group.
- **Volume** builds early, then holds. The chant is the lake hold.

**Candle count is the volume meter.** Points of light are added from 0:00 until the volume
plateaus (around 0:50). After that the count is fixed. No candle is added or put out after the
chant enters, with one exception: the last reflection at the end.

## Callbacks pulled (`notes/callback-bank.md`)

1. **Bag at chest height.** The candles are carried exactly like the farm's handed objects: level,
   at chest height, never commented on. Faces off and lipsync off is the farm's house style
   already, so this packet inherits it rather than inventing it.
2. **Sign-off at 3:44, twice, identical.** The reflection is *the same take, played late*, not a
   second performance. When comping, the water group is the shore plate flipped and shifted about
   one bar later, then darkened and rippled. It is identical on purpose, and that repetition is
   the point.
3. **Nothing happening, extremely well.** We borrow the labelled-hold *form*, not the silence. The
   lake hold is written with its length (H, 28s below). Nothing new enters, and no performer fills
   it. The chant underneath is the take, not a score added to the hold.
4. **Worlds do not mix.** See the kill list at the bottom.
