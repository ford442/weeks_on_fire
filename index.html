About the series

Weeks on Fire is a personal short-film series that blends musical cutaways with tracks from Minimax Music and visuals generated with Grok Imagine (xAI). This site is the live production hub: visual archive, soundtrack browser, scene timeline, and crew notes.

Source repository: github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire
