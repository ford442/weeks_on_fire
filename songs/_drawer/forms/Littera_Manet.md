# Littera Manet — drawer sketch

**Drawer.** `promote?: no`. Cousin of *The Light in the Stair*, not a rewrite. Farm #18 / #33. Not #42.

| field | value |
|---|---|
| form | motet, a cappella, syllabic |
| text | Littera in ianua manet. |
| meaning (not sung) | The letter on the door remains. |
| who | Nilsson, then Lillith, then Rubella |
| flaw | Rubella enters a whole step low and is never corrected |

STYLE:

Latin motet, a cappella, 60 BPM, about 90 seconds. Ecclesiastical Latin, one note per syllable, straight tone, no melisma, no vibrato, no belting, no instruments, no English word, no translation spoken or sung. Reverb is a concrete stairwell, short, not a church. Do not sing their names. Christine Nilsson enters first, lyric soprano on D, one statement of the sentence. Lillith enters second, a fifth above, on A, same sentence, while Nilsson repeats. Rubella enters third. The imitation asks her for D. She enters on C and stays on C. The other two do not move down. Three statements are enough. On the last syllable, "net," they stop on D, A, and C. No cadence after it. Nilsson is a voice on the track, not a person in the stair, and not the Christina wooden-house lane.

LYRICS:

[Nilsson — on D]
Littera in ianua manet.

[Lillith enters — on A]
NILSSON: Littera in ianua manet.
LILLITH: Littera in ianua manet.

[Rubella enters — on C, not the D she was given]
NILSSON: Littera in ianua manet.
LILLITH: Littera in ianua manet.
RUBELLA: Littera in ianua manet.

[Last syllable — stop, no cadence]
NILSSON: net.
LILLITH: net.
RUBELLA: net.

NOTES:

Same machine as *The Light in the Stair*. New sentence. No English in the singer's mouth. Syllables, ecclesiastical: LIT-te-ra in ya-NU-a MA-net. Do not Italianize the vowels into an aria.

Not Christina's hill. Not a Mass. Runtime about 90 seconds.
