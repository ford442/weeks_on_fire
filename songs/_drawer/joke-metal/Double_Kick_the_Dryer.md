STYLE:

Warm joke metal, 112 BPM, A mixolydian, about one minute forty. One riff on a warm cranked amp, mid-gain, standard tuning, not scooped. Dry close kit, short room, no wash. The double kick is not a bass-drum roll and not a metal cliché: it is two dryer-drum thunks grouped on beat 2 of every bar, one heavy item in a tiled laundry room. Snare on 4. Hats closed and quiet. Bass doubles the riff and stops when the riff stops. Verses are one dry close voice, committed, no belting. Gang vocals on the chorus only, clean shout-sung, no scream, no growl, the single word Delicates. Structural flaw: when the riff ends, the dryer thunk keeps going for two full bars, and nobody cuts it. Sing only the lines below. No djent, no dropped tuning, no blast beat, no double-bass barrage, no occult lyric, no choir, no Kenji, no product name, no Glamora. Mood: the cycle name is doctrine. Runtime about 1:40.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: the thunk keeps going two bars after the riff stops. It is never cut back to the riff, and the chorus does not change the name.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not Neutral Zone (Laundry Bed).** That loop is 92 BPM, a thunk on every beat, no vocal. This thunk sits on beat 2, under one riff, and the gang has a name for it.

- **Not Well Fall.** No drop, no down-tuning, no wordless fall.
