# H11 · EyeWash master control, 4 AM (the monitor a few seconds ahead)

**Shot:** H11
**Episode / beat:** EyeWash Station · *Master Control, 4 AM* Beat A *Levels* ·
[`notes/scenes/master-control-4am.md`](../../notes/scenes/master-control-4am.md) ·
`content/cutaways/master-control-4am.json` · [`songs/Haunted_or_Unscheduled.md`](../../songs/Haunted_or_Unscheduled.md)
**Why it matters:** the network frame for the whole series. One calm man, twenty CRTs, a clock that
will not advance past 3:44, and one monitor showing this room a few seconds from now. It is the
warmest room in the show and the dread is entirely structural: shoot the clock and the monitor,
never the man, as the strange thing.
**Locks:** [`eyewash-master-control`](../locks/eyewash-master-control.lock.md) ·
[`house-negative`](../locks/house-negative.lock.md)
**Aspect / model:** 16:9 · `grok-imagine-image-quality` (repo default)

**Geography (all three framings):** Oz sits at the long board facing the monitor wall, his back
three-quarters to camera. The wall is four across, five down. **Bottom row, second from the left**
shows this room from behind his chair a few seconds ahead: in it, the chair is already empty. In
reality he is still seated and writing. He looks at the audio meters, never at that monitor. The
analog clock above the wall reads 3:44. A small clamp lamp (warm 3000K) is aimed at the log sheet;
everything else is lit by the monitors.

**Never on screen:** Marguerite, the Continuity Voice (no booth, no silhouette, no speaker with a
face), the tower, a channel number, readable text on any monitor. Nobody looks at camera.

**Callbacks spent (as image, not dialog):** 3:44 (the stuck clock) · the log is the log (laminated
sheet, grease pencil on a shoelace, capped) · tea, not coffee · the monitor he never looks at ·
other episodes airing on the wall (lava, serpents, laundromat, mirrors).
