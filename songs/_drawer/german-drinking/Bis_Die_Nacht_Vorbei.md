STYLE:

German Schunkelwalzer, slow party Schlager closer, 96 BPM in 3/4 waltz time, major key. Accordion lead, soft brass pads, tuba bass, gentle oom-pah-pah drums, warm male lead vocal, whole room singing the chorus together, swaying, sentimental but happy, gets quieter at the end, traditional Oktoberfest band, no synth, no electronic production. About 2:00. Simple German. Sing only the lines below. No character names, no product.
