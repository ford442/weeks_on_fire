# Christmas songs — four carols, four videos

*Drawer. Text only. 2026-11-09. `promote?: no` on every song. Not codegen'd.*

The owner asked for entries for **Sleigh Ride**, **Silver Bells**, **Silent Night** and
**The First Noell**, using Rubella, Lillith, Riley and Madelin in the show's own places (the
house, the cliff, the coffee house). Each file follows the owner lock in
[`../../../docs/song-method.md`](../../../docs/song-method.md): the **video comes first**, then
the video use map, the picture's own sound, and only then `STYLE:` / `LYRICS:` / `NOTES:`.
Each file holds one fully built video with a timed shot table plus two shorter "other videos
this song could sit on" pitches.

Shape matches [`../wet-enamel.md`](../wet-enamel.md): frontmatter, field table, `STYLE:`
(MiniMax paste), `LYRICS:` with official section tags only, `NOTES:`.

**Owner decisions, 2026-11-09:** none of these is promoted to `songs/` yet (the catalog stays
untouched until the `songs/Softcore.md` frontmatter problem is fixed and the overlaps below are
cleaned). The hidden-crowd reveal stays on *Tap the Mug*; *The First Noell* was rebuilt on a
different video.

## Laugh lines (a 9-year-old can say them back)

- **Sleigh Ride:** "One little marmot can't pull the car, and then there are eight!"
- **Silver Bells:** "She tapped her friend's mug with a spoon, and a whole street full of people rang their bells back."
- **Silent Night:** "She put Christmas socks on all the noisy things, even the building."
- **The First Noell:** "The dog herded the whole coffee line like sheep."

| Song | Status of the original | Lyrics in the file |

| Silver Bells | in copyright | wholly original; no hook, line or rhyme of the standard |

MiniMax makes its own tune from the text, and the `STYLE:` blocks name no real artist or
existing recording. Every lyric uses official `[Section]` tags and no parenthetical marks. The
carols are sung straight; the joke is always the picture, never the faith. No nativity props,
robes, wise men or halos appear in any picture.

## Overlap clean-up (done 2026-11-09, before any promotion)

- **Hidden-crowd reveal:** kept on *Tap the Mug* only. *The First Noell* no longer has a choir
  reveal (*Choir of One*, the roof and the star, is parked as a one-line note in that file).
- **Counts:** the number "forty" had spread across the set. Now the crowd is **twenty-six**
  (*Tap the Mug*), **eight** marmots (*The Team*), **nineteen** goats (*The Ledge Choir*),
  **seven** customers (*Keeping Their Sheep*), a **dozen** bells (*Bells on the Hall Table*).
  "Forty" survives only as the canon **forty folding chairs** at the curb (*Forty Bells*), and as
  Riley's forty-liter pack in her verbatim Appearance line where it is pasted.
- **Lillith's kit** (speaker wire, safety glasses, vacuum tube): spent **once** for the whole set,
  in *The Team* (the marmot harness). The other three files and *The Team*'s own pitches say
  "deliberately NOT spent here", and carry reroll rules against a render showing the glasses.
- **Cliff lane:** the Ep5 cutaway
  [`content/cutaways/no-other-human-sounds.json`](../../../content/cutaways/no-other-human-sounds.json)
  owns the Pulpit Overlook plate (the 1962 bolted picnic table, the 400-ft drop, golden hour).
  Checked: there is **no lock against goats or other animals** there (the "no birds / no jay" lines
  cover the woods and the eight-second stop; the only animal rule is mountain lions at six-forty).
  Both goat and Christmas-star pitches were still moved to an **unnamed ledge** on the same ridge,
  no bolted table, so neither leans on that plate. *The Team* (the main for Sleigh Ride) is the
  one video that does stand on the overlook, with the picnic table and the sedan, because the
  owner named the cliff; see below.
