**Juxtaposition:** The channel changes *down*. After a tango in an elevator and a
neon corridor, the show becomes a late cabaret ruin piece where a structural
failure is treated as a furniture question. The wrongness is the calm.

## V1 — The No Is Longer

**Status:** alt
**Beats covered:** B
**What it adds:** Lillith pushes three more lines before she sits. Makes the no
land harder; costs the scene its best quality, which is how *fast* she takes it.
**Risks:** Any version where she argues is a version where the change gets marked.
The parent is right. This exists so the room can steal from it.

### B — The One Time (V1)

```
                         LILLITH
          Give me a reason that isn't the packet.

                         RUBELLA
          You'd close it by Sunday and it'd be a ceiling
          again by Monday and on Tuesday somebody would
          rent it.

(Four seconds.)

                         LILLITH
          That's the reason?

                         RUBELLA
          That's the reason.

(She sits.)
```

**Note:** if V1 is promoted, cut "That's not a no / It's a no" from the parent —
the beat cannot be paid for twice.

## V2 — No Rain

**Status:** alt
**Beats covered:** E
**What it adds:** The rectangle of rain never comes. The scene ends on the extra
bar, the outro, and two people not leaving a room with no ceiling. Colder, purer,
four seconds shorter.
**Risks:** Loses the berries-moved-six-inches beat, which is the only physical
comedy in the scene and the only thing either of them does about the weather.

---

## V3 — Lillith's Unit

**Status:** shelved
**Why shelved:** If the hole is in 6-C, Lillith has to want it fixed for real
reasons and the scene becomes a housing problem instead of a ruin ballad. The
vacancy is what makes the calm legal. Kept so nobody re-proposes it.
