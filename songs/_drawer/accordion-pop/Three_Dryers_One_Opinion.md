STYLE:

Upbeat nylon-string pop, 124 BPM, F major, about one minute thirty-five. Short lines, specific nouns, deadpan sincerity, a small argument between a count and a sock. Nylon guitar plays a short major figure, close and dry. A woodblock is the only percussion: three taps, then a rest, marking three machines. No accordion, no kit, no dryer-thunk kick. Two female voices, no belting, no wink. Lillith brighter. Rubella drier. Do not sing their names. Three dryers, one sock, one verdict: the sock is done. Structural flaw: on the last chorus the third dryer is counted as the first, and the toe that belonged to dryer three is sung as dryer one. The woodblock still taps three times. Nobody corrects the count. Sing only the lines below. No choir, no product name, no distorted guitar. Mood: the verdict is sincere and the number is wrong. Runtime about 1:35.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: the third dryer is counted as the first on the last chorus. The toe moves to dryer one. There is no later chorus that puts it back. The woodblock still counts three.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not The Laundromat Saints.** No waltz, no congregation, no thump on bar 5. The verdict is a sock.
- **Not Double Kick the Dryer.** That thunk is a metal kick on beat 2, and the gang has a cycle name. This bed is nylon and a woodblock. Three machines, one opinion.
- **Not Neutral Zone (Laundry Bed).** Not a seamless loop, and not one dryer.
