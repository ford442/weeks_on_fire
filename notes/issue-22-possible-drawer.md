returning its own envelope; a directory-board globe clicking to a floor number; a

**Jokes.** Four inserts, one object each: a thermostat with an HOA setting; a laminate on

**Ladies' house.** Six 20–40s beats, max three bodies: the intercom chime against the

under a wrong hallway light; one lace glove; a folder to the hill returned unopened and a
white-house postcard on the fridge.
