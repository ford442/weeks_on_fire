# Le Fromage

**Genre collision:** bal-musette java × spaghetti-western soundtrack (whistled melody, twangy baritone guitar, a lone trumpet, whip-crack snare). The cheese is the stranger who rides into town.

**Visual concept:** A regional train compartment on a sunny morning. Rubella sits by the window with a basket. Lillith sits opposite with a hollow-body electric guitar, playing twangy western lines. Rubella unwraps one small round cheese from paper. Every section someone else leaves, politely and without a word: the man opposite, the whole carriage, the conductor, and then the locomotive itself, which uncouples and drives off alone. By the end the single carriage stands in a green field, and the cows come over slowly, the way the town comes back at the end of a western.

**Laugh (kid words):** *Her cheese is so stinky the whole train leaves without her, but the cows come to say hello.*

**BPM:** 132, a java swing, E minor, lifting to G major in the chorus

**Target length:** ~2:25 · 13 segments
