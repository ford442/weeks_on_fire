# Open Vowel — drawer sketch

**Drawer.** `promote?: no`. Lied machine, no lexicon. Farm #18 / #33. Not #42.

STYLE:

Phonetic lied for lyric soprano and piano, 63 BPM, 4/4, D minor, strophic, about 90 seconds. The only lyric is five vowels, one note each: ah, eh, ee, oh, oo. No English, no Latin, no consonant, no melisma, no cadenza, no high note actually sung, no belting. One small room, soft pedal, no kit, no strings. Christine Nilsson, intimate, not a resident, not the Christina wooden-house lane. Do not sing her name. Passes 1 and 2 use the same five notes. Pass 3 stops before the last vowel. Structural flaw: the piano finishes its cadence one bar before the voice arrives at oo. She does not hurry. On pass 3 she does not sing oo at all. The piano has already cadenced, early, into silence. Do not fill the bar.
