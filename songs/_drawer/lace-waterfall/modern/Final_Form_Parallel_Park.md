STYLE:

Sincere anime-opening J-rock theme in TV size, 190 BPM, D major, about one and a half minutes, sung in English. Bright overdriven twin guitars with fast tapping licks, a busy melodic bass, a driving drum kit with fast fills, a soaring string section, piano glissandos on every section change, and a big brass hit on the title. Lead one: bright, conspiratorial, soaring, a heroic belt with sudden energy spikes. Lead two: dry, slightly raspy, flat, calling distances and directions in short spoken lines between phrases. A dramatic sudden stop before the final chorus, one held power chord, then everything slams back in up a whole step. Ends on a big unison hit and a single bell ding. Cinematic, glossy, sincere, no parody. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
RUBELLA: There's a space.

[Verse 1]
LILLITH: The street is wide, the sky is burning
A gap appears between the cars
I hear the engines of the world turning
I check my mirrors, I check the stars

[Pre-Chorus]
RUBELLA: Two feet.
LILLITH: Turn the wheel!
RUBELLA: One foot.
LILLITH: Turn it back!

[Chorus]
LILLITH: Final form! Reverse into the light!
Final form! The curb is on my right!
Every horn is calling out my name!
RUBELLA: You're on the curb.
LILLITH: Final form! Again!

[Verse 2]
LILLITH: The bus is waiting, the crowd is watching
A pigeon lands upon the hood
The sun goes down, the moon is rising
RUBELLA: Still not good.

[Pre-Chorus]
RUBELLA: Six inches.
LILLITH: Turn the wheel!
RUBELLA: Three inches.
LILLITH: Turn it back!

[Final Chorus]
LILLITH: Final form! Reverse into the light!
Final form! It's parked! It's parked just right!
Every horn is calling out my name!
RUBELLA: It's a two-hour space.

NOTES:

**The joke:** an opening theme's full hero grammar (wind, transformation, rising music)
for a three-point turn that takes from sunrise to moonrise. The last line ruins the victory:
it's a two-hour space, and they have a long night.

**No callback here** (the batch spends its callbacks in the other songs). The pigeon on the
hood is just a pigeon.

**Do-not:** no real anime title, character, studio or song quoted. No car brand visible. No
Japanese text on screen (no fake opening credits to read).
