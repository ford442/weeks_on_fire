STYLE:

Hold-music accretion / civic dispatch ballad at 92 BPM. Cheap on-hold Rhodes that sounds like a municipal phone tree, staccato muted brass on each transfer click, fluorescent buzz as the bed, cordless-phone compression on the lead. Female vocal, Karen, professionally furious, never louder than a kitchen, zero vibrato, no belting, no “mad mom” comedy voice. Dark top end; kitchen practicals only. **Structural flaw:** every transfer adds one extra bar of hold music instead of cutting — the phone tree accretes. Verse 1 is 8 bars of hold under the line; after transfer one it is 9; after transfer two it is 10; the last chorus is sitting on a pile of leftover bars nobody scheduled. Do not reset the hold loop. Do not “edit for radio.” Mood: 911 for a recycling bin. Runtime ~1:55–2:15. Use a dry close suburban female lead; operator is a second dry voice, tired, never sarcastic.
