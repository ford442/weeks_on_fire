STYLE:

Sincere steel-pan calypso, 112 BPM, C major, about two minutes ten. Double-second and tenor steel pans carry the melody and answer every vocal line. Bright nylon-string guitar strumming the calypso chop, round electric bass, congas, shaker, cowbell on the off-beat, and a small horn section (trumpet and trombone) with sunny stabs on the chorus. A small brass handbell rings once at the top and once before each chorus, the same pitch every time. Lead vocal one: bright, conspiratorial, delighted, crisp diction. Lead vocal two: dry, slightly raspy, unhurried. A warm mixed backing trio answers each chorus line, and their answer must be **sung with the identical rhythm and pitch** as the lead's line so the two phrases sound the same; only the word split differs. Hot, bright, tape-warm mix, earnest, no parody, no laugh track, no sound-effect jokes. Final chorus lifts a half step. Ends on one dry spoken word answered by the trio. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.

NOTES:

**The device is the tactic, not the subject.** Nobody sings about words. The song is
about a heat wave, and the oronyms do the work: each pair is one sound split two ways,
and on the block both meanings are literally true. He is a nice man *and* an ice man;
the hat is nice *and* made of ice; the hour is nice and cold *and* it's an ice-cold
shower. The last pair, *a nice pool / an ice pool*, is the payoff, because the truck has
melted into one.

**The production rule:** the trio's answer must copy the lead's rhythm and pitch
exactly. If they sing it as a different melody, the ear stops hearing the double meaning.
If a take comes back with a new tune on the answers, regenerate.

**Callback:** Rubella's World's Okayest Tenant mug (verse 2). She holds it out for one
cube and gets a block. Not named in the lyric, just "my mug."

**Picture, if it ever gets one:** the drop is the final chorus. The truck slumps into a
pool and the whole block is floating in it, everyone wearing ice-block hats. Rubella is
the only one standing at the edge, dry, mug in hand.

**Do-not:** no brand on the truck, no real ice-cream jingle, no melting animals.
