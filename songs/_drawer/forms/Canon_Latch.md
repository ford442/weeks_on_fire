STYLE:

Canon at the fifth, a cappella, 66 BPM, 4/4, about one minute thirty. Two voices, no instruments, no continuo, no third voice, no beat beyond the tactus. The only sung text is "Sera manet." Four syllables, one note each, quarter notes, one bar. Strictly syllabic. No melisma, no ornament, no English, no second line. Christine Nilsson, lyric soprano, leads on F, straight tone, no vibrato, no belting. Lillith enters one bar later on C, a perfect fifth above, with the same four syllables, and she stays one bar behind for the rest of the piece. Do not sing their names. Reverb is a small dry room, short, not a church, not a stairwell, not open air. The leader repeats the line. The follower repeats it one bar later. She does not enter sooner. She does not catch the leader. Structural flaw: the piece ends on the leader's last syllable, while Lillith is still inside the previous bar. Do not add a bar for her to finish. Do not land them in unison. No cadence after the cut. No harpsichord, no cello, no aria, no petition, no hill, no parallel organum. Mood: the latch stays, and the second voice is still a bar away when it is over.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: the follower never catches the leader. The piece ends on Nilsson's last syllable, and Lillith is still one bar behind. Nobody adds the bar that would let her land.

Sera manet: the latch stays. It is left as it was found. Lillith does not step forward to close the bar.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not The Clause.** That one is secco. English clauses, a harpsichord and a cello, and the aria chord stops before anyone sings it. This is a canon. There is no continuo, and the aria is not the thing that fails to start.
- **Not The Light in the Stair.** That one is an English motet. Three voices, and Rubella enters a whole step low. They stop together. This is two voices, Latin only, and the flaw is the bar of delay, not an entry pitch.
- **Not Christina's hill.** No outdoors, no wind, no house. A small dry room.
- **Not Quiet on the Landing.** That card is reed-organ pop. The intercom answers with the previous English line. This grille is not here. The lag is a canon at the fifth.
- **Not Organum Stair.** That card moves in parallel on a different Latin sentence, and the fifth slips to a fourth. These two voices imitate, a bar apart, and the interval stays a fifth.
