Instrumental chamber miniature, 76 BPM, A minor. Small accordion / bellows lead as a paper lung, muted pizzicato bass, one dry clarinet, paper-shaker percussion. No 909, no 303, no choir, no soprano, no title chant, no harpsichord, no techno grid, no glam metal, no orchestral trailer. Phrases grow a generation on the exhale and withhold on the hold. Last 8 to 12 seconds smaller than the peak. Runtime about 45 to 70 seconds. Intimate, stopwatch-precise, lamp-warm, not a fairy-tale orchestra.
