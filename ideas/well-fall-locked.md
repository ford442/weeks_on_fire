# Well Fall — Locked Sights

Treatment: [`well-fall.md`](well-fall.md) · Prompts: [`../prompts/well-fall-locked-segments.md`](../prompts/well-fall-locked-segments.md)

All picks come from the Sight dump. One per slot. The fall never stops; each
sight is *in the shaft with us*. Arc: plumbing → wrong channel → scale break →
quiet domestic → weather → light dying → a bottom that is not a bottom.

| Slot | Time | Music | Sight (locked) | Family |
|------|------|-------|----------------|--------|
| **A** | 0:00–0:10 | clean harmonics + drip | Lip of the well. Stone ring. Dark cylinder. We go. | (pre-locked) |
| **B** | 0:10–0:20 | riff in (drop) | Junction of six cast-iron pipes, all leaking — a chandelier of thin streams we fall through, water going *up* past us because we are faster | Infrastructure |
| **C** | 0:20–0:32 | descending cycle 1 | An empty escalator set into the curve of the wall, steps climbing while we drop past | Wrong-channel |
| **D** | 0:32–0:44 | descending cycle 2 | The shaft widens into a cathedral of wet brick, then narrows back before we can read the far wall | Scale break |
| **E** | 0:44–0:56 | descending cycle 3 | A dining table set for one, falling slower than we are — we pass it | Domestic, falling |
| **F** | 0:56–1:08 | descending cycle 4 | Rain inside the shaft, falling slower than we are — we overtake every drop | Water and weather |
| **G** | 1:08–1:20 | descending cycle 5 | A ring of candle niches, each flame blowing out as we pass | Light |
| **H** | 1:20–1:32 | cadence that never lands | The bottom is a sky — the shaft opens into daylight below and we keep falling | H ending |

## Why each

- **B — pipe chandelier (riff-in drop).** The drop needs a hit you can feel in
  the body. Water reversing direction is the cleanest proof of speed: the
  instant the kick lands, gravity looks wrong. Still plain plumbing, so the well
  is still a well.
- **C — escalator (cycle 1).** First wrong-channel object. Its steps climb at a
  steady pace against our fall — a counter-motion that makes the first
  half-step down in the riff read as *lower*. No people, nothing to read.
- **D — cathedral (cycle 2).** The one scale break and the high-spectacle shot.
  The walls fall away mid-cycle, the riff fills the space, and the shaft
  clamps back before the eye settles. Generate this early.
- **E — table for one (cycle 3).** The quiet domestic beat. Something else is
  falling and it is losing the race. Deadpan, no diner, chair pushed in. The
  riff keeps pounding; the picture goes still-life.
- **F — slow rain (cycle 4).** Weather, and a speed check after the quiet
  table: we punch through drops that hang like beads. Shaft is wettest here,
  setting up the candles.
- **G — candles out (cycle 5).** Light becomes the countdown. Each niche goes
  dark as we pass; by the end of the last cycle the shaft is nearly black,
  only the coin above and the brick rim lit by our own speed.
- **H — the bottom is a sky (final non-cadence).** No impact, no stop. The
  dark brick ends in a ring of daylight *below*, we fall out of the shaft into
  open blue, the well mouth now a coin above *and* the shaft's end behind us.
  The riff reaches for the tonic and misses; we are still falling at cut.

## Not used (and why)

- Elevator / P4 / delay board / scoreboard: readable text breaks the no-text lock.
- Bats, cats, choir, hammock: people/animals pull focus from the fall; save.
- Inner well cover / bucket / bathroom drain: they stop or nearly stop the camera.
- Radio playing EyeWash: diegetic music fights the non-diegetic metal.
