STYLE:

Sincere early-2000s nu-metal and rap-metal, 98 BPM half-time groove, drop-A tuning, about two and a half minutes. Seven-string guitars chugging syncopated low riffs with dead stops, a slap-heavy bass, tight snappy drums with big snare, a DJ adding vinyl scratches and turntable stabs between lines, a creepy music-box sample in the intro. Lead one: bright, conspiratorial, rapping fast and furious with real attitude. Lead two: dry, slightly raspy, whispering each chorus line close to the mic and then screaming the same line, but even the scream sounds tired. Quiet-loud-quiet dynamics, a breakdown with one dead silence before the last chug. Raw, heavy, sincere, no parody, no comedy sound effects except the plastic clatter written in. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.

NOTES:

**Callback:** **"Not in this one."** That's Madelin's bag line, said by someone else about a
different container (a portable form from the bank). Rubella goes through three cupboards
with it.

**The ending:** the lid was already on the box the whole time. The rage was for nothing. The
final chorus is the only major-key moment in the song.

**Do-not:** no real container brand. No real band names in the STYLE. The DJ never gets a
face.
