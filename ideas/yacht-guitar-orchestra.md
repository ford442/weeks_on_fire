## Lock

White luxury yacht. Rubella and Lillith on white lounges in modern black lace, sunglasses, not performing. Entire deck perimeter lined with women holding electric guitars facing outward — a strange living orchestra. Mouths closed on the guitar line. Gentle synchronized sway only.

Three-song hard-cut journey:
1. Daylight open ocean → vine-covered volcanic island (tiny volcano plume, too many bright parrots, one dolphin leap). Guitar orchestra bed (96 BPM D major stack).
2. Dark island: orange/red lights from the vines into fog; controlled fire on the black beach; rusted cannons inert. Wallisian (Wallis & Futuna) bed, 88 BPM.
3. Night sail to Busan: approach Gwangan Bridge (RGB LED cables) + Marine City high-rises from the open sea. Korean K-pop bed, 128 BPM. Seoul and Pyongyang rejected.

No landing. No docking. Cannons stay inert. Hard cuts between songs. Mute Imagine audio.
