### How to read the cards

- **Start still** = paste the board's character block (verbatim, first) plus the framing sentence. **Motion** = the image-to-video line.
- **Clips.** Imagine gives 6, 10 or 15 s. Every card says which and the window it trims to (the board's 1.5–2 s; a few hold longer on purpose and say so). Slow motion is a retime in the edit (usually 55–65%), not an extreme-slow prompt.
- **Chains.** Where a move continues, grab the frame at the **out-point** you keep (not necessarily the clip's last frame) and use it as the next start still.
- **Edit-only effects.** Stutter-frame repeats on the IDM fills, ghost balls, mosaic pixelation and the single white frame are all done in the edit, never prompted.
- **Mouths** are closed on every plate except A-07, which owns the one spoken "meow". Mute Imagine audio on every take.
- **Look B camera.** Eye-level or table-level only. The subject of every B frame is the ball, the paddle, the feather, the sheet or the silk. Rubella's optional sheer black lace duster is used only where a card names it, and is never put on or taken off on camera.
- **Strobe.** One white frame in the whole final chorus (A-09), one strobe on the chug (O-chug lean). No flicker series.
