## #3 — The sea keeps time (daylight)

**Fits:** daylight leg, between C and D. **Clip:** 10s (4 bars at 96 BPM). **Bed:** Yacht Guitar Orchestra; the swell locks on the bar where Harmonic Choir stacks in. **Effect:** the swell. **Chain:** none (hard cut in and out).

**Start still**
```
A sleek white luxury motor yacht seen side-on from just above the water, bright midday, filling the middle of a wide 16:9 frame, white hull with no name or lettering, pale decks, glass-and-chrome rail. Along the entire deck rail stands a living orchestra of women in plain ivory sleeveless deck dresses, one every arm's length, each holding a clean electric guitar (Stratocasters and Telecasters) at playing height, facing outward toward camera, calm faces, eyes open, lips closed, all leaning the same few degrees to the left. Their job is to hold one long chord: right hands resting on the strings, no strumming. On the aft sun deck, small and still, two figures in black lace lie on two white lounges in dark sunglasses, not performing. Around the yacht, open turquoise ocean with no land and no other boats; a long, smooth, glassy swell is rising under the hull, the horizon line tipped a few degrees to the left in the same direction as the lean. Bright, clean midday sun, hard white highlights on the hull, soft blue shadows under the rail. Photoreal, 35mm lens at water level, fine grain, natural travel-film grade, no text.
```

**Motion prompt**
```
Locked camera at water level. One effect only: the sea. A long, slow, gentle swell rises and falls exactly with the guitar line's lean, one full cycle every five seconds, so the horizon tips a few degrees left, then right, in unison with the players, while the yacht itself stays perfectly level and the deck never tilts. No spray, no whitecaps, no peril. Nobody on board looks at the water. Gentle synchronized sway of the guitar line as if playing long sustained chords, mouths closed, no frantic strumming, no one boards or leaves the yacht, cannons rusted and still, boat does not land. 10 seconds, Grok Imagine style
```

**Last still**
```
The same locked side-on frame, same white yacht, same light. The guitar line has completed two sway cycles and leans the same few degrees to the left again; the swell has come back to the same height under the hull, the horizon tipped left exactly as in the start. The yacht is level; the two black-lace figures have not moved on their white lounges. Glassy turquoise sea, no land, no other boats, no spray. Photoreal, 35mm, fine grain, no text.
```

## #7 — Glowing wake (Busan approach)

**Fits:** night approach, right after the 2:24 hard cut and before P, or between R and S. **Clip:** 10s, trims to 8s (about 4 bars at 128 BPM). **Bed:** Seoul at Night; the glow switches on along the wake on the first downbeat of a chorus. **Effect:** the wake glow. **Chain:** none.

**Start still**
```
The stern of a sleek white luxury motor yacht seen from high behind and above, night, the yacht in the upper-middle of a wide 16:9 frame heading away from camera, white hull with no name or lettering. Along the stern rail and down both sides of the deck stands the living orchestra of women in plain ivory sleeveless deck dresses, one every arm's length, each holding a clean electric guitar at playing height, facing outward, seen as calm swaying silhouettes, lips closed. Their job is to hold one long chord, right hands resting on the strings. Inside the ring, on the aft sun deck, two small figures in black lace lie on two white lounges in dark sunglasses, not performing. Below and behind the yacht, black open water; the wake is a long, dark, faintly ruffled V running back toward camera, not yet glowing. No city, no bridge, no shore, no other boats; the sky is a deep blue-black with a faint cool glow low on the far horizon. Only cold light: a thin blue and green edge on the ivory dresses, no warm tones. Photoreal, 35mm, slight high angle, fine grain, cool night grade, RGB color only, no orange, no text.
```

**Motion prompt**
```
Slow steady glide following behind the stern at the same height. One effect only: on the first downbeat the wake begins to glow with blue-green bioluminescence, starting right under the stern and running back along both arms of the V until a long luminous trail stretches into the dark like a giant snail's trail; the glow lights the guitar line softly from below in green and blue. No sparks, no splash. Nobody on board looks back. Gentle synchronized sway of the guitar line as if playing long sustained chords, mouths closed, no frantic strumming, no one boards or leaves the yacht, cannons rusted and still, boat does not land. RGB color only, no orange, no city in frame, 10 seconds, Grok Imagine style
```

**Last still**
```
The same high stern view at night. The wake is now a long, even, luminous blue-green V running from the stern back to the bottom of frame and fading into the dark; the ivory dresses and guitar bodies are lit softly green and blue from below, the line in silhouette mid-sway. The two black-lace figures lie unchanged on the white lounges. Black open water, no city, no bridge, no other boats. Photoreal, 35mm, fine grain, RGB color only, no orange, no text.
```

## #11 — The fires lean too (dark island)

**Fits:** dark island, after K (before L). **Clip:** 10s, trims to 8s (3 bars at 88 BPM). **Bed:** Nofo i te Vai; the flames start leaning on the first beat of the chorus. **Effect:** the flames lean. **Chain:** none (hard cut from K's last frame, which already holds the row of fires). Use this **or** F's vine variation, not both.

**Start still**
```
A straight row of small, controlled, evenly spaced campfires on a black volcanic-sand beach at night, seen across black water through a long lens, filling the lower half of a wide 16:9 frame, every flame standing straight up and still. At the right end of the row, two rusted iron cannons lie half-buried at the tideline, dark, sealed and inert, lit orange on one side, no smoke. The fires' only job is to burn: low, steady, untended, nothing spreading. Above the beach the vine-covered volcanic island rises into low fog, small orange and red lights glowing inside the vines. Along the very bottom edge of frame, soft and out of focus, the silhouettes of the guitar line on the white yacht's rail, guitar necks dark against the firelight, facing the island, lips closed. The wet sand mirrors each flame as an orange thread. Orange and red light only against deep blue night, no other color. Photoreal, long telephoto lens, compressed depth, fine grain, warm firelight grade, no text.
```

**Motion prompt**
```
Locked long lens. One effect only: the flames. With no wind and no smoke drift, every flame in the row leans the same few degrees to the left, then to the right, all together, in unison with the soft-focus guitar line at the bottom of frame, one full cycle every five seconds. The fires stay the same size and do not spread or spark. The cannons stay dark and still. Nobody on board reacts. Gentle synchronized sway of the guitar line as if playing long sustained chords, mouths closed, no frantic strumming, no one boards or leaves the yacht, cannons rusted and still, boat does not land. Orange and red light only, 10 seconds, Grok Imagine style
```

**Last still**
```
The same locked long-lens frame. Every flame in the row leans the same few degrees to the right, the foreground guitar silhouettes leaning right with them. The fires are the same size and count as at the start; the two rusted cannons sit dark and inert with no smoke; fog and orange vine lights unchanged above. Orange and red light only, deep blue night. Photoreal, long telephoto, fine grain, no text.
```
