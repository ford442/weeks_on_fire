STYLE:

Educational cabaret duet at 92 BPM. Detuned upright piano (±5 cents), soft brushed snare, low-string pad. Lillith & Rubella in parallel thirds — **deadpan, never smiling**, like a 1960s language-lab film that cracked open. Lyrics showcase **English triphthongs** [aʊ̯ə̯ aɪ̯ə̯ ɔɪ̯ə̯] (flour/flower, hire/higher, coir/coyer/lawyer), plus **Danish** [iɐ̯u̯ uɐ̯u̯] (hvirvle, spurv, færge), **Spanish** [u̯ei̯ u̯ai̯] (buey, Uruguay), and rolling / r-heavy clusters (rural, ferrocarril, Børge). Diction forward in mix — consonants dry, vowels gliding. **Structural flaw:** the **pronunciation guide** (spoken IPA-ish prompts) is always **one vowel glide behind** the sung melody — the singers land the triphthong while the guide is still on the diphthong. Smash-cut optional Twilight Time handoff only if requested. Mood: Lillith & Rubella's gothic living room becomes a phonetics classroom. Runtime ~55–70 seconds. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.

NOTES:

## Phonetic research basis
- **English triphthongs (RP / non-rhotic):** [aʊ̯ə̯] flour vs disyllabic flower; [aɪ̯ə̯] hire vs higher; [ɔɪ̯ə̯] coir vs coyer/lawyer/loir; also society, iron, sour, player, slower (Wells *Accents of English*).
- **Danish triphthongs:** [iɐ̯u̯] hvirvle 'whirl'; [uɐ̯u̯] spurv 'sparrow'; [ɛɐ̯u̯] færge 'ferry'; [œ̞ɐ̯u̯] Børge.
- **Spanish triphthongs:** [u̯ei̯] buey; [u̯ai̯] Uruguay; [i̯ai̯]/[i̯ei̯] cambiáis / cambiéis (European Spanish).
- **Shibboleth / cluster garnish:** Polish *W Szczebrzeszynie chrząszcz brzmi w trzcinie*; Czech *strč prst skrz krk*; rural juror (legal tongue-twister meme); ferrocarril (Spanish rolling r).

## Production notes
- **Episode placement:** Standalone musical cutaway; pairs with *Choose Your Basalt* (edu-cabaret family) but **phonetics** not geology.
- **Voices:** Lillith & Rubella, parallel thirds, deadpan. Pronunciation guide is a third voice or the singer breaking character — still flat.
- **Structural flaw:** Guide always one glide behind — never syncs by the final chorus. Do not fix in edit.
- **Runtime target:** 55–70 seconds (jingle-length, basalt sibling).
- **Button line:** "That's phonetics for us" (echo of *That's nature for us*).
