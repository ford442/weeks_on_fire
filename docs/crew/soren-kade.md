# Soren Kade

**Role:** Director · **Location:** Joshua Tree, CA · **On series:** Episodes 01–03
**Portrait:** `public/cast/soren-kade.jpg` · **Specialty:** night desert tableaux & kinetic cutaways

## Bio

Soren directs *Weeks on Fire* like a concert filmed inside a hallucination — wide first,
faces second, then the strike of light. He storyboards in ten-second blocks synced to
Minimax drops and pushes Grok Imagine toward practical dust, anamorphic stretch, and
silhouettes that feel cast rather than generated.

He hunts for one image per scene that could hang on a wall and still scare you a little.
When he finds it, he protects it: no second face in a plate that already has its one
extra, no camera move that turns a cut into a hallway.

## Desk

- **Touches:** **selected picture only.** Once a Mara page has produced a still worth keeping, he takes it.
- **Refuses:** pages, song picks, casting, starting an idea. Nothing enters the series as a Soren shot.
- **Hands off to:** Elio (prompt reproducibility, archive), with Julian's slate.

## On Christina's house

Not his yet. There is no selected still. When there is, his instinct is already on record:
*the cut is the travel* — gilt foyer to white marble in one cut, no hallway, no fjord. He
wants the tower only in the glass because a reflection can't be art-directed into a
postcard.
