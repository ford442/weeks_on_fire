# Song × picture sync points: five places to tie them together

*Head-writer pass, 2026-11-26. Text only. Nothing in the repo was changed. Everything here is a proposal for the files named.*

I read the songs filed since 2026-11-01 next to their pictures. The five picture-led catalog songs (*Box, Box, Hat*, *Just a Sunday*, *Eleven Stops Since Thursday*, *Pans Down*, *Thank You for the Lava*) and the four Christmas carols are already timed to the frame, so I left them alone. The places below are where a lyric or a change in the music is close to a moment in the picture but doesn't land on it yet, or where the song has an obvious moment that no picture uses yet.

All times are worked out from the BPM in each file. Snap them to the real bar lines once you've heard the track.

## 1. Good King Wenceslas (Brass Polka): the footsteps land on the tuba, and verse 4 is 4 seconds early

**Files:** `ideas/good-king-wenceslas.md` (beat sheet, shots 10–14) · `songs/_drawer/Good_King_Wenceslas_Polka.md`

**The math.** The tempo is 132, so each half-line of the carol is 8 beats, or 3.64 s. That fits the board's own verse windows (0:08, 0:37, 1:06, 1:43, 2:12), which are all exactly 29 s apart. Inside verse 4, though, the shot labels are wrong:

| Line | Actually sung at | Board puts it at |
|---|---|---|
| "I can go no longer" | **1:53.9** | shot 10, 1:43 |
| "Mark my footsteps, good my page" | **1:57.5** | shot 11, 1:53 |
| "tread thou in them boldly" | **2:01.2** | shot 12, 2:03 |

As the board stands, Lillith's glowing boots appear while Rubella is still singing that she can't go on.

**The tie.**
- **Shot 10, 1:43.0–1:57.5** (15 s clip, trim to 14.5): Rubella trudges and stops. She sits down in the snow and the tree lands across her lap on **"lon-"** of "longer" (about **1:55.7**). Mug level, face flat.
- **Shot 11, 1:57.5–2:01.2** (6 s clip, trim to 3.7): three steps, and each one lands on a **tuba downbeat**: **Mark** (1:57.5), **foot-** (1:58.4), **good** (1:59.3). Each step melts a hole and puffs steam on the tuba note. On "page" (2:00.2) she stands still.
- **Shot 12, 2:01.2–2:12.1** (15 s clip, trim to 10.9): Rubella steps into the first print on **"tread"** (2:01.2) and the next one on **"bold-"** (2:03.0).
- **The drop, shot 14:** the flowers spread down the trail **one print per bar** (every 0.91 s). The first print bursts on **"Heat"** (2:19.3) and the eighth, at the foot of the snow mountain, on **"printed"** (about 2:25). The tuba is doing the oom-pah, so the flower path travels on the oom. Add to the prompt: *one print blooms per beat, nearest first*. This also fixes the board's named risk (the model filling the whole street with flowers at once).

**Same file, also worth fixing:** in shot 6, Rubella sings *mountain → forest fence → fountain* (0:55.2 / 0:58.8 / 1:02.4), but the camera push goes *fountain → mountain*. Reverse the move so each place is on screen as she sings its name, and end on the icicle hydrant on "fountain".

**Laugh:** *her boots are so hot that flowers grow in her footsteps, right on the oom-pah.*

## 2. Second Step: she jumps over the creaky stair and it creaks anyway

**File:** `songs/_drawer/electronic-jokes/Second_Step.md` (no video yet; this is the video beat for it)

**The tie.** The STYLE block already fixes the creak at the same place every second bar, as part of the 2-step skip. Verse 2 sings the plan: *"I tried to skip you, I tried to jump / I landed on the third like a feather / And you creaked from underneath, out of love."* Shoot exactly that:
- On **"jump"**, Rubella (heels in hand) hops over step two. She lands on step three without a sound, during a bar that has **no** creak.
- Next bar, **"And you creaked"**: the creak comes in its usual slot and **step two glows, with nobody standing on it.** She doesn't look down.
- Locked low side-on view of the staircase. Subject motion: one hop. One effect: the glow.

The music's fixed creak does the joke. Nobody has to act it.

**Sharpen the look line.** It currently says "every second step glowing", which can be read as every other stair. Make it **only step two, ever.**

**Laugh:** *she jumped over the creaky stair and it creaked anyway.*

## 3. Neon Nightfall: sing "Flash" on the flash, and let the silence be the freeze

**Files:** `songs/Neon_Nightfall.md` · `content/cutaways/neon-nightfall.json`

**What's off.** The billboard flash and the music cutting out are at **0:18**. The chorus that sings *"Flash!—the billboard burns, a moment frozen thin / Silence bleeds, then beats return"* starts at **0:20**, two seconds after the thing it describes. The lyric is describing the edit, and it's late for it.

**The tie (108 BPM, 2.22 s per bar).**
- Move **"Flash!"** to be the last word of the pre-chorus and put it on the **bar 9 downbeat, 0:17.8**. The billboard fires on the "sh", and the music cuts out on the word.
- **0:17.8–0:20.0:** the picture freezes. The runner hangs in mid-stride and the raindrops hang in the billboard light ("a moment frozen thin", taken literally). You hear only rain and the buzz.
- **0:20.0 (bar 10 downbeat, which falls exactly on the second):** the chorus comes back with *"The billboard burns, a moment frozen thin / Silence bleeds, then beats return, we dive in"*. The runner unfreezes on the first beat.
- Also move the drone swoop from 0:08 (mid-bar) to **0:08.9**, the bar 5 downbeat, so the verse starts on it.

**Gatekeeper note:** this file is out of house spec. The section labels are `(Verse)` instead of MiniMax `[Verse]` tags. There's no cast and no kid laugh, and the board note reads like a generic chase. It needs a joke before it's promoted, not just a retime. **Hold.**

## 4. Softcore: on "Rawr is a feeling", the hood roars and Rubella doesn't

**Files:** `ideas/softcore.md` (second-pass cut map, chorus 0:50–1:10) · `songs/Softcore.md`

**What's unused.** Every chorus line on the board has a picture except **"Rawr is a feeling, ooh-ooh"**. It's the third line of the first chorus, about **0:55**, between the 0:53.2 S10 and the 0:56.4 S6 recut.

**The tie:** a new Look A insert, about 1 bar (1.6 s). Close on Rubella at the mic, hood up. On **"Rawr"**, the plush tiger face on her hood drops its padded jaw wide open and shows its felt fangs, then closes. Rubella's own face doesn't move and her mouth stays shut. Camera: slow push-in. Effect: the hood's jaw, and nothing else. 6 s clip, keep 1.6 s.

**Why it's worth a slot.** It sets up the breakdown. The costume roars at 0:55, and at 1:43 the most Rubella herself can manage is a polite "meow". Music big, face still. It doesn't break the mouth lock either, because a plush jaw isn't her mouth and A-07 still has the only open mouth.

**Laugh:** *her hat roars but she doesn't.*

## 5. Less: when she says "Hi-hats", everybody's hat disappears

**File:** `songs/_drawer/electronic-jokes/Less.md` (look line only, no video yet)

**The tie.** The song's rule is that each spoken word removes one element from the track for good. The picture removes the same thing on the same word. Make the first three removals literal, as a rule of three with a wrong third:
1. **"Shaker."** The dancer who has been shaking a shaker all along vanishes mid-shake. The shaker leaves the mix on the same frame.
2. **"Hi-hats."** Every hat in the club (caps, beanies, one tall top hat on the DJ) vanishes from every head at once, as the hi-hats drop out of the mix. Wordplay a kid can say back.
3. **"Delay."** From the first frame, Rubella has been trailed by two faint copies of herself, each one a beat behind (the delay you can see). On "Delay" they catch up and disappear. Only she is left.

Locked wide view of the club floor. One vanish per word, and only on the word. Rubella's face never changes.

**Laugh:** *she said "hi-hats" and everybody's hat disappeared.*
