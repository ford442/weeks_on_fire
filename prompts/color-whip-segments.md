**Working title:** Color Whip
**Alts:** Hard Chroma · Phosphor Rush · Afterimage Engine · The Test Pattern That Panicked

Same no-object law as *Chroma Bath*. Opposite clock. Color does not drift. Color
**replaces**. If a generation comes back looking like a lava lamp, reject it —
that is Bath's job.

**Music lean:** *Twilight Time* — 150 BPM four-on-the-floor. Picture locks to the
kick (0.4s). Vocals may play; mouths never appear. This **is** the beat-sync
job *Molten Silver Sphere* already proved, with the last object removed.

---

## Structural flaw (hold for the full runtime)

**Black exists only as a one-frame punch on the kick.** Between kicks there is
no pure black — the field is saturated. On the kick, one frame (1/24s) punches
to black and the next hue is already there. Do not fade. Do not dip to grey.
The absence is a drum.

**Second law (enforced every shot):** nothing is allowed to become a thing. The
instant a swirl starts to read as a face, an eye, a flower, a planet, a galaxy,
or a butterfly, it must be torn off on the next kick. If a generation comes
back looking like a fractal poster, a nebula, or a kaleidoscope, reject it.

**Third law:** one camera crime per shot, and it is fast. Whip, smash, spin,
or lock-plus-strobe. Never a slow drift. Never two crimes.

## How to maximize detail (prompt stack)

Do **not** write "psychedelic, trippy, vibrant colors, surreal, LSD, kaleidoscope."
Those words return mush, symmetry, and mandala clip art. Stuff the prompt
through these eight slots. Every new idea drops into **one slot** as a
verb + material.

| Slot | What it holds | Example you can swap |
|------|---------------|----------------------|
| **1. FIELD** | What the color *is* made of this beat | CRT phosphor · motion-blurred gel · stacked afterimage · smash-zoom tunnel |
| **2. FORM** | The fast geometry this beat | Invert pair · horizontal streak comb · delayed ghosts · accelerating spiral |
| **3. PROCESS** | The named operation — the hero of the segment | Strobe · whip · phosphor · smash · chase · cuts · vortex · hit |
| **4. PALETTE** | Two or three hues, *named*, that this beat spends | Magenta + acid lime · cyan + sodium orange · violet + gold |
| **5. GRAIN** | The artifact sitting on top | Kick-black flash frame · scanline tear · RGB split · shutter smear |
| **6. EDGE** | What happens where two colors meet | Hard swap · smear into a third hue · chromatic split racing |
| **7. CAMERA** | One FAST move only | Whip · smash-zoom · spin-up · lock-off with strobe. Never a drift. |
| **8. MICRO** | 2–4 countable events, timed to kicks | "Fifteen kicks. Three hue swaps. One smear that does not heal." |

**Rules that keep a stuffed prompt from collapsing**

1. **Name the process, not the mood.** "The field inverts magenta and lime on every kick" survives. "Mind-bending psychedelic energy" dies.
2. **Count kicks.** Fifteen kicks in six seconds. Models keep numbers.
3. **Name the hues.** Never "rainbow."
4. **One processing dialect per segment.** Strobe *or* whip, never both.
5. **Say "no objects."** Generators want a nebula or an eye.
6. **If a generation comes back mushy, cut GRAIN poetry first.** Never cut FIELD, FORM, or PROCESS.
7. **Video continues the still at kick speed.** Animation should not invent a new dialect mid-shot.
8. **Keep the anti-symmetry clause.** Asymmetric, off-center, not kaleidoscopic, not mandala, not mirrored.

**Psychedelic = processing at tempo, not a filter.** Phosphor, shutter, afterimage, smash-zoom. The trip is the frame rate.

## Visual lock (paste into every prompt)

Pure non-representational color field filling the entire frame. No objects, no
horizon, no ground, no sky, no figures, no faces, no text. Saturated color as
the only subject, shot like photographed light rather than digital illustration.
Motion is fast — whip, strobe, smash, or spin — never a slow drift. One-frame
black punch on each musical kick, then the next hue already present. Asymmetric
and off-center; never kaleidoscopic, never mirrored, never a mandala.
Photoreal materials, cinematic, anamorphic 2.39:1.

**Palette spend rule:** each segment burns its named pair and does not return to
it. Magenta/lime → cyan/orange → violet/gold → teal/coral → red/green/cobalt →
ultramarine/sulphur → oxblood/mint → all of them into white.

## Generate order

1. Shot **A** still first. If the invert comes back as a blend or a gradient, the kick logic is dead — fix A before any video.
2. Then **F** (hard cuts) — generators want dissolves; refuse them.
3. Then **E** (channel chase) — the RGB split must not become a rainbow graphic.
4. Then B, C, D, G in any order.
5. **H last**, matched to whatever hues A–G actually produced.
6. Animate each still at kick speed. Concatenate A→H on straight cuts, **no crossfade**. Crossfades are Bath.

If you only generate one thing first: **Shot A still, then A's 6s loop.** If the loop fades, the whole piece is in the wrong show.
