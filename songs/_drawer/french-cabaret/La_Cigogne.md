# La Cigogne

**Genre collision:** 1950s chanson à texte × mambo. Brass section stabs, congas and bongos, a piano montuno, and a chanson singer who refuses to dance.

**Visual concept:** A narrow balcony above grey rooftops on a bright morning. Rubella sits with her World's Okayest Tenant mug of tea and no spoon. Lillith leans on the railing playing an electric guitar through a small amp. A stork lands with a parcel in its beak, and inside is one teaspoon. Then another stork brings a cup and saucer, a third a teapot. Then a flock of a hundred storks arrives holding the corners of an enormous white sheet, and in it is a whole house, which they set down on the roof next door. Rubella stirs her tea.

**Laugh (kid words):** *She ordered one tiny spoon, and the storks brought her a whole house.*

**BPM:** 112, mambo feel, C minor verses, E♭ major chorus

**Target length:** ~2:20 · 13 segments
