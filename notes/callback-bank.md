# Callback Bank

Last refreshed: 2026-10-05 (commit dbefb15)

A reusable index of running bits, props, places, cameo rules, name-droppable songs, and recurring
numbers across *Weeks on Fire*. **Index, not retelling.** Each block says what the bit is, where it
lives, what the bible already locks, and one to three ways it can show up in somebody else's scene.

**How to use (any writing or joke pass):** pull at least three callbacks from this file. Prefer a
*portable form* over a quote. Check **Locks** before you spend anything. If a callback has a
"spend once" rule and a file already spent it, you are referencing it, not re-doing it.

Series-wide rules that govern everything below (cite `characters/bible/README.md`,
`characters/bible/relationship-map.md`, `notes/scenes/README.md`, `docs/season-arc.md`):

- Nobody names the horror. Nobody comments on the frame. Nobody is frightened.
- Open files stay open: haunted vs. unscheduled · Continuity Voice vs. Radio Voice · whether the
  Pizza Guy knows · the Kestrel Ridge antenna · why the skull glows · Biscuit smart vs. assigned ·
  the red lamp pulse · the thirteenth floor.
- Four Vales, no stated relation, never remarked on. Madelin ≠ Madelyn.
- No copyrighted lyrics. Original Minimax tracks or `[Section]` tags only.
