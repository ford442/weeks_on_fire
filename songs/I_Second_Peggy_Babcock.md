STYLE:

Parliamentary patter at 100 BPM. Cracked gavel as kick (wood on wood, never a sample pack). Folding-chair creaks as hats. Cheap clubhouse organ holding one late chord. Fluorescent hum. Deadpan female secretary — precise, unhurried, never cute, never smiling. She can say her name. The room cannot. No belting. No vibrato. **Structural flaw:** after every failed second she re-reads the **entire motion including her name**; bar count does not change. Optional purple-light pulse under the organ, very quiet. Mood: HOA clubhouse that will not adjourn. Runtime ~1:15–1:40. Use a deadpan female secretary voice; do not use Rubella or Continuity Voice (this is the clubhouse, not EyeWash).

NOTES:

## What the song is
An HOA hearing that cannot take a second. **Peggy Babcock** is recording secretary. She has the floor. The motion is petty and physical: the thistle strip stays short; the special shrub sits six inches off the walk. She reads it correctly. Dale breaks on the first syllable of her name. Patricia's hand never lands. Karen logs `second pending`. The Two do not vote. Biscuit watches the gavel.

The joke is the meeting, not a lesson. Nobody says "tongue twister." Nobody asks her to slow down.

## Craft (keep this off-mic)
- **Peggy Babcock** is the classic rapid-repeat name (hard p/b + overlapping vowels). It is her name on the roster, not a drill.
- The motion carries s/sh and short-i clusters (thistle, strip, shall, special, shrub, six, sit) because that is how HOA English sounds when someone typed it tired.
- Do not import Peter Piper, woodchucks chucking wood, lorries, wristwatches, or Schnapps. *Recycling Bin Rebellion* owns the bin. *Pad Kid* owns the recall.

- **Episode placement:** Episode 4 HOA clubhouse; insert inside or after [`item-three-decorative-accent.md`](../notes/scenes/item-three-decorative-accent.md). Sibling to *Recycling Bin Rebellion* (protest) — this is **procedure**, not the anthem.
