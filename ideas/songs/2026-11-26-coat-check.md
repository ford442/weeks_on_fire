# Coat Check

**Genre collision:** bass house (126 BPM, wobbling mid-bass, chopped vocal stabs, big claps) × 1940s supper-club swing (muted trumpets, a walking upright bass, a hotel desk bell). A coat-check bell that becomes the hi-hat.

**Visual concept:** A packed night club, all lasers and haze. On the side wall is a small, old-fashioned coat-check window with a brass desk bell and a dry-cleaner rail of hangers running back into the dark. Rubella stands at the counter holding ticket 47, waiting for her plain dark work coat. Every time she rings the bell the rail turns and brings her the wrong thing, taken literally. A **coat of arms**: a heraldic coat with real arms that wave. A **coat of paint**, which sloshes out and turns the whole club pink. A raincoat that is raining. A big furry coat with a bear inside it. Across the room, Lillith plays guitar in the DJ booth and goes pink with everyone else. When the club finally empties and the lights come up, the rail brings Rubella's own coat.

**Laugh (kid words):** *She wants her coat back, and the coat check gives her a coat with arms, a coat of paint, a raining coat and a bear.*

**BPM:** 126, G minor

**Target length:** ~3:26 · 16 segments

**Video use map:**
- **Pattern:** verse on action, with one wrong coat per couplet, each arriving on a bell ding. The chorus holds on the club dancing behind her while she waits.
- **Breaks:** everything stops for the breakdown except the bell.
- **Mouths:** Rubella's mouth is on for the chorus hook and the breakdown and off elsewhere. Lillith's mouth stays closed. The bear's mouth stays closed.
- **Diegetic sound:** none beyond the song (its bell is in the Styles). Mute Imagine audio.
