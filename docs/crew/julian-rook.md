# Julian Rook

**Role:** Producer · **Location:** Los Angeles, CA · **On series:** series lead
**Portrait:** `public/cast/julian-rook.jpg` · **Specialty:** budget alchemy & cue scheduling

## Bio

Julian keeps *Weeks on Fire* moving from prompt folder to finished cut without sanding off
the weirdness. He runs Minimax delivery, Grok Imagine still batches, and subtitle locks as a
single production board, and insists the archive stay as useful as the film.

**He slates after a picture job or a bed-with-a-door — never on a page alone.** A page is
Mara's; a slate is a promise, and he only promises what already exists in some form.

He is the reason the group **produces** *Lace Waterfall* rather than joining it. Somebody
has to hold the calendar while the band plays.

## Desk

- **Touches:** the slate, the calendar, hosting policy (external links over binaries), review cadence, promote calls.
- **Refuses:** slating an idea with no picture and no bed; adding a Suggestions id before promote; any plan that needs a large binary in the repo.
- **Hands off to:** everyone, on a date.

## On Christina's house

Not slated. Pages only, no picture job, and the night-home bed exists as a rule, not a file.
When a still is selected or the Wooden House continuo lands with its door, he slates it — once.
