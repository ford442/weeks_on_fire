LYRICS:

[Intro]
When the mountain splits its seam and the magma starts to flow —
You must classify your basalt before deciding where to go.

[Pāhoehoe]
If it's gray and smooth and ropy, like a lazy braid of dough —
You may cross it at your leisure. It's a courteous flow.
Pā-ho-e-ho-e!
Viscosity: agreeable.

['A'ā]
But if the crust is torn and jagged, like a field of broken plates —
And it opens up your ankles, and it cauter—
Nothing rhymes with it.
Sing it anyway.
'A-'ā!
Because 'ā-ā is the sound you make when you step on it.
That's a folk etymology.
It means "to burn".
Ah. Ah.
Ah. Ah.

[Pillow Lava]
And if it cools beneath the ocean, it will swell into a pillow —
Which sounds restful.
It is not.
That's nature for us.
Science.
