STYLE:

Sincere indie-folk breakup ballad, 68 BPM, addressed to a smoke detector.
Nylon-string guitar close-mic'd with finger squeak left in, upright bass, one
brushed floor tom, no cymbals. Rubella singing at 4 AM volume — the volume you
use when the neighbors are asleep and you are not. Genuinely tender. No wink,
no comedy voice, no "funny song" phrasing; the joke is that the arrangement
never learns it is a joke. **Structural flaw:** the detector's low-battery
chirp lands every 47 seconds — it does not fall on a bar line, a beat, or a
lyric, and it never will. It is also the only perfectly in-tune pitch in the
recording (Bb6); tune the entire band six cents flat against it. Do not gate
it, do not quantize it, do not fade it under the vocal. Second voice optional:
the chirp is the duet partner. Ends on a chirp with no music under it. Runtime
~2:10–2:30.

NOTES:

## What the song is
A love song to a dying smoke detector, played completely straight. Rubella
cannot reach it, will not replace it, and has stopped pretending either of
those is the point. The building is full of people announcing things that
aren't true; the one honest device in it is announcing its own death on a
fixed schedule. Nobody in the song laughs.

## Craft (keep this off-mic)
The comedy is entirely structural. Do not "perform" the absurdity — every
lyric is literally true and delivered as such. The chirp is not a sound
effect, it is the other singer, and it is the only element allowed to be in
tune. If a take has the chirp landing musically, that take is wrong; re-cut it
so the chirp collides with a word.

## Production notes
- **Episode placement:** Building cast / hallway 4 AM. Sibling to *Third Shift
  Porcelain* (labor) and *Haunted or Unscheduled* (master control) — this one
  owns **domestic** 4 AM. Not an EyeWash sign-off; *Rinse Cycle* has that job.
- **Voice:** Rubella, close and dry, zero vibrato, no belting anywhere
  including the final chorus.
- **Structural flaw:** 47-second chirp cycle, unquantized, Bb6, band tuned six
  cents flat against it. Do not fix in mix.
- **Runtime target:** 2:10–2:30. Fade is not allowed; end on a bare chirp.
