# H13 · Series key art (horizontal, vertical one-sheet, square tile)

**Shot:** H13
**Episode / beat:** series. Not a scene from any episode: a single composed street where every
lane of the show is visible at once without anyone acknowledging the others.
**Why it matters:** the one image that has to say *Weeks on Fire* with no title on it yet: glamour
walking away from catastrophe, a deadpan man on a porch, a cat who has been right all night,
suburban cosmic trouble behind blinds, and a TV that is always on. If a stranger can tell it is a
comedy and a little dangerous, it works.
**Locks:** [`the-two-identity`](../locks/the-two-identity.lock.md) ·
[`pizza-guy-identity`](../locks/pizza-guy-identity.lock.md) (`distant`) ·
[`black-cat-identity`](../locks/black-cat-identity.lock.md) (`small`) ·
[`house-negative`](../locks/house-negative.lock.md) (`poster`)
**Aspect / model:** H 16:9 · V 2:3 · S 1:1 · `grok-imagine-image-quality` (repo default; pass
`--aspect` to match the framing)

**Geography (all three framings):** one wet suburban street at night, shot straight down its
center line toward a dead end. Vivienne (left) and Liliane (right) walk toward camera down the
middle of the asphalt in heels, not looking back. At the far end of the street a two-story house
burns, roof open, embers rising: the fire is the deepest layer and the main backlight. Right
side, mid-ground: a lit porch with the Pizza Guy standing on it, tiny and perfectly vertical.
Left side, mid-ground: a cedar fence with the black cat on a post, small. Between two houses on
the left, cyan and magenta laser light flickers from a backyard. On the right, a low clubhouse
with horizontal blinds glowing violet. In one front window, a CRT's grey-blue glow. A full moon
sits high and to one side.

**Title-safe areas (reserved for type added in post; the prompt keeps them clean):**
H, upper-left third of dark sky · V, top quarter of sky above the moon · S, lower fifth of wet
asphalt in front of their feet.

**Lane rule:** worlds do not mix, so nobody here interacts. The Two do not look at the porch,
the Pizza Guy does not look at the fire, the cat watches the camera. Not Rubella or Lillith
(different pair, different key art if ever). No EyeWash tower, no channel number.

**Callbacks spent (as image, not dialog):** walking away from the fire, not looking back · don't
smudge the mascara · the Pizza Guy arrives exactly when it gets worst · the Black Cat's second look
(at us) · the purple light behind blinds · EyeWash always on somewhere.
