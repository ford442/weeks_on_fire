# Lace Waterfall — joke songs and the early tapes

*Drawer. Text only. 2026-11-08. `promote?: no` on every song. Not codegen'd.*

Lace Waterfall is Rubella and Lillith's band: **the two of them only, no Kenji, no third
member.** Their catalog on `main` is warm 1970s rock anthems (*Lace Waterfall*, *Heavy
Water*, *Both Coats* and the rest). Alongside that there's a modern joke lane:
[`../Softcore.md`](../Softcore.md) (metalcore in tiger onesies) and the *Big City*
video, where they arrive late with giant metal hair.

This folder adds two sets.

## `modern/` — five joke songs, in the Softcore and Big City lane

Huge, modern, high-energy genres, played completely sincerely, about something tiny. Lillith
goes all in; Rubella stays flat inside the noise. The size contrast is the joke.

| Song | Genre | Premise | Laugh |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`Bring_a_Cardigan.md`](modern/Bring_a_Cardigan.md) | symphonic power metal, 180 | the epic quest is bringing a cardigan to a cold restaurant | a giant dragon-fighting song about bringing a sweater |
| [`Indoor_Voice.md`](modern/Indoor_Voice.md) | hyperpop, 170 | the loudest possible song asking everyone to use their indoor voice | the song screams at you to use your inside voice |
| [`Wrong_Lid.md`](modern/Wrong_Lid.md) | nu-metal / rap-metal, 98 half-time | rage at a cupboard full of containers with no matching lid | they're furious about a lunchbox lid, and it was on the box all along |
| [`Power_Nap.md`](modern/Power_Nap.md) | big-room festival EDM, 128 | a main-stage anthem about a twenty-minute nap; the drop is a snore | the biggest party song in the world, and everybody falls asleep on the drop |
| [`Final_Form_Parallel_Park.md`](modern/Final_Form_Parallel_Park.md) | anime-opening J-rock, 190 | a hero's transformation theme for parallel parking | it's a superhero song, and the superpower is parking |

## `early/` — five songs by the younger band (the early tapes)

Lace Waterfall before the building: younger, rawer, recorded on cassette four-tracks, in
rehearsal rooms, basements, stairwells and a storage unit. The voices are the same two, just
younger. Rubella was already dry, and Lillith was already setting things on fire.

| Song | Genre | Premise | Laugh |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`It_Passed.md`](early/It_Passed.md) | lo-fi post-punk, drum machine, 138 | Rubella's quality-assurance job: a kettle-lamp, a smoke-alarm teddy bear, a fan-toaster, and they all pass | she lets a toaster that's also a fan pass inspection |
| [`Three_Sectors.md`](early/Three_Sectors.md) | garage punk, 196 | Lillith's three evictions for noise and fireworks, each louder | she keeps getting kicked out for being too loud, and every time she's louder |
| [`Stairwell_Reverb.md`](early/Stairwell_Reverb.md) | lo-fi shoegaze, 84 | the first demo, recorded in a stairwell for the free echo; doors keep opening | someone walks through every time a door opens |
| [`Buskers_Hat.md`](early/Buskers_Hat.md) | anti-folk with a toy drum machine, 112 | busking: the hat collects a button, a key, half a sandwich, and a pigeon | the last thing in her hat is a pigeon, and it stays |
| [`Practice_Space.md`](early/Practice_Space.md) | lo-fi grunge, 76 | a storage-unit practice space where the freezer next door steals the power | every time they get to the loud part, the freezer turns them off |

## Canon notes (read before adding more early tapes)

- **Used, from `characters/building-cast.md`:** Rubella's quality-assurance job at a mega-corp
  whose products were "technically" safe. Lillith's busking, "vibes consultant" days and her
  evictions from three arcology sectors for noise and pyrotechnics.
- **Not stated, on purpose:** how Rubella and Lillith met, how old they were, whether they
  lived together, or when the band formed. The early tapes just *are* early. Don't answer it.
- **Four Vales, no stated relation, never remarked on.** Nobody in these songs calls them
  sisters.
- **No Kenji, on either set.** He's on the *Big City* record as the club's house guitarist,
  never on a Lace Waterfall song.
