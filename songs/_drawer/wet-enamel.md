STYLE:

Slow instrumental bed at 96 BPM in B-flat minor, about one minute forty.
Brushed snare on two and four, felt-muted kick, no hi-hat.
Prepared piano with paper on the strings playing a four-note figure that never resolves.
A single electric bass note held under each bar, slightly detuned, like a fluorescent ballast.
One tape-saturated Rhodes chord every eight bars, swelled in from silence and cut before it peaks.
No melody instrument. No pad. No strings.
The arrangement moves horizontally: nothing rises, nothing builds, one element leaves every sixteen bars.
Room sound is a small tiled laundry room, close mics, short reflections.
At 1:32 everything stops on a downbeat except the bass note, which holds four beats and cuts.
No vocals except one spoken line in the final two seconds, dry, no reverb.
Low dynamic range, mixed to sit under picture.
