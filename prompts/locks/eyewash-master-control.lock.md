## What the blocks lock (and why)

- **Windowless room, calm room, wrong clock.** Shoot it warm, not creepy. The dread is structural:
  the clock and one monitor, nothing else.
- **Twenty CRTs, four across and five down**, none showing the same station. Rows one to four, by
  the scene: lava and basalt, two serpents in a hedge, an empty laundromat with dryers turning, a
  corridor of mirrors. Row five is bars and tone **except position two, which shows this room a
  few seconds ahead.** The ahead-feed is how the room reads as "slightly ahead of reality": in it,
  the chair is already empty. **Oz never looks at it.** Not in any framing.
- **The clock above the wall reads 3:44.** It is stuck there; it is never "4:00".
- **Oz:** fifties, cardigan over a collared shirt washed a hundred times, reading glasses pushed
  up into grey hair, thermos (tea) and a mug that is not from the station, a laminated log sheet
  with a grease pencil tied to it by a shoelace, capped between entries. Unhurried. Kind face.
- **No tower, no channel number, no logo.** "Channel 12½" is a tagline, never on screen. No
  readable text on any monitor; programs are pictures, not captions. Nobody looks at camera.
- **The Continuity Voice is never seen.** No booth, no silhouette, no speaker grille with a face.
- **`screen`** is EyeWash on any TV in another set (laundromat corner TV, clubhouse CRT, the Ep3
  porch TV). It shows a picture or snow, never a readable card in a hero still.
