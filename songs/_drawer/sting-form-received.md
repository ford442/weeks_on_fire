STYLE:

Ten-second sting, roughly 108 BPM feel, no tonal center.
Beat one: a rubber stamp hitting paper on a desk, close, dry.
Beat two: one dry snare hit, no reverb, brushes not sticks.
Beats three and four: a wooden office chair creaking back, slowly, as if someone leaned away.
Bar two: silence for three beats, then a single intercom tick — the click of a grille going live and then not.
No pitched instrument, no chord, no tag melody, no whoosh, no riser.
Room is a small tiled building office; reflections under 200 ms.
Everything mono, everything close.
The last second is room tone, then hard cut.
Designed to sit after a spoken claim and before the next thing, never under picture.

NOTES:

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not Ultra Screech.** No scream, no gang vocal, no guitar, no "GLAM-SHAM-POO!" This
  sting is for building spots that are not the shampoo ad, and it does not echo the ad.
- **Not Sting, Four Frames Late.** That is a Nova song with a Session Voice, 100 BPM in B
  minor. This has no voice and no key.
- **Not an EyeWash ident sting.** No Continuity Voice, no station logo, no title card. The
  building airs these, not the network.
- **Not Big City / Daisy / Hanshaw / Lightning / Well Fall.** Nothing about hair, hearts,
  charge, or drops.

Use: end card of `chest-height-delivery-academy`, `neutral-zone-laundry-minutes`,
`elevator-inspection-notice`, `decorative-accent-removal`. One sting, four spots.
