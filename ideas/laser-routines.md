# Laser Routines — beam choreography for four music videos

*Drawer, not a farm. Text only. 2026-11-01. `promote?: no` on every card.*

**Rules:** [`funny-rules.md`](funny-rules.md) · **Prompt craft:**
[`../prompts/the-soft-gyre-segments.md`](../prompts/the-soft-gyre-segments.md) (count things,
events not adjectives, one camera move per clip) · **Callbacks:**
[`../notes/callback-bank.md`](../notes/callback-bank.md)

This file adds a laser layer on top of four music-video ideas. It does not rewrite them.
Each video gets a **laser kit** (colours, emitters, and what the haze is made of), a
**cue sheet** by song section, **three interactions** where the world plays with the
beams, one **drop**, and a Grok Imagine clip plan for the drop.

those worlds. The lasers are an overlay, so they don't invent cast or plot for videos that
already exist. When a lead is in frame, the rules below say how they share the frame

## Laser locks (all four videos)

1. **Straight lines only.** Beams are razor-straight and stiff. They never wiggle, curl,
   slither or get a head. **Laser snakes belong to Episode 3 and stay there.** If a
   generation makes a beam move like an animal, the take failed.
2. **Never into a face or eye.** Beams pass behind, above, between and under people.
   Audience scanning stops at shoulder height. No beam ever hits a body
   (`home-sweet-void.md`: "No beams into bodies").
3. **No laser weapons.** No laser fights, cannons or blasters. Lasers here do jobs: they
   measure, slice fruit, draw targets, lead the way and whisk.
4. **The cast is the calmest thing in frame.** Nobody points at a beam, ducks under one,
   or reacts to one, except for the one end reaction a card names.
5. **Beams need haze, and every world makes its own.** Powdered sugar, rain, bath steam,
   fairground dust. Name the haze in every prompt or the beams come back invisible.
6. **Count the beams.** "Six green beams in a fan" holds up shot to shot. "Lots of lasers"
   turns to mush.
7. **One laser look per section.** Fan, tunnel, sheet, scan, graphic or dot. Changing the
   look on the section line is the choreography. Stacking two looks at once is noise.
8. **The drop changes the picture.** A laser drop has to *do* something to the world
   (appears, falls, multiplies, transforms or reverses). Colour change and strobe alone
   don't count.

**Laser vocabulary**, so every card means the same thing:
- **fan:** 3–9 beams spread from one point like fingers.
- **sheet / liquid sky:** a flat glowing plane made by one fast-scanning beam. It shows as a
  ceiling of light with haze rolling through it.
- **tunnel / cone:** one beam drawing a circle fast enough to read as a hollow cone.
- **scan:** a fan sweeping side to side on the beat.
- **chase:** beams firing one after another, left to right, on the hi-hats.
- **graphic:** a beam drawing a flat shape (circle, bullseye, outline) on a surface.
- **dot:** one point of light on a surface. No visible beam.

## 1. Strawberry ocean: *The Lasers Whisk the Ocean*

| field | value |
|---|---|
| id | `laser-strawberry-ocean` |
| world | the whipped-cream / strawberry ocean. One world. No paint rivulets, no lions, no Daisy tandem. |
| laugh | **The lasers are a giant mixer, and they whip the whole ocean into frosting.** |
| drop | **Transforms.** The laser sheet drops onto the sea and spins. The rolling cream stiffens into a horizon of glossy whipped peaks in one beat. |
| end reaction | none. The ocean is the performer. |
| promote? | no |

**Laser kit.**
- **Colours:** strawberry red (638 nm), vanilla-white, mint green as the single accent.
  No blue: the cream turns grey under blue light.
- **Emitters:** six projectors on a sandbar of sponge cake, plus one on a floating meringue
  buoy.
- **Haze:** powdered sugar blown off the wave tops. Every beam shows up in sugar dust and
  sparkles where a grain crosses it.

**Cue sheet.**

| Section | Laser look | What it does to the world |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | one red **dot** | The dot sits on the cream like a cherry. A wave rolls over it and the dot rides the wave. |
| Verse 1 | 3-beam red **fan**, low over the water | Beams skim the swell. Wherever a beam touches a wave crest, the crest leaves a tiny curl of piped cream behind it. |
| Pre-chorus | **chase** on the hi-hats, white | Strawberries bob up between waves on the beat. |
| Chorus | **liquid sky**: a red sheet 3 m above the water | The waves rise into the sheet like a crowd's hands, and the sheet slices each crest flat. |
| Verse 2 | mint **scan** | A strawberry the size of a fishing boat surfaces. Sugar dust pours off it. |
| Break | everything off, then one white **tunnel** pointed straight down | The tunnel draws a slow circle on the sea. The cream starts turning inside it like batter in a bowl. |
| **Drop** | the sheet **lowers onto the water** and spins | **Transforms:** the whole ocean whips into stiff peaks, horizon to horizon. |
| Last chorus | 9-beam red **fan** from the cake sandbar | The beams sit on the peaks like candles on a cake. |

**Interactions (three kept):**
1. **Things keep happening:** every strawberry that bobs up through the chorus sheet comes
   out sliced into two perfect halves. The halves float off as little red boats. It
   happens four times.
2. **Wrong job:** the meringue buoy's beam works as a lighthouse. Strawberries steer by it
   and line up in a neat convoy.
3. **Callback, the Vale berries and two glasses:** a small round table floats past on a
   meringue floe. On it are two wine glasses (one stem-down) and a bowl of berries. A red
   beam sweeps over the table and cuts nothing; the glasses ring once. Nobody is at the
   table. It's a prop only; neither Vale pair appears.

**Clip plan (drop, ~22s):**
1. **10s → trim 8, the break.** *Start still:* photoreal ocean made of rolling whipped cream
   at golden dusk, glossy strawberry-red swirls in the swell, whole strawberries bobbing,
   a sandbar of golden sponge cake on the left with six small laser projectors, a cloud
   of powdered-sugar haze over the water, one thin vanilla-white laser beam pointing
   straight down from above and drawing a circle on the cream, 2.39:1, 35mm, fine grain,
   Grok Imagine style. *Motion:* the circle speeds up and the cream inside it starts to
   turn like batter. Locked camera.
2. **6s, the drop.** Start = last frame of 1. A flat red laser plane lowers onto the sea
   and spins once. The whole ocean stiffens into glossy whipped peaks, horizon to horizon.
   Sugar haze bursts up.
3. **6s, last chorus.** Nine red beams fan out from the sandbar and rest on nine peaks like
   candles. One sliced strawberry half drifts through frame. Slow push-in.

**Do-not:** no blue beams; no paint rivulets or flowers (Daisy and the lion own those); no
Glam bottle; no beams on faces; the ocean never drinks or eats anything.

## 2. Lightning Strike: *Lightning Strikes Twice*

| field | value |
|---|---|
| id | `laser-lightning-strike` |
| world | the Lightning warehouse. One world. No well, no metal, no Big City hair. |
| laugh | **The lasers draw a bullseye, and the lightning hits it, and then hits it again exactly the same way.** |
| drop | **Multiplies.** The lasers draw forty bullseyes across the floor. Forty bolts hit forty targets at once. |
| end reaction | none |
| promote? | no |

**Laser kit.**
- **Colours:** acid green (532 nm) for the beams; lightning stays white-violet so the two
  never blur together.
- **Emitters:** a rail of eight projectors along the roof truss and one floor unit in the
  middle of the warehouse.
- **Haze:** rain leaking through the broken skylights. The beams light every falling drop
  so each one looks like a tiny bead of green.

**Cue sheet** (the 303 acid line drives the beams; the kick drives the bolts):

| Section | Laser look | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | one green **dot** walking across the concrete | It stops in the middle of the floor and waits. |
| Build 1 | the dot grows into a **graphic** bullseye, three rings | Rain falls through the rings. Nothing happens yet. |
| First hit | lasers blackout, then **one bolt** comes down through the skylight into the bullseye | The bullseye flashes once and redraws itself. |
| Verse | **scan** following the 303 squelch: every filter sweep is one beam swinging across the room | The scan pauses on the scorch mark each pass, like it's checking. |
| Second hit | **the same bolt, identical frame**, same bullseye | **Literal phrase:** lightning strikes twice. Cut it as the same insert, so it plays like the 3:44 sign-off. |
| Breakdown | 8-beam **tunnel** pointing up through the skylight | The tunnel lights a path in the rain from the clouds to the target. |
| **Drop** | the floor unit fires a **graphic grid of forty bullseyes** | **Multiplies:** forty bolts hit forty targets at once. The warehouse goes white, then green. |
| Outro | one **dot** in the original bullseye | A drop of rain lands on it. Black. |

**Interactions (three kept):**
1. **Wrong job:** the lasers are the lightning's ground crew, like the people who wave
   planes in. They draw the target, light a runway through the rain, and the bolt lands
   on the mark like a plane.
2. **Callback, sign-off at 3:44 (twice, identical):** the second strike is the exact same
   frame as the first. It's an editorial rule, not a new shot. Nobody comments.
   (`callback-bank.md`: "a cue plays twice identically on purpose.")
3. **Things keep happening:** after every strike, a laser redraws the bullseye a bit
   neater, like it's proud. By the drop the rings are perfect.

**Clip plan (drop, ~20s):**
1. **6s, the second strike.** Reuse the first-strike clip **unchanged**. Don't generate a
   new one.
2. **10s → trim 8, the breakdown.** *Start still:* photoreal abandoned concrete warehouse
   at night, rain falling through four broken skylights, eight thin acid-green laser
   beams forming a hollow cone that points up through the centre skylight, the rain inside
   the cone lit green like falling beads, a three-ring green laser bullseye on the wet
   concrete floor with a fresh scorch mark in the centre, 2.39:1, 35mm, Grok Imagine
   style. *Motion:* the cone slowly spins. Locked camera.
3. **6s, the drop.** A green laser grid draws forty bullseyes across the floor in one
   sweep. Forty white-violet bolts hit all forty at once. The frame flashes white and
   settles green.

**Do-not:** no beams that curve like bolts (lasers stay straight, lightning stays
lightning); no well, no Big City hair, no laundry room; don't explain the moon receipt;
nobody gets struck.

## 3. Ocean boat scene: *The Lighthouse on the Soap Dish*

Matched to [`silly-songs-drawer.md`](silly-songs-drawer.md) #3, *The Bathtub Is the
Ocean* (sea shanty, 100 BPM, ~2:40). The card's drop, lyrics and Pizza Guy rules stay as
written. This only adds a laser layer.

| field | value |
|---|---|
| id | `laser-bathtub-ocean` |
| world | ladies' building unit, bathroom → the open sea (last verse). Same as the card. |
| laugh | **The tiny lighthouse on the soap dish has a real laser, and it measures how deep the bath is. It's deeper every time.** |
| drop | **Falls.** On the third "DEEPER!", the laser sheet that marks the waterline drops straight down through the tub and keeps going, lighting a whole sea floor under the bath, whale included. |
| end reaction | none (the card's Pizza Guy line is the out) |
| promote? | no |

**Laser kit.**
- **Colours:** one emerald beam for the lighthouse, plus one warm-amber sheet for the
  waterline.
- **Emitters:** the lighthouse on the soap dish, 20 cm tall, with one green beam that
  turns.
- **Haze:** bath steam, which is already in the room. When the walls fall away in the last
  verse, the haze becomes sea fog.

**Cue sheet** (timings approximate from the card):

| Section | Laser look | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Verse 1 (a cappella) | none. Candle-dark bathroom, steam | Holding back the lasers makes verse 2 land. |
| Verse 2, "a lighthouse grew on the soap dish" (~0:45) | the lighthouse's one emerald **beam turns** once every two bars | It sweeps across the steam, the tiles and the duck. It passes over Rubella's head, never across her face. |
| Chorus 2 | same beam, plus the duck **steering by it** | Each sweep, the duck turns to follow the light like a ship. |
| Verse 3, the pirate ship (~1:20) | the beam stops and **holds** on the ship's sails | The pirates read their map by it. No cannons, no fighting. |
| Bridge, "How deep is the bath?" ×3 (~1:45) | an amber **sheet** marks the waterline. Each "Deeper!" it **steps down** 30 cm *under* the water, then 3 m, then out of sight | The bath is lit from inside, deeper and deeper. Rubella's knees are still at the surface. |
| **Verse 4, the whale (drop)** | the sheet **falls** all the way | A whole sea floor appears under the tub. The whale's tail rises through the amber light. |
| Final chorus, the building under sail | the lighthouse beam goes **wide** into the fog | It sweeps the sea around the floating building like a real coastline. |
| Outro, three knocks | beam keeps turning | It passes over the rowboat. See interaction 3. |

**Interactions (three kept):**
1. **Wrong scale:** a 20 cm lighthouse with a beam that reaches the horizon once the walls
   fall away.
2. **Things keep happening:** the waterline sheet steps down three times on the gang's three
   "Deeper!"s. It's always deeper than the last time and never touches bottom until the drop.
3. **Callbacks, Pizza Guy at chaos peak + twenty-three percent:** the lighthouse beam
   sweeps over the delivery rowboat. In the one second it's lit, he checks his phone
   (23%), then knocks. He doesn't look at the beam, the whale or the building. The beam
   doesn't stop for him; it keeps its rhythm. Then "Extra napkins are in the bag," as on
   the card.

**Clip plan (drop, ~22s):**
1. **10s → trim 8, the bridge.** *Start still:* photoreal steamy bathroom, Rubella (a
   glamorous, exhausted woman with long dark hair and blood-red lipstick) sitting bored in a
   claw-foot tub, a yellow rubber duck by her knee, a 20 cm white lighthouse on the soap
   dish throwing one thin emerald laser beam through the steam, a flat warm-amber sheet of
   laser light glowing just under the bathwater surface, 2.39:1, 35mm, fine grain, Grok
   Imagine style. *Motion:* the amber sheet steps down twice, lighting the water deeper
   each time, while the green beam turns. Rubella doesn't move.
2. **6s, the drop (falls).** Start = last frame of 1. The amber sheet falls straight down
   out of sight and lights a whole ocean floor far below the tub. A whale's tail as wide as
   the room rises up through the light behind her.
3. **6s, the outro.** Night sea in fog. A small rowboat at the front door of a floating
   apartment building. The emerald beam sweeps over it once. The rower in a delivery
   uniform, pizza box level at chest height, glances at his phone in the light. Locked
   camera.

**Do-not:** all the card's rules still hold (the Pizza Guy is unnamed, unhurried, and says
only the napkin line; the whale is friendly). Lasers never cross Rubella's face. No laser
cannons on the pirate ship.

## 4. Crazy mouse: *The Ride Chases the Dot*

| field | value |
|---|---|
| id | `laser-crazy-mouse` |
| world | a town fair at night, the wild-mouse coaster. One world. Not the Ep3 lawn, not the HOA clubhouse. |
| laugh | **The roller coaster is a mouse, and it chases the laser dot like a cat. The real cat doesn't care.** |
| drop | **Transforms.** The dot jumps off the end of the track into the sky. The mouse car follows it off the rails and runs along a laser beam like a tightrope, all the way round the fair, and lands back where it started. |
| end reaction | **The Black Cat's second look:** dot, rider, dot. She doesn't chase it. She leaves at a walk. |
| promote? | no |

**Laser kit.**
- **Colours:** one red **dot** (the toy), plus magenta and cyan fans for the fair.
- **Emitters:** a projector on top of the ride's sign, plus two on the Ferris wheel hub.
- **Haze:** dust off the midway and the fog machine at the haunted house next door.

**Cue sheet.**

| Section | Laser look | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | one red **dot** on the loading platform | The mouse-shaped car (grey, round ears, whiskers, a tail behind the back seat) twitches its nose at it. |
| Verse 1, the lift hill | dot moves **up the track** ahead of the car | The car climbs *because* it's following the dot. The chain lift is just for show. |
| Chorus, the hairpin turns | the dot zigzags; magenta **scan** on each hairpin | The car takes every corner late, the way a cat skids on a kitchen floor. |
| Verse 2 | cyan **fans** from the Ferris wheel, rotating with it | The dot hides behind a pole. The car stops dead and waits, ears up. |
| Break | everything off except the dot, which **shivers** | Dead silence. The car wiggles its back end like a cat about to pounce. |
| **Drop** | the dot **leaps off the end of the track** into the sky; one red beam stretches from the ride sign to the Ferris wheel | **Transforms:** the car leaves the rails and runs along the beam like a tightrope, all the way around the fair. |
| Last chorus | the beam **bends at the Ferris wheel hub** (two straight beams meeting at a corner, never a curve) and comes back | The car lands back on the platform exactly where it started. The dot stops under its nose. |
| Outro | dot only | The Black Cat. See interaction 3. |

**Interactions (three kept):**
1. **Animals doing the wrong animal's job:** a mouse-shaped ride chases a laser dot.
2. **Calm rider, loud world:** whoever is riding sits upright, bored, holding on with one
   hand. They never look at the dot. Hair and lipstick are untouched after the tightrope lap.
3. **Callback, the Black Cat's second look:** she's sitting by the ticket booth. The dot
   stops on the ground in front of her paws. She looks at the dot, then at the rider,
   then back at the dot. No pounce, no knock, no reaction shot afterwards. She leaves at a
   walk. (Deliberate inversion of `content/cartoons/laser-dot-jury.json`: those cats
   chase; this cat is the one animal in the show who doesn't.)

**Clip plan (drop, ~22s):**
1. **10s → trim 8, the break into the drop.** *Start still:* photoreal small-town fair at
   night, a compact wild-mouse roller coaster with tight hairpin turns, a single grey
   coaster car shaped like a cartoonish mouse with round ears, whiskers and a tail, paused
   at the end of the top track, one red laser dot shivering on the rail just ahead of its
   nose, midway dust and haunted-house fog hanging in the air, a Ferris wheel lit magenta
   and cyan behind, 2.39:1, 35mm, Grok Imagine style. *Motion:* the car wiggles its back
   end like a cat about to pounce.
2. **6s, the drop.** Start = last frame of 1. The dot jumps off the end of the track into
   the night. One straight red laser beam appears from the ride sign to the Ferris wheel
   hub. The mouse car leaves the rails and runs along the beam like a tightrope.
3. **6s, the outro.** Low angle by the ticket booth. A sleek black cat with glowing
   yellow eyes, real animal proportions, sits beside a red laser dot on the asphalt. She
   looks at the dot, then up at the coaster, then back at the dot. She stands and walks
   out of frame. Locked camera.

**Do-not:** the Black Cat never chases, pounces, gets cute, or knocks more than one thing.
She stays in town, so don't move this fair up the ridge. The mouse car is a ride, not a
creature with a face that talks. The beam bends only at a hub, as two straight lines.
Riders never fall and are never in danger.
