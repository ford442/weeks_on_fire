STYLE:

Instrumental heavy metal, 144 BPM, no vocals, no gang chants, no glam screech. This is not Ultra Screech and not a joke commercial. Intro (about 8–10 seconds): clean guitar harmonics, a single well-drip as percussion, distant air in a stone cylinder, almost no kit. Then the drop: drop-A seven-string rhythm, tight palm-muted chug that becomes a descending riff, twin-lead harmony guitars that never sing a lyric, double-kick only after the drop, close snare, triggered kick, dark top end, tight modern metal mix. No stadium reverb, no choir, no breakdown with a spoken line. The riff is written to cadence on the tonic at “the bottom,” but the bottom never arrives — each cycle starts a half-step lower, last bar is the first bar inverted and still falling. Runtime about 90 seconds. Mood: committed, cinematic, no wink. A quiet well, then full metal for the fall.

Full stack. Descending riff in. Double-kick. We are falling.

[0:20–1:20 Cycles]
Five falling cycles. Each starts a half-step lower. Twin-lead answers, never a chorus. No vocal.

[1:20–1:32 Cadence that does not land]
The riff reaches for the tonic and misses. Last bar is the first bar inverted. Still falling. Hard out or a drip leftover — not a fade-out ballad.
