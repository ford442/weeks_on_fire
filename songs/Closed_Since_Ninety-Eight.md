STYLE:

Nocturnal electro-chanson at 84 BPM. Soft muted kick, sine bass, AM-bandwidth coloring kept musical, dashboard glow as the only bright practical, dark top end, tape hiss and a dead carrier as the bed — not live-station static. Radio Voice lead: intimate, slightly worn, never seen, never confirmed to be Continuity Voice, no belting, no pop vibrato. A second female close-mic (Rubella or Lillith, one sister only on the “live” line) sings the present-tense part. **Structural flaw:** the harmony / ghost double is always the other sister’s line from one bar ago — it answers late, with last bar’s words, every phrase, the entire runtime. Do not align the stack. Do not “correct” the delay in the final chorus. Mood: wrong-reflection carpool; the station closed in 1998 and is still in the car. Runtime ~2:10–2:30. Radio Voice on the dead-station lines; one Vale on the live lines; never a sweet unison.

NOTES:

**What the song is**
Scratchpad seed: two strangers (or The Two) leave a house of mirrors arm in arm with each other’s reflections. Neither notices. The car radio plays a station that closed in 1998. The Radio Voice is the third passenger. Never confirm this is the Continuity Voice.

The joke is the delay, not the haunting. Nobody says ghost. Nobody says haunted. The year is a filing system.

**Craft (keep this off-mic)**
- *Static Between Stations* owns leaving the dial **between two live songs**. This is a **dead** station that is still occupying the car.
- *Quarter to Seventy-Five* owns Ep2 dashboard drift and 3:44. Do not freeze a clock here; freeze a **year**.
- *Rinse Cycle* owns sign-off. The intro line “this is not a sign-off” is load-bearing — do not let the arrangement become an anthem.
- *Les Ondes Courtes* owns French-cabaret shortwave costume. Keep this electro-chanson, not accordion.
- Empty Highway Confession may quote the Radio Voice; this track is the **1998 carpool**, not the 4:17 confession bed (*Whatever Lets Us Be* / *Les Ondes Courtes* still own that scene’s diegetic sing-along unless a later pass retargets).

**Production notes**
- **Episode placement:** Standalone night-drive musical cutaway; house-of-mirrors exit into a wet lot. EyeWash not required in frame; the radio is enough.
- **Voice:** Radio Voice (dead-station lines) + one Vale live line. Harmony is always last bar’s lyric.
- **Structural flaw:** Harmony one bar late for the whole runtime. Final chorus still late.
- **Runtime target:** 2:10–2:30.
