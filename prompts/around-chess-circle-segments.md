# Around — chess circle — Imagine segments

Four start/last pairs, no more. Board, game and nuances:
[`ideas/around-chess-circle.md`](../ideas/around-chess-circle.md). Bed: the user's *Around* MiniMax
track (stays on the player). Farm [#22](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/22) · song
[#18](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/18) / [#33](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/33) ·
not [#42](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/42). Worlds do not mix.

**Prompt policy:** written long on purpose, in Monster Mash order: **subject → bodies → job → room
→ light → lens/grade**. Repeat the continuity tokens **word for word** in every prompt. If a
generation comes back mushy, cut room layers first, never the dog's circle or the floor grid.

**Block labels:** `**Grok Imagine**` is the start frame. `**Last frame**` is the matching last frame
for the Imagine start/last pair. `**Motion**` is the image-to-video text.

Shared negative tail (already folded into every prompt below; paste it into any short one):

> no other people, no audience, no readable text, no clock, no windows, no plants, no props beyond
> the table, two chairs and the chess set, no cartoon dog, no toy dog, no Boston Dynamics robot,
> no bandana, the dog never stops or touches the table, no glam, no snow, no field, no neon, no
> marble machine, no HUD, Grok Imagine style

A white queen stands upright off the board at Rubella's right elbow; two black pawns stand off the board at Lillith's right elbow. Behind them, on the far side of the table and slightly out of focus, a small robot poodle, compact chrome chassis, white synthetic poodle-cut panels on head, ears and tail, two amber LED eyes and a thin amber LED collar ring, mid-stride at a fast run, crossing from right to left on a perfect circle about three floor squares out from the table, counter-clockwise seen from above, paying no attention to the board. Lillith's hand is setting a white knight down on the square d5, fingers just releasing it; Rubella sits still, hands flat on the table edge, eyes on the board, mouth closed. Weighted Staunton chess pieces, ivory-white and black lacquer, felt bases: the black king stands on e7, a white bishop on f7, a white knight on e5, a black bishop deep in White's back rank on d1. A white queen stands upright off the board at Rubella's right elbow; two black pawns off the board at Lillith's right elbow. Behind them, out of focus, a small robot poodle, compact chrome chassis, white synthetic poodle-cut panels on head, ears and tail, two amber LED eyes and a thin amber LED collar ring, crossing the far side of the table at a fast run, counter-clockwise seen from above, not looking over. The job of this frame: the last move, made without ceremony. Two plain black bentwood chairs, large empty hall, pale plaster walls, no windows in frame, continuous black-and-white checkerboard floor, high-gloss lacquer, squares about 1.2 metres, perfectly registered to the table board. One soft overhead light pool over the table falling off to dim walls; the dog's amber LEDs leave short streaks of reflection in the lacquer floor. 50mm, locked camera, shallow depth of field on the board and hands, neutral cool grade, faint film grain, no other people, no audience, no readable text, no clock, no windows, no plants, no props beyond the table, two chairs and the chess set, no glow on any piece, no bandana, the dog never stops or touches the table, no glam, no snow, no field, no neon, no marble machine, no HUD, Grok Imagine style
