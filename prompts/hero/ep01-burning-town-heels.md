# H02 · Burning town, heels, not looking back (series signature)

**Shot:** H02
**Episode / beat:** Episode 01 lyric imagery ("we rule this burning town", "watch the ashes fall",
"rain pouring, silver and shadow", "neon light", "the apocalypse here, but we're passing through") ·
[`episodes/episode-01/synopsis.md`](../../episodes/episode-01/synopsis.md) ·
[`characters/the-two.md`](../../characters/the-two.md) (recurring gag: walking away from an explosion, not looking back)
**Why it matters:** the series signature. Catastrophe as a scheduling inconvenience: the town is
on fire, it is raining, and two women in heels walk out of it at exactly their usual pace. This is
the image the show is sold on.
**Locks:** [`the-two-identity`](../locks/the-two-identity.lock.md) ·
[`house-negative`](../locks/house-negative.lock.md)
**Aspect / model:** 16:9 · `grok-imagine-image-quality` (repo default)

**Geography (all three framings):** a narrow old-town street of three- and four-story brick and
stucco facades, wet cobblestones, running straight away from camera. The far end of the street
is on fire. The Two walk down the center of the street **toward camera, away from the fire**, side
by side, Vivienne frame left with a champagne flute, Liliane frame right with the closed leather
notebook held flat against her hip. Neither looks back; their eyes are on something past the lens.
It is raining; ash falls with the rain. Neon signs over shuttered shops buzz and are unreadable.

**Callbacks spent (as image, not dialog):** don't smudge the mascara (rain on skin, liner intact) ·
"watch the ashes fall" (ash with the rain, unbrushed on the shoulders) · heels on every surface
(cobbles, rubble) · the flute she never sets down · the closed notebook · silver, never gold.
