**Laugh:** *Everything on the bouncy castle goes flying, and the singer goes up to the sky and back without messing up her hair.*

## Generate first (locked order)

1. **01 Intro** — sets the castle, the court, the lace and the hair lock that every other clip is judged against. If hair or lace does not land back exactly, stop the batch.
2. **05 Chorus drop** — the first drop (multiplies) and the first chorus close-up with Rubella's mouth on; proves the flat, never-pitched-up lipsync and the crown-on-headstock handoff into 08.
3. **08 Upside down + pigeon** — the kid laugh in its purest form: Lillith calm upside down, mascara flawless, and the pigeon as the only face that reacts.
4. **09 Breakdown** — the only still section and the two spoken lines; the shoe row and the horse in socks are the shots most likely to come back wrong.
5. **11 Final drop** — the transform (the whole castle bounces away over the trees); the hardest physics shot, so find out early.
6. **13 Outro** — the deflate and the one end reaction; it must read different from Ring War 15 before anything else is cut.

Then fill 02, 03, 04, 06, 07, 10, 12 in order.

## Locks

- Kid-silly. Lace Waterfall only: Rubella flat dry vocal (never pitched up, no chipmunk), Lillith on guitar.
- Mouths closed, except Rubella on the chorus hooks and the two spoken breakdown lines. Lillith's mouth is closed in every frame.
- Canon black lace only. No new cast, no tour bio, no venue fact. The royal court (king, queen, knight, jester, pantomime horse) are one-off props for this video.
- Distinct from Bounce House Ring War: no shared shots, no snake band, no pink ring, ropes, streamers, party hall or cartoon switch.
- One kick = one bounce. Hair, lace and mic settle exactly back into place every time. Rubella never looks down.
- End reaction spent once (13b): one downward two-finger brush of vinyl off her lace.
- Worlds do not mix: no Glam bottle, no Daisy field, no night-lot, no Annex wings, no Christina house.
- Mute Imagine audio. Retime in the edit; never prompt slow motion.
