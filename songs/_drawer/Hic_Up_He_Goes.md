# Hic, Up He Goes — drawer sketch

**Drawer.** `promote?: no`. Not in `songs/` proper, not codegen'd, no `songId` bound.

## Video first

**Sits on:** `content/cartoons/hiccup-frog.json` (cartoon seed, status `sketched`, ~6–8s).
The seed already says "three build hits and a drop" — this is that track, stretched to a
20-second, two-clip edit.

**Deliberate variation from the seed:** the frog's face is **bored and flat**, not puffed and
alarmed. He holds his belly like he is waiting for a bus. The geese are the loud part.

`laugh:` His hiccups are so big he bonks a goose out of the sky.
`drop:` **Falls.** At 0:15.0 the goose spins out of the V (distant honk-bonk, feathers), and
the frog falls back into frame onto his lily pad.
`end reaction:` the frog, one tiny last hic at 0:19.0, a two-inch hop. Spent once.
`do-not:` let the frog look worried or at camera; hurt the goose (dizzy, stars, fine); add a
second animal on the pond; put words on screen.
`promote?:` no.

- **Target length:** 20.0s. 96 BPM, 4/4, 2.5s per bar, 8 bars. Song length = picture length.
- **Clips:** Clip A, **10s, used whole** (0:00–0:10): frog on pad, hic, hic, HIC — out of frame

- **Pattern:** hard hits on action beats. Each hiccup is a bar downbeat (0:05.0, 0:07.5,
  0:10.0); each one is a bigger hop and a bigger steel-pan hit.
- **Breaks:** (1) **written silence, 0:12.5–0:15.0, 2.5 seconds, unscored** — empty pond,
  ripples, one dragonfly; music fully out; (2) music back at 0:15.0 on the bonk; (3) out again
  at 0:18.75 so the last tiny hic is heard alone.
- **Mouths:** **off** for every animal. The singer is an off-screen calypso voice. The frog's
  mouth opens only to hic (a bubble pops).
- **Diegetic sound:** three hiccups (small, bigger, huge — the frog's own, picture sound,
  sitting on top of the pan hits), lily-pad slap, pond ripples, distant honk + bonk, falling
  whistle, wet *plap* landing, the tiny final hic.
