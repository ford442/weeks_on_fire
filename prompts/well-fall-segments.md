Fill the bank in the idea file, then replace `[SIGHT]` here.

## Visual arc

Stone well mouth → we fall → six open sights on the way down → bottom or none.

## Continuity lock (paste into every take)

POV falling down a circular masonry well. Wet brick or fieldstone. One moving
system: the fall. A shrinking disk of sky or porch light when we look up.
Photoreal cinematic, 2.39:1 anamorphic, crushed blacks. No faces unless a
sight names them. No HUD, no titles, no lyrics, no band in the well, no marble
cabinet, no Daisy bicycle, no mountain lion, no Glam bottle, no Riley Annex.

The well is silent. Metal is non-diegetic.

## A — Lip of the Well (0:00–0:10)

**Start still:** stone / brick well ring, dark cylinder, dry hills or a backyard
that forgot it had a well.  
**Motion:** we step / we go; the mouth becomes a tunnel.  
**Sound:** clean harmonics, one drip. No kick yet.

**Grok Imagine**
```
Cinematic 2.39:1 photoreal still at the lip of an old stone well in dry California hills, circular brick and fieldstone ring, dark cylinder dropping out of frame, a pale disk of sky or porch light on the water far below or none at all, no people, no text, crushed blacks, 35mm anamorphic, short-film production still, Grok Imagine style
```

**Gemini Omni**
```
10-second photoreal cinematic video at the mouth of an old stone well. Camera holds the ring, then tips and commits to the fall so the circular masonry becomes a tunnel. One drip. Quiet. No people, no titles, no band. Photoreal short film, 2.39:1.
```

**Prompt variations**
- Looking straight down the cylinder from the lip, sky as a thin ring, Grok Imagine
- Wide: well in a dry backyard, HOA fence distant, mouth as a black coin, Grok Imagine
