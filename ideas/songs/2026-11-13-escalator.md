# Escalator

**Genre collision:** 1990s dream trance (rolling piano lead, warm pads, offbeat bass) × department-store easy-listening muzak (vibraphone, soft flute, nylon-string bossa comp, the two-tone store chime).

**Visual concept:** Rubella stands on the right of an up escalator in a bright shopping mall, holding a vintage mic. Lillith rides one step below her with a guitar and a small amp on the step. Neither of them moves their feet, and the escalator never ends. It carries them past the perfume floor, the shoes, a floor of nothing but lit lamps, then through the glass roof into clouds, past a flock of geese, a jet, the stars and the moon. In space the Pizza Guy rides the down escalator next to them without looking over. At the top they step off and are on the ground floor of the same mall, at the bottom of the same escalator.

**Laugh (kid words):** *They ride the escalator so far it goes to space, and when they get off at the top they're back at the bottom.*

**BPM:** 140, A minor (chorus lifts to C major)

**Target length:** ~2:38 · 13 segments
