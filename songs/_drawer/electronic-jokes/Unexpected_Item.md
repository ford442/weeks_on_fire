STYLE:

Sincere early-2000s electroclash, 126 BPM, E minor, about two and a half minutes. Cheap, punchy drum machine with a dry clap, a buzzing distorted analog bassline, cold detuned synth stabs, a robotic arpeggio, and a fashion-runway attitude. Lead one: dry, slightly raspy, completely deadpan, half-spoken in a bored monotone, cool and unimpressed. Second voice: a robotic vocoded female machine voice, polite and insistent, singing short lines in a rigid melody. A third voice appears in the last verse: bright, conspiratorial, delighted. Each chorus ends with a two-tone error beep used as a musical hook, the same two notes every time. Minimal, sleek, sincere, no parody, no other sound effects. Use Rubella and Lillith voices and one vocoded machine voice.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
MACHINE: Please scan your first item.

[Verse 1]
RUBELLA: A banana. I scanned it. It's yellow.
MACHINE: Unexpected item in the bagging area.
RUBELLA: It's the banana.
MACHINE: Unexpected item.
RUBELLA: It's expected. I expected it.

[Chorus]
MACHINE: Unexpected item
RUBELLA: I'm not surprised
MACHINE: Unexpected item
RUBELLA: Nothing surprises me

[Verse 2]
RUBELLA: A loaf of bread. I scanned it. It's bread.
MACHINE: Unexpected item in the bagging area.
RUBELLA: There's a cat in the bag.
MACHINE: Unexpected item.
RUBELLA: That's not mine. That was unexpected.

[Chorus]
MACHINE: Unexpected item
RUBELLA: I'm not surprised
MACHINE: Unexpected item
RUBELLA: Nothing surprises me

[Verse 3]
RUBELLA: I scanned the milk.
MACHINE: Unexpected item in the bagging area.
LILLITH: Hi.
RUBELLA: Hi.
MACHINE: Unexpected item.
RUBELLA: She's expected. She's always expected.

[Bridge]
MACHINE: Please wait for assistance.
RUBELLA: I am the assistance.

[Final Chorus]
MACHINE: Unexpected item
RUBELLA: I'm not surprised
MACHINE: Unexpected item
LILLITH: I'm in the bag!
RUBELLA: Nothing surprises me

NOTES:

**Rule of three, wrong third:** a banana (the machine is wrong), a cat (the machine is right,
and Rubella's mildly surprised for once), then Lillith sitting in the bagging area (the
machine is technically right, and Rubella isn't surprised at all).

**The cat** is any cat, sitting in a shopping bag, never the Black Cat. It leaves on its own.

**Do-not:** no real supermarket name or logo. The machine's line is generic; don't copy any
real brand's exact voice prompt beyond "unexpected item in the bagging area."
