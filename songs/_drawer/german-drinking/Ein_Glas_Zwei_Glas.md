German party Schlager counting song, 104 BPM, major key. Accordion lead, tuba bass, brass hits on every number, simple oom-pah drums, male lead vocal, crowd shouting the numbers, festive Oktoberfest band, traditional instruments, no electronic production. About 1:45. Simple German. Sing only the lines below. No character names, no product.
