STYLE:

Broadcast dream-pop / test-pattern shoegaze, 84 BPM, E major that keeps slipping down a half step into E-flat on every "slide" and then climbing back as if nothing happened. Washed-out guitars in tremolo and reverse reverb, a 1 kHz test tone held under the intro and hidden inside the pad for the rest of the track, soft tape-flutter drum machine, sub bass felt not heard. Vocals: one female voice, breathy, close, far away in the same take — like a CRT in the next room. A second voice (the Radio Voice) is processed as if between stations: AM band-limited, slightly off-pitch, only on the bridge. Chorus adds a wall of hazy guitar but the vocal stays small. Mood: the picture changes before the sound does; the couch is the same, the world is not. No breakdown, no build, no chorus louder than the last. Ends on the test tone alone, then the tone drops a half step and cuts.

NOTES:

## Production notes
- **Episode placement:** EyeWash station-break that runs across an actual channel slide — any moment where a character has EyeWash on in the background and the show changes without anyone touching it. Sister to *Static Between Stations* and *This Program Contains Dialogue*; this one is the slide itself, not the static.
- **Emotional arc:** Domestic and calm → the room stays, the world doesn't → the Radio Voice is the only one who admits it's a broadcast → final chorus accepts the new room.
- **Characters:** Unnamed viewer voice (Rubella-compatible, small). The Radio Voice on the bridge only. The Two appear on-screen inside the TV, never in the room.
- **Structural flaw:** the half-step slip on every "slide" should be a real key drop, not a pitch bend; if Minimax smooths it, keep the take with the worst seam.
- **Runtime target:** ~3:00. Trim to 20s (intro + one chorus) as a hard channel-slide sting.
- **Reference energy:** Cocteau Twins as a test card; Beach House at 3 AM on a dead channel; Broadcast, the band, obviously.

## Music video treatment
One locked shot of a living room. The CRT in the corner slides through EyeWash blocks (talk show with The Two, desert porch light, the Ultra-Refreshed Water Lab). Each slide, one object in the room is different — a lamp, a rug, the window's time of day — and the viewer never looks away from the screen. On the outro the room is empty and the TV is still on.
