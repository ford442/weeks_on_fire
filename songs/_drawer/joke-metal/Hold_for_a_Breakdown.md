STYLE:

Warm joke metal, 96 BPM, B mixolydian, about one minute forty-five. One riff on a warm amp, standard tuning, dry kit, no scream, no growl. Verses are two dry voices reading a cue out loud, in time, calm, not shouted: the column says four, the picture stays up. They are not a named singer and not a session contralto. Gang vocals on the chorus only, clean, the title and nothing else: Hold for a breakdown. The break is eight bars of that same riff at the same volume. No half-time, no mute, no dump, no drop, no bend, no feedback hold. Structural flaw: the lyric says four and the break holds eight, and the band does not shorten it. Sing only the lines below. No djent, no dropped tuning, no blast beat, no belting, no male guest, no product name. Mood: the cue is the law and the bar count is not. Runtime about 1:45.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: the breakdown holds eight and the lyric says four. The break is not cut to four. The lyric is not changed to meet the eight. The hold is a cue, not a bent note.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not Hold for Eight.** That count climbs and restarts before eight, in dry downtempo. This lyric never counts. It says four, and the metal holds eight.
- **Not Hold the Note.** That hold is a long bend on Lace, two beats past the band. This hold is eight bars of a riff that refuses the drop. Nobody bends.
- **Not Ultra Screech, not Well Fall, not Halloween Snake Battle.** No product, no fall, no lawn.
- **Not the Lace pack.** Heavy Water, Open Gate, Hold the Note, Second Sun, Lace Waterfall, and Through the Lace stay where they are.

The reader of the cue is not on the song. No name, no desk, no mic credit.
