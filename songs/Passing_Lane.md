STYLE:

Outlaw country chase song, advertised as 140 BPM, performed at 48 BPM, E major. Deadpan baritone lead (professionally proud, zero wink, not Pizza Guy). Telecaster with slight slapback, pedal steel that takes four bars to finish a two-note phrase, upright bass on one and three only. A click track at 140 BPM is audible in the headphones and leaks into the left side of the mix; the band ignores it. **Structural flaw:** the kick is one beat every two bars. A distant skeleton car-horn sits on the 140 grid and never desyncs from the click; it is the only on-time performer. A drum fill is counted off at the end of every chorus and never arrives because the fill would take four bars at this tempo. Mood: left lane, slime trail, cobwebs on the bumpers, a checkered flag that has not moved. Runtime ~1:45–2:05. Do not use Rubella. Do not swing it into a waltz to be clever.

NOTES:

## What the song is
A chase song whose hero is a snail in the passing lane. He is not embarrassed. He has the correct equipment. The skeleton in the convertible has been honking since Episode 3 and is still on the clock; that is respect, not a complaint. The cobwebs are a time signature.

The joke is the tempo. Nobody says "snail's pace." Nobody asks him to move.

## Craft (keep this off-mic)
- *Pepperoni or Margherita* owns professional-neutral delivery. This baritone is proud, not calm, and he is not bringing food.
- *Quarter to Seventy-Five* owns the night-drive that will not arrive. This is a **daylight highway** and a pass that will not complete.
- *Article Seven* owns the skeleton as steward. Here the skeleton is a driver. Same bones, different local — do not give him a laminated card.
- Pairing still: [`content/cartoons/snail-passing-lane.json`](../content/cartoons/snail-passing-lane.json). Cobwebs and the unused checkered flag have to read; if it is only a cute snail the song is pointing at the wrong picture.

## Production notes
- **Episode placement:** EyeWash cartoon block / unmotivated highway cutaway. Drop after anyone says they will just pass them.
- **Voice:** Deadpan proud baritone. Skeleton horn as the only 140 BPM instrument. No backup singers.
- **Structural flaw:** click at 140, band at 48, kick every two bars, drum fill counted and never played. Do not "help" them meet.
- **Runtime target:** 1:45–2:05.
