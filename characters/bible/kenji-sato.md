## Logline bio

Kenji "Shred" Sato is the official face of Ultra Screech Formula and a proud alumnus of
Glam-Sham-Poo College, Class of Loud. He is always mid-pitch, and the noise meter is always
already in the red. He sells whichever of the two is in front of him, the product or the
school, and has never noticed that they are different things. **He is not a resident of
anywhere in this show.** He lives inside one commercial and visits the rest of the series as
a four-second interruption.

## Voice guide

- **Sentence length:** three to nine words. Spoken tagline rhythm, with a hard stop and a held grin.
- **Register:** spoken hype over guitars. A sincere salesman voice, warm, with no irony anywhere in it. He is gang-vocal-adjacent on "GLAM-SHAM-POO!" and nowhere else. **He does not sing a verse, ever.**
- **Vocabulary he overuses:** *Glamora* · *formula* · *approved* · *Class of Loud* · *available* · *succeeding* · *authorized* · *alumni* · *your building*. Every sentence wants to end on a product noun.
- **Vocabulary he refuses:** *quiet* (see backstory) · *sorry* · *side effects* · *tired* · any number below the meter's red line. He has never said "maybe" on camera.
- **Humor type:** **sincerity at the wrong volume.** He never makes a joke. The joke is that every line is a perfectly good line for the other product.
- **The drift:** the signature move. A pitch for the shampoo slides, with no audible gear change, into a pitch for the college: *"Available in your building's vending machine… and in our admissions office. Applications still open."* He does not hear it. Nobody corrects him.
- **Established lines (do not rewrite):**
  - "Konichiwa… from the neon temples of Glamora!"
  - "But the hair… is succeeding!"
  - "Available in your building's vending machine…"
  - "Approved by the Glam-Sham-Poo College Class of Loud."
  - "Heavy metal hair detox. Split ends become power chords."
  - *(accidentally)* "Applications still open."
  - *(Big City, both tags, use the take)* "The hair is in big city man."

### Sample monologue: "Campus Tour" (6–10 lines; walking backwards, meter in the red)

For the handbook §12 insert or an EyeWash *Continuing Education* block. He is giving a tour and an ad at the same time and believes they are one thing.

> On your left: the chemistry building. Fume hoods. Respect.
> On your right: the dorms. Better pyrotechnics. *More* respect.
> Student handbook, section twelve, Authorized Volume. Four pages. One is scorched.
> (the meter climbs; he taps it, delighted)
> We don't measure success here. Success measures *us.*
> Ultra Screech was born in that lab. Ultra Screech was raised in those dorms.
> Freshman Orientation Size is available at move-in, right next to the meal plans.
> Split ends become power chords. Undeclared majors become *declared.*
> (beat; to the lens, sincere)
> Applications still open.

### Sample monologue: "The Quiet Formula" (the closest he gets to a sad scene; four seconds of air, then the riff)

He passes the vending machine. One slot is taped over with a handwritten sign. He doesn't stop walking, but he slows down.

> That slot was the Quiet Formula.
> Discontinued. Nobody will say by whom.
> (he touches the tape, once)
> It was a good formula. It just didn't… announce itself.
> (beat; the meter, for one frame, dips out of the red)
> (then the riff, and he is back, louder than before)
> But the hair… is succeeding!

### Sample exchange: with Rubella (the register: the commercial as hostage)

From the laundry room the shampoo company rented. She is in her own building and did not agree to the decibel.

> **KENJI:** Rubella! How does your hair feel?
> **RUBELLA:** *(flat)* Loud.
> **KENJI:** Loud!
> **RUBELLA:** It has never been quieter, either.
> **KENJI:** *(to the lens, thrilled)* Never been quieter, either!
> **RUBELLA:** That wasn't an endorsement.
> **KENJI:** Everything is an endorsement. Alumni immunity.
> **RUBELLA:** I'm not an alumna.
> **KENJI:** *(beat; genuinely)* Applications still open.
> **RUBELLA:** *(under, fading)* Please stop.

### Sample exchange: with the Pizza Guy (the register: maximum pitch vs. the control group)

The laundry-room TV is running the ad. The Pizza Guy is in front of it with the bag at chest height. Kenji is only on the screen. Neither of them knows the other is there.

> **KENJI (ON TV):** Konichiwa… from the neon temples of Glamora!
> *(The Pizza Guy checks his phone. 23%.)*
> **KENJI (ON TV):** Heavy metal hair detox!
> *(The Pizza Guy does not look up.)*
> **KENJI (ON TV):** Split ends become power chords!
> **PIZZA GUY:** *(to someone off, who has arrived)* Order for… Rebella?
> **KENJI (ON TV):** Available in your building's vending machine!
> **PIZZA GUY:** Extra napkins are in the bag.
> *(He leaves. The ad keeps going for nobody. The meter stays in the red.)*

### Sample exchange: with Lillith (the register: two people who both want it louder)

*Big City* club stage, between tags. Lillith is the only person in the series who matches him for volume, and she does it for completely different reasons.

> **LILLITH:** Can the hair go higher?
> **KENJI:** The hair can always go higher. Section twelve.
> **LILLITH:** What's the ceiling?
> **KENJI:** *(pointing up, delighted)* There is a ceiling.
> **LILLITH:** I'll take it out.
> **KENJI:** *(to the lens)* Approved!
> **RUBELLA:** *(off, one stop down)* It is load-bearing.

## Backstory (committed)

- **Origin.** Glam-Sham-Poo College, Class of Loud. He majored in chemistry for one semester and pyrotechnics for three, because the dorms had better pyro than the labs and he went where the volume was. The college and the shampoo company share a founder, a logo, a building and a legal department, and **Kenji's ambassador contract was signed with "Glam-Sham-Poo," full stop.** That is why he cannot tell the product from the school: legally, nobody can. He is not confused. He is accurately describing a corporate structure nobody else has read.
- **Formative incident (series-relevant).** As a sophomore he was in the focus group for the **Quiet Formula**. He was the only "yes." The formula was banned the following term, and nobody has ever said by whom. Kenji has never mentioned the vote. The taped-over slot in every vending machine in the series is, as far as he's concerned, his.
- **Why he's never tired.** It is not product. It is not a joke about stimulants. It is the meter: he has spent so long at a volume the handbook calls *authorized* that the red line is now his resting state. Silence would take effort.
- **Alumni immunity.** He sincerely believes Glam-Sham-Poo alumni cannot be cited under any HOA noise ordinance. The HOA has never agreed to this and has never successfully argued it either. He has never been cited, which he considers proof.
- **Secret he protects.** **He has never been to any of the buildings with the vending machine.** He films everything in Glamora. "Available in your building" is the warmest thing he says, and he says it about a place he has never seen.
- **What would break him on screen.** The meter dropping to zero **while he is still in frame.** It is fine when the ad ends and the meter drops a beat late with nobody in the shot. That is the established joke. If we ever see his face in the silence after the music stops, the show is over for him. Use it once at most, and then cut to the sting.

## Arc hooks

1. **"The Sting"** *(any episode; already licensed)*: the 4-second "GLAM-SHAM-POO!" cut from the final chorus interrupts a scene that does not want it, such as the HOA minutes, the elevator or a 4 AM station log. Kenji's face is on screen for eight frames, and nobody in the interrupted scene reacts. This is how he appears in the series from now on. **It is a recurring interrupt, not a second ad** (kill list, `ideas/short-packet-farm.md`).
2. **"Authorized Volume"**: an EyeWash *Continuing Education* block. Kenji reads handbook §12 aloud, all four pages, over dive bombs, as a public-service lecture. The Continuity Voice introduces it and does not return after it. Thirty seconds. Use the "Campus Tour" monologue.
3. **"Citation"** *(a reference, not a set)*: Karen's clipboard, in the HOA material, has a noise citation pinned to it addressed to *GLAM-SHAM-POO (ALUMNI)*, marked RETURNED — IMMUNITY CLAIMED. It is never read aloud. Kenji never enters the clubhouse. *(Worlds do not mix: the callback crosses lanes as a card, not a body.)*
4. **"The Quiet Formula"**: a ninety-second commercial for the banned variant, made by somebody else, that EyeWash airs once at 4 AM. Kenji is not in it, and the absence is the joke. The S&P Skeleton stamps it PROBABLY FINE. Oz logs it as *unscheduled*. Never explained.
5. **"Telethon Pledge"**: the EyeWash telethon (`ideas/eyewash-station.md`). Kenji's sting fires mid-telethon, and the tote board goes **down** by exactly the price of a Freshman Orientation Size. Nobody connects them.

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Keep him mid-pitch. Every shot of Kenji is a shot of somebody halfway through a tagline.
- Let him drift from the shampoo to the college without a seam, and let nobody correct him.
- Keep the bottle at his side. The product is furniture; Kenji is the commercial.
- Let the people around him (Rubella, salarymen, the Pizza Guy) be the reaction, or the lack of one.
- Let the meter drop late, after the music, with nobody in frame.

**Don't**

- **Don't make his accent, his English, or his nationality the joke.** The joke is a salesman who sincerely can't tell a product from a school. "Konichiwa… from the neon temples of Glamora!" is the ad agency's opening line and he delivers it with full commitment, exactly as he delivers every other line the agency wrote. Give him no broken-English lines and do no "funny foreign" timing.
- Don't make him a resident. He is never in the building, the HOA, the ridge, or the lawn **in person**. He arrives as a screen, a sting, a card, or a citation.
- Don't let him sing a verse, and don't put him in Lace Waterfall.
- Don't let him get tired, sad, or quiet, except the single "Quiet Formula" beat, which lasts four seconds of air.
- Don't make a second Ultra Screech commercial. One ad, one sting, forever.
- Don't put the bottle in the well, the HOA, or the space lounge. (`ideas/big-city-cutaway.md` and `ideas/short-packet-farm.md` lane rules.)
- Don't let anybody pitch Christina's gown. He's thrown at the door ([`relationship-map.md`](relationship-map.md#christina-hill--who-does-not-visit-this-week)).
