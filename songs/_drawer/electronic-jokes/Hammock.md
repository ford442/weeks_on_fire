STYLE:

Sincere mid-2010s tropical house, 102 BPM, D major, about two and a half minutes. Soft plucked marimba and steel-pan melody, a mellow four-on-the-floor kick, finger snaps, a warm sub bass, airy pads, a breathy pan-flute hook in the drop, and lush summery reverb. Lead one: bright, conspiratorial, dreamy and breathy, singing a blissful summer song. Lead two: dry, slightly raspy, flat, one short spoken line right after each drop starts. On the first beat of every drop, there is a quick spinning whoosh and a soft thud into sand, then the pan-flute hook plays on calmly. Breezy, sunny, glossy, sincere, no parody, no other comedy sound effects. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
LILLITH: Okay. Relaxing.

[Verse 1]
LILLITH: Swaying in the summer breeze
One leg in, and now I'm in, nearly
Sun is going down like honey
Palm trees swaying, so am I

[Pre-Chorus]
LILLITH: Lie back, lie back, let the evening come
Lie back, lie back

[Drop 1]
RUBELLA: You're in the sand.

[Verse 2]
LILLITH: Second try, I'm feeling it
Hold the rope, and sit down slowly
Spread my weight across the middle
Lift my feet up, and I'm floating

[Pre-Chorus]
LILLITH: Lie back, lie back, let the evening come
Lie back, lie back

[Drop 2]
RUBELLA: Still in the sand.

[Bridge]
LILLITH: Maybe if I get in sideways
Maybe if I take a run up
Maybe if the hammock trusts me
RUBELLA: It doesn't.

[Pre-Chorus]
LILLITH: Lie back, lie back, let the evening come
Lie back, lie back

[Drop 3]
RUBELLA: There's room in mine.

[Outro]
LILLITH: Oh, this is nice.

NOTES:

**The joke:** tropical house is the genre of lying perfectly still on a beach. Every drop
flips Lillith out of the hammock (the whoosh and thud are written into the STYLE). The third
drop is the turn: Rubella offers room in hers, and the song ends with both in one hammock,
finally relaxed.

**Do-not:** no real resort or drink brand. Nobody gets hurt: it's soft sand. Not the yacht
(a different world).
