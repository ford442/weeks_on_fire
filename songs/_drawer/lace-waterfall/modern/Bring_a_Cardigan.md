STYLE:

Sincere symphonic power metal anthem, 180 BPM, E minor verses lifting into a triumphant G major chorus, about two minutes forty. Galloping double-kick drums, twin harmonised lead guitars, fast palm-muted rhythm guitar, a full orchestral layer with soaring strings, brass fanfares and timpani, and a huge mixed choir chanting the hook in Latin-style unison. Lead one: bright, conspiratorial, soaring operatic belt with sudden energy spikes, completely sincere about the quest. Lead two: dry, slightly raspy, flat, speaking short practical lines in the gaps as if reading a weather app. A neoclassical guitar solo, a half-time breakdown where the choir whispers, and a key change up a whole step into the final chorus. Huge cinematic production, no parody, no comedy sound effects. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.
