## Video first

**Sits on:** `content/cartoons/egg-xylophone.json` (cartoon seed, status `sketched`, ~6–8s).
The seed is the picture; this sketch stretches it to a 16-second, two-clip edit.

**Deliberate variation from the seed:** the hen's **face stays flat** the whole time (she
is the calm thing in frame); only her feathers blow back. The cow and pig do the covering
of ears. That keeps rule 1.

`laugh:` The hen lays a song, and the last egg is so big it plays the tuba.
`drop:` **Falls.** At 0:10.0 the ostrich egg falls into the tuba bell: BWAAAMP, every
feather on the hen blows straight back.
`end reaction:` none from the hen. The pig uncovers one ear at 0:15.5 (spent once).
`do-not:` let the hen panic, scream, or look at camera; put any words on screen; add a
farmer; let the ostrich appear (only the egg).
`promote?:` no.

## Video use map

- **Target length:** 16.0s. 120 BPM, 4/4, 2.0s per bar, 8 bars. Song length = picture length.
- **Clips:** Clip A, **10s, used whole** (0:00–0:10): hen on stool, four eggs roll down the
  xylophone, the giant egg wobbles out. Clip B, **6s, used whole** (0:10–0:16): the egg drops
  into the tuba, feathers blow back, feathers float down. Chain: the last frame of A (giant
  egg falling, one inch above the tuba bell) is the start still of B.
- **Pattern:** hard hits on action beats. One egg = one plink = one sung number. The song
  is a counting song and the picture is the count.
- **Breaks:** (1) bar 5, 0:08.0–0:10.0, **full stop**, two seconds of barnyard room tone while
  the giant egg wobbles; (2) after the drop, the band is gone and only the tuba note rings;
  (3) 0:15.0–0:16.0 silence except one folding-chair creak where applause would go.
- **Mouths:** **off** for every animal. The hen does not sing. The voice is an off-screen
  narrator. Beaks closed except to lay.
- **Diegetic sound:** four egg plinks (the xylophone notes are the picture's own sound; the
  editor pitches them to C, D, E, G of the track), a cluck at 0:00.5, a wobble creak at 0:09,
  the BWAAAMP (picture tuba, doubled by the track), feather flutter, the chair creak.
