STYLE:

Sincere early-2010s dubstep, 140 BPM in half-time, F minor, about two minutes twenty. Big cinematic intro with a soft piano and a sweet vocal, then long builds with snare rolls, white-noise risers and a slurping sound used as a rhythmic riser. Every drop is a massive wobble bass with growls and screeches, a heavy half-time kick and snare, and a frozen crystalline shimmer layered on top. Lead one: bright, conspiratorial, excited, quick, singing the builds. Lead two: dry, slightly raspy, flat, speaking a short warning right before every drop. Each drop ends in a sudden stop with a soft "ow" from lead one, then the piano returns. Huge, aggressive, glossy, sincere, no parody, no comedy sound effects except the slurp. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
LILLITH: Blue raspberry. Large.
RUBELLA: That's a bucket.

[Build 1]
LILLITH: One little sip, one little sip
Cold on the tongue and it's sweet on the lip
One little sip, one more sip, one more
RUBELLA: Slow down.

[Drop 1]

[Verse]
LILLITH: Ow.
The sky's gone blue and the boardwalk's spinning
The seagulls are singing a song in my teeth
My forehead is a snowman, my eyebrows are icicles
RUBELLA: Press your tongue to the roof of your mouth.

[Build 2]
LILLITH: One little sip, one little sip
Cold on the tongue and it's sweet on the lip
One little sip, one more sip, one more
RUBELLA: Slow down.

[Drop 2]

[Bridge]
LILLITH: Ow.
I can see through time.
RUBELLA: You can see the bottom of the cup.

[Build 3]
LILLITH: One last sip
RUBELLA: It's empty.
LILLITH: One last sip

[Drop 3]

[Outro]
LILLITH: Ow.
RUBELLA: Medium next time.

NOTES:

**The joke:** dubstep's drop is a brain freeze. Each one ends with a tiny "ow." Rubella's
tip in verse 1 (tongue to the roof of the mouth) is a real one, and it doesn't help.

**Picture rule:** Lillith's face barely moves on the drops. The world wobbles, frosts and
shakes around her.

**Do-not:** no real slush brand or cup logo.
