# H12 · 2 AM laundromat slow dance (musical cutaway)

**Shot:** H12
**Episode / beat:** Episode 02 extension, *2AM Laundromat Slow Dance*, Beat D–E (the dance) ·
[`notes/scenes/laundromat-slow-dance.md`](../../notes/scenes/laundromat-slow-dance.md) ·

**Why it matters:** the tenderest frame in the series, and the proof that the show can hold a
picture with nobody on fire. Two strangers almost tell the truth; the room says it for them. The
attendant mops around them and never looks up. It must look like the only room in town that is
lit up and doesn't want anything.
**Locks:** [`eyewash-master-control`](../locks/eyewash-master-control.lock.md) (`screen`) ·
[`house-negative`](../locks/house-negative.lock.md)
**Aspect / model:** 16:9 · `grok-imagine-image-quality` (repo default)

**Geography (all three framings):** twelve top-loading washers in a row on the left, eight
front-loading dryers stacked two-high on the right, one aisle of wet grey tile between them, big
plate-glass front windows at the far end with rain running down them. Seven dryers are dark; one,
the off-balance one, is running warm at aisle level. The corner TV hangs on a bracket high in the
back corner and nobody watches it. The two dance in the aisle beside the running dryer; the
attendant works mid-ground, head down. Light: harsh fluorescent tubes above, warm dryer glow
below, sodium-orange streetlight through the wet glass, a small grey-blue CRT patch high up.

**Naming rule (binding):** the two are **never named**: not in the prompt, not in a slate, not in
a filename. Prompts call them "a woman" and "a man". The attendant is "a night attendant".

**Non-lock cast, identical wording in every framing they appear in:**
HER = "a woman in her mid-thirties in a long charcoal wool coat over a black sequined dress that
was formal eight hours ago, a fine scatter of leftover Halloween glitter on one cheekbone" ·
HIM = "a man in his forties in a wrinkled navy work uniform shirt and trousers, the name patch
over the pocket turned inward so no name shows" ·
ATTENDANT = "a night attendant in his late forties in a fluorescent hi-vis vest over a plain grey
long-sleeve shirt, over-ear headphones with the left ear pushed off, head down over a string mop,
his eyeline never visible".

**Callbacks spent (as image, not dialog):** the off-balance dryer (the percussion bed) · the
attendant mops around people and never asks them to move · glitter that refuses to come off ·
EyeWash always on somewhere (the corner TV, 2 AM block) · the patch we can't read.
