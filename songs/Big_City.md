STYLE:

Sincere late-80s glam metal strut, 126 BPM, A major. Open bright rhythm guitar riff, big gated snare, one cowbell used straight, heavy lead guitar with wide vibrato and a single dive bomb at the last chorus exit. Neon synth sits underneath the guitars, never on top. Anthemic gang vocals chanting "Big City" only — no product name, no "Glam-Sham-Poo," no comedy samples, no Japanese salaryman shouts. Two female vocals: one brighter and committed on verse 1 and the pre-chorus, one dry and slightly raspy on verse 2 and the bridge. One male spoken line only, twice, same sentence both times, not sold, not announced. High-energy, earnest glam about hair as a city skyline. Use Lillith, Rubella, and Kenji voices.
