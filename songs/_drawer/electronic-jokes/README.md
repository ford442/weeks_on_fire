# Electronic joke songs — modern genres, Rubella and Lillith

*Drawer. Text only. 2026-11-08. `promote?: no` on every song. Not codegen'd.*

Ten joke songs in post-2000 electronic, pop and Euro genres, each played completely
sincerely. The jokes come three ways: **the whole subject** (a festival anthem about a robot
vacuum), **wordplay** (2-step / the second step; minimal / less; the hardstyle kick), and **a
serious song with a joke tone** (a silky garage love song to a creaky stair). Lillith goes
all in. Rubella stays flat inside the noise.
