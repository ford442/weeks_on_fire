# Last Down the Slide — drawer sketch

**Drawer.** `promote?: no`. Not in `songs/` proper, not codegen'd, no `songId` bound.
Register: **kid-silly**. New premise for a song (not Egg Xylophone, Hic or Eight Arms).

## Video first

**Sits on:** `content/cartoons/walrus-cork.json` ("Last Down the Slide", cartoon seed,
status `sketched`, ~6–8s). The seed already says "sync POP to the drop." This is that
track, stretched to a 20-second, two-clip edit.

**Deliberate variation from the seed:** the walrus's face is **calm and pleased**, not
eyes-popping. He waits in the slide like he is in a warm bath. The slide creaks, bulges and
wobbles; the penguins and the seagull are the worried ones. Same seagull lifeguard as the
seed; not the goose lifeguard of `Whistle_Twice.md` (different world, no goose here).

`laugh:` The walrus is so big that when he pops out of the slide, all the water jumps out of the pool.
`drop:` **Falls.** At 0:10.0 the walrus pops out like a cork, falls into the pool, and the whole pool leaves in one giant splash.
`end reaction:` the seagull lifeguard, at 0:19.0, slowly lowers his whistle and pulls his visor down over his eyes. Spent once.
`do-not:` let the walrus look scared, hurt or at camera; hurt any penguin (they end up sitting on the dry pool floor, fine); add a sign or any words; add a second big animal.
`promote?:` no.

## Video use map

- **Target length:** 20.0s. 144 BPM, 4/4, one bar = 1.667s, 12 bars. Song length = picture length.
- **Clips (Grok Imagine, chained):** Clip A, **10s, used whole** (0:00–0:10): penguins zip,
  zip, zip; the walrus climbs in; the slide bulges, creaks and wiggles. Clip B, **10s, used
  whole** (0:10–0:20): POP, splash, the dry pool, the seagull. Chain: the last frame of A
  (the walrus's calm face squished at the slide exit, slide bulging) is the start still of B.
- **Pattern:** hard hits on action beats. Each penguin "zip" is a snare-and-twang hit (0:01.7,
  0:03.3, 0:05.0, the last three penguins as one triple zip). The walrus is a low baritone
  sax note that holds.
- **Breaks:** (1) **the stuck stop, 0:08.3–0:10.0, unscored**: band out, only the slide
  creaking and squeaking; (2) the full band back on the POP at 0:10.0; (3) out again at
  0:18.3 so the last drip from the slide is heard alone, then the whistle-lowering in silence.
- **Mouths:** **off** for every animal. The singer is an off-screen deep, flat male voice.
  The seagull's whistle is in his beak the whole time and never blown after the POP.
- **Diegetic sound:** six slide whooshes, small penguin splashes, slide creak and rubber
  squeak, the POP (a huge cork), the mega-splash, water raining back onto the deck, one drip.
