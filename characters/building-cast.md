# Building Cast — Expanded Character Bios

Core residents of the questionable multi-unit building / arcology sector that keeps showing up across *Weeks on Fire*. These four form the emotional and comedic spine of the more grounded (yet still completely unhinged) living-situation episodes and cutaways.

They live in the same decaying, rule-obsessed structure where the HOA memos arrive before the apocalypse does, the elevators have opinions, and the laundry room is a neutral zone only until the music starts.

---

## Rubella

**Role / Vibe**  
Endlessly exhausted, cynical survivor with a razor-sharp wit. She is the one who has already seen this exact flavor of nightmare three times this week and is still somehow the most functional person in the room. Protagonist energy delivered through pure survival fatigue.

**Backstory**  
Formerly a low-level quality assurance inspector for a mega-corp that specialized in products that were "technically" safe. She lost her pension the day the company "accidentally" erased its entire human resources department (and the backups, and the backups of the backups). She moved into the building seeking cheap rent and something resembling quiet. She found neither. The building found her first.

**Quirk / Flaw**  
Keeps an emergency stash of contraband pre-dystopian laundry detergent (the kind that still smells like actual fabric softener instead of "regulated cleansing agent #47"). Trusts literally no one who smiles before 10 AM. Will trade almost anything for a working dryer cycle that does not produce a low-frequency hum that attracts things.

**Voice / Minimax Notes**  
Dry, slightly raspy, zero fucks left to give. Perfect for deadpan call-and-response duets and late-night musical cutaways. Think exhausted cabaret — the kind of voice that can sell both a love song and a threat assessment in the same breath.

**Sample Lines**  
- "The elevator just tried to negotiate. I told it I already pay rent."  
- "If the floor is on fire again, I'm charging it for emotional damages."  
- "I don't need a hero. I need the detergent that still works."

**Grok Imagine Seeds**  
- Rubella leaning against a flickering fluorescent hallway light at 3 AM, holding a half-empty bottle of illegal laundry detergent like a sacred relic, expression of pure resignation, cinematic low-key lighting.  
- Close-up: her eyes reflecting the glow of a "World's Okayest Tenant" mug while something impossible happens just over her shoulder.

**Episode / Cutaway Fit**  
Any late-night building sequence, laundry-room musical numbers, HOA confrontation aftermaths, and every scene where someone needs to say the quiet part out loud with maximum dryness. Spoken packet: [`notes/scenes/rubella-stand-up.md`](../notes/scenes/rubella-stand-up.md) — Item Seven at the laundry intercom.

**Status**  
Core protagonist. Already heavy in Episode 3 scripts, audio cuts, and multiple musical cutaways (Choose Your Basalt, Monster Mash, etc.). Expand freely.

## Lillith

**Role / Vibe**  
The chaotic catalyst. Bold, slightly unhinged, and remarkably resilient. Where Rubella endures, Lillith *provokes*. She treats the building's escalating insanity as an artistic challenge rather than a hazard. If something can be improved with duct tape, neon, or an unauthorized fireworks display, she will try it.

**Backstory**  
Former underground busker and self-proclaimed "vibes consultant" who was evicted from three different arcology sectors for noise violations and unsanctioned pyrotechnics. She views the current building as her largest, most interactive installation yet. The management board has a special file on her that is already thicker than most novels.

**Quirk / Flaw**  
Borderline dangerous obsession with repairing retro tech (ping-pong tables, neon signs, old arcade cabinets, anything with vacuum tubes) using parts that clearly do not belong together. The results usually work for exactly one glorious, slightly radioactive night.

**Voice / Minimax Notes**  
Bright, conspiratorial, full of sudden energy spikes. Excellent for call-and-response with Rubella — she is the one who starts the song, Rubella is the one who finishes it with a sigh that somehow still lands on the beat.

**Sample Lines**  
- "The neon sign only works if you insult it first. I have the data."  
- "It's not a fire hazard. It's ambient lighting with ambition."  
- "I fixed the ping-pong table. It now occasionally predicts the weather."

**Grok Imagine Seeds**  
- Lillith mid-repair on a half-disassembled neon sign in a dim corridor, sparks flying, her face lit by the wrong colors, grin of pure creative mania.  
- Wide shot: her standing on a ladder with a soldering iron while the hallway behind her subtly warps.

**Episode / Cutaway Fit**  
Musical cutaways that start in the living room and explode outward, spy-heist subplots (see Monster Mash finale), any scene requiring sudden energy or an improvised solution that makes everything worse in the most beautiful way.

**Status**  
Key dynamic force alongside Rubella. Already present in dialogue cuts, scene drafts, and multiple Minimax tracks.

## Madelyn

**Role / Vibe**  
The passive-aggressive HOA / Building Committee Enforcer. She is the human embodiment of laminated rules and the quiet terror of a well-organized clipboard. Authority figure energy with the soul of someone who peaked in middle management and never recovered.

**Backstory**  
Spent twenty years in corporate middle management before taking over the tenant board. She treats trash disposal compliance, hallway noise ordinances, and unauthorized plant life with the same gravity as state treason. The building's bylaws are her scripture. She has never once smiled without calculating the consequences first.

**Quirk / Flaw**  
Hides intense existential dread behind impeccably laminated rule sheets, passive-aggressive memos, and a mug that says "World's Best Supervisor." The mug has a small crack she refuses to acknowledge. She once issued a formal warning to a cockroach for "unauthorized occupation of common areas."

**Voice / Minimax Notes**  
Crisp, slightly nasal, perfect for spoken-word interludes and bureaucratic cabaret numbers. The kind of voice that can turn a simple "good morning" into a performance review.

**Sample Lines**  
- "Item four on the agenda: the purple light is now classified as a decorative accent. No further discussion."  
- "That is not a portal. That is an unapproved hardscape feature."  
- "I don't make the rules. I *am* the rules. Dale just holds the gavel."

**Grok Imagine Seeds**  
- Madelyn standing in a fluorescent-lit hallway, clipboard raised like a shield, perfectly pressed blouse, the world ending softly behind her.  
- Extreme close-up on her laminated memo while something eldritch drips onto the edge of the paper and she carefully blots it without breaking eye contact.

**Episode / Cutaway Fit**  
HOA meeting episodes, any scene requiring an authority figure who is both ridiculous and terrifying, musical numbers about compliance and the slow death of the human spirit under fluorescent lights.

**Status**  
Strong antagonist / authority energy. Already appears as Madelin in Episode 3 concepts and HOA sketches. Name standardized here as Madelyn.

## Qing Rao

**Role / Vibe**  
The cool, calculating neighbor who knows too much about how the building's backend systems actually work. Hyper-competent wild card. Speaks calmly even when the floor is literally on fire. Treats absolute chaos like a mild scheduling conflict.

**Backstory**  
Ex-systems engineer or black-market logistics specialist who retired to the lower levels specifically to keep a low profile. He still has access keys that no longer officially exist. The building's infrastructure occasionally listens when he talks to it. He never confirms or denies this.

**Quirk / Flaw**  
Always seems to be eating a bowl of noodles or tinkering with a small gadget during life-or-death situations. Treats absolute chaos like a mild inconvenience. Will pause mid-crisis to adjust the seasoning.

**Voice / Minimax Notes**  
Calm, measured, almost soothing — the perfect counterpoint to everyone else's rising panic. Excellent for spoken asides under high-tension musical beds or deadpan explanations of impossible systems.

**Sample Lines**  
- *(noodles in hand, hallway on fire)* "The primary loop is overloaded. Secondary should hold for another four minutes."  
- "I wouldn't stand there. The floor remembers."  
- "Chaos is just a system that hasn't been named yet."

**Grok Imagine Seeds**  
- Qing Rao sitting cross-legged on the floor of a smoke-filled corridor, calmly eating noodles while emergency lights strobe around him.  
- Close-up of his hands assembling a small, glowing gadget while the background warps with impossible architecture.

**Episode / Cutaway Fit**  
Any scene that needs a character who already understands the rules of the current absurdity and is simply waiting for everyone else to catch up. Natural foil to Madelyn's rule-obsession and perfect deadpan partner for Rubella.

**Status**  
Enigmatic wild card. Already listed in suggested-characters as crystal-skull keeper / elegant observer. Expanded here into full building resident with systems knowledge.

## Group Dynamics Notes

- **Rubella + Lillith**: The core duo. Exhausted cynicism meets chaotic creativity. Their musical cutaways are the emotional and comedic heart of many sequences.  
- **Madelyn vs. Everyone**: She is the external pressure that forces the others into alliance (or temporary truce).  
- **Qing Rao**: The quiet stabilizer. He rarely starts the trouble, but he is almost always the one who understands how to survive it.  
- **Shared Setting**: The building itself is a character — elevators with mood disorders, laundry rooms that double as temporary neutral zones, hallways that rearrange themselves when no one is looking.
- **Outside the building**: Rubella and Lillith moonlight as the dual frontwomen of the Glam-Sham-Poo *Ultra Screech* commercial alongside Kenji "Shred" Sato — the same laundry room, rented by a shampoo company. Kenji is not a resident; his card lives in `suggested-characters.md`. Cutaway: [`ideas/glam-sham-poo-commercial.md`](../ideas/glam-sham-poo-commercial.md).
