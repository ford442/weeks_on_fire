## Year-by-year scene cards

### First year — two lines / one cube
- **Room:** training floor with two painted rings and one cube.
- **One job:** hold the cube centered between lines long enough to file it.
- **Motion verbs (2–4):** hover, steady, write.
- **What Imagine will break:** adds a second Riley, adds HUD overlays, turns class into combat.
- **Kill criteria:** any HUD text, any extra Riley, any fight pose.

### Second year — nights bunk / window planet
- **Room:** dorm desk at night with one moon; optional window-planet hold.
- **One job:** repeat the same cube diagram on night shift while bunkmate sleeps.
- **Motion verbs (2–4):** balance, mark, glance.
- **What Imagine will break:** upgrades wings too early, adds sword, turns it into a heroic reveal.
- **Kill criteria:** full wings, sword presence, action choreography.

### Third year — yard + further exercises + Appendix C
- **Room:** empty canyon yard, then Appendix C round room.
- **One job:** chest-height endurance hold, then file into Appendix C.
- **Motion verbs (2–4):** hold, shake, turn, shelve.
- **What Imagine will break:** yard hologram cards, new planets, duck-bill extras.
- **Kill criteria:** hologram/HUD cards, invented planets, wrong civilian design.

### Fourth year — bestowal hall
- **Room:** bestowal aisle over two lines.
- **One job:** receive hardware and assignment posture.
- **Motion verbs (2–4):** approach, open, receive, inhale.
- **What Imagine will break:** sword swing/fight beat, feather wings, beam-lamps.
- **Kill criteria:** any swing, any feather/hex-chain/snowflake wing read, any lamp beam into bodies.
