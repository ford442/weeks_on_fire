STYLE:

Warm joke metal, 104 BPM, G major, about one minute fifty. One riff on a warm amp, standard tuning, dry kit, no scream, no growl. The verse is spoken in time, on the grid, dry, one voice, six items and no more: hinge pin, brass screw, blank card, cloth-covered wire, spare fuse, brass screw. Then the lead guitar alone copies those six lines as six bends, one bend per item, same order, no shredding, no taps. Structural flaw: item six is item two again, the same brass screw, and the sixth bend is a new pitch, higher than the second, and it is not corrected. Gang vocals on the chorus only, clean, the title and nothing else. Sing only the lines below. No djent, no dropped tuning, no blast beat, no occult lyric, no choir, no Kenji, no product name. Mood: the list is the law. Runtime about 1:50.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: item six is item two again, and the guitar plays it as a new note. The list is not revised. The second bend is not retaken.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not Bed With a Door.** That clipboard is four pen taps and a latch. Nobody reads a list, and there is no guitar.
- **Not Recycling Bin Rebellion.** The wrong-shade bin stays in that song. Nothing on this list is a bin, a swatch, or a color.
- **Not a bestowal.** No sword, no annex, no one is given anything. The solo is the list, played back.
