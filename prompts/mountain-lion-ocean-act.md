## E — The crossing (1:08–1:30)

The quietest, widest, strangest part of the piece. `Come Further` is gone by the middle of E1, and `Here They Are`
blooms in under the far horizon. The path should not hold weight, and it does. Nobody comments.

### E1 · 10 s · start still

```
First-person POV still, photoreal: an adult mountain lion seen from behind at frame center, tawny coat, tail low, deadpan, walking a narrow causeway across open ocean at the same even pace it walked the field, the thin black leather leash running back to a hand at the bottom edge of frame. The causeway is a dense raft of packed floating blooms, lupines poppies foxgloves and daisies laid flat and woven with dark kelp, about a body-width wide, sitting level on the water and holding the cat's weight without sagging. Calm glassy sea on both sides to the horizon, kelp and drifting blossoms visible just under the surface. Where the causeway meets the sea there is no hard edge: outer petals soften into foam, one substance. A faint hairline seam of alizarin and ultramarine tint runs inside the packed blooms like a wet seam, never on the open water. Brilliant silver-gold light, low sun ahead, salt haze. 28mm, walker's-eye height, centered one-point perspective down the causeway, light grain, natural grade, dreamlike but grounded, short-film still, Grok Imagine style
```

### E1 · motion

```
Steady forward glide at walking pace down the causeway; the mountain lion keeps the same unbroken stride and never looks at the water; the slack part of the leash dips onto the sea surface and floats there beside the path. Calm water, no splashing, no swimming. 10 seconds.
```

### E1 · last still (= E2 start)

```
First-person POV still, photoreal: an adult mountain lion seen from behind at frame center, tawny coat, deadpan, mid-stride on a narrow causeway of packed floating blooms and dark kelp across open ocean, completely unimpressed by the sea, paws damp, coat dry. The thin black leather leash runs back to a hand at the bottom of frame, and a loose loop of it lies on the calm water beside the causeway, floating, unattended. Just off the right edge of the causeway a single large pink-and-white blossom floats alongside at the same pace as the cat. The causeway's edges dissolve into foam with no seam, petal and foam one substance; a faint alizarin hairline tint runs inside the packed blooms only, open water clear and untinted. Wide calm sea to the horizon on both sides, kelp below the surface. Brilliant silver light warming toward gold at the horizon, low sun ahead. 28mm, walker's-eye height, one-point perspective, light grain, natural grade, short-film still, Grok Imagine style
```
