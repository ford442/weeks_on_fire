STYLE:

Lo-fi anti-folk recorded on a handheld cassette recorder at a busy subway entrance, 112 BPM, G major, about two minutes ten. A slightly out-of-tune steel-string acoustic guitar strummed hard, a cheap toy drum machine playing a tinny preset beat, a kazoo solo, and real street background: footsteps, a distant train, a little crowd murmur. Lead: a younger, bright, conspiratorial female voice, chatty and quick, telling the song to passers-by. Second voice: a younger, dry, slightly raspy female voice reading out the contents of the hat in a flat inventory tone between verses. Ends with one coo, the guitar stops, and the recorder clicks off. Sincere, unpolished, no parody, no added comedy effects. Use Lillith and Rubella voices, both younger.

LYRICS:

[Verse 1]
LILLITH: I put my hat down on the pavement
I tuned my guitar to the train
The first man smiled and walked on past me
The second man put in a button
RUBELLA: One button.

[Chorus]
LILLITH: Throw it in the hat, throw it in the hat
Anything you've got, you can throw it in the hat
Throw it in the hat, throw it in the hat
RUBELLA: That's not money.

[Verse 2]
LILLITH: A lady in a hurry gave a key
I don't know what it opens, maybe me
A kid gave half a sandwich, ham and cheese
A man gave us a library card, it's his
RUBELLA: One key. Half a sandwich. A library card. His.

[Chorus]
LILLITH: Throw it in the hat, throw it in the hat
Anything you've got, you can throw it in the hat
Throw it in the hat, throw it in the hat
RUBELLA: Still not money.

[Kazoo Solo]

[Verse 3]
LILLITH: And then the sun went down behind the station
And something with grey feathers landed in
It ate the sandwich and it sat down on the button
And it listened to the whole set to the end
RUBELLA: One pigeon.

[Final Chorus]
LILLITH: Throw it in the hat, throw it in the hat
Anything you've got, you can throw it in the hat
Throw it in the hat, throw it in the hat
RUBELLA: It's staying.

NOTES:

**Canon used:** Lillith's bio: a former underground busker, self-proclaimed "vibes
consultant." This is from that era.

**Kid structure:** button, key, sandwich, library card, then the wrong third grows feathers.
The pigeon is not the Black Cat, not Biscuit, and not named.

**Do-not:** no real transit system name or signage. No money joke where the hat finally
fills up; it never does.
