STYLE:

Sincere turn-of-the-millennium UK garage, 2-step rhythm at 134 BPM, E flat minor, about two minutes forty. Skippy shuffled 2-step drums with the kick dropping beats, crisp rim shots, a deep warm sub bass, chopped R&B vocal stabs, lush Rhodes chords and soft strings. A wooden stair creak is used as percussion on the skipped off-beat of every second bar, the same pitch every time. Lead one: dry, slightly raspy, smooth, singing a sincere, intimate late-night R&B love song. Lead two: bright, sleepy, muffled, as if from upstairs, one short line per chorus. Silky, warm, sincere, no parody, no comedy sound effects other than the creak. Use Rubella and Lillith voices.

NOTES:

**The pun is the rhythm.** 2-step garage drops beats to make its skip. Here the skip is a
stair creak, landing in the same place every second bar, the same way the second step
always creaks.

**Do-not:** no real artist names in STYLE. Not the building's sixth-floor corridor (this is
the stairs). Nobody is woken angrily; Lillith is fond of the stair too.
