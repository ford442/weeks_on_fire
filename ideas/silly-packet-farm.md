# Silly Packet Farm — loops, drops, and wrong thirds

*Farm sheet built from [`funny-rules.md`](funny-rules.md). Text only. 2026-10-19. `promote?: no` on every card.*

**Rules:** [`funny-rules.md`](funny-rules.md) (tone) · [`loop-grammar.md`](loop-grammar.md) (loop tiles)
**Format parent:** [`short-packet-farm.md`](short-packet-farm.md) — card shape only, not tone
**Cast locks:** [`../characters/building-cast.md`](../characters/building-cast.md) ·
[`../characters/bible/`](../characters/bible/)

Twelve packets: four loop tiles, four joke interrupts, four gag sketches. No stills
generated, no `content/` file, nothing on Suggestions. Every card names one world, one
kid-level laugh, and the visible thing that happens on the drop.

**Counts.** Funny with the sound off: **10 of 12** (all but `drop-the-bass` and
`pigeon-conga`, which need the beat to land fully). Animals: **6 of 12** (`goose-revolving-door`,
`hamster-building`, `lap-cat`, `drop-the-bass`, `pigeon-conga`, `watering-can-giraffe`).

**Callbacks spent as references, not sets:** Lillith's retro-tech repairs that work for one
glorious night (`claw-machine-wins`) · Rubella's contraband detergent (`fitted-sheet-wins`) ·
the Pizza Guy walking through the back of someone else's wide at the same pace, box at chest
height (`sprinkler-launch`) · "Extra napkins are in the bag." (`popcorn-snow`).

## Clip math (Grok Imagine)

Grok Imagine clips come in **6s, 10s, and 15s**. Every shot below names the clip it is
generated as and what it is trimmed to. Multi-clip packets chain: the **last frame of
clip N is the start still of clip N+1**, so only the first start still and the final last
still need writing from scratch.

| Shelf | Length | Clip plan |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| Loop tile | 2–8s | One 6s clip (trim to cycle) or one 10s clip trimmed to 8s. Start still = last still. |
| Joke interrupt | 3–10s | One 6s or one 10s clip. Drop lands in the back half. |
| Gag sketch | ~30s | Three 10s clips (one per beat), or 6 + 10 + 15 trimmed to 30. Drop on beat three. |

**Default still style** (append to every still prompt): *photoreal cinematic still, 2.39:1,
Grok Imagine building-cast, no real actor names, locked camera, 35mm, fine grain, Grok
Imagine style.* **Rubella** = a glamorous, exhausted woman with long dark hair and
blood-red lipstick in a modern black lace evening dress. **Lillith** = a glamorous woman in
black lace with flawless, never-smudged mascara and a bright, conspiratorial half-smile.
Both faces stay calm in every frame unless the card names an end reaction.

## Loop tiles (4)

Loop grammar holds: last frame = first frame, one moving system, a rest pose for ~15–20%
of the cycle, silent picture with one diegetic click. The **drop** on a loop is the
cycle's turn frame: when a tile sits under a bed in the stacker, put that frame on the
downbeat.

### `goose-revolving-door` — The Goose Who Can't Leave

- **Laugh:** *A goose goes through the spinning door and comes out exactly where it started. Forever.*
- **Length:** 5s cycle · **World:** building lobby · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** yes
- **Shots:** one 6s clip, trimmed to 5s. 0–1s rest: goose faces the revolving door. 1–4s:
  goose waddles in, the door turns a full circle, goose waddles out on the **same side**,
  facing the door again. 4–5s rest.
- **Start still = last still:** a white goose standing on polished lobby tile, facing a
  glass-and-brass revolving door head-on, door panels square to camera, street daylight
  behind the glass, empty lobby.
- **Drop:** 3.5s — **reverses**: the goose pops out of the door's last quarter-turn back on
  the inside, as if spat out.
- **Music feel:** silent picture + one door *whump*. Under a bed: plucky pizzicato, 100 bpm.
- **End reaction:** none — it's a loop.
- **Do-not:** no people in the lobby. The goose never honks on picture. Never cut to the street side.

### `pancake-moon` — The Pancake That Orbits

- **Laugh:** *She flips a pancake and it goes around the lamp like the moon before it lands.*
- **Length:** 6s cycle · **World:** ladies' building unit, kitchen · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** no
- **Shots:** one 6s clip. 0–1s rest: Rubella holds the pan, pancake in it. 1–5s: flip; the
  pancake rises, makes one slow flat orbit around the hanging ceiling lamp, comes back down.
  5–6s rest: lands in the pan, same spot.
- **Start still = last still:** Rubella at a small stove in a lived-in apartment kitchen,
  frying pan held level at chest height, one golden pancake in the pan, a round pendant lamp
  hanging above her, morning light, World's Okayest Tenant mug on the counter, her face bored.
- **Drop:** 3s — **transforms**: at the far side of the orbit the lamp light catches the
  pancake and it goes full crescent-moon silhouette for one beat.
- **Music feel:** silent + one pan *tsss* on landing. Under a bed: lounge vibraphone, slow swing.
- **End reaction:** none. Rubella never looks up at it.
- **Do-not:** the pancake never stacks, never burns, never drips. One pancake. Lamp light stays locked.

### `hamster-building` — The Building Is a Hamster Wheel

- **Laugh:** *A tiny hamster runs so hard the whole apartment building rolls over.*
- **Length:** 8s cycle · **World:** building exterior, street · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** yes
- **Shots:** one 10s clip, trimmed to 8s. 0–1.5s rest: building upright. 1.5–7s: in one top
  window a hamster runs in a wheel; the whole building rolls one full turn in place like a
  wheel, Rubella on her balcony riding it upside down and back, still sipping from her mug.
  7–8s rest: upright.
- **Start still = last still:** wide, a five-storey brick apartment building on a quiet
  street, one lit top-floor window with a small hamster wheel visible inside, Rubella on a
  second-floor balcony holding a mug, calm, overcast daylight, parked cars not moving.
- **Drop:** 4s — **reverses**: the building is fully upside down and Rubella's coffee
  pours *up* into the mug.
- **Music feel:** silent + one squeaky-wheel squeak at the top. Under a bed: oompah tuba, 120 bpm.
- **End reaction:** none.
- **Do-not:** no other window moves. No debris, no falling furniture. The street does not roll.

### `stair-slinky` — The Slinky That Never Gets to the Bottom

- **Laugh:** *A slinky as big as a couch walks down the stairs, and the stairs flip so it has to start again.*
- **Length:** 6s cycle · **World:** building stairwell · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** no
- **Shots:** one 6s clip. 0–1s rest: slinky coiled on the top step. 1–5s: it walks down
  three steps; the whole flight tips like a seesaw so the bottom becomes the top. 5–6s
  rest: coiled on the top step, same pose.
- **Start still = last still:** a giant rainbow metal-and-plastic slinky the size of a sofa
  coiled on the top step of a narrow apartment stairwell, cream plaster walls, steel
  handrail, one caged bulb, Lillith sitting on the landing below with a paperback, not looking.
- **Drop:** 4s — **reverses**: the staircase seesaws and the slinky is on top again.
- **Music feel:** silent + one *boing* on the last step. Under a bed: descending xylophone run.
- **End reaction:** none. Lillith never turns a page: one moving system.
- **Do-not:** the slinky never tangles, never reaches the landing. Lillith never steps over it.

## Joke interrupts (4)

Cut in, punch on the drop, cut out. One line maximum on camera, and none is better.

### `lap-cat` — The Kitten Gets Big

- **Laugh:** *A kitten falls asleep on her lap and turns into a lion, and she keeps reading.*
- **Length:** 6s · **World:** ladies' building unit, living room · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** yes
- **Shots:** one 6s clip. 0–3s: Rubella on the couch with a book, a tiny kitten curled on
  her lap, purring. 3s: drop. 3–6s: the kitten is a full-grown lion curled in exactly the
  same pose across her lap, couch sagging; she turns a page.
- **Start still:** Rubella seated on a worn velvet couch reading a hardback, a tiny grey
  kitten curled asleep in her lap, lamp light, face calm and bored.
- **Last still:** the same frame, but a full-grown sleeping lion is curled across her lap
  and spills off both ends of the couch, her book resting on its mane, face unchanged.
- **Drop:** 3s — **transforms**: kitten to lion in one cut on the beat, same curl.
- **Music feel:** music-box lullaby that drops into a big brass sting.
- **End reaction:** none. That's the joke.
- **Do-not:** the lion never wakes, never roars. Not the Black Cat — this is a new animal.

### `drop-the-bass` — She Drops the Bass

- **Laugh:** *She says "drop the bass" and a giant fish falls on the DJ table.*
- **Length:** 8s · **World:** ladies' building unit, house party · **Sound off:** no (the beat sells it) · **Animal:** yes
- **Shots:** one 10s clip, trimmed to 8s. 0–5s: Lillith at a homemade DJ rig (two
  mismatched turntables, a neon tube she rewired), one hand raised, the room waiting.
  5s: drop. 5–8s: a huge glossy largemouth bass falls from above onto the decks with a
  splat; the music keeps going; Rubella hands her a towel from off frame.
- **Start still:** Lillith behind a DJ table built from a ping-pong-table half and two
  mismatched turntables, one finger raised, a buzzing pink neon tube behind her, a small
  crowd of party guests in silhouette, cosy apartment living room.
- **Last still:** a giant wet bass fish lying across both turntables, Lillith's finger still
  raised, a towel being handed in from frame left, crowd mid-cheer, Lillith calm.
- **Drop:** 5s — **falls**: the bass.
- **Music feel:** big-room EDM build, original Minimax, 128 bpm; the drop is the whole joke.
- **End reaction:** none. Lillith nods once like it went to plan.
- **Do-not:** no sung lyric "drop the bass" — the build carries it. The fish is unharmed and comic, never gross.

### `popcorn-snow` — The Popcorn Doesn't Stop

- **Laugh:** *The popcorn keeps popping until the whole kitchen is full like a snow day.*
- **Length:** 10s · **World:** ladies' building unit, kitchen · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** no
- **Shots:** one 10s clip. 0–4s: Lillith presses START on an old microwave; it pops.
  4s: drop. 4–9s: popcorn **multiplies** out of the door, waist-deep, chest-deep, ceiling.
  9–10s: Rubella's head pokes up out of the popcorn; she eats one piece.
- **Start still:** Lillith pressing a button on a battered avocado-green microwave with one
  mismatched knob, apartment kitchen, Rubella leaning on the fridge with her arms folded,
  both calm.
- **Last still:** the kitchen filled to the ceiling with white popcorn like snowdrifts,
  only Rubella's head and one hand visible above it, holding one piece of popcorn to her
  red lips, deadpan; the microwave light still on.
- **Drop:** 4s — **multiplies**: the popcorn.
- **Music feel:** tight funk groove; the drop opens into a horn section.
- **End reaction:** Rubella eats one piece, chewing slowly. Optional off-camera knock and
  *"Extra napkins are in the bag."* if a 10s cut has room — never on camera.
- **Do-not:** nobody is buried in a scary way; heads are up. No butter, no fire.

### `pigeon-conga` — One Pigeon Becomes a Conga Line

- **Laugh:** *She feeds one pigeon and a hundred show up and do a conga line.*
- **Length:** 8s · **World:** ladies' building unit, balcony · **Sound off:** no (the conga needs the beat) · **Animal:** yes
- **Shots:** one 10s clip, trimmed to 8s. 0–3s: Rubella sprinkles birdseed on the balcony
  rail; one pigeon lands. 3s: drop. 3–7s: pigeons **multiply** — a hundred land and form a
  conga line hopping along the rail in step. 7–8s: Rubella sweeps the seed off the rail
  with one hand.
- **Start still:** Rubella on a small iron balcony holding a paper bag of birdseed, one grey
  pigeon on the rail, city rooftops behind, flat afternoon light, bored.
- **Last still:** the balcony rail packed end to end with pigeons in a hopping conga line,
  Rubella's hand mid-sweep, seed scattering, her face unchanged.
- **Drop:** 3s — **multiplies**: the pigeons.
- **Music feel:** Latin conga percussion, original; the drop adds the full band.
- **End reaction:** Rubella sighs, once, on the sweep.
- **Do-not:** not the bird-comedian flock — these pigeons never talk or do bits. No mess on the balcony.

## Gag sketches (4)

Rule of three: beat one sets it, beat two confirms it, beat three is **wrong** and lands on
the drop. One 10s clip per beat unless noted.

### `claw-machine-wins` — The Claw Machine Wins Rubella

- **Laugh:** *The claw machine grabs a bear, another bear, and then it grabs Rubella.*
- **Length:** 30s · **World:** ladies' building unit, Lillith's repair corner · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** no
- **Shots:**
  1. **10s** — Lillith finishes rewiring an old arcade claw machine (parts that do not
     belong together; works for one night). Rubella feeds it a coin. The claw drops,
     grabs a plush bear, drops it in the chute.
  2. **10s** — Second coin. The claw grabs a second plush bear, drops it in the chute.
     Rubella holds both bears, unimpressed.
  3. **10s** — Third coin. On the drop the claw reaches **out through the glass**, grabs
     Rubella by the shoulders, lifts her, and drops her neatly down the prize chute. She
     lands sitting in the tray, still holding both bears.
- **Start still:** Lillith with a screwdriver kneeling beside a vintage arcade claw machine,
  side panel off, mismatched wires and a vacuum tube glowing inside, Rubella standing beside
  it holding a coin, cluttered apartment corner, lamp light, both calm.
- **Last still:** Rubella seated inside the claw machine's oversized prize tray, two plush
  bears in her arms, the claw hovering empty above, Lillith beside the machine looking
  pleased, Rubella deadpan.
- **Drop:** 22s (beat three) — **transforms**: the claw grows long enough to leave the glass.
- **Music feel:** chiptune arcade loop; the drop goes full orchestra for two bars.
- **End reaction:** Lillith quietly gives the machine a thumbs-up.
- **Do-not:** not Pong-Bot 3000. No prizes with logos. Rubella never struggles.

### `watering-can-giraffe` — She Waters a Giraffe

- **Laugh:** *She waters the plant: a leaf, a flower, and then a giraffe.*
- **Length:** 30s · **World:** ladies' building unit, living room window · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** yes
- **Shots:** generate as **6 + 10 + 15**, trim to 30.
  1. **6s** — Lillith tips a watering can over a small potted plant. One new leaf pops out.
  2. **10s** — More water. A single bright flower opens. She nods.
  3. **15s** — Last splash. On the drop the pot **grows a giraffe** — neck first, up
     through the plant, until its head bends against the ceiling. It eats the flower.
     Lillith keeps watering; Rubella walks through the back of the shot carrying laundry
     and does not look.
- **Start still:** Lillith holding a small brass watering can over a tiny potted plant on
  a windowsill, apartment living room, soft morning light, calm half-smile.
- **Last still:** a full-grown giraffe standing in the same terracotta pot, head bent under
  the ceiling, a flower in its mouth, Lillith still pouring from the can at its feet,
  Rubella in the background with a laundry basket, both calm.
- **Drop:** 18s (beat three) — **appears**: the giraffe.
- **Music feel:** gentle acoustic guitar; the drop is a jungle-drum hit.
- **End reaction:** none. The giraffe chews.
- **Do-not:** the window and ceiling don't break. No other plants in the room.

### `fitted-sheet-wins` — The Sheet Folds Her

- **Laugh:** *She folds a shirt, she folds a towel, and the sheet folds her.*
- **Length:** 30s · **World:** building laundry room (a different night from Rubella Stand-Up) · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** no
- **Shots:**
  1. **10s** — Rubella folds a T-shirt into a perfect square, sets it on the table.
  2. **10s** — Folds a towel into a perfect square, sets it on top.
  3. **10s** — Picks up a fitted sheet. On the drop the sheet wraps around her and **folds
     her** into a neat square bundle, which sets itself on top of the stack. Only her face
     shows in one side.
- **Start still:** Rubella at a laundry-room folding table holding a white T-shirt by the
  shoulders, washers and dryers behind her, fluorescent light, a contraband unlabeled
  detergent bottle on the table in the background, no readable text, bored.
- **Last still:** a neat stack on the folding table: folded shirt, folded towel, and on top
  a perfectly folded floral fitted-sheet square with Rubella's face peeking out of one fold,
  deadpan, detergent bottle still in the background.
- **Drop:** 22s (beat three) — **transforms**: the sheet becomes the folder.
- **Music feel:** cosy lo-fi; the drop is a single cartoon accordion squeeze.
- **End reaction:** Rubella, folded, blinks once.
- **Do-not:** not `lint-inventory`. The detergent bottle is background only, never the hero.

### `sprinkler-launch` — The Sprinkler Blasts Off

- **Laugh:** *The sprinkler sprays left, sprays right, then blasts off like a rocket.*
- **Length:** 30s · **World:** building front lawn · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** no
- **Shots:** generate as **15 + 15**, trimmed to 30.
  1. **15s** — Rubella in a lawn chair with sunglasses. The sprinkler sprays left (0–7s),
     then sprays right (7–15s). She doesn't move.
  2. **15s** — The sprinkler turns straight up. On the drop it **launches** on its own jet of
     water like a rocket, out of frame. Five seconds of nothing. In the back of the wide, the
     Pizza Guy walks past at his usual pace, box at chest height, and never looks up. Then
     the sprinkler **falls** back into its spot under a tiny parachute and starts spraying
     left again.
- **Start still:** Rubella reclining in a striped lawn chair on a green apartment-building
  lawn, big sunglasses, mug in hand, a little brass lawn sprinkler beside her mid-spray to
  the left, bright summer afternoon, deadpan.
- **Last still:** the same lawn and chair, the sprinkler back in place spraying left, a
  small fabric parachute settling on the grass beside it, Rubella has pushed her sunglasses
  up onto her head.
- **Drop:** 19s — **appears**: the water jet; second hit at 26s — **falls**: parachute landing.
- **Music feel:** surf-rock guitar; the drop is a launch-countdown synth swell.
- **End reaction:** Rubella pushes her sunglasses up. That's the one reaction.
- **Do-not:** the Pizza Guy is never named, never stops, never reacts. Not Brad's bins. No lawn damage.
