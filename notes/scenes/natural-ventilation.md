# Natural Ventilation

*Long-form dialog scene — building cast. The ceiling of 12-A has opened. Nobody is going to fix it tonight, and one of them has to agree to that.*

**Slugline:** INT. UNIT 12-A (VACANT) — NIGHT
**Runtime target:** 3–4 min (≈3 script pages)
**Music cue:** **"The Roof Knows How to Wait"** — original Minimax track, dark cabaret ruin ballad, 72 BPM. Enters Beat C and runs to the end. Structural flaw is load-bearing: **every verse holds one extra bar after the last lyric line.** Do not fill it with a fill, a harmony, or a witty tag. Silence plus dust.
**Status:** draft
**Dialog versions (alts):** [`versions/natural-ventilation-versions.md`](versions/natural-ventilation-versions.md)

**Cast:** RUBELLA (lead) · LILLITH (lead) — **two-hander. Nobody else. No voices, no intercom, no memo.**

**Spine:** A vacant top-floor unit has a hole in its ceiling and a rectangle of sky where a ceiling should be. Lillith wants to fix it. Rubella says no. It is the one time in the series Lillith is told no and does not argue, and the scene never marks the change.

**Juxtaposition (wrong channel):** This one changes channels *down*. After the elevator tango and the neon corridor, the show drops into a **late ruin ballad** — two women in a room that has lost an argument with weather, at cabaret volume, with an extra bar of nothing after every verse. The wrongness is the **calm**. Nobody treats a structural failure as an event. Same commitment rule, opposite direction.

**Emotional notes:** Warmth, not melancholy. This is the third scene of a set and the only one where neither of them has a job to do, which is why it is the hardest one for both of them. **No confession. No backstory on camera. No crying.** The most either of them admits is a preference about furniture.

**Hard rules for this scene:**
- **Nobody fixes anything.** No tarp, no ladder, no bucket, no call. The extinguisher is not in this scene; neither is the toolbox.
- **They do not sweep the plaster.** It stays where it landed. It knows where to sit.
- **No rain lands on either of them.** It starts in the last ten seconds and neither moves, and the scene ends before anyone gets wet enough to comment.
- **Lillith accepts the no.** Once. It is not discussed, not thanked, not marked. Nobody says "you're not fixing it?" a second time.
- **Do not import the ballroom.** *The Roof Knows How to Wait* is a cousin to the House of Mirrors ruin, but this is a **vacant apartment**, not the ballroom: no chandelier, no paddles, no ping-pong metronome, no mascara beat, no champagne flute. Berries and two glasses only — the song's props, not Episode 1's.
- **The building is never established as unsafe or safe.** Nobody rules on it.

### A — The Seating Chart

```
INT. UNIT 12-A (VACANT) — NIGHT

A one-bedroom that has been empty long enough to stop smelling like
anyone. Parquet floor, most of it. A radiator under a window. No
furniture except a kitchen chair and a second kitchen chair that does
not match it.

Above: the ceiling is gone in one section — a clean-edged opening
roughly the size of a dining table, straight through lath, plaster and
roof deck. Not jagged. Not dramatic. It looks less like damage than
like a decision.

Through it: sky. Real sky, with the orange underlight a city puts on
its clouds.

Plaster on the parquet, spread wide, in a shape that is almost
deliberate.

RUBELLA is already here, in the mismatched chair, coat on, two wine
glasses on the floor beside her and a plastic clamshell of
supermarket blackberries, open.

LILLITH comes in. Stops in the doorway. Looks up for a long time.

Eleven seconds. Time it. Eleven.
```

```
                         LILLITH
          How long.

                         RUBELLA
          Tuesday.

                         LILLITH
          It's been open since Tuesday and nobody's — 

                         RUBELLA
          It's a vacant unit on twelve. There's no ticket
          category for this. I looked.
          (beat)
          I looked for a while.
```

```

                         RUBELLA
          No.
          (beat)
          I sat down where it told me to, though.
```

### C — Inventory (Song)

```
INT. 12-A — CONTINUOUS

The piano is slightly detuned and the soft pedal is down. A low cello
that does not bow on the downbeat. The high end of the mix is plaster
dust and nothing else — no cymbal, ever.

Rubella pours. Two glasses, on the floor, from a bottle we never see
the label of. She hands one up without looking. Lillith takes it
without looking. They have done this.

The vocal is Rubella's, close-mic'd, almost spoken, bored glamour.
It is **not** diegetic — she does not sing in the room, her mouth does
not move, and nobody acknowledges the music. It is the inventory she

DIALOGUE STOPS FOR VERSE 1 AND THE CHORUS. What follows is staging.
```

### E — And So Do We

```
INT. 12-A — CONTINUOUS

Last chorus. Then the last word of the song, held, and the room echo
takes it — and the echo is Lillith's voice, which is a mix decision
and not a performance. She does not sing. She does not open her mouth.
The room does it.

Do not explain this. Do not shoot her lips.

The extra bar arrives for the last time and nothing fills it.

Then:

A sound above. Small, then not small. On the roof deck, on the lath,
on the parquet:

Rain.

It comes through the opening in a clean-edged rectangle and begins to
darken the plaster in the shape of the hole, a perfect bright
rectangle of wet appearing on the floor between them and the door.

Neither of them moves.

Neither of them looks at it.

Rubella reaches down, picks up the clamshell of blackberries, and
moves it six inches to the left, out of the rectangle.

That is the only acknowledgment the weather receives.
```

```
                         LILLITH
          I could — 

                         RUBELLA
          I know.

(Beat.)

          Okay.

```

## Production Notes

**Runtime target:** 3–4 min

**Music Sync Summary:**
- A–B: no score. Outdoor-inflected room tone, distant traffic twelve floors down.
- B out: piano and one empty bar on Lillith's "Okay."
- C: full track. **No dialogue through Verse 1 and the Chorus.** Extra bar after every verse is silence plus dust — locked-off hold, no cutaway, no fill.
- D: dialogue rides on top of the bridge. **No duck.** Move the dialogue, not the song.
- E: room echo on the last "wait" is Lillith, unexplained. Outro decays. Rain only afterward.

**Written silences (non-negotiable):** 11s (open) · 4s (before "Sit down") · 3s (before she sits) · 6s (after the no) · 4s (the radiator) · every extra bar · 5s (final hold past the last note). Timed, not suggestions.
