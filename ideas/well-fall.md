# Well Fall

**Kind:** musical cutaway / visual experiment  
**Lane:** pure fall. Instrumental heavy metal. No vocal.  
**Catalog:** `content/cutaways/well-fall.json` (`kind: musical`)  
**Song:** `songs/Well_Fall.md` (`well-fall`)  
**Prompts:** `prompts/well-fall-segments.md`  
**Suggestions id:** `well-fall`  
**Status:** suggested — lip locked, sights open

We stand at a well. We fall. The picture is what we see on the way down.
The metal is the bed, not a joke commercial. Ultra Screech owns glam-with-lyrics.
This file is the room for the sights.

---

## Lock

- **The well is real.** Stone or brick ring, dark cylinder, enough mouth to
  step into. California dry hills or a backyard that forgot it had a well.
  Not a CGI portal. Not a marble cabinet. Not a Daisy bicycle.
- **The camera falls.** Default is POV looking down the shaft, with looking-up
  at the shrinking mouth as the twin. Character faces are optional and unassigned.
- **The well is quiet.** Drip, air, brick. The heavy metal is non-diegetic —
  it starts when we go. Commit. Do not wink. Do not put a band in the well.
- **No lyrics.** No HUD. No on-screen titles. No gang vocals.
- **Worlds do not mix:** no marble magnets, no mountain lion, no Glam bottle,
  no Riley Annex, no laser snakes, no HOA gavel.

The juxtaposition is the same family as Monster Mash on a laser lawn:
wrong channel, full commitment. A nearly silent show drops into a metal
instrumental because someone went down a well.

## Camera grammar (paste into stills)

POV down a circular masonry shaft, falling. Wet brick / fieldstone.
A shrinking disk of sky or porch light at the top when we look up.
One moving system: the fall. Sights occupy the walls, the air, or the
vanishing point. Photoreal cinematic, 2.39:1, crushed blacks, no text.

Looking-up twin: the mouth of the well as a bright coin getting smaller.
Same fall. Same metal.

---

## Why it earns a slot

The series already has lava under a living room, a gel gyre over clouds,
and a chrome sphere in a cave. A well is simpler and meaner: one hole,
one direction, one riff that refuses to cadence. The open middle is the
point — we do not know what is down there until someone writes it in
the bank.
