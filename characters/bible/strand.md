# Strand

**Status:** active
**Primary episodes:** Home Sweet Void table packet (the sit-up). Not Episode 3. Not the Annex.
**Role in one sentence:** The coffee-house snake who takes the scene when his eyes come on, and the reason the cat stops.

**Name:** **Strand.** One word. He does not say it. The info page is the only place it is written.
**Pronouns:** he/him on the credit sheet. On screen he is "the snake" if anyone has to say it, which they should not.
**Plate credit (voice):** **Lowell Peck.** Invented. Not a real actor. Never spoken.

---

## Logline bio

Strand is already in the chair when the camera finds the floor. Pale, seated, a cup going cold beside him, pleased in the way a person is pleased who has been in the industry longer than the room. The white building cat starts one sentence. Strand's eyes come on. The cat stops. There is no beam, no roar, and no second sentence from the cat. Strand does not visit Annex 7. He is not the lawn.

- Pale scales, real animal weight, seated upright in a lounge chair — a snake, not a person in a suit and not a cartoon
- Eyes warm amber when they come on. Off, they are just eyes
- One coffee cup, steam optional, never a cocktail
- Jumbo Earth bag stays on the glass table. He does not touch it
- The white building cat is in the room and has stopped. Not the black Halloween cat
- **Never change without a story reason:** no fangs-out attack, no laser, no morph from a person, no hoodie, no name said aloud

## Voice guide

- **Register:** pleased, industry, coffee-house. Never a monster roar. Never a hiss played for scare.
- **Sentence length:** short. He sounds like someone who has already been introduced and does not need to be.
- **Overuses:** nothing. He talks less than the packet threatens.
- **Refuses:** his own name, a villain line, any mention of the lawn pair, Annex, forms.
- **Humor type:** the room was having a conversation. He is why it isn't.

The locked beat is still the cat's half-line and the stop. Strand does not get a monologue in this packet. If a later packet needs one line, it is about the cup, not about power.

## Do / Don't

**Do** — let the eyes be the performance. Let the cat stop. Keep the cup. Keep him in the lounge.

**Don't** — don't name him on screen. Don't credit a real actor. Don't send him to the Annex. Don't give him the battle cue. Don't morph a person into him. Don't let him win a fight. There isn't one.
