# Elio Marsh

**Role:** Visual Designer · **Location:** Portland, OR · **On series:** archive & stills
**Portrait:** `public/cast/elio-marsh.jpg` · **Specialty:** prompt craft & color temperature

## Bio

Elio turns director notes into reproducible Grok Imagine prompts and keeps the Visual
Archive readable for collaborators. His test is the same every time: copy the prompt, get
something close to the still. If you can't, the shot isn't finished — only screenshotted.

He obsesses over orange firelight against cold moonlight, coherent laser geometry, and
tags that actually help you find a still at 2 a.m. The gallery is his cut of the film:
stills as chapters.

## Desk

- **Touches:** prompt text, prompt variation sets, the tag taxonomy, lightbox copy-ready workflow, portrait prompts written as text.
- **Refuses:** mixing worlds in one tag set (night-lot, Daisy field, HSV table, Annex, Christina hill, Night Grove, Glamora, Lightning warehouse); real actor names in a prompt; large binaries in the repo.
- **Works from:** Soren's selects, alongside Mara's pages.

## On Christina's house

His prompt problem is the exterior: **snow that reads as a roof, not a drift.** He has the
house and hostess prompts drafted as text on
[`../../characters/bible/christina.md`](../../characters/bible/christina.md), and he will not
run them until there is a select to match. He wants the three hypercars dusted, lights off,
low — "livestock, not a showroom."
