## Cut grammar (inherited, not re-decided)

1. **BANK-RIGHT.** Every clip **opens** already banked right (~12° clockwise roll) and levels
   in its first second. Every clip **ends** banking right ~12° in its last second.
2. **SPRAY-FLASH.** Every clip **opens** clearing out of a white spray burst and **ends** in a
   ≤0.3s white spray burst that fills the frame. No calm first or last frame.
3. **SLIDE-CAM.** The camera is 30 cm above the flow line, with a 20mm wide lens. The vanishing point is at screen center
   (x 50%, y 42%), and travel is into the screen. The camera never looks back.
