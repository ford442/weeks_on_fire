STYLE:

Sincere 1930s Paris manouche swing cabaret at 200 BPM, D minor, opening into D major for the last chorus, about two minutes ten, sung entirely in French. Two acoustic gypsy-jazz guitars, one playing the driving la pompe rhythm and one playing fast, glittering lead runs between lines, a slapped upright bass, a violin trading licks with the voice, and a clarinet. A muted brass stab section enters only on verse 2, a soft choir only on verse 2's third line, a harp glissando only on its fourth line, and a full orchestral swell with timpani only on verse 3. Lead one: bright, conspiratorial, fast, swinging, delighted with every pocket. Lead two: dry, slightly raspy, flat, answering every refrain line on the beat. Before the outro the band stops for one bar of silence, then a single soft triangle note sounds alone. Warm, crackly, small-club sound, earnest, no parody, no sound effects other than the triangle. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.

[Verse 1]
LILLITH: Dans la poche de mon manteau, j'ai un petit orchestre
Trois violons, un piano, un chef et tout le reste
Quand j'ouvre le bouton, ils jouent rien que pour moi
Quand je le referme, ils dorment au chaud, tout bas

Et dans mon gant gauche, une harpiste qui a froid

[Chorus]
LILLITH: Ouvre la poche, l'orchestre joue
RUBELLA: Ferme la poche, merci beaucoup

[Verse 3]
LILLITH: Sur le quai de la gare, j'ouvre ma valise en cuir
Un orchestre en grandeur nature commence à en sortir
Quatre-vingts musiciens, les pupitres, et le chef en frac
Et tout le train qui danse sur la voie, clic-clac

[Final Chorus]

NOTES:

**English gloss:**
- *V1:* In my coat pocket I have a little orchestra: three violins, a piano, a conductor and
  all the rest. When I undo the button they play just for me; when I do it up, they sleep in
  the warm, quietly.
- *Chorus:* "Open the pocket, the orchestra plays." / "Close the pocket, thank you very much."
  / "Open the pocket, it swings everywhere." / "Close the pocket. That's all."
- *V2:* In my handbag there's a brass section, six trumpets in a row that only know how to
  follow me. In my hat, a choir of eighteen voices. And in my left glove, a harpist who's cold.
- *V3:* On the station platform I open my leather suitcase. A full-size orchestra starts
  climbing out: eighty musicians, music stands, the conductor in tails. And the whole train
  dances on the rails, clickety-clack.
- *Final:* "Open the suitcase..." / "...Me too, I've got something."
- *Break:* one bar of silence, then one triangle *ting* (from Rubella's handbag).
- *Outro:* "That's all."

**Escalation:** pocket (a trio and a conductor) → handbag, hat, glove (brass, choir, harp,
each entering the arrangement as it's named) → suitcase (a full orchestra, and the track
swells to match). Wrong third: after all that, Rubella's whole contribution is one triangle.

**Do-not:** the tiny musicians are never in danger, never squashed, and stay small and real.
No conductor's face in close-up. No real orchestra or railway names.
