STYLE:

Vending-machine Motown / girl-group soul, 108 BPM, F-sharp minor. Dry close female lead (Lillith, bright, transactional, no belt). Three deadpan backing singers who only sing inventory counts — "three," "two," "one" — never "love," never "baby." Kick is a coin slot. Snare is a coil-release thunk. Tambourine on the ands. Baritone sax that plays the vending-motor hum as a riff. No strings. No key change. **Structural flaw:** a sampled coil-drop ding after every chorus, counting down remaining stock. Intro has three dings. After chorus 1, two. After chorus 2, one. After the final chorus, silence where the ding belongs; she still holds the last note as if the heart is coming. Do not add a fake ding in the outro to be kind. Mood: 2 AM sidewalk, one streetlamp, a machine that is better at accounting than anyone she knows. Runtime ~1:40–2:00. Do not use Rubella (she already has a dying device). Do not use Continuity Voice.

NOTES:

## What the song is
Lillith at a vending machine that sells lives. She is restocking, not praying. The cat from the cartoon parking lot (`life-vending`) is the usual customer; tonight she is doing the errand herself and does not want a witness. The dog with the newspaper can judge. The machine cannot. That is why she likes it.

The joke is inventory. Nobody says "nine lives." Nobody thanks the coil.

## Craft (keep this off-mic)
- *Nine Percent Battery* owns the dying-device love song. This is a **working** machine and a woman who can reach it. Do not chirp. Do not plead with the ceiling.
- *Heart Rate Optimal* owns the metric-as-god. This is **stock-as-manners**.
- *Receipt Dated Thursday* owns Biscuit's future-dated proof. The "receipt in my mouth" is a fish in a coin slot, not that gag.
- Pairing still: [`content/cartoons/life-vending.json`](../content/cartoons/life-vending.json). Promote together or not at all.

## Production notes
- **Episode placement:** EyeWash cartoon block / 2 AM sidewalk cutaway. Not an ident. Not a commercial.
- **Voice:** Lillith, bright and even. Backing singers count only. No "whoo."
- **Structural flaw:** coil-drop dings count down 3-2-1-0. The zero is silence. Do not resolve it.
- **Runtime target:** 1:40–2:00.
