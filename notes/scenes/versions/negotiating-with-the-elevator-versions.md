## V1 — Rubella Negotiates

**Status:** alt
**Beats covered:** B
**Register:** defect-report procedural instead of rapport procedural
**What it adds:** Lillith's method is charm; Rubella's is a repro case. Funnier if
you want the scene colder, but it loses the tango set-up — Lillith has to be the
one who wants something from the car, or Beat E has no singer.
**Risks:** Rubella becomes the active party for the whole scene. Do not promote
without rewriting E.

### B — Rapport (V1)

```
                         RUBELLA
          Steps to reproduce. One: two residents enter car
          two. Two: doors close on twelve. Three: nothing.
          (beat)
          Expected result — motion. Actual result — this.
          (beat)
          Severity two. Not one. One is a fire.

                    THE ELEVATOR (V.O.)
                         Twelve.

                         RUBELLA
          That's not a steps-to-reproduce. That's where we
          already are.

                         LILLITH
                    (delighted)
          You're doing it.

                         RUBELLA
          I am filing. There's a difference and it is the
          whole difference.
```

## V2 — Cold Open on the Doors

**Status:** alt
**Beats covered:** A
**What it adds:** Open on the doors already closed and the indicator already on
twelve, nine seconds of nothing, then "Twelve." Loses the boarding but gains the
cleanest cold open in the building-cast set.
**Risks:** The basket, the mug, and the speaker wire all have to be established
inside the car instead of carried in.
