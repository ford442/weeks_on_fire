# Downward Dog

**Genre collision:** full-on psytrance (145 BPM, rolling sixteenth-note bass, acid squelches, cosmic risers) × spa-and-yoga ambient (singing bowls, ocean wash, breathy bamboo flute) × electric sitar. It's a meditation tape that wandered onto a festival main stage at 5 AM.

**Visual concept:** A bright, calm yoga studio with a pale wooden floor, tall windows and plants. Eight students sit on mats. Rubella teaches from the front mat in black lace with a headset mic, completely calm. Lillith sits cross-legged in the corner playing an electric sitar-guitar. **Every pose Rubella calls becomes the real thing.** "Mountain pose": a snowy mountain rises through the middle of the floor. "Downward dog": a dog appears under every student, holding the same stretch. "Cat, cow": a cat and a cow. Then a polite cobra, a cloud of butterflies, an eagle on the ballet barre, and a camel at the door. By the final chorus the studio holds all of it, and on "Namaste" every animal bows, and so does the mountain.

**Laugh (kid words):** *Every yoga pose she says turns into the real animal, until there's a dog on every mat, a cow, a camel and a mountain in the room.*

**BPM:** 145, F♯ minor (psytrance drone on F♯), chorus lifts to A major

**Target length:** ~3:38 · 16 segments

**Video use map:**
- **Pattern:** verse on action (one pose per couplet, one new thing per pose), chorus on the hold (everyone holding the pose with the animals). Hard cuts on the downbeats.
- **Breaks:** music drops to singing bowls for the breakdown (Biscuit's exit).
- **Mouths:** Rubella's mouth is on for the chorus hook, the breakdown and "Namaste", and off elsewhere. Students' mouths stay closed. Lillith's mouth stays closed.
- **Diegetic sound:** none; mute Imagine audio.
