STYLE:

Warm joke metal, 120 BPM, D mixolydian, about one minute thirty. One riff on a warm amp, standard tuning: five hits, then a rest, then the same five hits. The five hits are the whole guitar part. They do not become six. Dry kit, no scream, no growl. Verses are one dry close voice reading a filing, committed, no belting: six points on a sheet the size of a plate, put in the wrong sleeve, category other, table-scale, a clerical error. Gang vocals on the chorus only, clean shout-sung, the words Wrong form, six times, against the five-hit riff. Structural flaw: the riff has five hits and the chant insists on six, every chorus, and neither side changes. Sing only the lines below. No djent, no dropped tuning, no blast beat, no occult lyric, no rite, no choir, no smoke, no Kenji, no product name. Mood: the form is wrong and the count stays wrong. Runtime about 1:30.
