## What it is

The unit, as the two of them actually live in it. One object per card. One room per card.
Nobody explains the building. The comedy is that the building has rules and the rules are
followed.

## What it is not

Not Christina hill, not the Annex, not the HSV table, not the Lightning warehouse, not
Night Grove, not Glamora. Not a party. Not act two. Not a tour.

## Locks

- **Max 3 bodies** in any card. Rubella and Lillith count as two.
- **Qing** may identify one object and leave. 3–8 words, a material, a number. No sting on
  her behalf. She does not adjudicate anything.
- **Pizza Guy** may arrive and not ask. "Order for [almost-right name]." Extra napkins are
  in the bag. Bag at chest height. No question that is not a menu question.
- **Madelyn** is voice and laminate only. No face, ever.
- **Kenji** does not amp the living room. He is not in the building.
- **Riley** does not retrieve. She is not in the building.
- **Christina** gets a fridge postcard at most.
- **Rubella**: dry, raspy, rationed. She cuts the scene. One line ends it.
- **Lillith**: brighter, starts the thing, sudden energy. Not a tour guide. Never explains
  a room to the audience.
- No mystical register. Nothing is haunted. It is unscheduled at most, and nobody says so.

### 1. `intercom-vs-laundry-music` — laundry, 35s

**Bodies:** Rubella, Lillith. **Object:** the intercom grille above the dryers.
**Bed:** `neutral-zone-laundry-bed` sketch (`songs/_drawer/`), the dryer tumble as the kick.

**Start JOB:** first dryer thunk. **Last JOB:** last intercom tick.

The laundry room has its own music: the dryer, a fluorescent hum, the bed. The intercom
chimes once every eight bars and it is a quarter-beat late. Lillith hears it first. She
tries to fix it by hand — tapping the grille, then tapping the dryer door, then both,
trying to get the two clocks to agree. They will not. Rubella walks past, turns the intercom
knob down one notch, and keeps walking.

> **Lillith:** It's almost on.
> **Rubella:** It's neutral. Leave it neutral.

The chime keeps going, quieter, still late. End on the grille.

**Do-not:** No Madelyn voice on the intercom (this is the chime, not her). No song with
lyrics. No third body. Not the Laundromat Saints.

### 2. `elevator-form-at-chest-height` — elevator, 25s

**Bodies:** Rubella, Lillith. **Object:** a clipboard on a chain, hanging from the brass
panel at exactly chest height.

**Start JOB:** doors close. **Last JOB:** doors open on the same floor.

A laminate on the clipboard: *SIGN AT CHEST HEIGHT.* Lillith signs, holding it where it
hangs. Rubella takes the pen and initials it without lowering her arm or raising the board.
Neither of them looks at what the form is for. The elevator has not moved.

> **Lillith:** Do we get a copy?
> **Rubella:** We are the copy.

Doors open. Same floor. They step out.

**Do-not:** Nobody explains the height. No counteroffer, no negotiation line (banked for
the elevator scene set). No unlisted floor. No Riley.

### 3. `qing-identifies-the-mailbox` — mailbox, 20s

**Bodies:** Rubella, Lillith, Qing (three — the cap). **Object:** the lobby mailbox bank.

**Start JOB:** Qing's hand on the mailbox face. **Last JOB:** the lobby door closes behind
her.

Lillith is trying to get the mailbox door to sit flush. Qing, passing through with a
noodle cup, stops, puts one finger on the door, and reads it like a date on a coin.

> **Qing:** It's brass. Plated. Approximately 1974.
> **Lillith:** Is that good?
> **Qing:** It's brass.

She leaves. Lillith looks at Rubella.

> **Rubella:** Good to know.

**Do-not:** No skull, no glow, no Episode 3. Qing does not open the box or fix it. No
sting under her line. Low, even, no upward inflection. Rubella's line is the cut.

### 4. `pizza-at-the-door` — apartment door, 25s

**Bodies:** Rubella, Pizza Guy. Lillith off camera. **Object:** the bag, at chest height.

**Start JOB:** two knocks. **Last JOB:** door closes.

The hallway light outside the unit is doing something it should not — flickering in a
pattern, or too warm, or gone. He is standing in it. Bag at chest height. Order correct.

> **Pizza Guy:** Order for Rebecca.
> **Rubella:** Close enough.
> **Pizza Guy:** Extra napkins are in the bag.

He does not look at the light. He does not ask about the light. Rubella takes the bag,
looks at the light for exactly one beat, and closes the door.

> **Lillith (off):** Was it the light again?
> **Rubella:** It was pizza.

**Do-not:** No name on the tag. No "careful," no "are you okay," no question that is not a
menu question. Phone at 23% if it is seen. He does not run. Not the Ultra Screech laundry
shoot — no shampoo, no camera crew. Not a second commercial.

### 5. `dryer-eats-one-lace-glove` — laundry, 30s

**Bodies:** Rubella, Lillith. **Object:** one lace glove.

**Start JOB:** dryer door opens. **Last JOB:** the laundry-room door closes.

Lillith pulls the load. Two lace gloves went in. One comes out. She checks the drum, the
lint screen, the drum again. Rubella watches from the folding table. Lillith holds the one
glove up. They look at each other. Rubella nods once. Lillith puts the one glove on, picks
up the basket, and they leave.

No line. Or one:

> **Rubella:** One's a look.

The dryer is not opened again. Nobody looks behind it.

**Do-not:** 1–1, they leave. No retrieval, no Riley, no second trip. No glove reappears
later in the season. Not the lint-inventory joke. The dryer does not hum a tone.

### 6. `mailbox-folder-returned-unopened` — mailbox to fridge, 20s

**Bodies:** Rubella, Lillith. **Object:** a thin folder with a Swedish address, stamped
*RETURN TO SENDER*, seal intact.

**Start JOB:** mailbox opens. **Last JOB:** magnet clicks on the fridge.

Lillith sent something to the house on the hill. It has come back unopened. She reads the
stamp twice. Rubella takes it, does not open it, and slides it into the drawer under the
kitchen counter. Lillith takes the postcard that came in the same bundle — a white house,
a lot of snow — and puts it on the fridge with the one magnet.

> **Lillith:** She didn't open it.
> **Rubella:** She didn't have to.

Magnet click. End on the postcard.

**Do-not:** Christina is a postcard at most. No hypercars, no gilt, no harpsichord, no
Swedish line. Nobody says her name. The folder is never opened on camera and never comes
back.
