# Fire Girls — short video ideas (drawer)

*Text only. 2026-11-18. Agent: claude. `promote?: no` on every card.*

**Framing note.** These are idea seeds, not locked boards. They give no Imagine prompts, no
shot timings and no lore. The reference stills set the look: young-Rubella on a red electric
guitar and young-Lillith carrying a red-and-white striped flag. They lead a troop of fire
girls in red coveralls with reflective stripes, red helmets, tubas, sousaphones, trumpets and
snares. Four settings: an alpine road by day, an old-town cobblestone street at night with
crowds, the same street with torches, smoke and fire cauldrons, and a forest-fire road at
night. The world is loud and the two leads are not. Nobody flinches above the waist. The
troop is background texture, not new cast: no faces get featured and nobody gets a name.

**Long-form generate-first prompts:** `prompts/fire-girls-generate-first.md` (2026-11-19 — #6, #1, #15)

**Lane locks for every card.** Rubella and Lillith only as featured cast. Canon black lace
shows at collar and cuffs under the coverall (see *Open questions*). Performance is flat:
Rubella endures, and Lillith's energy goes into the thing (the flag, the band, the
cauldron). Mouths stay off unless the card says it is a musical cutaway that needs a hook.
No mystical register, no wings, no real peril: fire stays scenery and never threatens
anyone. One kick or drop gives one visible change. One world per card, and the fire-girls
road/street is that world. This is not the building corridor band from
`songs/_drawer/Ring_Once.md` (that one is the elevator and dark-red tall hats). Picture
ideas farm to [#22](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/22). Songs farm to
[#18](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/18) /
[#33](https://github.com/ford442/weeks_on_fire/issues/33). Never #42.

**Callbacks spent here (as reference only, never as a set):**
- **The full extinguisher** (callback bank: *World's Okayest Tenant mug / … / the full
  extinguisher*, and *Ambient lighting with ambition*). It is carried full, ends full, never
  discharges and nobody mentions it. Spent in #3 and #11.
- **"Finally. Natural ventilation."** as a gesture: someone moves one small thing three
  inches instead of moving herself, flat, as if the disaster were an amenity. Spent in #7.
  No line is spoken.
- **Rubella's smoke-detector chirp** as a sting: one bare, in-tune chirp, never quantized.
  Spent in #14, once, off-screen.
- **World's Okayest Tenant mug** as a prop crossing over. Spent in #9.
- **The very-end reaction** engine (funny-rules): spent once per card where it is named.

## The ideas

### 1. Tuba Avalanche
- **Laugh:** One tuba toot makes a whole mountain of snow slide down, and it is all tubas.
- **Type:** 10–30s gag.
- **Core visual:** The alpine road by day. The troop marches toward camera. A lone sousaphone
  player in the back row hits one low note, and the snowy peak behind them rumbles.
- **Drop / change:** The avalanche comes down the slope as a smooth wave of brass tubas that
  roll to a stop politely at the road's edge (*multiplies*). Rubella keeps strumming. Lillith
  plants the flag.
- **Length:** about 15s. One 15s clip, trimmed to 12.
- **Why it stays in lane:** It is the alpine road set, the troop is the only crowd, and the
  "danger" is made of instruments.

### 2. Cobblestone Bounce
- **Laugh:** The drum hits so hard that every cobblestone jumps up like popcorn.
- **Type:** 3–6s joke interrupt.
- **Core visual:** The old-town street at night, with crowds on both sides. The snare line
  is mid-march. Lillith raises the flag one notch.
- **Drop / change:** On the snare hit, every cobblestone pops a foot into the air and lands
  back in place, perfectly (*reverses*). Nobody in the troop breaks step.
- **Length:** 6s clip, trimmed to 5.
- **Why it stays in lane:** It is one street, one hit and one physical gag. No props or
  people come in from other worlds.

### 3. The Extinguisher Solo
- **Laugh:** Everyone thinks the fire-girl is going to spray the fire, but she uses the
  extinguisher as a trumpet.
- **Type:** 10–30s gag.
- **Core visual:** The torch-and-cauldron street. A fire cauldron flares and a fire girl in
  the back row lifts a full red extinguisher. Rubella glances at it once.
- **Drop / change:** The extinguisher is played like a horn, a clean brass note comes out,
  and the cauldron flame bends to the beat (*transforms*). The extinguisher stays full and
  nothing is sprayed.
- **Length:** 10s clip, trimmed to 9.
- **Why it stays in lane:** It is the troop's own gear. The full extinguisher crosses over
  as the callback prop only.

### 4. Flag Wind
- **Laugh:** Lillith waves her flag so big that it blows the whole crowd's hats off.
- **Type:** 3–6s joke interrupt.
- **Core visual:** The night street with crowds. Lillith gives the striped flag one wide,
  calm sweep.
- **Drop / change:** Hats, scarves and one umbrella lift off the whole crowd and swirl away
  down the street (*falls / reverses*). Rubella's helmet does not move.
- **Length:** 6s clip.
- **Why it stays in lane:** The crowd is the street's own extras, and the action is a flag
  and the wind.

### 5. Wrong Third Instrument
- **Laugh:** Trumpet, trumpet, and then a girl marching with a whole grand piano.
- **Type:** 10–30s gag (rule of three).
- **Core visual:** The alpine road. The camera pans along the back row: a trumpet, then a
  trumpet, then a fire girl carrying an upright grand piano on her back at the same steady
  pace.
- **Drop / change:** On the kick, the piano lid flips open and the piano plays itself, one
  big chord (*appears*). Rubella keeps strumming without turning around.
- **Length:** 10s clip, trimmed to 8.
- **Why it stays in lane:** It is a marching-band joke on the band's own road. Note this is
  not the woods piano from the callback bank: no forest and no bathing cabinet.

### 6. Guitar Sparks, Cauldrons Light
- **Laugh:** Every time Rubella strums, another fire bowl lights up down the street.
- **Type:** musical cutaway (mouths off).
- **Core visual:** The torch street, dark at first, with a row of unlit cauldrons stretching
  away. Rubella strums one flat chord at a time.
- **Drop / change:** On the drop, all the remaining cauldrons light at once, front to back,
  like dominoes (*multiplies*). Rubella does not look up.
- **Length:** 15s clip, trimmed to 12.
- **Why it stays in lane:** Fire is set dressing on the troop's street. It is ambient
  lighting and puts nobody in peril.

### 7. Natural Ventilation
- **Laugh:** A whole roof flies off a house and Lillith just moves her flag over a little.
- **Type:** 3–6s joke interrupt.
- **Core visual:** The night street. The troop halts in front of an old-town house, and the
  sousaphones hit one blast.
- **Drop / change:** The house roof lifts off clean and floats away (*falls / reverses*).
  Lillith shifts the flagpole three inches to the left, as if the house had just gained an
  amenity. Nobody else moves.
- **Length:** 6s clip.
- **Why it stays in lane:** The callback is spent as a gesture only. There is no building
  interior and no ballroom dressing.

### 8. Tiny Fire Truck
- **Laugh:** The fire truck that shows up is the size of a toy, and the girls salute it.
- **Type:** 10–30s gag (wrong scale).
- **Core visual:** The forest-fire road at night, with a distant orange glow on the ridge
  that stays far away. A siren gets louder, and a fire truck the size of a shoebox rolls up
  between the boots.
- **Drop / change:** The tiny truck raises a ladder and sprays one little arc of water, and
  the whole distant ridge glow goes out at once (*transforms*). End reaction: Rubella blinks
  once.
- **Length:** 10s clip.
- **Why it stays in lane:** It uses the fire-girls set, and the fire is out of reach and
  never a threat.

### 9. Tea Break on the March
- **Laugh:** The whole band keeps marching, but Rubella's mug never spills a drop.
- **Type:** visual experiment.
- **Core visual:** The alpine road. Rubella marches with the guitar slung and a mug in one
  hand (*World's Okayest Tenant*, the print barely visible). The road tilts steeper, then
  steeper again.
- **Drop / change:** The road turns fully vertical and the troop marches straight up the
  cliff face. The mug's surface stays level (*transforms*).
- **Length:** 15s clip, trimmed to 12.
- **Why it stays in lane:** The mug crosses over as a prop only, with no laundry and no
  basement.

### 10. Goats Join the Band
- **Laugh:** Mountain goats march along and keep perfect time on little drums.
- **Type:** 10–30s gag (animals doing human jobs).
- **Core visual:** The alpine road by day. Three mountain goats with snare drums strapped on
  step out of the rocks and fall into the rear rank.
- **Drop / change:** On the kick, there are suddenly forty goats, a full goat drumline
  (*multiplies*). Lillith tips the flag to them once.
- **Length:** 10s clip.
- **Why it stays in lane:** The animals are local to the alpine set. None is a named animal
  from another world (no Biscuit, no mountain lion).

### 11. The Fire That Wants In
- **Laugh:** A cauldron flame keeps hopping into the band like it wants to march too.
- **Type:** 10–30s gag (things that keep happening).
- **Core visual:** The torch street. A small flame leaps out of a cauldron and lands on a
  trumpet bell, then on a helmet, then on Lillith's flag tip, harmlessly and like a
  candle. A fire girl walks beside it with a full extinguisher and never uses it.
- **Drop / change:** On the drop, the flame jumps onto Rubella's guitar headstock and stays
  there like a tiny lantern (*appears*). End reaction: Rubella gives it one side glance.
- **Length:** 15s clip.
- **Why it stays in lane:** It is a slapstick flame that never burns anything, and the
  callback is the never-used extinguisher.

### 12. Crowd Wave Goes Too Far
- **Laugh:** The crowd does a wave and it keeps going up the church, the clock tower and the
  moon.
- **Type:** 3–6s joke interrupt.
- **Core visual:** The night street. The crowd starts a stadium wave as the band passes.
- **Drop / change:** The wave keeps traveling up the facades, the bell tower and into the
  sky, and the moon dips and comes back (*reverses*). The troop does not look up.
- **Length:** 6s clip.
- **Why it stays in lane:** It is the street's own crowd and sky, with nothing imported.

### 13. Smoke Formation
- **Laugh:** The smoke from the fire pots makes a picture of the band, and it is marching
  too.
- **Type:** visual experiment.
- **Core visual:** The torch street, thick with smoke. The smoke above the troop slowly
  sharpens into a giant smoke copy of the band.
- **Drop / change:** On the drop, the smoke band turns and marches the other way
  (*reverses*). Nobody below looks.
- **Length:** 10s clip.
- **Why it stays in lane:** It is a pure picture of smoke shapes. Keep it physical and
  playful, never ghostly, with no mystical register.

### 14. One Chirp
- **Laugh:** The whole loud band stops dead because one tiny beep went off.
- **Type:** 3–6s joke interrupt.
- **Core visual:** The forest-fire road at night, at full brass volume with the glow far
  off. Off-screen comes one bare, in-tune chirp.
- **Drop / change:** The entire troop freezes mid-step in one frame (*reverses*: motion to
  stillness happens on the cut, and the change is the freeze itself, every boot in the air).
  Hard cut to black.
- **Length:** 6s clip, trimmed to 4.
- **Why it stays in lane:** The chirp crosses over as a sound sting only. No detector, no
  building and no notice appear.

### 15. Helmet Rain
- **Laugh:** It starts raining helmets, and they all land right on the girls' heads.
- **Type:** 10–30s gag (literal-ish "hats off").
- **Core visual:** The alpine road. The troop marches bareheaded and carries nothing on top.
  One red helmet falls from a clear blue sky and lands on Lillith's head.
- **Drop / change:** On the kick, hundreds of red helmets fall at once, and each one drops
  onto a head with a clean clack (*falls*). Rubella's lands last. End reaction: she adjusts
  it a quarter inch.
- **Length:** 10s clip.
- **Why it stays in lane:** It is a uniform gag inside the troop. The look sits on the
  reference stills, and no outside props come in.

### 16. The Band Gets Smaller
- **Laugh:** Every time they turn a corner the band shrinks, until it marches past a cat.
- **Type:** 10–30s gag (wrong scale).
- **Core visual:** The night street. The troop rounds a corner and comes back half size,
  then rounds another and comes back quarter size.
- **Drop / change:** On the third corner the troop is tiny and marches under a sitting
  street cat, and the cat stays still (*transforms*). Rubella and Lillith are still in
  front, still flat.
- **Length:** 15s clip.
- **Why it stays in lane:** The cat is an ordinary stray, not the Black Cat from the
  callback bank: no second look, and none of its lore.

## Generate-first three

1. **#6 Guitar Sparks, Cauldrons Light.** Its start still is the torch-street reference
   with the cauldrons unlit, so it needs the fewest new elements. The domino lighting is an
   easy-to-read single drop and it works muted.
2. **#1 Tuba Avalanche.** The alpine still is already strong. It tests wrong-scale and
   multiplication in one move, and the laugh is clear to a kid.
3. **#15 Helmet Rain.** It sits on the established look, its drop is a clean "falls" with a
   satisfying sound, and the very-end adjust gives Rubella one deadpan beat.
