STYLE:

Dark cabaret ruin ballad at 72 BPM. Wet upright piano, slightly detuned, soft pedal. One low cello that does not bow on the downbeat. Distant plaster-dust as the high-end texture — never bright, never a cymbal. Rubella, close-mic, almost spoken, bored glamour, no belting, no vibrato. Room reverb of a ballroom that has already lost a wall. **Structural flaw:** every verse holds one extra bar after the last lyric line — the roof has not fallen yet, and the bar is the wait. Do not fill the extra bar with a fill, a harmony, or a witty tag. Silence plus dust. The extra bar never resolves into the next section on time; the piano arrives late like a beam that sagged and then held. Mood: finally, natural ventilation. Runtime ~2:00–2:20. Use the Rubella voice. Optional Lillith only as a room echo on the last “wait,” not a duet.

NOTES:

**What the song is**
A ruin inventory for The Two. The roof has not fallen; it is **waiting**, which is a kind of hospitality. “Finally. Natural ventilation.” is load-bearing and must stay deadpan. Scratchpad hook: *We said we'd leave before the roof came down / Turns out the roof knows how to wait.*

Cheap to picture: one ballroom, one hole, two glasses, berries, plaster.

**Craft (keep this off-mic)**
- *Neon Exit Sign* owns mascara and the burning-town walk. This is **the ceiling**, not the road.
- *Empty Parking Lot Waltz* owns aftermath in a lot. This is aftermath that is still a room.
- *The Pour* owns hired climate (foundry). Do not import molten iron.
- *House of Mirrors Rally* owns the ping-pong metronome and the mascara beat. Do not score the rally. No paddles.
- Do not belt “wait.” Do not make the extra bar a joke fill.

**Production notes**
- **Episode placement:** Standalone musical cutaway; visual cousin to the abandoned ballroom in `notes/scenes/house-of-mirrors-rally.md` without replacing that scene’s bed. The Two as silhouettes or red-lip close-ups, not named on screen.
- **Voice:** Rubella. Optional Lillith room-echo on last “wait” only.
- **Structural flaw:** Extra bar after every verse (and the bridge). Piano late into the next section. Never corrected.
- **Runtime target:** 2:00–2:20.
