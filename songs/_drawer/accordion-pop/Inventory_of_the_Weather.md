STYLE:

Upbeat reed-organ pop, 124 BPM, G mixolydian, about one minute fifty. Poemesque field list, deadpan and sincere, no wink at the listener. Lead instrument is a small pump reed organ playing a bouncing staccato left hand and a bright, slightly wheezy right-hand hook. A light snare played on rim and brushes, upright bass walking in two, a tambourine on the offbeat in the last verse only. Vocal: Rubella solo, dry and level, close mic, conversational, each item landing squarely on a beat, plainly sung, no belting, no chant, no plainchant, no held notes, no rise on any word, no harmony, no backing vocals. The first line is a full sentence. Every line after it is a bare noun phrase with no verb. Room is a small wooden room, close mic, reed organ slightly forward. No guitar, no piano, no strings, no horns, no choir, no drone, no wind sound effect, no field recording, no laughter, no mugging. Structural flaw: the list repeats the phrase wind from the west, as item four and again as item nine, sung identically both times, and nothing in the arrangement marks it.

NOTES:

The flaw: sixteen list lines, numbered by position across the four verses; item one is the only sentence and items two to sixteen are noun phrases. Item four and item nine are the same words. The repeat is never marked, never corrected, and never answered with a different word.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not From the West.** That is Riley's plainchant: Dorian on D, one voice, no pulse, "still" rising once. Here Rubella sings it with a beat, a reed organ and a snare. No chant, no neume, no rising word, no hut, no Kestrel Ridge. "Wind from the west" is borrowed as an item only because the card asks for it as the repeat.
- **Not Two of Three.** It also sings "Wind from the west" and the card list omits it. This song does not take its other lines ("Last grade is on the card", "The hut is shut", "It is still there").
- **Not The Honest Height.** The date-and-wind-direction title ("Southwest. Ten, maybe twelve.") stays unspent. No direction, no wind speed.
- **Not Riley.** She is ridge-only and never cues a musical cutaway. Rubella sings this, in the building's weather, not the field notebook's.
- **Not the filing jurisdiction.** No "indoor weather", no category, no complaint form. The weather is only listed.
- **Not Ground Rent / Article Seven.** No passacaglia, no clock, no fourteen minutes.
