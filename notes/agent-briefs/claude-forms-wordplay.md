## Cards

- **Organum Stair.** Parallel organum, Latin, a cappella. One sentence: "Lux in scala manet." Nilsson on D, Lillith a fifth above, moving in parallel. Syllabic, no melisma, no English. Flaw: on the third statement the fifth becomes a fourth and stays a fourth. Nobody retunes. Not *The Light in the Stair* (that one is imitation, and Rubella is the low entry). Not Christina's hill.
- **Missing Click.** Hocket. The cell is tih / kah / tah / koh, split so no singer has two syllables in a row. Lillith and Rubella only. Flaw: "tah" has no singer. The hole stays. Not *Four Clicks* (that one is imitation, late and low). Not Marble's instrumental bed.
- **Keh Tih Keh.** Phonetic palindrome. The cell is keh tih keh. It reads the same backward. Sung forward, then the pitches reverse, then forward again. Flaw: the reverse pass is a whole step low and the next forward pass does not correct it. No famous palindrome. No Sator, no Panama, no Madam I'm Adam — *Tattarrattat* owns that knock.
- **Drip on the Landing.** Onomatopoeia as the lyric. The only word is plink. It is the drip, and it is the pulse. One piano note under it. Flaw: the drip is late by a sixteenth and the piano is on the grid. Not a knock. Not *Tattarrattat*. Not *The Landing Stays Dark* (that one has English and a chorus bass).
