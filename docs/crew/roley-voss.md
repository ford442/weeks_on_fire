## Bio

Roley owns *Marble Tick Tack*: the cabinet chorus, the night-lot, the magnets that orbit,
hop, and never notice the episode regenerating around them. The lane is his end to end —
segment bible, punch-in maps, the *Wrong Sets* sibling — and it runs on mechanical rules,
not jokes: one lot, no creatures, Rodin, tower, ferrofluid rain.

His one standing order is the one everyone repeats: **the cabinet must come home.** It does
not visit the HOA clubhouse, the laundry room, the space lounge, or anybody else's cutaway.
It comes home to its own room.
