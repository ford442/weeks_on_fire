## Accordion pop

They Might Be Giants as a method, not a cover and not a parody. Upbeat, poemesque, short lines, specific nouns, a small argument with itself, deadpan sincerity. Rotate the bed. Major or mixolydian. 104–132. Two voices max (Lillith brighter, Rubella drier) unless the card says otherwise. No belting. No wink at the listener. One structural flaw.

- **The Other Button.** Accordion. The elevator has two buttons. Both say lobby. The lyric is the argument about which one is the other one.
- **Inventory of the Weather.** Reed organ and a light snare. A field list, bouncy, nouns only after the first line. Not Riley's plainchant (*From the West*). She may be the singer. She does not chant. Flaw: the list repeats "wind from the west" as item four and item nine.
- **Left the Light.** Nylon guitar. Laundry corridor, one bulb, the poem is whether leaving it on is a decision. Flaw: the last line is the first line and the guitar has already ended.
