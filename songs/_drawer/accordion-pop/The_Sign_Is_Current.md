STYLE:

Upbeat toy-piano march, 104 BPM, B-flat mixolydian, about one minute thirty. Short lines, specific nouns, deadpan sincerity, a small argument about what "current" means. Toy piano plays a small march in two-bar phrases. A shoebox snare marks the march. No brass band, no accordion, no guitar. Two female voices, no belting, no wink. Lillith brighter. Rubella drier. Do not sing their names. The only subject is a paper sign that says on break, and that the sign is current: this week's tape, ink that has not curled, words that match the door today. One voice treats current as the tape. The other treats current as the words. Structural flaw: the march resolves to the tonic in the last bar, a clean cadence, and the last lyric line stops before its predicate. Do not finish the sentence. Do not add a button. Sing only the lines below. No spoken time, no clock, no union hall, no lawn, no product name. Mood: the sign is current and the sentence is not done. Runtime about 1:30.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: the march resolves and the lyric does not. The toy piano cadences. The last line has no predicate. Do not complete it in the mix, and do not add a chorus after it.
