### The visit as it now stands

The visit is **two times of day.** Noon porch first. Later the same day it is dark, and that cut is
the walk-in on the song. The thirty-second night door (beat 2, "Thirty Seconds") is **the second
beat, not the whole visit.**

is cracked, the three cars are asleep, and a little harpsichord is playing. It is not the aria. One
or two Swedish friends are already there. The ladies step in, the marble files two clacks, and the
song is promised for later.

At **night** the ladies walk in on the song. The aria is *Det Begravda Huset*. The door beat is one
of Versions A, B or C below. Then mouths go off and the song holds.

**After the last *Ah*** the continuo comes back up. There are a few lines, and then they leave. She
stays in the white and does not follow them past the cars. The friends stay.

### Contradictions and soft spots

Ranked by how much they affect this scene. Rulings received from the owner on 2026-10-08 are marked.

**C1. The night-home is a party by the house's own count.** *Resolved, owner 2026-10-08.* The
September seats (accompanist + listener + both ladies + Christina) came to five faces. The fix: the
**accompanist is hands and back only** (this page's fix holds), and the **Friend is the one extra
face**. So there are four faces at most, and Christina never shares a frame with both ladies and the
Friend (lock 20).

**C2. Speaking across the aria.** *Ruled, owner 2026-10-08:* **"She may speak in the rests. Guests
may talk across the sung notes. She does not."** The asymmetry stays a joke: the guests talk across
the song and the hostess waits for the gaps.

**C3. "Heard first" vs. ducked.** *Dissolved by the noon porch.* The harpsichord is heard first
at noon, alone, "a little harpsichord, not the aria." At night the continuo ducks about 10 dB under
*Det Begravda Huset* and comes back up after the last *Ah*. It is a Nova call on the exact number.

**C4. *Hovsångare* is the masculine form.** A female court singer in Swedish is a
**hovsångerska**. All four September files say *hovsångare*. It's either a spelling bug or the
clerk got the title almost right. Either way it belongs to clerk night. **It is not spent on this
door** (lock 33).

**C5. Drift vs. roof.** The bible exterior says "from outside it could be a drift," and beat 1 says
"a drift that isn't." Elio's note on issue-22 says it "has to read as a **roof, not a drift**." The
noon board (Lt3Td) and night hill (JyN7M) are the reference. Don't invent a new dome.

**C6. Is the accompanist ever seen?** *Settled:* **hands and back only, never a face.** The bible's
"she" is not used. There are no names on the sleeve.

**C7. Saying "Thirty seconds" out loud.** *Holds.* The bible's sample exchange has Rubella say the
number. **Nobody speaks it.** Rubella measures in other units.

**C8. The seam is Qing's.** *Holds.* Lillith **looks** toward where the gilt stops and never reaches
for it.

**C9. Who opens the door?** *Rewritten.* At noon the gold door is **already cracked** and Christina
is **already outside** on the snow. At night the ladies walk in on the song. Nobody is shown opening
it either time.

**C10. Subtitles.** *Ruled:* **her spoken Swedish only, never the aria.** The Friend's Swedish glosses
itself in the next English line ("De kom ner. Good."), so it gets no subtitle. The accompanist's
*Ja* needs none.

**C11. Other nights (not this scene, noted while reading).** On Nova night, Nova sits in the gilt
hall listening to the continuo. If the accompanist is playing live, that's Christina plus two.
Wrap-student night has the same problem. The faceless accompanist fixes both.

**C12. The callback bank has no Christina entries.** It was last refreshed 2026-10-05. The bank's
own rule is "add the path here in the same commit." Not done here, per instructions. It's a
follow-up for whoever promotes.

**C13. The Friend's *Inte förlåt*.** *(new, flag for owner)* It refuses Lillith's apology. It isn't
a *no* to a Lillith offer, and it isn't Christina's line, so lock 26 holds. It's flagged so nobody
"fixes" it into Christina's mouth.

## 3. Three versions of the night door

**These are night-door options after the porch. They don't replace it.** Each is the 30s door beat
between the walk-in lines and the song hold.

### Shared rules for all three

- **Plates.** The ladies are in the gilt. Christina is in the white. Christina never shares a frame
  with both ladies and the Friend. The accompanist is hands and back only.
- **Clock.** At 68 BPM a 4/4 bar lasts 3.53s, so **the night door is eight and a half bars.**
- **Walk-in.** Every version opens on the owner's night lines ("The door is warm." / "The house
  isn't.") under the first bars of *Det Begravda Huset*. She does not answer them.
- **Mix.** The continuo ducks about −10 dB under the aria for the whole door. The aria runs on
  through the song hold, and **the last *Ah* lands while the ladies are still in the house.** Then
  comes the after-dialog with the continuo back up.
- **Exits move.** Each version's old exit material (A4–A5, B3) is now the **leaving coda**, after
  "We're not staying for the encore." The door rows stay as the walk-in.
- **Rests.** "Rest" means a breath between sung phrases. Times are proposals. Nova moves the
  **line** to the nearest real rest in *Det Begravda Huset* and never the rest to the line. **She
  speaks only in rests. The guests may talk across sung notes.**
- **Her mouth.** Spoken Swedish lipsync is the riskiest thing Grok Imagine will be asked to do here.
  Each version says how it handles that. Version C avoids it entirely.
- **Generation.** Generate silent. Lay ***Det Begravda Huset*** and the **Wooden House continuo** in
  post, as two files and never one stem. Add this negative block to every prompt:
  > *no crowd, no jewelry, no crown, no tiara, no fjord, no village, no chimney smoke, no lit windows, no headlights, no television, no hallway, no likeness to any real singer or celebrity*

  Frames with only the principals also add *no other people*. Frames that hold the Friend or the
  accompanist's hands add *no third face* instead.

### Version A — "Agreed"

**Comic angle: two professionals of the threshold.** Rubella keeps a private list of every door in
the building and how long it's safe to stand at each one. The house keeps a secret number. They
**agree on everything without either side naming the number**. All the comedy is agreement: she
welcomes them, they decline to stay, she approves, and then they stay for the song anyway, which is
exactly as long as she meant.

**Her *Nej* agrees with Rubella's "We're not staying."** It is not an answer to a Lillith offer.
Christina never tells Lillith no. "We're not staying" comes back after the last *Ah* as "We're not
staying for the encore."

gilt door at night, yV2gK for the white hold.
**Her mouth:** she's on camera in A2 and A5. Every line is two words or fewer and lands in a rest,

### Version B — "Nothing to Fix"

**Comic angle: a mechanic's ear in a house that already works.** Building-cast says what breaks
Lillith: "being handed something beautiful that already works, with nothing to fix." This version
is that sentence played out. Lillith checks the house the way she'd check a building: the flame,
the gold, the pitch. Everything is fine. Even her knock comes back in tune. She starts to offer

## 4. Swappable beats

Each of these can drop into any night-door version. Every line is Swedish with a subtitle, or
English from the ladies or the Friend.

*Cut in the 10-08 revision:* the old **hostess gift** (because there is no vacuum tube in the house)
and the old **shoes / buckle-off** beat (superseded by the clack fact; shoes stay on).

**1. "Välkommen åter."** *(for A or B; it's already C's closer)*
Replace A5's *Tack för att ni kom* or Coda 1's *Hej då* with **Välkommen åter.** *(Come again.)* The
shop-door goodbye. She has said "welcome" twice and meant two opposite things.
*Risk:* none. Don't use it in C as well as its own closer.

**2. "Load-bearing."** *(any gilt shot)*
**LILLITH** *(looking up at a ceiling that is snow)*: Is the roof—
**RUBELLA:** Load-bearing.
Callback: *Natural Ventilation* Beat D ("load-bearing, not a punchline"). It also answers Elio's
note in dialogue: the snow is a roof.
*Risk:* "Nobody rules the building safe or unsafe" is a rule for the building. This is the house,
and Rubella's line describes it without ruling on it. Deliver it flat.

**3. Rubella closes the door.** *(already in A4 and C2; for B, between B2 and B3)*
Rubella pulls the carved door shut from inside, gently, with one hand. The latch clicks and the gown
pile goes still at the same instant. Callback: *the never-closed gate* (Forest Road 4N22). Rubella,
who keeps a list of every door, is the one who shuts this one.
*Risk:* don't show anyone opening it.

**4. "Extended dwell is an amenity."** *(A3 or C1)*
**LILLITH** *(quoting the building packet, hopefully)*: Extended dwell is an amenity.
**RUBELLA:** Not here.
Callback: *Madelyn's laminated memo* ("Extended dwell is an amenity. It's in the packet. Page
four."). Madelyn can't come up the hill, but her memo crosses lanes as a line, which the lane rule
allows.
*Risk:* "Not here" is Rubella to Lillith, not Christina. It's a correction, not the one *no*. Keep
it flat and kind.

**5. The Friend, unnamed.** *(C1 or any gilt shot)*
**LILLITH** *(squinting past the gold toward someone in the white)*: Who's—
**RUBELLA:** Not ours.
The Friend was here first, so "Not ours" is accurate. It names no one, so there are still no names on
the sleeve.
*Risk:* the ladies can only see into the white because the house "folded." Keep it a glance, and
never an eyeline match that puts Christina, both ladies and the Friend in one frame.

**6. "Gå försiktigt i backen."** *(replace "Tack för att ni kom" in any version, or Coda 1's Hej då)*
**CHRISTINA** *(rest)*: Gå försiktigt i backen. *(Careful on the hill.)*
**RUBELLA**: Always.
A farewell phrased as weather advice. At four words it fits her two-to-seven.
*Risk:* it nudges toward "refuses questions about the house." It's a statement, not an answer, so
it's fine. Don't follow it with anything about the cars; *Lambon sover* already has them.

## Recommendation

**Order:** the noon porch (Lt3Td, D6d1m) → night walk-in (JyN7M) → **Version C** as the night door →
song hold (yV2gK) → after the last *Ah* (PtDqx) → **Coda 2**.

**Version C** is the strongest picture and the cheapest to make: two generations, no lipsync, and the
one cut does the "two centuries" work by itself. Now that the evening really happens, its door-front
goodbye pays off on "We stayed for the dark part." **Version A** is the runner-up. Its *Nej* is the
cleanest agreement with Rubella, and it ends on Rubella's latch. **Version B** develops a character
the most, and it's the only one that shows the accompanist's hands.
