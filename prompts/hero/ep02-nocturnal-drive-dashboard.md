# H04 · Nocturnal drive, dashboard at 3:44 (Ep2)

**Shot:** H04
**Episode / beat:** Episode 02, 10:41–13:15 *Nocturnal Drive* → *Mercury Blur and Drifting Signals* →
*Winding Down* · [`episodes/episode-02/synopsis.md`](../../episodes/episode-02/synopsis.md) ·
[`songs/Quarter_to_Seventy-Five.md`](../../songs/Quarter_to_Seventy-Five.md) (music-video treatment)
**Why it matters:** the liminal poetry half of Episode 2 and the source of the series' 3:44 motif
(the EyeWash sign-off, the master-control clock). The dashboard is the only light; the city
smears into notes; the same café comes round again. It has to feel half-asleep without going soft.
**Locks:** [`house-negative`](../locks/house-negative.lock.md) only. No recurring character is in
frame: the driver is anonymous (hands at most), and is **not** The Two.
**Aspect / model:** 16:9 · `grok-imagine-image-quality` (repo default)

**Geography (all three framings):** an older sedan with a full analog dash (sweep gauges, a sliding
needle AM/FM tuner, a small round dash clock) on a wet two-lane road through a sleeping town.
On the right, the same small corner café, lit, chairs up, passed again; behind it a stone clock
tower with a lit face reading 3:44. Mercury-vapor streetlamps (blue-white) stretch into blur. The
dashboard's green glow is the only light inside the car. Wipers mid-sweep. Yellow center lines.

**Clock rule:** 3:44 is shown with **hands, not digits**: hour hand three-quarters of the way from
the three to the four, minute hand four ticks past the eight. Faces are numberless (baton markers)
so the text rule holds. Not 4:17: that is *Empty Highway Confession*, a different drive.

**Callbacks spent (as image, not dialog):** 3:44 (tower and dash, identical) · the same café passed
again · the FM needle drifting left · the mercury blur.
