STYLE:

Absurdist chamber pop / art-gallery performance piece, 92 BPM. Polite cello and viola ostinato, very serious. Espresso machine hiss sampled as hi-hat. Spoken-word verses over the quartet. Fog-release: strings detune slightly; a chorus of gallery guests hum one note wrong on purpose. End: everything snaps back to normal on a single polite clap. Intimate spoken female lead, Laurie Anderson gravity, never belted. Mood: pretentious until it is accidentally sincere, then collective denial. Use a dry close spoken voice (Justine-adjacent) with a polite quartet that refuses to smile.


LYRICS:

[Intro — Ostinato]
(Cello + viola, espresso hiss as hi-hat)

[Verse 1 — Spoken]
The melon asks what you'll absorb tonight
I absorbed the truth and the canapés
The tubes are honest. The fog is on time.
Artforum says it's brave.

[Chorus]
The melon asks
What you'll absorb tonight

And the canapés

[Verse 2 — Spoken]
Ninety seconds of weather that tells the truth
Someone proposes. Someone files. Someone hates berries.
My watch says be present. I am. Unfortunately.
Justine eats a cracker like a verdict.

[Chorus]
The melon asks
What you'll absorb tonight
I absorbed the truth
And the canapés
The fog lifts
And we pretend we're fine
Artforum says
It's brave

[Bridge — Detuned]
(Strings sag. Guests hum one note wrong.)
I absorbed my ex-wife's lawyer. Does that count.
The melon does not answer. That is the installation.

[Outro]
(One polite clap. Quartet in tune again. Nobody makes eye contact.)


NOTES:

Gallery Fog / Caffeinated Melon scene. Starts pretentious, becomes uncomfortably sincere, ends in collective denial — comedy with a bruise. Structural flaw: during the fog the quartet is a quarter-tone flat; the clap snaps it back and nobody mentions the confessions. Visual: melon on pedestal with tubes and fog; guests frozen mid-confession; Justine in sharp focus eating crackers while the world blurs. Ready for Minimax.
