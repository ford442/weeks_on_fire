# Softcore — Lace Waterfall

**Band:** Lace Waterfall (Lillith + Rubella)  
**Type:** joke-metal / bubblegum hybrid (metalcore × IDM glitch × ABBA-teen pop)  
**Length:** ~2:30  
**Joke:** enormous breakdowns resolve into a sweet, completely deadpan hook. Heavy but in fleece.  
**Visual anchor:** oversized puffy plush tiger onesies, hoods up.  
**Farm:** songs #18/#33, picture #22, not #42. Worlds do not mix.

---

## Sound lock
- Lillith: chunky drop-C chugs + squealing pinch harmonics.
- Around her: IDM glitch (chopped stutters, granular blips, Squarepusher hi-hat rolls) wrapped in bright ABBA-teen pop (stacked “ooh” harmonies, sing-along chorus, disco four-on-the-floor under the heavy parts).
- Deadpan vocal throughout. The size contrast is the joke.

Generator prompt seed (Suno/Udio style):  
`metalcore x IDM x 70s Swedish pop, drop C chugging guitars, glitchy stutter edits, granular blips, disco four-on-the-floor in choruses, stacked female harmonies, deadpan female vocal, bubblegum hook, half-step key change, 150 bpm`

---

## Structure
1. Intro — music box plays the chorus melody, glitches/stutters. One palm-muted chug, everything stops.
2. Verse 1 — tight chugs + skittering IDM beat. Rubella flat and bored, close to the mic.
3. Pre-chorus — guitar drops out. Twinkly arps + handclaps (“Waterloo”-style).
4. Chorus — full pop + full metal at once. Hook: “It’s soft, it’s core, it’s softcore”
5. Verse 2 — same as V1 but beat chopped into stutters.
6. Breakdown — slow and crushing. “BLEGH” is a polite little “meow.” Guitar glitched into off-beat stutters.
7. Guitar solo — shred, then pitch-shifts up into chiptune.
8. Final chorus — key change up a half step (the full ABBA move). End on a single chug and silence.
