STYLE:

Lo-fi early-1980s-style post-punk demo, recorded on a cassette four-track in a small rehearsal room, 138 BPM, A minor, about one minute fifty. A cheap drum machine with a clanky snare, a trebly chorus-pedal bass carrying the riff, one scratchy clean guitar playing a two-note hook, and a toy synth plinking one note. Tape hiss, slight warble, a bit of room echo, a mistake left in. Lead vocal: a younger, rawer version of a dry, slightly raspy female voice, cool and deadpan, half-spoken, like reading from a clipboard. A second, younger bright female voice shouts "It passed!" from further from the mic each time. Ends abruptly when the tape runs out mid-bar. Earnest, unpolished, no parody, no comedy sound effects. Use Rubella and Lillith voices, both younger.

NOTES:

**Canon used:** Rubella's backstory in `characters/building-cast.md`: a low-level quality
assurance inspector for a mega-corp whose products were "technically" safe. This is the
song she wrote about it, before the company deleted its HR department and before the
building.

**The flaw:** the tape runs out mid-sentence. The fourth product is never named.

**Do-not:** no real company or product brands. The stickers say PASS and nothing else. The
song doesn't explain how Rubella and Lillith met (see the folder README).
