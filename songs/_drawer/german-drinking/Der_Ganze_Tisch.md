STYLE:

German party Schlager, Ballermann table song, 120 BPM, major key. Accordion and brass, simple driving drums with a hit on every AUF and RUNTER, male lead vocal, whole crowd shouting the answers, a tempo push in the bridge, festive and loud, traditional instruments, no electronic production. About 1:35. Simple German. Sing only the lines below. No character names, no product.
