## Cards

- **Canon Latch.** Canon at the fifth, Latin. The line is "Sera manet." Leader is Nilsson. Follower is Lillith, one bar later, a fifth up. Syllabic, no English, no melisma. Flaw: the follower never catches the leader, and the piece ends while she is still one bar behind. Not *The Clause* (that one is secco, and the aria never starts). Not Christina's hill.
- **Ah Eh Ah.** Phonetic palindrome round. The cell is ah eh ah. Three entries, each a bar apart, unison. Flaw: the third entry starts on the second syllable and never finds the first. No Latin lexicon. Not *Open Vowel* (that one is a lied, and oo is withheld).
- **Tenet.** One English word, because it is already a palindrome. Sung forward, then the pitches run backward, then forward. No second word. No Sator square, no Panama, no Madam I'm Adam — *Tattarrattat* owns the knock and that name. Flaw: the backward pass is a whole step low and the return does not lift it.
- **Thunk After.** Onomatopoeia as a pedal. The only lyric is thunk. It is the dryer, not a kick drum. A lied-scale piano holds one note. Flaw: the thunk continues two bars after the piano has cadenced. Not *Double Kick the Dryer* (that card is joke metal). Not Marble's click bed.
