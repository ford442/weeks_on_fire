STYLE:

German party Schlager anthem, 110 BPM, major key. Accordion lead, bright brass fanfare on each "Hoch!", simple four-on-the-floor drums, male lead vocal, crowd shouting "Hoch!", festive and warm, traditional carnival band, no synth, no electronic production. About 1:40. Simple German. Sing only the lines below. No character names, no product.
