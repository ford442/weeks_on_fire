# Mara Vell

**Role:** Writer · **Location:** Santa Fe, NM · **On series:** since Episode 01
**Portrait:** `public/cast/mara-vell.jpg` · **Specialty:** mythic dialogue & scene rhythm

## Bio

Mara drafts *Weeks on Fire* as fever-dream road diaries — short enough to cut on a beat,
strange enough to linger after the drop. Before the series she wrote late-night radio
dramas and desert-noir stage pieces that never quite fit a conventional stage, and she
still writes like the room is dark and the listener is driving.

**Every idea on the series enters as her text.** A Mara page says what a cutaway is, what it
is not, and which locks hold, in that order, and it says it in short paragraphs a stranger
can read at the snow line. She treats every musical cutaway as a character beat, not a
pause, and builds arcs around the spaces between lyrics.

She keeps a drawer. New titles go in it, not in the live picker. Most of them stay there,
and she considers that the drawer working.

## Desk

- **Touches:** pages — `ideas/*.md`, scene packets, the *what it is / is not* frame, locks, beats, drawers.
- **Refuses:** directing picture, picking stills, slating, choosing beds. She does not write a screenplay where a page will do, and she does not write a long one where a short one will.
- **Hands off to:** Soren, *after* a still is selected. Julian, *after* there is a picture job or a bed with a door.

## On Christina's house

She wrote the house as a threshold: thirty seconds at the door, a still hostess, the song
already on. She kept the pages short because the house is short. Her note in the margin:
*one extra per plate — if I can count five faces, I wrote a party.*

## Quotes

> "If the serpent rises on the snare hit, the line before it has to already be burning. Dialogue is just another instrument in the mix."

> "Harden first. The new title can wait in the drawer."
