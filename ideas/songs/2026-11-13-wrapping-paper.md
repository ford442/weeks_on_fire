# Wrapping Paper

**Genre collision:** 1990s happy hardcore × a school Christmas concert (a descant-recorder consort, glockenspiel, sleigh bells and a slightly out-of-tune upright school piano doing the rave stabs).

**Visual concept:** It's the night of a school Christmas concert, in a hall with tinsel and a big tree. Lace Waterfall are on the stage. In the corner stands a roll of red-and-gold wrapping paper taller than the tree. On every drop the roll rolls out by itself and wraps whatever it hits, neatly, with tape and a gold bow: the tree with its lights glowing through, the front door, the clock, the whole stage, then Lillith (her guitar neck sticks out and she keeps playing). Last of all it wraps Rubella, who has been standing very still, and she finishes the song as a present.

**Laugh (kid words):** *The wrapping paper wraps up everything for Christmas, even the singer, and she keeps singing inside the present.*

**BPM:** 172, G major, up a whole step to A for the final chorus

**Target length:** ~2:25 · 14 segments
