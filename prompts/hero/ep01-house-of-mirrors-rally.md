# H01 · House of Mirrors rally (Ep1 core motif)

**Shot:** H01
**Episode / beat:** Episode 01 register · *We Play to Win (House of Mirrors Rally)* Beats A–C ·
[`notes/scenes/house-of-mirrors-rally.md`](../../notes/scenes/house-of-mirrors-rally.md) ·
`content/cutaways/house-of-mirrors-rally.json` · Ep1 lyric motifs ("ping-pong in the dark",
"try not to hit the chandelier this time", "the roof is caving in")
**Why it matters:** the series' core image. Two women, one regulation table, a chandelier hung too
low, and a room coming down around a rally neither of them will stop. Every later Vale shot is a
variation on this blocking.
**Locks:** [`the-two-identity`](../locks/the-two-identity.lock.md) ·
[`house-negative`](../locks/house-negative.lock.md)
**Aspect / model:** 16:9 · `grok-imagine-image-quality` (repo default)

**Geography (all three framings):** the table runs left to right across the ballroom floor.
Vivienne at the left end, directly under the chandelier (closer to it than is wise), paddle in
hand, mid-serve. Liliane at the right end, receiving. Behind Liliane, tall arched windows with
broken panes let in cold moonlight and a little rain; behind Vivienne, a pair of open gilt doors
onto the mirror corridor. The side table (two red wines, one glass stem-down on its side, a bowl
of berries, the closed notebook) sits downstage left. This is Beat C: plaster dust sifting through
the chandelier light, before the cherub comes down. The table is still whole; no pizza box yet.

**Callbacks spent (as image, not dialog):** "Try not to hit the chandelier this time" (she is
inches from it) · Finally. Natural ventilation (plaster through the light, nobody looks up) ·
the score, never shown (no scoreboard, no tally) · berries and two glasses, one stem-down ·
the closed leather notebook · mirror-corridor reflections that lag.
