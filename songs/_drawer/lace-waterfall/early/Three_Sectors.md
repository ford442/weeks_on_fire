STYLE:

Raw garage-punk shout-along demo recorded live in a basement on a single cassette deck, 196 BPM, E major, about one minute forty. One fuzzy, over-driven guitar playing three chords, a borrowed bass slightly out of tune, a thrashy drum kit with too much crash cymbal, a tambourine. The whole room gang-shouts the numbers. Lead vocal: a younger, rawer, bright, conspiratorial female voice, shouting with pure joy, sudden spikes. Second voice: a younger, dry, slightly raspy female voice counting "One," "Two," "Three" flat and unimpressed. Each verse is a little louder and faster than the last. Ends with one huge chord, feedback, and a muffled knock on the wall. Lo-fi, distorted, sincere, no parody, no sound effects beyond the knock. Use Lillith and Rubella voices, both younger.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
RUBELLA: One.

[Verse 1]
LILLITH: Sector One had carpet and a rule about the hum
I plugged in at midnight and I didn't use a drum
I used a radiator and a pan and seven bells
They gave me thirty days and a letter, very well

[Chorus]
LILLITH: Three sectors! Three sectors!
I'm too loud for the whole arcology!
Three sectors! Three sectors!
RUBELLA: Two.

[Verse 2]
LILLITH: Sector Two was quieter, I made it ring like a church
I did a little fireworks, a small one, on the porch
It was ambient lighting, it was lighting with ambition
They gave me thirty minutes and a sprinkler demonstration

[Chorus]
LILLITH: Three sectors! Three sectors!
I'm too loud for the whole arcology!
Three sectors! Three sectors!
RUBELLA: Three.

[Verse 3]
LILLITH: Sector Three was perfect, I was good for half a day
Then I built an amplifier out of a vending machine and a tray
The windows in the next block did a little wobble dance
RUBELLA: They didn't give her a letter. They gave her a chance.
LILLITH: To leave!

[Final Chorus]
LILLITH: Three sectors! Three sectors!
I'm too loud for the whole arcology!
Three sectors! Three sectors!
RUBELLA: Four?

NOTES:

**Canon used:** Lillith's backstory in `characters/building-cast.md`: a former underground
busker evicted from three arcology sectors for noise violations and unsanctioned pyrotechnics.
Rubella's last word, "Four?", is the building, said as a question and never answered.

**Callback:** *"It was lighting with ambition."* That's the early shape of Lillith's repair
creed ("It's not a fire hazard. It's ambient lighting with ambition."). It's not the full line,
so it's not her coining it on record; it's the sound of her getting there.

**Do-not:** no real place names, no injuries, nobody's home burned. The fireworks are small
and the sprinkler is the joke.
