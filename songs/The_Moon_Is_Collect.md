Direct score for gag #2 The Moon Is Collect. Quietest of the six. Liliane has already stepped out; Vivienne’s gloves are the exhibit. EyeWash “late movie” bed under a still, or penthouse insert. Structural flaw: last line of every verse and each chorus is missing its final word — leave 1.5 beats of rest, not a fade. The missing word is always a synonym of payment or answer. Never sung.
