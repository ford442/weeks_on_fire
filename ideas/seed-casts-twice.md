## One-line story

A child on a noir paper street has to *slow their breath* so the garden growing out of the shadows will shrink and give the seed back.

The stolen seed is a small hard disc. The thief is already gone. What remains is the thief's *shadow*, which keeps walking and keeps blooming.

Combine: **Fractal Garden** (breath iterates plants) × **Synth-Shadows** (shadows walk out of phase). The garden *is* the independent shadow.
