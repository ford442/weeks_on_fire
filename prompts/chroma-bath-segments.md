# Chroma Bath — 2:00 pure-color psychedelic cutaway

Copy-ready prompts for **Grok Imagine** (stills / first frames) and video
(**Gemini Omni** long-take, or Grok `image_to_video` per shot).

**Working title:** Chroma Bath
**Alts:** Unscheduled Color · The Test Pattern That Kept Going · No Signal, Only Color

Third sibling on the abstract shelf, after *Molten Silver Sphere* (cave + chrome
+ strobe) and *The Soft Gyre* (a body above weather). This one has **no object at
all.** No blob, no sphere, no horizon, no ground, no figure. The subject is
color itself being *put through things*: bleeding, folding back on itself,
smeared through time, torn by compression, refracted, misregistered,
granulated, and finally bleached. Swirl is the only grammar.

Each segment is a different **processing dialect**. That is the whole structure
— the piece is a suite of eight ways to abuse a color field, in an order that
goes wet → electronic → mechanical → dissolved.

**Music lean:** *Deep Space Drift* — weightless processed pedal steel, no vocal,
no beat to hit. The picture must not pulse to a kick; this is not the *Molten
Silver Sphere* beat-sync job. Color moves at pedal-steel speed: swells, not hits.

---

## Structural flaw (hold for the full runtime)

**There is no black.** The darkest value in the frame is a saturated indigo that
still emits. Shadows glow instead of absorbing. Nothing in the two minutes ever
resolves to 0,0,0 — not the corners, not behind a fold, not between segments.

**Second law (enforced every shot):** nothing is allowed to become a thing. The
instant a swirl starts to read as a face, an eye, a flower, a planet, a galaxy,
or a butterfly, it must be torn or folded away inside one second. If a
generation comes back looking like a fractal poster or a nebula, reject it —
those are objects wearing color, and this piece has no objects.

---

## How to maximize detail (prompt stack)

Do **not** write "psychedelic, trippy, vibrant colors, surreal, LSD, kaleidoscope."
Those words return mush, symmetry, and mandala clip art. Stuff the prompt
through these eight slots instead. Every new idea drops into **one slot** as a
verb + material.

| Slot | What it holds | Example you can swap |
|------|---------------|----------------------|
| **1. FIELD** | What the color *is* made of this beat | Wet ink in water · phosphor on glass · oil on hot metal · compressed video · ground glass |
| **2. FORM** | The swirl geometry this beat | Double vortex · folded ribbon · recursive tunnel · sheared comb · lattice of threads |
| **3. PROCESS** | The named operation — the hero of the segment | Bleed · feedback · slit-scan · datamosh · refraction · misregistration · granulation · bleach |
| **4. PALETTE** | Two or three hues, *named*, that this beat spends | Magenta + acid lime · cyan + sodium orange · viridian + hot coral |
| **5. GRAIN** | The artifact / texture layer sitting on top | Halation bloom · scanline · macroblock · dot crawl · film grain · lens breathing |
| **6. EDGE** | What happens where two colors meet | Refuse to mix · mix into a third impossible hue · fringe chromatically · tear |
| **7. CAMERA** | One move only | Push · pull · roll · lock-off · drift. Never two moves in one shot. |
| **8. MICRO** | 2–4 countable events | "Three threads peel off the vortex. One ribbon folds back through itself. One tear heals." |

**Rules that keep a stuffed prompt from collapsing**

1. **Name the process, not the mood.** "Chromatic aberration fringes every edge as the field shears" survives. "Mind-bending psychedelic energy" dies.
2. **Count things.** Three threads. Two folds. One tear. Models keep numbers far better than "many."
3. **Name the hues.** "Magenta, acid lime, sodium orange" beats "rainbow" every single time. "Rainbow" gets you a rainbow — a literal one, with an arc.
4. **One processing dialect per segment.** Feedback *or* datamosh, never both. Stacking three processes returns brown.
5. **Say "no objects."** Every prompt carries the no-object clause. Generators want to give you a nebula or an eye; refuse in the prompt, not in the edit.
6. **If a generation comes back mushy, cut GRAIN and EDGE poetry first.** Never cut FIELD, FORM, or PROCESS.
7. **Video shots carry one PROCESS + one CAMERA.** Extra MICRO events belong in the *still*. Animation should only continue what the first frame already shows.
8. **Keep the anti-symmetry clause.** Add "asymmetric, off-center, not kaleidoscopic, not mandala, not mirrored" to any still that comes back looking like a snowflake.

**Psychedelic = processing, not a filter.** Ink, phosphor, oil, compression,
glass, ink again. The trip is the signal path.

## Visual lock (paste into every prompt)

Pure non-representational color field filling the entire frame. No objects, no
horizon, no ground, no sky, no figures, no faces, no text. Swirling, folding,
saturated color as the only subject, shot like photographed material rather than
digital illustration. Every edge carries visible processing — bloom, fringe,
tear, or grain. No pure black anywhere; the darkest value is a glowing indigo.
Asymmetric and off-center; never kaleidoscopic, never mirrored, never a mandala.
Photoreal materials, cinematic, anamorphic 2.39:1.

**Palette spend rule:** each segment burns its named pair and does not return to
it. The piece walks the wheel once — magenta/lime → cyan/orange → violet/gold →
teal/coral → emerald/rose → ultramarine/sulphur → oxblood/mint → white.

### FIELD
- Wet ink dropped into cold water, filmed macro, tendrils still unfolding.
- Phosphor glowing on the inside of curved glass, seen slightly off-axis.
- Oil and alcohol on a hot steel plate, boiling into cells that migrate.
- A video signal of itself, one frame late, amplified until it blooms.
- Ground optical glass with color trapped in the grinding scratches.
- Soap-film interference: thickness reads as hue, and the film is draining.
- Powdered pigment suspended in a slow current, never quite dissolving.

### FORM
- A double vortex whose two eyes orbit each other and never touch.
- One continuous ribbon that folds back through its own body without breaking.
- A recursive tunnel that recedes at a constant rate and never reaches an end.
- Threads combed sideways so the swirl shears into parallel lanes.
- Concentric shells peeling like an onion cut off-center.
- A slow braid of three currents where the middle strand runs the wrong way.
- A standing wave that looks like a fold from one side and a hole from the other.

### PROCESS
- **Bleed** — hard edges surrender; color walks into neighboring color by capillary action.
- **Feedback** — the field re-enters itself, each generation smaller, hotter, and rotated a few degrees.
- **Slit-scan** — time is smeared across the horizontal; a single instant is stretched into a whole frame.
- **Datamosh** — motion vectors survive after the picture is gone; macroblocks carry old color into new shapes.
- **Refraction** — the field is seen through moving glass; chromatic dispersion splits every edge into its parts.
- **Misregistration** — cyan, magenta, and yellow plates slip out of alignment; moiré and dot crawl appear in the gap.
- **Granulation** — the field shatters into countable grains, drifts as a cloud, and reassembles imperfectly.
- **Bleach** — every hue is spent into a single overexposed white that is still, faintly, warm.

### GRAIN
- Anamorphic halation blooming a soft skin around the brightest ribbons.
- One drifting scanline, brighter than the field, crossing the frame at its own speed.
- Macroblock quantization visible only in the smoothest gradients.
- Dot crawl along a single misregistered edge, like a bad print.
- 16mm grain that swims, heavier in the indigo shadows than in the highlights.
- Lens breathing on the push, so focus and framing argue by a few percent.

### EDGE
- Two hues refuse to mix; they hold a hard seam and vibrate along it.
- Two hues mix into a third that neither one contains.
- Every boundary fringes red on one side and cyan on the other.
- A boundary tears like wet paper, then heals from the middle outward.
- A boundary is a thin line of pure white that is one frame behind the color.

### MICRO
- Three ink threads peel off the vortex and stop halfway across the frame.
- One ribbon folds through itself; the fold is brighter than either face.
- One tear opens and heals inside two seconds.
- A single grain travels the full width of the frame without changing color.
- One feedback generation arrives late and lands out of register.
- The darkest corner brightens once, unprompted, then settles.

Camera makes only slow single moves, one per state — drift, push, lock-off, roll, pull-back — never two at once. Asymmetric and off-center at all times, never kaleidoscopic, never mirrored, never a mandala. Nothing is ever allowed to resolve into a recognizable object; any shape that begins to read as a face, eye, flower, or planet is torn or folded away within one second. Photoreal short film, no cuts.

## Music / cutaway notes

## Generate order

1. Shot **E** still first, not A. Refraction is the hardest look to get without
   the model handing you a prism graphic or a nebula. If E works, the vocabulary
   works.
2. Then **D** (datamosh) — the second-hardest, because generators want to make
   compression artifacts *pretty*. They should be ugly and rectangular.
3. Then A, B, C, F, G in any order. These are well-trodden looks.
4. **H last**, matched to whatever the real palette of A–G turned out to be.
   The bleach only reads if it cancels colors the viewer has actually seen.
5. Animate each still with the short video line. Camera-only where the still is
   already dense — especially G.
6. Concatenate A→H. Do not re-encode. Crossfade 6–8 frames between segments so
   the processing dialects bleed into each other slightly at the joins.

If you only generate one thing first: **Shot E still.** If refraction comes back
as a rainbow prism on a black background, the whole prompt stack needs the
no-object and no-black clauses moved to the very front.
