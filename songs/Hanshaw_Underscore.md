STYLE:

Deep house, organic house, slight swing, 120 BPM, C minor. Instrumental only, no vocals, no scat, no choir. Warm Rhodes electric-piano stab as the hook, short and charming. Round analog bass, shakers, soft ride cymbal. House kick, not a techno kick. Harmonic 1920s song-shape without a singer, without banjo, without clarinet, without scratchy 78 noise. Smile without becoming cute. Clean modern club production, analog warmth. About four minutes fifty. No festival dump, no guitar solo.
