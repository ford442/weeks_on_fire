# Ten Pins

**Genre collision:** hard techno (140 BPM, distorted rumbling kick, metallic percussion) × 1960s bowling-alley lounge (a Hammond organ, a surf-guitar twang, the rumble of the lane and the crash of a strike as the drop). Almost instrumental: a flat voice says a few words.

**Visual concept:** No people. One black bowling ball with three finger holes rolls through world after world. In each world, ten of something stand in a perfect bowling triangle, and on every drop the ball hits and all ten fall: real pins, cacti on a salt flat, snowmen on a frozen lake, stacks of books in a library, sandcastles on a beach, milk bottles in a dairy. Then a giant chessboard where ten kings wait. The ball rolls toward them, slows, and stops one inch short. Four seconds of nothing. Then one king tips over by itself, and all ten go. On the last chorus the ball is huge, rolling along a coastline into ten lighthouses, which lie down gently in the sea with their lamps still turning. The outro is the ball returner back at the alley, and ten fresh pins standing.

**Laugh (kid words):** *A bowling ball goes all over the world knocking down ten of everything, cacti, snowmen, books, even lighthouses, and once it stops right before the end and they fall down anyway.*
