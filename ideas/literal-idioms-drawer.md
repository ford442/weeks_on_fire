# Literal Idioms Drawer — say the phrase, the world does it

*Drawer, not a farm. Text only. 2026-10-19. `promote?: no` on every card.*

**Cast locks:** [`../characters/building-cast.md`](../characters/building-cast.md) ·

Ten joke interrupts, 6–15s each. Every card is one everyday phrase a kid already knows,
said flatly by somebody in the building, and then the world **does it, literally**, on the
music hit. The speaker never notices. The phrase is spoken, never written on screen.

Works as a series: same open (one flat line), same hit, same end beat (one calm reaction
or none). Could run as EyeWash bumpers, or three in a row as a ~30s gag block.

**Already spent elsewhere — do not farm:** raining cats and dogs · hold the elevator
(`funny-rules.md` examples) · drop the bass (`silly-packet-farm.md`) · bull in a china shop
· the cow jumped over the moon (`content/cartoons/`).

**Counts:** funny with the sound off: **7 of 10** (the three marked *no* need the spoken
phrase to land). Animals: **6 of 10**.

**Callbacks, spent as references** (`notes/callback-bank.md`): the elevator that tries to
negotiate (`when-pigs-fly`) · the Pizza Guy arriving at chaos peak, box at chest height
(`hold-your-horses`) · Rubella's World's Okayest Tenant mug (`spill-the-beans`,
`couch-potato`) · forty folding chairs that creak instead of clapping
(`butterflies-in-my-stomach`) · Lillith's retro-tech repairs (`head-in-the-clouds`).

## Clip math

Grok Imagine clips are **6s, 10s, 15s**. Each card is a single clip: generate the start
still, image-to-video to the last still. The spoken line sits in the first 1–2s; the hit
lands in the back half. Default still style for every prompt: *photoreal cinematic still,
2.39:1, Grok Imagine building-cast, no real actor names, locked camera, 35mm, fine grain,
Grok Imagine style.* Rubella = long dark hair, blood-red lipstick, modern black lace
evening dress. Lillith = black lace, flawless mascara, bright half-smile. Faces calm.

### 1. `spill-the-beans` — She Spills the Beans

- **Line:** LILLITH (leaning in, conspiratorial): *"Come on. Spill the beans."*
- **Laugh:** *She tips her mug and beans pour out until the whole lobby is a bean pool.*
- **Clip:** 10s · **World:** building lobby · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** no
- **Hit / drop:** 5s — **multiplies**: Rubella tilts her mug a little and baked beans pour,
  and pour, and keep pouring, up to everyone's knees, then waists.
- **Start still:** Rubella and Lillith on a lobby bench, Rubella holding her World's Okayest
  Tenant mug, Lillith leaning in to whisper, mailboxes behind them, tile floor, afternoon.
- **Last still:** the same bench, the lobby filled waist-deep with orange baked beans,
  Rubella still holding the tilted mug, a trickle still coming out, Lillith calm, beans
  lapping at the mailboxes.
- **End reaction:** Rubella: *"That's all of them."*
- **Music feel:** sneaky tiptoe pizzicato that drops into a tuba glug.
- **Do-not:** no secret is ever told. No mess on the characters' faces.

### 2. `butterflies-in-my-stomach` — Butterflies Before the Song

- **Line:** LILLITH (at the mic, to Rubella): *"I've got butterflies."*
- **Laugh:** *She opens her mouth to sing and a thousand butterflies fly out instead.*
- **Clip:** 10s · **World:** HOA clubhouse, karaoke night · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** yes
- **Hit / drop:** 5s — **appears**: Lillith takes a breath for the first note and a cloud
  of bright butterflies pours out of her mouth and fills the room.
- **Start still:** Lillith holding a vintage microphone on a small clubhouse stage, a
  karaoke screen glowing behind her with no readable text, rows of beige folding chairs with
  a few bored residents, Rubella in the front row, fluorescent light.
- **Last still:** the clubhouse full of a swirling cloud of butterflies in every colour,
  resting on the chairs and residents' heads, Lillith at the mic mid-note, calm, Rubella
  in the front row with a butterfly on her nose.
- **End reaction:** the folding chairs creak where applause would go.
- **Music feel:** a lounge piano intro that blooms into a harp-and-strings glissando.
- **Do-not:** no lyric sung. Butterflies only — no moths (*Moth Hour* owns moths).

### 3. `cat-out-of-the-bag` — Let the Cat Out of the Bag

- **Line:** RUBELLA (flat, handing a grocery bag to Lillith): *"Don't let the cat out of the bag."*
- **Laugh:** *A cat comes out of the bag, then another cat, and then a cow.*
- **Clip:** 10s · **World:** ladies' building unit, kitchen · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** yes
- **Hit / drop:** 7s — rule of three: a cat hops out (2s), a second cat hops out (4s), and
  on the hit a full-size **cow** steps out of the small paper bag.
- **Start still:** Rubella handing a brown paper grocery bag across a kitchen counter to
  Lillith, both calm, fruit bowl, morning light.
- **Last still:** the same kitchen, two tabby cats sitting on the counter, a full-size
  black-and-white cow standing beside the fridge chewing, the small empty paper bag on the
  floor, Lillith holding the bag's handles, Rubella with her arms folded.
- **End reaction:** the cow moos once. Nobody else moves.
- **Music feel:** a jaunty clarinet ditty; the cow lands on a single tuba blat.
- **Do-not:** not Madelin's tote — this is a plain grocery bag. Not the Black Cat.

### 4. `head-in-the-clouds` — Her Head Is in the Clouds

- **Line:** RUBELLA (not looking up from the paper): *"Your head's in the clouds."*
- **Laugh:** *Her neck stretches up through the ceiling and her head is sitting in the sky with a bird on it.*
- **Clip:** 10s · **World:** ladies' building unit, Lillith's repair corner · **Sound off:** no · **Animal:** yes (a bird)
- **Hit / drop:** 5s — **transforms**: Lillith, daydreaming over a half-fixed radio, rises
  — her neck stretches straight up through the ceiling. Cut to exterior: her head above the
  roof among fluffy clouds, a little bird landing on her hair.
- **Start still:** Lillith sitting at a cluttered workbench with a half-open vintage tube
  radio, chin on her hand, gazing up dreamily, Rubella beside her reading a newspaper.
- **Last still:** exterior, Lillith's calm, smiling face above the apartment building's
  roof among soft white clouds, a small bluebird on her head, a plane passing far behind.
- **End reaction:** Rubella, below, turns a page.
- **Music feel:** a dreamy whistled melody over a slow vibraphone.
- **Do-not:** not the 12-A roof and nobody walks on it. No hair joke — Big City owns hair.

### 5. `couch-potato` — Couch Potato

- **Line:** LILLITH (passing behind the couch with a laundry basket): *"Don't be a couch potato."*
- **Laugh:** *She turns into a giant potato on the couch, still holding the remote and wearing lipstick.*
- **Clip:** 6s · **World:** ladies' building unit, living room · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** no
- **Hit / drop:** 3s — **transforms**: Rubella on the couch becomes a huge brown potato
  wearing her black lace dress and red lipstick, remote still in a potato hand.
- **Start still:** Rubella slumped on a worn velvet couch holding a TV remote, mug on the
  arm, flickering TV glow, Lillith walking behind with a laundry basket.
- **Last still:** the same couch with a giant brown potato in Rubella's black lace dress
  and blood-red lipstick, holding the remote, her mug on the arm, Lillith gone.
- **End reaction:** the potato changes the channel.
- **Music feel:** a lazy slide-guitar loop; the transform is a single cartoon *bwomp*.
- **Do-not:** the potato never speaks. Never cooked, never mashed, never threatened.

### 6. `time-flies` — Time Flies

- **Line:** RUBELLA (glancing at the kitchen clock): *"Time flies."*
- **Laugh:** *The clock grows wings and flies around the kitchen, and then all the clocks fly away like birds.*
- **Clip:** 10s · **World:** ladies' building unit, kitchen · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** no
- **Hit / drop:** 5s — **multiplies**: the wall clock sprouts feathery wings and flaps a lap
  of the kitchen; on the hit, the oven clock, a wristwatch, and an alarm clock join it and
  they fly out the window in a V like geese.
- **Start still:** a homely apartment kitchen, a round white wall clock above the doorway,
  an alarm clock on the counter, Rubella at the table with toast, Lillith at the sink.
- **Last still:** the open kitchen window with a V-formation of winged clocks flying away
  over the rooftops, a pale clock-shaped mark on the wall, Rubella still eating toast.
- **End reaction:** Lillith, without turning: *"We're late."*
- **Music feel:** a ticking woodblock groove that lifts into soaring French horns.
- **Do-not:** **no countdown**, no resetting clock — that belongs to EyeWash ident B. Clock
  faces have no readable numbers.

### 7. `frog-in-my-throat` — A Frog in Her Throat

- **Line:** LILLITH (clearing her throat before a song): *"Sorry. Frog in my throat."*
- **Laugh:** *She coughs and a frog in a bowtie hops out and sings the song for her.*
- **Clip:** 10s · **World:** building laundry room, late-night jam (a different night from Rubella Stand-Up) · **Sound off:** no · **Animal:** yes
- **Hit / drop:** 4s — **appears**: one cough, and a small green frog in a tiny bowtie hops
  onto the folding table, takes the mic stand, and croaks the melody beautifully.
- **Start still:** Lillith standing at a laundry-room folding table with a small mic stand,
  hand to her throat, washers behind, Rubella sitting on a dryer with her mug.
- **Last still:** a small green frog in a red bowtie standing at the mic on the folding
  table, mouth open mid-note, Lillith beside it with her hands folded, calm, Rubella on the
  dryer, a spotlight somehow on the frog.
- **End reaction:** Rubella raises her mug a quarter inch.
- **Music feel:** a doo-wop backing track; the frog sings in pure, sweet croaks.
- **Do-not:** no real song, no lyric. Not the fence-cat frog chorus — one frog, no tuxedo.

### 8. `hold-your-horses` — Hold Your Horses

- **Line:** RUBELLA (to Lillith, who's halfway out the door): *"Hold your horses."*
- **Laugh:** *She is suddenly holding the reins of twelve horses in the hallway.*
- **Clip:** 10s · **World:** building corridor · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** yes
- **Hit / drop:** 4s — **appears**: Lillith stops, and in her hands are the reins of twelve
  horses filling the corridor behind her, nose to tail. At 8s, three ordinary knocks; a
  delivery man threads politely between the horses, box level at chest height.
- **Start still:** a narrow apartment corridor, Lillith with her hand on a door handle
  mid-step, Rubella in a doorway behind her with her arms folded, cream walls, one caged
  bulb.
- **Last still:** the corridor packed with twelve calm horses in a row, Lillith holding
  all their reins, Rubella in her doorway, a delivery man in a plain uniform squeezing past
  the horses holding a pizza box at chest height, nobody looking at anybody.
- **End reaction:** none. The Pizza Guy never looks at the horses.
- **Music feel:** a Western clip-clop rhythm with a lone harmonica.
- **Do-not:** the Pizza Guy is never named and says nothing here. No stampede, no kicking.

### 9. `when-pigs-fly` — When Pigs Fly

- **Line:** LILLITH: *"Will the elevator ever work?"* RUBELLA: *"When pigs fly."*
- **Laugh:** *The elevator door opens and pigs fly out of it like a flock of birds.*
- **Clip:** 10s · **World:** building lobby, elevator doors · **Sound off:** no · **Animal:** yes
- **Hit / drop:** 5s — **appears**: *ding*, the elevator doors slide open and a flock of
  pink pigs with little white wings flies out over their heads, loops the lobby, and flies
  out the front door. The doors close.
- **Start still:** Lillith and Rubella standing in front of closed brass elevator doors,
  Lillith pressing the call button, Rubella holding her mug, lobby tile, flat light.
- **Last still:** the same two women in front of the elevator, doors now closed again, a
  single small white feather drifting down between them, the front door swinging shut.
- **End reaction:** Lillith presses the button again.
- **Music feel:** a sweet music-box waltz that swells into a big string-section soar.
- **Do-not:** no elevator **interior**, and the elevator does not sing or negotiate on
  camera (that's the tango and the counteroffer). No floor-number text.

### 10. `go-bananas` — The Whole Room Goes Bananas

- **Line:** RUBELLA (watching Lillith rewire the ping-pong table): *"You're going to make me go bananas."*
- **Laugh:** *Everything in the room turns into bananas — the lamp, the chair, the table, and her.*
- **Clip:** 15s · **World:** ladies' building unit, living room · **Sound off:** yes · **Animal:** no
- **Hit / drop:** rule of three on three hits — **transforms**: 5s the lamp is a banana;
  8s the armchair is a bunch of bananas; on the 11s hit **Rubella** is a banana in black
  lace and red lipstick, still sitting.
- **Start still:** Lillith on her knees beside a ping-pong table with its underside open
  and wires showing, Rubella in an armchair beside a floor lamp watching, mug in hand,
  evening lamplight.
- **Last still:** the same living room where the floor lamp, the armchair, and the
  ping-pong table's legs are giant bananas, and a tall banana in a black lace dress with
  red lipstick sits holding a mug, Lillith still working on the table, calm.
- **End reaction:** Lillith, not looking up: *"It's not broken. It's early."*
- **Music feel:** a bouncy marimba calypso; each hit is a steel-drum accent.
- **Do-not:** not the Two's regulation table. No peeling, no eating anyone.
