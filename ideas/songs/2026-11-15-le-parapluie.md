# Le Parapluie

**Genre collision:** 1950s Paris chanson × bossa nova. A nylon-string bossa groove under a chanson melody, with accordion. The bossa is the rain.

**Visual concept:** A bright, cloudless summer day on a Paris street: café terraces, people in sunglasses. Rubella walks with a black umbrella open. **It rains only under the umbrella.** Lillith walks beside her in the sun, bone dry, playing a nylon-string guitar on a strap. Every section the weather under the umbrella gets bigger: drizzle, a downpour, July snow, a tiny thunderstorm with a lightning bolt the size of a match, and a rainbow on the solo. In the outro Rubella closes the umbrella. Her little rain stops, and the whole city is rained on at once except for one dry circle around her.

**Laugh (kid words):** *It only rains under her umbrella, and when she closes it, it rains everywhere else.*

**BPM:** 128, bossa feel, D minor, lifting to F major in the chorus

**Target length:** ~2:20 · 13 segments

- *V1:* It's sunny over all of Paris, not a cloud in the sky. The terraces are full, the pigeons are sunbathing. I open my umbrella out of habit, on principle, and under it, it rains a little, just underneath. Typical. People drink their coffee and watch me go by. I walk in a downpour while they work on their tans.

- *Chorus:* The umbrella: a little roof you take for a walk. It's sunny everywhere else, and underneath it rains anyway.
- *V2:* On the bridge it snowed, only under my cloth. Snow in the middle of July, and nobody else saw it. At the market, a flash of lightning as small as a match. A pocket-sized thunderclap made the gulls jump.

- *Outro:* "I close it again."
