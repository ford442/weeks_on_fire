STYLE:

Upbeat nylon-string pop, 108 BPM, D major, about one minute fifty-five. Poemesque, deadpan and sincere, a small argument with itself, no wink at the listener. Lead instrument is one nylon-string guitar, fingerpicked in a bouncing bass-and-chord pattern, close-miked with a little finger noise. A single shaker, upright bass playing in two, no drum kit. Vocal: Lillith solo, bright, clear and quick, conversational, close mic, no belting, no vibrato, no harmony, no backing vocals, asking a question and answering it in short lines. Middle: one eight-bar nylon-guitar interlude. Room is a small tiled laundry corridor, short reflections, a faint bulb hum, no machine sounds. No piano, no organ, no accordion, no strings, no horns, no choir, no laughter, no mugging. Structural flaw: the nylon guitar plays its last chord and stops one bar before the final line, and the last line is sung unaccompanied, close in the room tone, word for word the same as the first line of the song. Nothing answers it and nothing is added.

NOTES:

The flaw: the guitar is finished before the song is. The last line repeats the first line exactly, sung into the room with no instrument, so the poem ends where it began and nobody has decided anything. Do not add a final chord, a reverb tail, a fade, or a shaker under it.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not The Light in the Stair / Unspent.** Those own the Nilsson voice-credit and the stair and the lamp. No stair, no lamp, no motet, no piano, no borrowed voice. This is a bulb, a switch and a corridor.
- **Not Moth Hour.** That owns one bulb on the porch, in D minor, as a gallop. No porch, no moth, no insects.
- **Not Eight Seconds.** That owns "The light is on / The door is shut." No door lines.
- **Not Neutral-Zone Laundry Bed.** The drawer bed owns the room as an instrumental neutral zone. This is a sung poem in the corridor outside it.
- **Not E X I.** No neon, no red, no letters, no three-week fault. This bulb is plain and has no sign.
- **Not Ground Rent / Article Seven.** No "the floor is still the floor", no fourteen minutes.
- **Not Inventory of the Weather / Name Tag / The Other Button.** Bank-mates in other beds. Lillith sings this one alone.
