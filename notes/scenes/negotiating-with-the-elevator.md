# Car Twelve, This Is Twelve

*Long-form dialog scene — building cast. A hostage negotiation in which nobody is a hostage and one party is an elevator.*

**Slugline:** INT. FREIGHT ELEVATOR, CAR 2 — NIGHT
**Runtime target:** 4–5 min (≈4½ script pages)
**Music cue:** **"Negotiating with the Elevator"** — original Minimax track, Argentine tango duet, 116 BPM. Enters in Beat E and never pretends to be diegetic; the bandoneón is simply in the car. Structural flaw is load-bearing: every verse drops a beat as the car descends (5/4 → 4/4 → 3/4 → 2/4) and neither singer adjusts. **Do not correct it.**
**Status:** draft
**Dialog versions (alts):** [`versions/negotiating-with-the-elevator-versions.md`](versions/negotiating-with-the-elevator-versions.md)

**Cast:** LILLITH (lead; negotiates) · RUBELLA (lead; documents) · THE ELEVATOR (vocoded low tenor, PA bandwidth, **floor numbers only**) · MADELYN (V.O., car intercom, one interruption, never seen) · QING (optional; lobby, noodles, no line)

**Spine:** The car stops between floors with both of them in it. Lillith opens negotiations. Rubella opens a ticket. The building splits the difference and the doors eventually work, and no one establishes why.

**Juxtaposition (wrong channel):** The show changes into a **crisis-negotiation procedural** — the kind with a phone, a whiteboard, and a man saying *we have all night*. Lillith plays it completely straight: rapport, small concessions, never say no, mirror the subject's language. The subject's entire vocabulary is integers. Same family as Monster Mash on a laser-snake lawn. The joke is never "ha ha, an elevator." The joke is that the negotiation technique is **correct** and it **works**.

**Emotional notes:** Nobody is frightened. This is Tuesday. Lillith is having the best night of her week; Rubella is having her Thursday. Warmth arrives through procedure, not confession — same anti-daytime-drama rule as the Long Way Up family. Neither of them says anything true on purpose.

**Hard rules for this scene:**
- **The Elevator says floor numbers and nothing else.** Not a word, not a chime-with-intent, not a syllable. If a draft gives it language, the draft is wrong.
- **Never adjudicated.** Malfunction or negotiation — Lillith says one, Rubella says the other, the scene ends. Same as haunted vs. unscheduled.
- **Rubella does not say the counteroffer line in this scene.** She writes it down. "The elevator just tried to negotiate. I told it I already pay rent." is a **stand-up line** ([`rubella-stand-up.md`](rubella-stand-up.md)) and this scene is where it was *filed*, not where it was *told*. If the line is spoken here, the gag is spent twice.
- **No one presses the alarm button.** It is discussed. It is never pressed.

### A — Twelve

```
INT. FREIGHT ELEVATOR, CAR 2 — NIGHT

A car built to move furniture and now mostly moving people. Quilted
moving pads on three walls, hung on hooks, sagging. A brass-stem
control panel with twelve buttons, of which nine are lit from behind
and three are lit from somewhere else.

One fluorescent tube in a cage overhead. It is fine. It is the only
fine thing in the building.

RUBELLA: work coat, World's Okayest Tenant mug, three-quarters full.
A laundry basket at her hip with a bottle of contraband detergent
standing in it like a trophy.

LILLITH: a coil of speaker wire over one shoulder, a soldering iron in
a hip pocket, safety glasses pushed up into her hair where they have
never once been used.

The doors close.

A pause the length of a held breath that nobody in this car is
holding.
```

```
                    THE ELEVATOR (V.O.)
          (vocoded, PA bandwidth, in tune)
                         Twelve.

                         LILLITH
          Twelve. Good. We agree on twelve.

                         RUBELLA
          We're on twelve. That's not an agreement, that's
          an address.
```

```
The car does not move.

Nine seconds. Time it. Nine.

Rubella drinks from the mug without lowering her eyes from the panel.
Lillith sets the speaker wire down on the floor, slowly, the way you
put down anything in a room you have decided to take seriously.
```

```
                         LILLITH
          Okay.

                         RUBELLA
          Don't.

                         LILLITH
          I haven't done anything.

                         RUBELLA
          You put the wire down. That's the thing you do
          before.
```

### B — Rapport

```
INT. CAR 2 — CONTINUOUS

Lillith addresses the panel. Not the ceiling, not the speaker grille —
the panel, at eye height, the way you address a person. This is the
first of many technically correct choices she will make tonight.

Play this entire beat as procedure. No mugging. She has read the
book. The book is not wrong.
```

```
                         LILLITH
          I'm going to say my name, and then I'm going to
          say what I want, and then I'm going to stop
          talking and let you have the floor.
          (beat)
          Which is a joke. That was a joke. I'm going to
          make one of those about every four minutes and
          you don't have to acknowledge them.
          (beat)
          Lillith. Six-C. I've been in this car more than
          anybody in this building, including the guy who
          services you, who I have met, and who does not
          like you.

                    THE ELEVATOR (V.O.)
                         Twelve.

                         LILLITH
          Twelve. Yes. Still twelve. I hear you.
          (to Rubella, not quieter)
          Mirroring. You repeat the last thing they said.
          It builds — 

                         RUBELLA
          I know what it builds.

                         LILLITH
          Then why are you making a face.

                         RUBELLA
          Because it's working.
```

```
(Four seconds.)
```

```
                         RUBELLA (CONT'D)
          I want that in the record. It's working, and I
          want that in the record, and I want somebody to
          come get us.
```

### C — The Ticket

```
INT. CAR 2 — CONTINUOUS

Rubella sets the mug on the floor beside the basket, takes out her
phone, and opens the resident maintenance portal, which loads.

This is the most alarming thing that has happened so far.
```

```
                         RUBELLA
          It loaded.

                         LILLITH
          It never loads.

                         RUBELLA
          It loaded in under a second. On twelve. In a box
          made of steel.
          (typing)
          Category. Elevator. Sub-category. Elevator —
          other.
          (typing)
          Description of issue.

                         LILLITH
          What are you putting.

                         RUBELLA
          "Car two is holding a position."

                         LILLITH
          That's generous.

                         RUBELLA
          That's QA. You describe the behavior. You never
          describe the intent, because the intent is what
          they fight you on, and then you're in a meeting
          about the word "refusing" for an hour and a half
          while the actual defect ships.
          (beat; submits)
          Eleven years. That's what eleven years gets you.
          A sentence nobody can argue with.
```

```
A soft, warm, low-frequency HUM comes up through the floor of the car.
It is roughly the pitch of a dryer with something in it. It lasts
three seconds and stops.

Both of them look down.
```

```
                         LILLITH
          Did the ticket just — 

                         RUBELLA
          The ticket hummed back.

                         LILLITH
          They do that now?

                         RUBELLA
                    (picking up the mug)
          It's a confirmation. It's a confirmation and I
          would like to stop talking about it.
```

### D — Item Seven

```
INT. CAR 2 — CONTINUOUS

The car intercom crackles — the good kind of crackle, the kind that
costs money. MADELYN's voice arrives at conversational volume, which
is worse than a shout and she knows it.

She is never seen. Not a silhouette, not a shoe.
```

```
                         MADELYN (V.O.)
          Good evening, car two.

                         LILLITH
                    (instantly, to the panel)
          Don't answer her.

                         RUBELLA
          I wasn't going to.

                         MADELYN (V.O.)
          This is not an emergency. I want to be clear,
          because two residents have used that word in the
          portal this month and neither of them had a fire.
          (beat)
          Extended dwell is an amenity. It's in the packet.
          Page four, under Vertical Transit, under
          Courtesies. The car is being courteous.

                         RUBELLA
                    (flat, to Lillith)
          We're being hosted.

                         MADELYN (V.O.)
          I'll add it to the agenda. Item Seven, resident
          comments. You're welcome to bring it.
          (beat)
          You'll be brought down. Everyone is brought down.

(The intercom clicks off. The good crackle stops. The cheap silence
comes back.)

(Six seconds.)

                         LILLITH
          "Everyone is brought down."

                         RUBELLA
          She rehearses.

                         LILLITH
          She rehearses in the car. That's why the car
          knows her.
```

### E — The Negotiation (Song)

```
INT. CAR 2 — CONTINUOUS

Lillith takes the safety glasses out of her hair, looks at them, and
puts them back. Then she steps to the panel and does the thing she has
been building to for four minutes, which is to offer a machine
something it cannot spend.

The upright bass enters. Then the bandoneón. 5/4.

The car begins to descend.

FROM HERE, THE SCENE IS THE SONG. Dialogue stops. What follows is
staging, not script.
```

```
STAGING — one floor per section, and the meter loses a beat each time:

  5/4   Lillith sings the deal. She has said kind things about him to
        the boiler. She has never once called him a lift. She dances
        it, badly, on purpose, with a coil of speaker wire for a
        partner, because there is nobody else in the car who will.
        THE ELEVATOR sings floor numbers. In tune. Better than her.

  4/4   Rubella does not participate and does not leave — there is
        nowhere to go. She stands in the corner with the mug and
        watches, and at the exact center of the section she picks up
        the beat with one heel against the car floor, twice, and
        stops, and never does it again.
        That heel is the only percussion in the mix. Record it in the
        car. Do not replace it with a kit.

  3/4   The quilted pads. Lillith turns one on its hooks, and behind
        it is not a wall but a sheet of plywood with a service diagram
        printed on it, and in the corner of the diagram is the
        building, and the building has thirteen floors.
        Hold two seconds. Do not push in. Never mention it again.

  2/4   The lobby. Lillith is now arriving late to a bar that has
        already left, and does not adjust, and does not notice, and
        this is the most romantic thing in the episode.

The doors do not open on the downbeat. They open a semitone late,
with a chime a semitone above the last chord, and the chime is the
last sound in the mix.
```
