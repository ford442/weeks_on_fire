## Years (she likes this story)

First year: live with a cube. Two lines on the floor. Hover. Eat. Sleep. Whole bay writes at 3am.

Second year: nights. One moon. Diagram that does not get more interesting. Small training wings off the rack — they do not lift you.

Third year: yard over the canyon. Instructor neck longer than the rule. Further exercises = cube at chest height until arms shake. Partner who filed everything as floor went to Appendix C (a round room; every spine is C).

Fourth year: lamps on (hardware). Full hollow-chevron wings lock. Annex theme (brass + paperwork tick). Instructor bestows an **odd sword**: short, hole in the blade, honeycomb guard, a key that was told it was a sword. Receive, do not swing.

She inhales for the first posting (canyon window, take-a-number that is not a number, market under two moons). Rubella cuts because the story has music now and another year in its mouth.

```
RUBELLA
We're expected back down to Earth. Right away.

LILLITH
Before the second verse.
```
