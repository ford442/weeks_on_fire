## Logline bio

Lillith is a former busker and self-styled "vibes consultant," evicted from three arcology
sectors for noise and unsanctioned pyrotechnics, who treats the building as her largest
installation yet. She repairs neon, tubes and ping-pong tables with parts that don't belong
together, and the result works for exactly one glorious night. She shares Six-C with Rubella.
She is not reckless. She is early.

### Micro-gestures (use sparingly, never explain)

1. **The head-tilt.** She tilts toward a sound before anyone else hears it. Every repair starts here.
2. **Pocket check.** She touches the parts pocket before she touches the broken thing.
3. **Glasses, not used.** She takes the safety glasses out of her hair, looks at them, and puts them back.
4. **She does not turn around for a heckle.** Hers or anyone's.
5. **Pricing it.** She looks up at something fixable and you can see her work out the parts. After *Natural Ventilation*, sometimes she looks up and **doesn't** price it, and nobody notices.

## Voice guide

- **Sentence length:** three to eight words, fast, with sudden energy spikes. She talks like she's reading a schematic out loud.
- **Delivery:** bright, conspiratorial, a little too close to the mic. Never loud. She lowers her voice when she's most excited.
- **Vocabulary she overuses:** part numbers and years ("a 6BQ5, 1962, Dutch") · *early* ("It's not broken. It's early.") · *the data* ("I have the data") · musical keys (E-flat, the wrong key on purpose) · *by Sunday* / *by midnight* · *ambition*
- **Vocabulary she refuses:** *broken* (it's early) · *hazard* (that's Rubella's word) · *sister* (never, about anyone) · *darling* (Vivienne's) · *sorry* about a repair
- **Humor type:** total commitment to the wrong channel. She plays the negotiation, the repair, or the makeover show completely straight, and the technique works. She never winks and she doesn't laugh.
- **In a Lace Waterfall song:** she starts the chant alone, then Rubella joins. She is the brighter voice, never belting, no vibrato show. She would be thrilled that "waterfall" doesn't fit the bar and would never say so.

### Sample monologue: "Forty-Seven" (laundry room, 2 AM, on her back under a dryer)

Lillith, to dryer forty-seven, with the panel off.

> You're in E-flat. Nobody told you that.
> Everybody thinks you're humming. You're not humming. You're *holding a note.*
> That's different. That's a choice.
> *(pocket check: one tube, two wing nuts, the cloth wire)*
> I have a 6BQ5 from the ping-pong table. It does Thursday's weather.
> You'd be in E by midnight. You'd be early.
> *(she looks at the safety glasses; puts them back in her hair)*
> Rubella says you're out of order.
> Rubella's never heard you at two.
> Okay. Let's talk about what you want.

### Sample exchange: with Rubella (corridor, the register: a weeknight)

> **LILLITH:** The EXIT sign's been E-X-I for three weeks.
> **RUBELLA:** It's still an exit.
> **LILLITH:** It's two-thirds of an exit.
> **RUBELLA:** I'll bring the extinguisher.
> **LILLITH:** It's not a fire hazard.
> **RUBELLA:** I know what it is.
> *(She brings the extinguisher. It comes back full.)*

## Backstory (committed)

**This is settled.** Alternates are in [Rejected / alternate](#rejected--alternate).

- **Origin.** An underground busker and self-proclaimed "vibes consultant." Her busking hat once collected a button, a key, half a sandwich and a pigeon, and the pigeon stayed (*Busker's Hat*, the early tapes).
- **Formative incident (series-relevant).** Evicted from three arcology sectors for noise violations and unsanctioned pyrotechnics, each one louder than the last (*Three Sectors*). Management keeps a file on her that is already thicker than most novels. She moved into the building and treats it as her largest, most interactive installation.
- **How she met Rubella, when the band started, whether they are related.** **Not stated, on purpose.** They share Six-C. That is the whole on-screen record.
- **Secret she protects.** Underneath the provocation is a very good ear. Most of her "improvements" start with a noise nobody else noticed. She lets people think she is reckless because "early" is harder to explain.
- **What would break her on screen.** Being handed something beautiful that already works, with nothing to fix. That is the Christina house, and it is why that visit lasts thirty seconds.

### The ten rules: would never / always

**She would never:**
1. Use the safety glasses.
2. Argue a no after *Natural Ventilation* (12-A). She stops herself: "I could —" / "I know." / "Okay."
3. Call a thing broken. It's early.
4. Turn around for a heckle, or laugh at her own line.
5. Call anyone her sister, or remark on anyone's surname.

**She always:**
6. Hears it first.
7. Knows where every part came from.
8. Starts the song, the repair, the deal.
9. Makes it work for exactly one glorious night.
10. Lets Rubella have the last word and the extinguisher.

## Arc hooks

Seeds, not scenes. Each one keeps the locks above.

1. **"In E by Midnight"** *(laundry room, silent)*: She retunes dryer forty-seven. For one night it hums in E and nothing gets summoned. In the morning it is back in E-flat. Nobody mentions it.
2. **"The Dish Towel"**: A vacuum tube wrapped in a dish towel sits on the Six-C counter for a whole episode like an egg. In the last shot it is in something. We never see what.
3. **"Unpriced"** *(after Natural Ventilation)*: In a corridor she looks up at a flickering fixture and doesn't price it. Rubella sees her not do it. Neither says anything.
4. **"The Pigeon"** *(early-tapes callback)*: A pigeon on the building's roof follows her for one scene. She doesn't look at it. The pigeon stays.

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Keep the register domestic: laundry rooms, elevators, corridors, lawns, ridge roads. She is the only bright thing in it.
- Give her a noise to hear first and a wrong part to fix it with. It works for one night.
- Play the wrong-channel technique completely straight. It is correct, and it works.
- Let her start the duet and give Rubella the finish.
- Keep a full extinguisher in frame at any repair she sets out to do. It never discharges and nobody mentions it.

**Don't**

- Don't call her or Rubella "sisters," "the Vale sisters," or "the Vale duo." On screen they are roommates. In notes they are **the building pair**.
- Don't explain the surname, and don't merge her with Liliane or Vivienne. No champagne flute, no paddle, no swap, no score.
- Don't make her a spy outside the Ep3 Monster Mash finale. The black trench is one cutaway's costume.
- Don't let her argue a no after 12-A, and don't let anyone ask "you're not fixing it?" twice.
- Don't let her be reckless for its own sake. She is early, not careless.
- Don't put Kenji, or any third voice, in a Lace Waterfall song or a building two-hander.
- Don't let her smudge her mascara, use the safety glasses, or slip in heels.
