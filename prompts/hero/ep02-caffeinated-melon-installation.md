**Why it matters:** the portable hydro art installation that went from a line in Ep2 to a cutaway:
a very large melon absorbing espresso and exhaling fake artistic fog, and ninety seconds of
enforced honesty in a white cube. The joke is in the frame, not a caption: everyone mid-confession,
one person eating a cracker, unaffected.

If she gets a lock, promote that sentence verbatim.

**Justine (paste unchanged):** *Justine, a younger woman with straight dark hair worn without
ceremony, bare lips, no jewelry, a plain charcoal heavy-knit sweater over a collared shirt, dark
jeans and flat black shoes, a plain white takeaway coffee cup half full in one hand and a plain
water cracker in the other, the resting face of someone reading a long email, assessing rather
than bored, completely unaffected by the fog.* Hard rules: no lace, no red lip, no matching
silhouette with anyone, flat shoes, the cup always in frame (half full: she has an opinion and is
holding it), never in costume.

**Geography (all three framings):** a white-cube gallery at night, polished grey concrete floor,
black windows. Center: a white plinth with a melon the size of a beach ball (a netted cantaloupe,
espresso-brown stain spreading from its brass ports). On a second, lower plinth beside it, a chrome
lever espresso machine feeds the melon through copper pipe and clear silicone tubing. Fog leaks from
hairline splits in the rind, spills off the plinth and pools at knee height. The crowd in black is
frozen mid-confession. Justine stands at the right, by the wall. No wall text, no placards.

**Callbacks spent (as image, not dialog):** the fog as ninety seconds of honesty · "Someone proposes.
Someone else files for divorce. Same person." (one man, ring box and folded filing) · Justine eats a
cracker like a verdict · Justine's cup · "I absorbed the truth and the canapés" (the tray).

## Regenerate notes

- **If the melon comes back as a watermelon or sliced fruit,** keep "netted cantaloupe" and "the
  size of a beach ball" adjacent and earlier; do not swap the fruit. The stain rings are what make
  it read as *absorbing* espresso.
- **If Justine gets lace, red lips or heels,** the crowd in black is bleeding into her: move her
  sentence before the crowd sentence and cut the crowd to two guests. She is the one person in the
  room the audience can pick out in a quarter second.
- **If the fog reads as smoke or a fire,** say "white" and "knee height" again and drop "dense".
- **If wall text or a title placard appears,** add "blank framed canvases" earlier; do not describe
  any label at all.
- **Trim order:** micro-detail → the extra confessing guests → the room description. Never Justine's
  sentence, never the melon/tubing sentence, never the light sentence.
