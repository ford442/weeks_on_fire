## What the blocks lock (and why)

- **Two bodies, always.** Never "one woman, two names" (retired). Never twins either: Vivienne's
  hair is a blunt architectural cut, Liliane's is long loose waves. That silhouette difference is
  how a viewer tells them apart in a wide, so it is in every block, including the distant one.
- **Black lace on both = a swap is live.** Every hero shot in this library is a swap night
  (Ep1 "Tonight we swap lives", the HOA back wall, the car), so Liliane's default block is the
  lace one. Her jewel-tone register (`liliane-jewel`) exists for a non-swap shot; do not mix it
  into a shot with Vivienne in lace unless the swap ending is the point.
- **Lace spec comes from the reference stills:** opaque lined bodice, sheer lace sleeves,
  scalloped lace hem at the knee. Vivienne's damage is a cigarette burn at the shoulder;
  Liliane's is a short tear at the hem. Damage is small and visible, never distressed-costume.
- **Silver, never gold.** Vivienne: one pendant. Liliane: wrist and ear.
- **Faces:** boredom, not menace. Vivienne is never photographed mid-laugh. Liliane's withheld
  half-smile is allowed once per sequence, not in every frame.
- **Not in the blocks on purpose:** props (flute, paddle, notebook, wine). They change per shot,
  so the shot text places them. Vivienne holds the flute or the paddle, never both; Liliane's
  notebook stays closed. Ages are a prompt anchor (thirties), not a bible fact.
- **Ages and builds stay matched** so neither reads as "the young one". Neither is ever seated
  except H03, which the-two.md's own car seed puts in the passenger seat (see that file).
