# Practice Space — drawer sketch (early tapes)

**Drawer.** `promote?: no`. Folder: [`README.md`](README.md). Farm #18 / #33. Not #42.

STYLE:

Lo-fi early-1990s-style grunge demo recorded on a cassette deck in a metal storage unit, 76 BPM, drop-D tuning, D minor, about two and a half minutes. Thick fuzzy guitar with slow heavy riffs, a distorted bass, a loose heavy drum kit with roomy crashes, quiet-verse/loud-chorus dynamics. Lead: a younger, dry, slightly raspy female voice, low and heavy, sincere. Second: a younger, bright female voice harmonising on choruses. Structural flaw that must be in the take: three times, right as the loud chorus kicks in, the guitar and bass amps cut out completely and all that's left is the acoustic drums and a low electrical hum of a freezer compressor for two bars, then the amps come back mid-phrase. Tape hiss, metal-room echo, sincere, no parody, no other sound effects. Use Rubella and Lillith voices, both younger.

LYRICS:

[Verse 1]
RUBELLA: Unit forty-one, past the boats and the bikes
We rented the dark for a month
One outlet, one cord, one heater, one rug
And next door, something that hums

[Chorus]
RUBELLA: Turn it up, turn it up
LILLITH: It's gone.
RUBELLA: Turn it up
LILLITH: It's back!

[Verse 2]
RUBELLA: We never met the man who rents forty-two
We only know he keeps a freezer
Every twenty minutes it wakes up and drinks
Our whole amplifier like a river

[Chorus]
RUBELLA: Turn it up, turn it up
LILLITH: It's gone.
RUBELLA: Turn it up
LILLITH: It's back!

[Bridge]
RUBELLA: We could write a quiet song
LILLITH: We could ask him
RUBELLA: We could bring a fork and some ice cream
LILLITH: We could ask him

[Final Chorus]
RUBELLA: Turn it up, turn it up
LILLITH: It's gone.
RUBELLA: Turn it up
BOTH: It's gone.

NOTES:

**The flaw:** the amps really cut out three times for two bars each. The drums keep going.
The song ends on a cut-out, and the freezer wins.

**Why it's early:** the version of the band that didn't yet have a building that hums back
(the laundromat dryer note, the elevator's in-tune floor numbers). Here, only the freezer
hums, and it isn't on their side.

**Do-not:** never show the neighbour in unit 42, never open the freezer, and never explain
what's in it.
