STYLE:

Upbeat reed-organ pop, 108 BPM, G mixolydian, about one minute thirty. Short lines, specific nouns, deadpan, bright. A small reed organ, dry, no church reverb, a simple repeating figure. No accordion, no drum kit, no guitar. Two female voices, no belting, no whisper, no hush. Lillith brighter, declaring the landing quiet as a fact the declaration makes true. Rubella drier, the intercom, no face, brass-grille narrow. Do not sing their names. Structural flaw: the intercom answers one bar late with the previous line, every time, including the last answer, and never catches up. Sing only the lines below. No metal, no distorted guitar, no scream, no product name. Mood: the landing is quiet because the song says so. Runtime about 1:30.
