STYLE:

Phonetic palindrome round, a cappella, 84 BPM, 3/4, about one minute ten. Three voices, unison, all on A, dry, close, no instruments, no piano, no reverb beyond the room. The cell is ah eh ah, one syllable to a beat, one bar. No words, no Latin, no English, no names. Entry 1 starts on ah. Entry 2 starts one bar later, on the same pitch, on ah. Entry 3 starts one bar after entry 2, on the same pitch, and begins on eh. Straight tone, no melisma, no slides, no harmony, no canon at the fifth. Structural flaw: entry 3 starts on the second syllable and never sings the first ah. She does not go back for it. The round keeps going with that syllable missing from the front of her cell, and it ends still missing. No lied, no withheld oo, no lexicon, no pronunciation guide, no clarinets, no hocket. Mood: three voices on one cell, and the last voice never finds the start of it.

(entry 3 never sings the first ah)
(stop)

NOTES:

Structural flaw: the third entry starts on the second syllable and never finds the first. The round ends with that ah still missing. Nobody hands it to her.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not Open Vowel.** That one is a lied, and oo is withheld. This is a round. The cell is ah eh ah, and the missing sound is the first ah of the third entry, not a vowel kept back from a poem.
- **Not Same Sentence.** That one is English, six syllables, two clarinets, and a phase shift of one syllable after a unison pass. This cell is three phonetic syllables, there is no clarinet, and the third voice is short from the start.
- **Not Triphthong Table.** No lesson, no piano, no guide a glide behind the melody. The syllables are not words.
- **Not Keh Tih Keh.** That card is one voice, three other syllables, sung forward and then with the pitches reversed. This is a unison round. The pitches do not reverse. The flaw is the late entry, not the height.
- **Not Missing Click.** That card is a hocket with a syllable assigned to nobody. Here all three voices sing. The third one simply never starts on ah.
