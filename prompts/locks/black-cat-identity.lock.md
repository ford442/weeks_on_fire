## What the blocks lock (and why)

- **Same animal, always.** One continuous real cat across Episode 3, the Cat POV cutaway and every
  background cameo. Not a cartoon cat, not stylized, not cute, not a second cat.
- **Glowing yellow eyes** read as eyeshine from a light source in frame (porch bulb, laser spill),
  not as an effect. That keeps it photoreal.
- **The tail is the performance.** High like a weather vane when she walks; curled when she sits.
- **Fur puffs once per appearance, maximum.** The blocks keep it sleek; the one shot that spends
  the puff (H05-C, the discovery) says so in its own text.
- **She is a witness.** Never a rescue, never helping. In H07 she watches from the edge of frame;
  never put her and the Pizza Guy in one shot *leaving*.
- **Not the tabby.** The brown-and-white tabby at the Monster Mash mic is a different cat. Not the
  white building cat either.
