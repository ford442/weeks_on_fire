# Big City — Hair Incidents (real scale)

Sight bank for **Big City** B-roll (`ideas/big-city-cutaway.md`,
`prompts/big-city-segments.md`). Everything here is **hair that could actually
happen to you**: big, expensive, over-committed, and one inch too wide for the
world it has to walk through. Not architecture. Not a building. Nothing fills a
car.

**Sibling, not twin:** [`big-city-hairdos.md`](big-city-hairdos.md) owns the
civic-scale Victorian lane (roundabouts, bus-shelter updos). That file is for
Daisy Bell. **This** one is modern Glamora, real physics, 10–20 seconds.

---

## Lock

- **Plausible volume.** Teased, sprayed, extended, wet-set, freshly out of a
  chair. Impressive at a party, a liability in a doorway. If a stranger would
  film it, it is too big — pull back.
- **The world stays normal size.** Doors, turnstiles, ceiling fans, sun visors,
  hoodies and headrests are all standard. The hair is the only variable.
- **Awkward, not slapstick.** Nobody falls. Nobody screams. The comedy is the
  three seconds of quiet negotiation and the decision to pretend it didn't
  happen.
- **Hair is never the punchline out loud.** No one comments. No one looks at
  camera. The person keeps their dignity and their schedule.
- **One moving system per clip.** Either the hair moves or the city does.
- **B-roll rule holds:** no lip sync, no one is singing, the hair performs the
  beat. Neon magenta / cyan lives in the gaps between strands.
- **Negatives:** no bottle, no product name, no EyeWash bug, no Daisy tandem,
  no Riley, no cartoon beehive, no wig flying off, no hair the size of a car.

**Length:** each entry is one 10–20s beat = 5–10 bars at 126 BPM. Build it as
start frame → one negotiation → last frame (the pretending-it-didn't-happen
pose). Two clips of 7.6s cut on the snare will carry most of these.

---

## Doorways, transit, thresholds

- **Turnstile.** Card taps, body goes through, hair arrives on the *outside* of
  the arm. She backs up half a step, tilts her head sideways, goes again.
  Behind her, one commuter has already chosen the other gate.
- **Subway door chime.** She steps on; a strand is still on the platform side.
  The doors close on four hairs. The train pulls out. She looks straight ahead
  for the whole ride with her head two inches from the glass.
- **Escalator behind.** The man on the step above has no face all the way to
  the mezzanine. He leans left. It follows. He leans right. It follows. He
  accepts.
- **Revolving door, modern.** She misjudges the compartment, taps the glass
  with the crown, and goes around a second full time rather than explain.
- **Bus, low ceiling.** Standing passenger's crown is flattened by the roof.
  She bends her knees the whole way. She does not take a seat; the seat is
  worse.
- **Rideshare headrest.** She gets in the back and cannot put her head back.
  She sits forward the entire ride like a woman in a confession booth. Driver
  adjusts the mirror twice and gives up.
- **Airport scanner.** The agent says "anything in your hair." Long pause. She
  starts removing bobby pins onto a gray tray. There are nine. There will be
  more.

## Small rooms, other people's faces

- **Elevator with one other person.** Six floors of him tipping his coffee
  around the volume. Neither of them says anything. He gets out one floor
  early.
- **Movie theater.** The couple behind slides two seats over mid-trailer. She
  notices, sinks down four inches, which achieves nothing, and stays that way.
- **Restaurant candle.** The waiter, without breaking eye contact or comment,
  moves the tealight to the far corner of the table. Everyone pretends it was
  a table-setting choice.
- **Selfie group of five.** In the photo, two friends are entirely hair. They
  take it again. Same result. They post it.
- **The hug.** Two people with the same hair go in for a cheek kiss and get a
  faceful. They finish it as a shoulder pat. Both say something at the same
  time and neither repeats it.
- **Meeting room, projector.** Her silhouette is on the slide. The presenter
  keeps presenting through it. Nobody asks her to move; she is at the back.
- **Video call.** She frames herself, crops the crown out, tries again, ends up
  sitting far enough back that she looks like she is in a different room than
  her own shoulders.

## Weather and the outdoors

- **First gust.** Twenty minutes of work undone in one second on a corner. She
  does not fix it. She keeps walking with the exact same expression.
- **Umbrella too small.** The umbrella covers her face. The crown is out in the
  rain, quietly deflating one inch per bar. She is dry. The hair is not.
- **Humidity.** Static sunrise on a summer platform: every strand slowly
  stands, reaching for the ad panel. She licks a palm. It gets worse.
- **Convertible / open window.** Not a comic blowout — she just raises the
  window six inches and rides with her head tipped at 20 degrees, sunglasses
  on, entirely composed.
- **Fan in a bodega.** Oscillating fan clocks past her every four seconds. Hair
  lifts, settles, lifts, settles. She keeps reading the label on a can.
- **Sudden sun.** Standing in the one patch of light, she is briefly a
  backlit halo the size of a satellite dish. A tourist takes a picture of the
  building behind her.

## Snags, contact, adhesion

- **Earring.** A hoop is in there. She locates it by feel for eight full
  seconds, smiling at whoever is talking to her, never breaking the
  conversation.
- **Coat zipper.** Zipping up catches three strands at the collar. She goes
  around all day with her chin slightly down.
- **Sunglasses on the head.** They go up, they get swallowed, they are never
  seen again this clip. She searches a bag. They are on her head.
- **Backpack strap.** He shrugs the bag on and takes half his own hair with
  it. Walks a block. Adjusts once. Accepts.
- **Velcro.** A friend's jacket cuff meets the crown in a hug. They separate
  with the care of two people defusing something.
- **Gum / candy on the train seat.** Her hair touches the seat back. She feels
  it. She sits forward. She does not look.
- **Lipstick.** One long strand crosses her mouth mid-sentence and stays there.
  She talks around it for the whole beat.

## The chair, the crown, the upkeep

- **Tuesday crown work.** Salon chair, teasing comb, mirror. A perfectly
  serious craft close-up. Then she stands and her head clears the mirror frame.
- **Hairspray cloud.** She holds her breath through it, exhales at the exact
  wrong moment, and coughs politely into a shoulder. Stylist doesn't stop.
- **Sleeping.** The silk pillowcase, the head propped, the careful roll onto
  one side. She is lying at a 30-degree angle to hold the shape. Alarm rings
  at 4:40 a.m., because of this.
- **Bobby pin count.** A dish of pins at the end of the night. Emptying it
  takes long enough to be its own clip.
- **Helmet.** She looks at the helmet. She looks at the hair. She takes the
  bus.
- **Hood.** She pulls the hoodie up, the hood does not reach, she wears it
  half-on like a monk and goes out anyway.
- **Roots at the wrong light.** Under the drugstore fluorescents, two inches
  that were invisible on the street. Everyone in the aisle is under the same
  light. Nobody says anything.

## Work, service, professional life

- **Barista.** She leans over the counter to hear the order and the crown
  clears the pastry case by nothing. The barista freezes mid-reach and waits.
- **Bar, low pendant lamp.** The lamp swings. She did not feel it. It swings
  for the rest of the clip behind her.
- **Shared desk fan** in an office, aimed at two people, only one of whom is
  affected.
- **Nightclub smoke machine.** The volume is doing wonderful work in the beam.
  Then the smoke sets off nothing and the lights come up and it is 2 a.m.
- **Coat check.** The coat goes over the head. It comes back off with the
  shape rearranged. She checks a phone camera, decides it is fine, and it is.
- **The doorway pause.** She stops short at the club entrance, turns her
  shoulders 45 degrees, and goes through sideways. So does the next person. So
  does the next. Nobody planned it; it is just how this door works now.

## Two-hander awkwardness (the good ones)

- **Two big dos on a narrow sidewalk.** The sidestep dance, twice, a small
  apologetic hand, and then both step down into the gutter to pass.
- **The shared cab.** Both get in the back. Neither can lean on the headrest.
  They both sit forward. They do not acknowledge it. Eleven blocks.
- **Concert row.** Two in the same row, staggered. The person between them has
  no peripheral vision and appears to be in a tunnel.
- **Photo booth.** Four frames. In all four, one of them is 60% hair. The
  strip prints. They split it and neither mentions it.
- **Elevator, both of them.** Doors open, they see each other, they both do
  the arithmetic. One says "I'll get the next one." Doors close.

## Endings / hold frames (do not overuse)

- Last one out of the club at 3 a.m., volume completely gone, walking flat and
  pleased.
- One person on a night platform, backlit by a train's headlight — a normal
  silhouette, just very slightly wrong at the top.
- A gray tray of nine bobby pins going through a scanner belt, nobody in frame.

## Second pass — food, errands, bodies, home

Same lock. Nothing here is bigger than a person.

### Food and drink

- **Birthday candles.** She gathers the whole crown in one fist, holds it at
  the back of her neck like a woman handling a live animal, leans in, blows,
  lets go. Everyone claps for the candles.
- **Soup.** Steam rises straight into the crown for the length of the bowl.
  She eats it anyway. The volume drops perceptibly by the last spoonful.
- **Drive-thru window.** The car is too close and the window is too high. She
  takes the bag at a 40-degree lean with the crown against the door frame. The
  worker holds the drinks out an extra beat, patient, having seen this.
- **Sharing a small table with a tall drink.** Every time she leans to sip, the
  straw disappears into hair. Third attempt she just picks the glass up.
- **Taco / anything two-handed.** No free hand to hold hair back. She uses her
  forearm. It works. It is not elegant. She keeps eating.
- **Restaurant booth.** She slides in and the crown catches on the coat hook
  on the divider. One inch of reverse, a small lift, she's in. Her friend
  pretends to read the menu.
- **Bar with a low shelf for glasses.** The bartender reaches over her head for
  a coupe and his sleeve parts the hair. Neither acknowledges it.

### Errands, retail, machines

- **Fitting room.** The sweater goes on. The sweater comes off. The hair is now
  a different shape and she leaves the store without buying the sweater.
- **Self-checkout camera.** The screen shows her from the chest up and she is
  mostly hair. The machine says *unexpected item*. She is already looking for
  an attendant.
- **Freezer aisle.** She opens the low door, the cold fog comes out, and the
  hair moves in it like something underwater. She takes the peas.
- **Pharmacy mirror, sunglasses rack.** Every pair she tries pushes the front
  of the hair forward into her eyebrows. She buys the first pair.
- **ATM canopy.** She has to stand back from the machine to see the screen
  because of her own reflection and her own volume. She squints. She counts
  the bills in the open.
- **Photo booth ID / passport photo.** "Ears visible." Long, dead pause. The
  clerk takes it anyway and neither of them says another word about ears.
- **Hardware store.** A low-hanging price sign parts the hair as she walks the
  aisle. It swings for four seconds behind her. She does not turn around.

### Bodies, sport, appointments

- **Dentist chair.** She lies back and the headrest is directly under the
  crown, so she's not lying back — she's at 25 degrees with her chin up. The
  hygienist adjusts the chair twice and then works around it.
- **Eye exam.** Chin on the rest, forehead to the bar, hair fully pinned back
  with one hand by *her*, for the duration, like a job.
- **Hard hat at a site visit.** It sits on top, an inch above her actual head,
  and stays perfectly level. She does the whole tour that way. It is fine.
  It is technically compliant.
- **Hairnet.** Same energy. The net makes a shape nobody has a word for.
- **Treadmill.** The ponytail is fine. The front is not. Twenty minutes of the
  same two strands returning to the same eye. She never stops to fix it.
- **Swim cap.** She looks at the pool. She looks at the cap. She sits at the
  edge and puts her feet in.
- **Yoga, floor pose.** Face down, the hair has its own footprint on the mat,
  larger than her head. The instructor steps over it without a word.
- **Getting a shirt over the head.** Two arms up, a pause in the dark inside
  the shirt while she decides how to do this, and it comes out on the other
  side almost right.

### Home, alone, no audience

- **Her own doorframe.** It fits. It only ever *just* fits, and she ducks half
  an inch every single time without being conscious of it.
- **Car sun visor.** She flips it down; it rests on the hair, not the
  windshield. She drives with it up and squints west.
- **Small bathroom.** In the mirror she is a perfect composition. She turns
  sideways and the crown is against the tile. She turns back. Composition.
- **Ceiling light pull-chain.** It's in there somewhere. She finds it by
  swinging one arm through the volume, twice.
- **Sharing a bed.** Two heads, one pillow's worth of room, a small
  renegotiation at 1 a.m. that ends with one person sleeping diagonally.
- **The cat.** Asleep in it on the couch. She is awake, on her phone, not
  moving her head. This is now a forty-minute situation.
- **Vacuum.** She bends to plug it in and the crown touches the floor before
  her hand reaches the outlet. She stops. She considers this. She plugs it in.
- **Washing it out.** Ten seconds of the whole architecture going flat under
  the tap, in total silence. The most honest shot in the bank.

### Other people's hands

- **Toddler.** A fistful, no malice, complete commitment. She keeps her head at
  the angle the toddler has chosen and continues the adult conversation.
- **Friend fixing it.** Someone reaches in to fix one thing without asking,
  three seconds of quiet work, a pat. She says thank you. It looks identical.
- **The photographer at the party.** He gestures twice for her to move left,
  which she does, and the hair is still in the shot. Third gesture is a small
  surrender. He takes it.
- **Dance floor.** A stranger's ring is briefly in her hair, unnoticed by both
  of them, and comes out on its own. The song continues.
- **Handing someone a phone over the shoulder.** The phone is passed *around*
  the hair, hand to hand, by two people who don't discuss it.

### More endings / hold frames

- Hair down, flat, wet, on the bathroom floor tile at 3 a.m., in one frame. No
  person above frame. Hold.
- The cat, still asleep in it, as the light in the room changes.
- Her own doorframe from behind, the duck, the door closing. End.

## Camera grammar (paste into stills)

daylight interior. Big, plausible, salon-real teased or extended hair — the
largest thing about the person, never larger than the person. Doors, seats,
turnstiles, ceilings, lamps at real size. Awkward negative space where a face
or an eye-line should be. Neon magenta and cyan only in the gaps between
strands, never as a logo. No lip sync — nobody is singing. Hair holds the beat.
