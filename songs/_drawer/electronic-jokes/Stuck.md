STYLE:

Sincere 2010s Dutch hardstyle, 150 BPM, B minor, about two and a half minutes. Massive distorted reverse-bass hardstyle kick, a euphoric supersaw lead, an epic orchestral breakdown with choir pads, and a long climax build with a pitch-rising snare roll. Lead one: dry, slightly raspy, flat, singing euphoric festival lyrics with no excitement at all. Lead two: bright, conspiratorial, one short line near the end. Each section of kicks sounds like they're hitting sheet metal. After the final build there is one bar of silence, a single soft metal tap, and then a cascade of small packets falling into a tray, before the last drop. Huge, euphoric, sincere, no parody, no other comedy sound effects. Use Rubella and Lillith voices.

NOTES:

**Callbacks:**
- **"The coil does not negotiate."** The vending machine's own lock from the bank, sung as the
  hook of verse 1.
- **"Lillith gets the working machine."** Rubella already has a dying device. Here the
  machine refuses Rubella's kicks and works for Lillith's one tap: everything falls out, not
  just the crisps.
- **"It's not broken. It's early."** Lillith's line about the ping-pong table, said about the
  machine, flipped. Nobody rules on which it was.

**Do-not:** no real snack or vending brand. No breaking glass; Rubella kicks, the machine
just hums. Not the Two Left fish-for-a-life machine.
