## The drop gag

**Drop 1 (05):** the sweetest kids' tune in the world gets shoved off the ring mid-bar, and the death metal is so loud the ladies fall out of reality and come back down as cartoons. Their faces don't change, only how they're drawn. It's funny because the music goes from lullaby to growl in one frame and the ladies treat it as weather.

**The snap-back (13):** at peak chaos, the delivery man in his crossing-guard uniform holds up a stop sign. A whole cartoon death-metal war freezes in mid-air so he can cross the ring with a pizza. He doesn't look at anyone. Then the kids' tune wins for good. For kids: *a snake band takes over the bouncy castle and only the pizza man can make them stop.*

**Kid-words laugh:** *every time the snake band plays, everyone turns into a cartoon.*
