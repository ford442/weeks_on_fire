# Log Drum

**Genre collision:** amapiano (113 BPM, the bouncing log-drum bass, shakers, soft jazz piano chords) × lumberjack fiddle folk (a stomping fiddle, foot stomps on wood, a jaw harp).

**Visual concept:** It's golden hour in a forest clearing by a river. Rubella sits on a tree stump with a vintage mic, and Lillith plays guitar on the next stump. A beaver rolls one log into the clearing, stands it on end and taps it with his tail, and the deep, bouncing log-drum bass comes in (the picture plays the bass). More beavers arrive with more logs, and each drop makes the drum kit bigger: a row of logs like a marimba, then a river full of floating logs bobbing on the bassline, then a beaver dam built as a giant drum riser.
