## What the blocks lock (and why)

- **Uniform #1, red.** Halloween is not an excuse for a costume; on Halloween he wears the red
  uniform and that *is* the costume joke. Another uniform needs its own block, not an edit here.
- **The name tag is never legible.** It is in every block as turned edge-on or blown out by light.
  This also keeps image models from inventing text on his chest.
- **23%.** Only legible in a close-up. The number never changes. In wide and medium blocks the
  phone is "lit", not read.
- **Same shoes, same posture, same face** in every framing. He is the straightest vertical line in
  any frame. He never runs, never flinches, never looks at the thing behind him.
- **Red cap, worn straight** (Ep3 screenplay Scene 4: "Red uniform. Matching cap.").
- **Not wise, post-surprise.** Avoid "serene", "enlightened", "knowing". Tired, correct, neutral.
- **The box is held level at chest height** (bag at chest height is the running form; the box is
  the Ep3 baseline). Extra napkins may show; the receipt pad is never signed.
- **Age** (late twenties) is a prompt anchor from the issue brief, not a bible fact.
- **Never** in the same exiting frame as the Black Cat; one of them has to stay.
