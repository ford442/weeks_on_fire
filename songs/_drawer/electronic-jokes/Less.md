STYLE:

Sincere 2000s Berlin minimal techno, 124 BPM, F minor, about three minutes. Starts dense and clicky: a round kick, shuffled clicky hi-hats, a rimshot, a dubby chord stab with long delay, a rubbery bassline, granular clicks, shaker, a filtered vocal chop. One female voice: dry, slightly raspy, flat, close-mic, speaking single words. Every time she speaks, exactly one element of the track is removed and never returns, in the order of the lyric. By the end only the kick remains, and on the last word the kick stops too, leaving silence. Precise, cold, hypnotic, sincere, no parody, no comedy sound effects. Use the Rubella voice.

NOTES:

**The structure is the joke.** Each word removes one thing. From section 6 on, the things
she removes aren't in the audio any more (lights, DJ, walls, a city), so the track has
nothing left to take away and just keeps the kick. The picture does the rest. The last
word, *less*, removes the kick.

**Production rule:** the removals must happen exactly on her words and never come back. If
MiniMax brings elements back later, regenerate with "removed elements never return."

**Do-not:** no real club or DJ names. "Berlin" is the one place name, and it's the genre's
home, said as a thing she removes.
