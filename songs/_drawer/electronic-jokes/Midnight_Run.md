STYLE:

Sincere 2010s synthwave and outrun anthem, 118 BPM, A minor, about two minutes forty. Driving arpeggiated analog bassline, gated reverb snare, big tom fills, lush polysynth pads, a soaring saw lead, a saxophone solo, and one sound of a small electric motor revving used as the "engine." Lead one: bright, conspiratorial, singing a heroic night-drive anthem with full commitment. Lead two: dry, slightly raspy, flat, speaking short lines in the gaps. A sax solo in the middle, a key change up a half step into the last chorus, and an ending where everything stops and the small motor beeps twice and powers down. Neon, nostalgic, glossy, sincere, no parody. Use Lillith and Rubella voices.

RUBELLA: It's stuck under the radiator.

NOTES:

**Callback:** Lillith's repairs with parts from the **weather-predicting ping-pong table**
work for exactly **one glorious night**. The vacuum tube on top is from that table; it does
one heroic midnight run, and it ends wedged under the radiator. "Twelve miles an hour" is the
outrun boast. A robot vacuum can't do that. This one can, tonight.

**Do-not:** no real vacuum brand. The cat in verse 1 is "the cat's old bed," not a cat in
shot (and never the Black Cat).
