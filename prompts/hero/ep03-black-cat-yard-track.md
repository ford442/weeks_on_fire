# H05 · Black cat low track through the Halloween yard (Ep3 open)

**Shot:** H05

**Geography (all three framings):** the cat walks from the porch side of the yard toward the
garden shed at the far end. The camera is at cat height, 15–25 cm off the grass, tracking with
her. This is **before the click** (0:35): 117 Elmwood's caged porch bulb is still dark, so the warm
tungsten in frame comes from the neighbors' porch bulbs over the fence and the jack-o'-lanterns.
The battle has not started; the only cyan and magenta in the yard is eye-glow deep in the bushes
by the shed. The plastic skeleton is still zip-tied to its fence post.

**Callbacks spent (as image, not dialog):** the porch-light contract with the neighborhood (every
house but this one is lit) · the Black Cat's one fur-puff (C only) · EyeWash always on somewhere
(the TV through the screen door) · the hay bale she will hide behind.
