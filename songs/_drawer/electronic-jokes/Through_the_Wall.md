STYLE:

Sincere late-1990s French-touch filter house, 124 BPM, G minor, about two minutes forty. A chopped, looped funk-disco guitar and string sample, a punchy four-on-the-floor kick, a disco bassline, claps, and the whole track running through a heavy low-pass filter so it sounds like a party heard through a wall. Three times the filter sweeps fully open for exactly four bars, bright and loud, then snaps shut again. Lead one: dry, slightly raspy, completely clear and unfiltered, close-mic, singing quietly in her room. Lead two: bright, conspiratorial, singing the party hook, muffled under the filter except during the four open bars. The song ends with the filter shut and the party fading. Sincere, groovy, no parody, no comedy sound effects except one door handle click before each open section. Use Rubella and Lillith voices.

NOTES:

**The trick:** a filter sweep is filter house's whole signature. Here it's literal: closed
door, muffled; open door, bright. Each open section is introduced by a door-handle click and
lasts four bars. The third open section is Rubella opening it herself.
