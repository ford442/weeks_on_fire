STYLE:

Vocal hocket, a cappella, 104 BPM, about one minute. Two female voices, close-mic, dry, no reverb tail, no instruments, no percussion, no claps, no beatbox. The cell is four short syllables, one per eighth note: tih, kah, tah, koh. The cell is split so no singer ever has two syllables in a row. Lillith sings tih on E and koh on E. Rubella sings kah on A. Nobody sings tah. Lillith panned slightly left, Rubella slightly right. Clipped consonants, short vowels, no slides, no harmony, no unison. Structural flaw: "tah" has no singer, so every cell has a one-eighth hole in the third slot. The hole stays empty on every pass. Nobody fills it, no breath or click covers it, and it is never double-tracked. Loop the cell for the whole track, add no variation, and stop cleanly on koh. No imitation, no canon, no late entry, no low entry, no third voice, no bed, no marimba, no mallets, no Marble. Mood: a dry relay with one runner missing.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: "tah" has no singer. The split is Lillith, Rubella, nobody, Lillith, so the alternation stays intact while the cell keeps a hole in it. The hole is written and never filled.

Callbacks:

- **Spy vs. Spy (Lillith in black, Rubella in white).** It's two figures handing the same object back and forth across a gap. Neither one looks at the gap.
