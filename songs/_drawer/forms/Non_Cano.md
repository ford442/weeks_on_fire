# Non Cano — drawer sketch

**Drawer.** `promote?: no`. Cousin of *Unspent*, not a rewrite. Farm #18 / #33. Not #42.

| field | value |
|---|---|
| form | lied, strophic, soprano and piano |
| text | Clavis in sera iacet. / Nemo eam vertit. / Ianua scit. / Non cano. |
| meaning (not sung) | The key lies in the lock. No one turns it. The door knows. I do not sing. |
| who | Nilsson and piano |
| flaw | piano cadences one bar early; stanza 3 withholds the last line |

STYLE:

Latin lied for lyric soprano and piano, 63 BPM, 4/4, D minor, strophic, about two minutes. Ecclesiastical Latin, one note per syllable, no English, no translation, no melisma, no cadenza, no high note actually sung, no belting. One small room, soft pedal, no kit, no strings, no choir. Christine Nilsson, intimate, reading a quatrain she was handed. She is not a resident and not the Christina wooden-house lane. Do not sing her name. Stanzas 1 and 2 use the same melody. Stanza 3 uses it and stops before the last line. Structural flaw: in every stanza the piano finishes its cadence one bar before the voice arrives at the last line. She does not hurry. On stanza 3 she does not sing "Non cano" at all. The piano has already cadenced, early, into silence. Do not fill the bar. Do not add a fourth stanza.

NOTES:

Same machine as *Unspent*. The withheld line is the title, and it is Latin, so the refusal is in the form rather than in an English punch. Syllables: CLA-vis in SE-ra YA-ket. NE-mo E-am VER-tit. Ya-NU-a skit. Non CA-no.

Not a cabaret ballad. Not Christina's aria. Runtime about two minutes.
