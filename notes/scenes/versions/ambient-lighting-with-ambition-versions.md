## V1 — Full Host Commitment

**Status:** alt
**Beats covered:** B
**Register:** the wrong channel, all the way up
**What it adds:** Lillith talks past Rubella to a camera that is not there, with
segment-break cadence. Funnier; riskier. The parent's version keeps her sincere,
which is warmer. Pick one — do not blend, half-commitment is the one failure mode
this family has.
**Risks:** If she is *performing*, the scene stops being a love scene.

### B — Sourcing (V1)

```
                         LILLITH
          Now, a lot of people, when they see a dark
          letter, they want to replace the whole sign.
          (holding up the tube)
          We're not going to do that, because the whole
          sign is eleven hundred dollars and this is four
          dollars and it came out of a table.

                         RUBELLA
          Who are you talking to.

                         LILLITH
                    (not breaking)
          After the break: voltage.

                         RUBELLA
          There's no break. There's a hallway.
```

## V2 — Rubella Counts

**Status:** alt
**Beats covered:** E
**What it adds:** She admits she counted the forty seconds. One word different,
entirely different scene — softer, more available, less Rubella.
**Risks:** Spends the relationship's whole account in one line. Parent's "I wasn't
counting" is the better lie and everyone in the corridor knows it is a lie.

### E — Forty Seconds (V2)

```
                         RUBELLA
          Forty-one.

                         LILLITH
          You counted.

                         RUBELLA
          I hold ladders and I count. That's the job I
          gave myself.
```
