# Scene Suggestions & Script Starters

Collect, develop, and iterate on new scene ideas and script fragments here. 
When an idea is solid, promote it into an episode folder (create `scenes.md` or integrate into `synopsis.md`).

Single-still / newspaper-comic gags live in [`one-panel-gags.md`](one-panel-gags.md) — eight animation-ready panels, not full scenes.

## How to Use
Copy the template below for each new scene idea. Fill in as much or as little as you have.

When a scene graduates to a full draft in [`scenes/`](scenes/README.md), give it a
**versions page** under [`scenes/versions/`](scenes/versions/README.md) so alternate
dialog can grow without overwriting the baseline. Copy
[`scenes/versions/_template.md`](scenes/versions/_template.md).

**Juxtaposition check:** if the scene is funny because it is the *wrong channel*
(Monster Mash on a laser-snake lawn, monkey-science lecture, sudden wordiness),
say so under **Juxtaposition**. Wrong register needs full commitment.

## Scene Idea: "Morning After the Portal"

**Scene / Beat Title:** Morning After the Portal (Suburban Cleanup Deadpan)

**Fits Episode / New Episode Concept:** Episode 3.5 or 4 — direct sequel/follow-up to the laser snake + HOA eldritch events. "The day after" tone piece.

**Setting & Atmosphere:** Quiet suburban street at golden-hour dawn the morning after Halloween. Dew on lawns. A few plastic skeletons still upright in yards. A faint purple shimmer evaporating from one driveway. Neighbors in bathrobes and yesterday's costumes half-heartedly cleaning up.

**Key Visuals (Grok Imagine prompt seeds):** Wide establishing shot of cul-de-sac with scattered folding chairs, glowing residue in a trash bag, a bored dad power-washing a sigil off his garage door, a woman calmly collecting "space rocks" into a recycling bin labeled "GLASS ONLY".

**Narrative Beats / Action:** 
- Neighbors exchange polite nods while one power-washes a smoking crater.
- Someone mentions the HOA meeting "got a little out of hand."
- A kid on a bike rides by holding a still-glowing serpent scale like it's a normal Pokemon card.
- Final beat: the pizza guy from Ep3 drives past, now delivering breakfast burritos, still completely unfazed.

**Dialogue, Voiceover or Narration (if any):** 
KAREN (holding a clipboard, robe flapping): "Item three on the emergency agenda — the purple light in the cul-de-sac is now considered a 'decorative accent' and does not require a permit. Moving on."

BRAD (power-washing): "My bin's still the wrong shade of blue, Karen."

**Emotional Tone & Themes:** Deadpan normalcy after absurdity. The quiet dignity of people who have seen cosmic horror and responded by checking the recycling schedule. Lingering unease beneath surface politeness.

**Music / Song Sync Opportunity:** Gentle, slightly off-kilter acoustic guitar or sparse piano (think "Whatever Lets Us Be" energy but at sunrise). Could work as a cold open or post-credits stinger. Low-tempo, warm but with a dissonant high note that fades like the shimmer.

**Suggested Grok Imagine Full Prompt:** 
"Golden hour dawn in a quiet suburban cul-de-sac the morning after Halloween, neighbors in bathrobes calmly cleaning up after some kind of strange event, one man power-washing a glowing sigil off his driveway, plastic skeletons still standing, faint purple residue evaporating, deadpan comedic atmosphere, cinematic lighting, Grok Imagine style"

**Variations or Alternate Takes:** 
- Make it rain lightly (more melancholic).
- Focus tighter on two neighbors having the conversation over coffee on the curb.
- End with the camera slowly pushing into the now-empty "portal" hole that has a "NO PARKING" cone in front of it.

**Status / Next Step:** Promoted as Episode 4 cold open — `episodes/episode-04/scenes.md`. Item Three is the meeting that follows, not a replacement.

**Key Visuals (Grok Imagine prompt seeds):** Two people slow-dancing between rows of dryers. One still wearing half a Halloween costume (cape half-on, smeared makeup). The other in a wrinkled work uniform. Reflections in the chrome and glass. Steam rising from an open dryer like fog.

**Narrative Beats / Action:** 
- They don't know each other. One helped the other carry a basket after a "weird night."
- A song comes on the jukebox (diegetic Minimax cue).
- They share a very slow, very serious dance as if this is the most normal thing in the world.
- A third person (the night attendant) mops around them without comment.

**Dialogue, Voiceover or Narration (if any):** 
(Almost none — mostly visual + the song. One quiet exchange mid-dance:)

STRANGER 1 (softly): "You don't have to..."
STRANGER 2: "I know. That's why I'm doing it."

**Emotional Tone & Themes:** Intimacy between strangers. The laundromat as a liminal safe space after chaos. Quiet human connection as the real music. Echoes Episode 2's half-conscious heart and Episode 1's "we're utterly bored but give me more fire" tension.

**Variations or Alternate Takes:** 
- Make the dance more awkward and funny at first, then it settles.
- Intercut with quick flashes of what they each escaped (flames in rearview, strange lights in a backyard).
- The jukebox song ends and they both pretend it didn't happen, folding laundry in silence.

**Status / Next Step:** (Idea → Draft → Ready for episode) Strong cutaway candidate. Easy to shoot / generate as a self-contained 45-60s piece. Could live in prompts/ or become its own mini episode segment.
