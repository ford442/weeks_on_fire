STYLE:

Parallel organum, a cappella, 56 BPM, D dorian, about one minute ten. Two female voices, no instruments, no drone, no percussion, no beat beyond the tactus. Latin only, one sentence: "Lux in scala manet." Strictly syllabic, one note per syllable, no melisma, no ornament, straight tone, no vibrato, no belting. Christine Nilsson, lyric soprano, is the principal voice and starts on D. Lillith is the organal voice a perfect fifth above, starting on A, and moves in exact parallel with Nilsson, note for note, same rhythm, same breath. Reverb is a short concrete stairwell, not a cathedral, not a choir hall. Sing the sentence four times with a breath between statements. Structural flaw: on the third statement Lillith's interval narrows from a fifth to a fourth, sitting on G over Nilsson's D, and she stays a fourth for the rest of the piece. Nilsson does not move. Nobody retunes. The last syllable ends on D and G with no cadence and no added chord. No English, no imitation, no canon, no third voice, no Rubella, no Gregorian choir pad, no organ, no hill, no outdoor air. Mood: two voices going up a stair in lockstep, and one step is short.

LYRICS:

[Statement 1 — Nilsson on D, Lillith a fifth above]

Lux in scala manet.

[Statement 2 — parallel fifths]

Lux in scala manet.

[Statement 3 — Lillith a fourth above, from here on]

Lux in scala manet.

[Statement 4 — parallel fourths, no correction]

Lux in scala manet.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: on the third statement the fifth becomes a fourth and stays a fourth. Lillith does not climb back to A, Nilsson does not come down to meet her, and the piece ends on the fourth.

Nobody asks Lillith to retune. This is the same mechanism as "Lillith is told no exactly once": it happens one time, nobody brings it up, and nobody asks twice.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not The Light in the Stair.** That one is imitation: staggered entries, three voices, and Rubella enters low on C. This is parallel motion with two voices entering together, there is no Rubella, and the flaw is the interval, not an entry pitch.
- **Not Christina's hill.** No outdoors, no wind, no hill. It's concrete and plaster with a short tail.
- **Not Littera Manet / Non Cano.** Different sentence, different form. Neither one is touched here.
