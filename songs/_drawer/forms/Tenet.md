STYLE:

One word, a cappella, 60 BPM, about one minute. Christine Nilsson, lyric soprano, one voice, close, straight tone, no vibrato, no belting, no instruments, no second singer. Do not sing her name. She is a voice credit, not a resident, and not the Christina wooden-house lane. The only lyric is the word Tenet, sung once per pass, on five pitches, one pitch to each letter-sound, still one word. Pass 1, forward: D, F, G, A, C. Pass 2, the same word with those pitches reversed and a whole step low: B-flat, G, F, E-flat, C. Pass 3, the forward contour again, still a whole step low: C, E-flat, F, G, B-flat. Long notes. No melisma past the five pitches of the word. No second word. Structural flaw: the backward pass is a whole step low, and the return does not lift it. Pass 3 does not go back to D. No cadence is added to fix the height. No knock, no door, no hallway, no trumpet, no Sator square, no Panama, no Madam I'm Adam, no other palindrome. Mood: the word comes back, and the pitch does not.

NOTES:

Structural flaw: the backward pass is a whole step low, and the return does not lift it. The third pass is the same word and the same forward shape, and it stays down.

The return was the pass that should have matched the first height. It comes back wrong, and nobody sings it again to put it right.

Why it is not the neighbors:
- **Not Tattarrattat.** That song owns the knock and Madam I'm Adam. This is not a hallway, not a door, and not a trumpet a half-step low. There is no second word.
- **Not Unspent.** That one is an English poem with a piano that cadences a bar early. This is one word, no piano, and the flaw is the height of the return, not a line left unsung.
- **Not Keh Tih Keh.** That card is three syllables and no English word, looped, one unnamed voice. This is the word Tenet, sung once each pass, five pitches, Nilsson.
- **Not a Sator square, not Panama.** Those names are not in the lyric. The word is already a palindrome, so nothing else is required.
