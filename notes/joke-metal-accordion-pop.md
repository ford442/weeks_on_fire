## Lane

**Joke metal.** Committed, no wink. The object is doctrine. Gang chant on the title only. One structural flaw, never corrected. 1970s–80s heavy weight is allowed. Modern djent, death growls, blast-beat wallpaper, and occult lyric are not. Not a fight song.

**Accordion pop.** Witty, poemesque, upbeat, various. They Might Be Giants as a method, not a cover and not a parody: short lines, specific nouns, a small argument with itself, deadpan sincerity, one instrument that is the subject. Rotate the bed per song (accordion, reed organ, nylon, toy piano, cheap brass). Major or mixolydian. No wink at the listener.
